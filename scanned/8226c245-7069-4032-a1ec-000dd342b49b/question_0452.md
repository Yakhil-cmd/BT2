## Question
Does `collateral-remove-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1211) let an unprivileged attacker who controls `min-underlying` reach `is-healthy-with-mask` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:663) in a state where it route a victim's mandatory payout through a principal that always rejects delivery? - Entrypoint: `collateral-remove-redeem` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1211`), unprivileged and publicly callable

- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - protocol insolvency
