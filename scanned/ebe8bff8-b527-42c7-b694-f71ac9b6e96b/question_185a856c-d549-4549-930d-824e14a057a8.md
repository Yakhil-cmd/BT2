[Function: liquidate] A borrows in grace-protected asset X and, right when grace is due to expire, submits a fresh `borrow` for a trivial extra amount of X (updating `last-borrow-block`) purely to re-trigger `ERR-LIQUIDATION-BORROW-SAME-BLOCK` for one more block against any liquidator who queued a transaction for the expiry block. Victim B is that liquidator/supplier-protector. Invariant: ORDERING NEUTRALITY. Impact: High, temporary freezing (one additional block, repeatable) of B's ability to liquidate a position that is otherwise eligible the instant grace ends. Proof: simnet, mine grace-expiry block with A's borrow vs without, assert B's `liquidate` call outcome differs.
