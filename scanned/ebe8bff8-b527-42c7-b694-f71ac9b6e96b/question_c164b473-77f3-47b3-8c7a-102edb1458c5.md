[Function: liquidate] A structures a position where debt asset X is graced and collateral asset Z is a low-liquidity asset whose `cap-supply`/available liquidity is thin. Victim B is a supplier of Z. Because A's position can't be liquidated during grace, other legitimate borrowers who want to withdraw Z-denominated collateral (competing for the same capped liquidity) get crowded out, and when grace finally ends, Z's price may have moved further against A, so B's Z-vault absorbs a bad-debt writedown larger than B ever priced in when supplying. Invariant: SEIZURE BOUND. Impact: Critical, protocol insolvency. Proof: simnet test comparing Z-vault's realized bad debt with grace active vs a control run without grace.
