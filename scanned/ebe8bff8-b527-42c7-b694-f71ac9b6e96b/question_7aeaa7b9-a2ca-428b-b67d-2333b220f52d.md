Invariant: SEIZURE BOUND. Impact: Critical, protocol insolvency - B's recoverable share shrinks purely due to the enforced delay. Proof: simnet, compute recoverable collateral at the LTV present when grace started vs at the LTV present when grace ends, assert the shortfall lands on B's vault balance.
