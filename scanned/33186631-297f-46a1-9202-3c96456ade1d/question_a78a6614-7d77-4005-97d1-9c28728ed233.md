[File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar -> Scope: High, temporary freezing of funds] [Function: system-borrow] Victim B is a borrower who wants to `borrow` USDC to refinance an expiring loan taken from a different, higher-interest vault (a cross-vault refinance the protocol design intends to allow). Attacker A borrows the remaining `CAP-DEBT` headroom on the USDC vault the block before B's refinance transaction. B's refinance `borrow` reverts with `ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED`, forcing B to remain on the more expensive loan and incur additional interest cost for the delay, a temporary freezing of B's ability to access cheaper liquidity B was otherwise entitled to under B's collateral/LTV. Proof idea: simnet, two accounts; show B's refinance borrow succeeds in isolation but fails when interleaved after A's cap-filling borrow in the same block.
