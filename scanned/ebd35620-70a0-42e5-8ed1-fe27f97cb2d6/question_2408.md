## Question
Does `collateral-remove-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1211) let an unprivileged attacker who controls remaining zToken collateral whose price moves with the redeem reach `unpack-u16` (mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:259) in a state where it make a victim's position resolve to a worse efficiency group than it chose?
