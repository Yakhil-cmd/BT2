Preconditions: Shipit has at least two stacks/repositories configured under the org whose `webhook_secret` will verify the payload, and a `Commit` row with the colliding `sha` already exists in the victim stack. Binding that breaks: the repository named in the verified payload == the repository owning the `Commit` row mutated; here it does not hold. Scoped impact: Critical, cross-repository/cross-tenant `Status` row write on a stack the attacker never touched.
