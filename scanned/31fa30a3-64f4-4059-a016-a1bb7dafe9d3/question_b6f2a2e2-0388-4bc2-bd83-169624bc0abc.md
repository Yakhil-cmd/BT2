[Function: system-borrow] Attacker A opens a large borrow to push `total-debt` right up against `CAP-DEBT`, then never repays. Victim B, a borrower who needs to increase their borrow to avoid a health-factor threshold breach (defensive top-up) submits `system-borrow` for a small top-up amount in the following block; B's transaction reverts ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED. Because B cannot top up, B's position is later liquidated for undercollateralization that B could have avoided had the cap not been externally saturated. Invariant broken: NON-INTERFERENCE and indirectly SEIZURE BOUND is put at risk since B's forced liquidation was caused by A occupying `cap-debt`, not by B's own risk choice. Impact: temporary freezing of funds (denial of B's defensive borrow window). Proof: simnet test showing B's defensive `system-borrow` succeeds in isolation but reverts when A's cap-saturating borrow precedes it in the same block.
