Victim B's `borrow` later in the same block skips a real accrual entirely, so newly-accrued interest that should be split among suppliers is never charged to B's debt for that block, permanently lost to the supplier side. Sequence: block N: (1) A's transaction sets last-update = stacks-block-time; (2) B borrows/repays under a no-op accrue. Impact: High - permanent freezing/theft of unclaimed yield for that block's worth of interest.
