Preconditions: `delegate-stx` op accepted and reflected in `ustx-delegated-per-cycle` before the delegatee ever calls `stake`/`register-for-bond` to actually lock the STX. Impact: Critical, unlocked/never-locked STX inflates threshold and signer weight allocation system-wide, and if any weight/slot is attributed to the attacker's delegation record specifically, that is an unbacked signing-weight credit. Proof idea: integration test issuing a `delegate-stx` burnchain op without a following successful `stake` lock, then asserting `get-total-ustx-stacked` for that cycle still reflects the delegated amount despite no corresponding pox-locking lock existing.
