[File: mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar, Scope: Critical] Function: accrue-and-cache / repay. Attacker A calls repay(aid=USDC, amount=large, on-behalf-of=A) right after opening a large borrow in the prior block, then immediately calls a second borrow(aid=USDC, small) within the same block N before victim B's already-queued deposit(aid=USDC,...) lands, so accrue-and-cache caches index/lindex at the transient near-zero-utilization instant A created between repay and re-borrow. Sequence: [Block N: A repay() -> A borrow() small -> cache set at low utilization] -> [Block N: B deposit()]. Invariant: ORDERING NEUTRALITY / NON-INTERFERENCE. B's supply-side interest accrual for the block is computed off a momentary utilization A engineered and then abandoned. Impact: theft of unclaimed yield from B (existing suppliers who would have accrued at the pre-repay utilization). Proof: simnet test comparing B's index-cache-derived lindex delta when B deposits alone in a block vs. after A's repay+re-borrow sequence in the same block.
