[File: mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar, Scope: Critical] Function: vault-accrue via accrue-and-cache feeding calc-utilization's available-liquidity term. Attacker A calls redeem(aid=stSTX, amount=X, recipient=A) first in block N, reducing available assets and spiking utilization, which is cached for the block. Victim B calls borrow(aid=stSTX, amount=Y) afterward in the same block, sized so that under the true (pre-A) available liquidity it would be healthy and accepted at rate R, but under A's cached post-redeem utilization the same borrow is priced at a materially higher rate R' baked into the index B is charged for the rest of the loan's life (since B's opening scaled-debt is computed against index at borrow time). Sequence: [Block N: A redeem(X)] -> [Block N: B borrow(Y)]. Invariant: NON-INTERFERENCE. Impact: theft of unclaimed yield - B's future interest payments are permanently higher than the rate B would have received absent A's same-block redeem, and that excess flows to suppliers/A rather than being a rate B ever should have been quoted. Proof: simnet test comparing B's opening borrow-index/rate when B borrows alone vs. immediately after A's redeem in the same block.
