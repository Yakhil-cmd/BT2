Attacker A calls borrow(aid=USDC, amount=X) first in block N to move USDC vault utilization, priming index-cache for USDC before victim B calls liquidate-redeem(borrower=C, collateral-ft=zUSDC,...) against an unrelated third party C. B's evaluation of C's zUSDC collateral price and C's health factor is computed off the cache A set, not off the state B expects, potentially causing B's liquidation to revert (ERR path) or execute at the wrong seize amount if C's health straddles the liquidation threshold depending on which utilization is used. Sequence: [Block N: A borrow() primes cache] -> [Block N: B liquidate-redeem(C,...)]. Invariant: EVALUABILITY / SEIZURE BOUND. Impact: temporary freezing of funds for B - B's liquidation attempt on a genuinely unhealthy C can fail or seize an incorrect amount purely due to A's block-ordering choice, delaying B's ability to execute the liquidation until a future block. Proof: simnet test asserting liquidate-redeem's outcome (success/amount) for the same C differs when A's borrow precedes B's call vs. when B calls alone.
