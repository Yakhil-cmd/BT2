Preconditions: staker has an active L1-lock bond membership; new bond accepts sBTC path. Call sequence: `register-for-bond` computes `sats-total` from the sBTC amount, checks `total-balance >= amount-ustx`, transfers in `new-sbtc` sats via `roll-sbtc`, then returns `(ok {amount-ustx, sats-total, ...})`; `handle_lockup_pox_v5` rolls the STX lock to `amount-ustx` via `pox_rollover_v5`. Equality: STX locked after the rollover (`amount_locked()`) == `amount-ustx` the contract validated against `total-balance` AND the sBTC now custodied (`total-sbtc-staked`/contract's sBTC balance) == `sats-total` for the SAME bond membership — i.e. STX and sBTC commitments for one `protocol-bond-memberships` entry must both reflect only this transaction's validated amounts, no residual credit from the abandoned L1 bond's un-transferred BTC.
