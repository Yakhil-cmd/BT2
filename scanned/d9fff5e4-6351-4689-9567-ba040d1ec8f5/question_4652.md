## Question
Does `collateral-remove` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1107) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the set of assets held reach `interpolate-rate` (mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:196) in a state where it push a third party's position past a fold bound so every evaluation of it aborts?
