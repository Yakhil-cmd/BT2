## Question
Does `supply-collateral-add` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1175) let an unprivileged attacker who controls `amount` reach `normalize-pyth` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:297) in a state where it make a victim's position resolve to a worse efficiency group than it chose? - Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of another user's collateral
