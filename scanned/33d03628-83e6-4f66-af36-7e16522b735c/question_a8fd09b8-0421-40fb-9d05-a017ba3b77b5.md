`stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar` `register-for-bond`'s `verify-l1-lockups` path computes `sats-total` from a caller-supplied Bitcoin timelock proof (`btc-lockup` `l1-lockups`), and `min-ustx-for-sats-amount sats-total ...` gates the required `amount-ustx`, but the bond's `stx-value-ratio`/`min-ustx-ratio` are captured once at `setup-bond` and used for every registrant regardless of when their individual L1 proof's timelock was confirmed. Can a staker submit an L1 lockup proof whose underlying Bitcoin timelock was confirmed long before `setup-bond`'s ratio was set (e.g., sats locked at a historically higher BTC/STX rate), so `min-ustx-for-sats-amount` computed against the *current* stale `stx-value-ratio` requires less STX than the sats are worth by the ratio active when the lockup was actually created, letting the staker under-collateralize STX relative to true sats value while still gaining full `weight`/reward-slot credit for the sats amount? Equality broken: sats credited by the L1 lockup proof translated through `min-ustx-for-sats-amount` == sats locked in the confirmed Bitcoin timelock valued at a ratio contemporaneous with, not stale relative to, the staker's actual commitment. Scoped impact: High, the staker's signer gains reward slots/weight backed by fewer effectively-committed STX than the bond's economic design intends. Proof idea: Clarity/Rust test in `contrib/core-contract-tests` setting `stxValueRatio` at bond setup, then registering with an L1 proof and confirming `minUstxForSats`-derived `amountUstx` never needs revalidation against a later or differently-priced ratio for the same sats (LOCK_CONSERVATION).
