## Question
Does `supply-collateral-add` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1175) let an unprivileged attacker who controls vault share price at the moment of the deposit leg reach `price-resolve` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:373) in a state where it make a victim's position resolve to a worse efficiency group than it chose?
