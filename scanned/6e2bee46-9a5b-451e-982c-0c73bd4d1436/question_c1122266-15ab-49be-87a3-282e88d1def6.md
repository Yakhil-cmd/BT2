[File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-sbtc.clar -> Scope: High, temporary freezing of funds] [Function: system-borrow] Victim B is a borrower whose position, while healthy, needs to `borrow` more sBTC in a later block to avoid becoming under-collateralized after a price move (a legitimate protective top-up). Attacker A, an unrelated borrower, calls `borrow` for the remaining `CAP-DEBT` headroom in the block immediately before B's protective top-up. B's transaction reverts with `ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED`, and B's position subsequently becomes eligible for liquidation purely because A's unrelated borrow denied B the buffer B needed. This is a NON-INTERFERENCE violation: A's borrow, which does not touch B's collateral or debt records, nonetheless determines whether B can protect B's own position. B loses the temporary ability to top-up borrow and risks being liquidated as a direct, provable consequence. Proof idea: simnet, two accounts; without A's borrow, B's top-up succeeds and B stays healthy after a simulated price drop; with A's borrow first in the same block, B's identical top-up reverts and B becomes liquidatable.
