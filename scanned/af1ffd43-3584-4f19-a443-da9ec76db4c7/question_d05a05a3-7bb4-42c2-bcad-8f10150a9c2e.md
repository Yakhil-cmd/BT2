LIQUIDATION GRACE IS RESOLVED PER ASSET AND THE ATTACKER PICKS THE ASSET.'] [Function: liquidate] Attacker A (borrower) opens a position with 100%% of debt drawn in asset X (e.g. sBTC) at a moment when the DAO has a legitimately-scoped `liquidation-grace-periods` entry live for asset X (set for an unrelated incident), and collateralizes with asset Y (e.g. STX) via `collateral-add`. Block 1: A borrows X against Y collateral. Block 2: Y's price craters (oracle feed update), making the position deeply undercollateralized. Block 3: victim B, an ordinary liquidator/other party, or the protocol itself, cannot recover funds because `liquidate` calls `(is-liquidation-paused debt-aid)` with debt-aid = X's id, which is true, so ERR-LIQUIDATION-PAUSED fires regardless of how underwater the Y collateral is. Victim B is a supplier who deposited Y into `v0-vault-*` for asset Y and expected normal liquidation discipline to protect vault solvency; B cannot withdraw supplied Y for the duration of the freeze and, once grace ends, receives a haircut via `socialize-debt-asset` if collateral is insufficient. This breaks SANCTIONED WRITES ONLY / EVALUABILITY (the position cannot be liquidated at all despite being liquidatable) causing protocol insolvency, the Critical impact borne by B.
