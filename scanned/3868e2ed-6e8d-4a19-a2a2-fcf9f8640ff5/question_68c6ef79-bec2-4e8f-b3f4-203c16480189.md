Can a staker whose old bond was an L1 lock (not sBTC) manipulate `old-sbtc`'s source value so `roll-sbtc` sends real sBTC out of the contract's reserve while the staker's bond was never actually backed by contract-custodied sBTC (only by an L1 Bitcoin timelock)? Impact: Critical, theft of protocol-held sBTC reserve if `old-sbtc` is read from stale/incorrect state for L1 bonds.
