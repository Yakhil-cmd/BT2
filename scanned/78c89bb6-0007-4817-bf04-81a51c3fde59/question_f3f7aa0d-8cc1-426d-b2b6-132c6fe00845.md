Binding: a token's validity window and scope must equal the operator's intended single-purpose grant; here it is unbounded in time and (per the two bugs above) unbounded in stack scope. Impact: Critical, permanent unauthenticated-equivalent read access to any stack once a single CCMenu URL leaks.
