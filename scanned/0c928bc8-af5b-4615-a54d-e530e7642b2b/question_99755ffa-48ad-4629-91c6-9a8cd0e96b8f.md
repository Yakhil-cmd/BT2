Binding: `repository.owner.login` verified by `webhook_secret` must equal the organization owning the `ReviewStack` resolved from `repository.full_name`. Impact: Critical, unauthorized deprovision/archive of another organization's live deployed stack (denial-of-service on real infra, unauthorized stack mutation).
