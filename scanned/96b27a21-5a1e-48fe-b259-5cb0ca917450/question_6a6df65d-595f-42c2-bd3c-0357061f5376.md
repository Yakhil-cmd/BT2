Attacker action: attacker's cross-site page auto-submits a state-changing form (PUT /merge_status/victim-org/victim-repo/master/pull/1) or a cross-site fetch with credentials:'include' targeting Shipit while a victim operator's session cookie is active. Proof idea: middleware unit test (test/middleware/same_site_cookie_middleware_test.rb pattern) confirming SameSite=None is applied, combined with a controller test performing a PUT to enqueue_merge_request_path with a valid session cookie but a forged/absent authenticity token and cross-origin Origin header, asserting whether protect_from_forgery actually rejects it.
