Target: Shipit::SameSiteCookieMiddleware#call cookie rewrite scope (`Rack::Request.new(env).ssl?` gate, applies to ALL Set-Cookie headers, not just the session cookie). Attacker action: after any authenticated action sets an additional cookie (e.g., a flash or CSRF-related cookie) over HTTPS, an attacker's cross-site page issues a request that receives that cookie back via a forged form submission, exploiting the blanket 'SameSite=None; Secure' rewrite applied indiscriminately. Preconditions: enable_samesite_middleware? true; any Shipit response sets more than one cookie. Call sequence: SameSiteCookieMiddleware#call iterates every cookie in headers['Set-Cookie'] and force-appends SameSite=None to each without distinguishing session/auth cookies from others, and without checking whether that specific cookie should ever be sent cross-site. Binding: the set of cookies intended to be scoped to same-site requests == the set of cookies actually marked SameSite=None; this middleware collapses that distinction to empty for every cookie the app issues. Scope: High, broadens CSRF exposure across all state-changing engine routes that rely on cookie-based auth (e.g., MergeStatusController#enqueue/#dequeue), not just one. Proof idea: middleware test setting two distinct cookies in one response and asserting both are unconditionally rewritten to SameSite=None with no per-cookie opt-out.
