`MergeRequest.request_merge!` (merge_request.rb) calls `merge_request.retry!` and `schedule_refresh!`, and downstream `RefreshMergeRequestJob` calls `refresh!` which calls `head.refresh_statuses!` (a properly stack.github_api-scoped call) — but this refresh happens asynchronously, leaving a window where `all_status_checks_passed?` reads whatever `Status` rows exist, including ones injected by `StatusHandler#process` cross-repo, before the authoritative `refresh!` overwrites them. Can `ProcessMergeRequestsJob` observe and act on the forged status during that window, admitting/merging before the legitimate refresh corrects it? Binding: timing of the authoritative per-stack `refresh_statuses!` call == timing of `ProcessMergeRequestsJob`'s read of `all_status_checks_passed?`; if the forged cross-repo status lands first and is read first, the binding is violated. Impact: Critical race enabling temporary unauthorized merge-queue admission from forged status data. Proof: minitest injecting the cross-repo status then immediately invoking `ProcessMergeRequestsJob.perform_now` before any `refresh!`, asserting the merge request advances.
