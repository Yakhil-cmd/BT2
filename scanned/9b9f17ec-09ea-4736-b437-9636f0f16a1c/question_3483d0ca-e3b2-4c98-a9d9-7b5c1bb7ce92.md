[Function: repay] Victim B holds debt in three assets forming a mask that resolves, via get-egroup, to the highest-liquidity-discount egroup (lowest liq-penalty-max). Attacker A targets whichever of B's three debts has decayed to scaled-debt=1 (checking all three via read-only calls) and dust-repays on-behalf-of=B to remove exactly the bit that keeps B in that favourable egroup, since egroup resolution in this design is all-or-nothing on the full bitmask rather than independently scoring assets. CALL SEQUENCE: single block, A's targeted 1-unit repay. INVARIANT BROKEN: EVALUABILITY / NON-INTERFERENCE - B's position now evaluates under an egroup B never chose based on an intentional debt/collateral basket, purely from A zeroing the smallest remaining balance. IMPACT: Critical, if followed by A's liquidate in the same block, or High as temporary freezing of B's preferred egroup borrowing capacity otherwise. Proof: simnet test enumerating B's three debts, identifying the dust one, having A dust-repay it, and asserting get-egroup(B's mask) returns a different group id with worse liq-penalty-max or LTV table than before.
