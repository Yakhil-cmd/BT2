[Function: repay] Victim B's collateral asset Z requires egroup-XY's LTV to remain above B's current debt-to-collateral ratio by a thin margin (B is close to but above the health threshold). Attacker A calls repay ft-X amount=1 on-behalf-of=B, shifting B to egroup-Y-only whose LTV table for Z is strictly lower, flipping is-healthy from true to false without any price movement or B action. CALL SEQUENCE: single block. INVARIANT BROKEN: NON-INTERFERENCE. IMPACT: Critical - A immediately calls liquidate(B, Z, X-or-Y, ...) in the same block, seizing Z collateral from a position that was healthy one transaction earlier. Proof: simnet test computing is-healthy for B before A's tx and confirming liquidate reverts with ERR-UNHEALTHY-if-still-healthy-check is absent (liquidate only requires B unhealthy, not healthy-before-A); assert the unhealthy state is solely caused by A's repay by re-running without A's tx and observing liquidate revert.
