# Q2946: convert_interest_bearing_config confuses account types or owners (parse_token_extension.rs)

## Question
Can an unprivileged attacker entering through one JSON-RPC call or websocket subscription from a single client, at most once per CLUSTER_SLOT_TIME_TARGET/2 reach `convert_interest_bearing_config` in `account-decoder/src/parse_token_extension.rs` with a truncated or over-long encoding whose declared length disagrees with its real length, and have `convert_interest_bearing_config` deserialize an account of the wrong owner or discriminant as valid, so that the invariant "`convert_interest_bearing_config` validates owner and discriminant before trusting deserialized content." breaks and the result is Loss of Funds?

## Target
- File/function: `account-decoder/src/parse_token_extension.rs` -> `convert_interest_bearing_config()` (around line 226)
- Entrypoint: one JSON-RPC call or websocket subscription from a single client, at most once per CLUSTER_SLOT_TIME_TARGET/2
- Attacker controls: a truncated or over-long encoding whose declared length disagrees with its real length
- Exploit idea: Pass an account of a different type/owner that `convert_interest_bearing_config` deserializes successfully, so a later check trusts the wrong discriminant.
- Invariant to test: `convert_interest_bearing_config` validates owner and discriminant before trusting deserialized content.
- Expected Immunefi impact: Loss of Funds - theft or creation of lamports/tokens without the owner's signature (6,250-25,000 SOL)
- Fast validation: Feed every other account layout into `convert_interest_bearing_config` and assert each is rejected rather than silently reinterpreted.
