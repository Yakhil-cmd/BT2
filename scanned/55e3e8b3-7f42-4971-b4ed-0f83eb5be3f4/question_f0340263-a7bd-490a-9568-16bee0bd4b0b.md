[Function: system-borrow] Attacker A, holding no genuine collateral need, borrows the maximum amount permitted by their own posted collateral specifically timed to coincide with the block window B relies on for a time-sensitive refinance forced by an approaching liquidation threshold (B's health factor is close to the liquidation boundary and B needs an emergency `system-borrow`-backed top-up of a different, healthier collateral type routed through the same vault's debt cap). Because A's borrow occupies `CAP-DEBT`, B's emergency top-up reverts and B is liquidated in a subsequent block by a third party at the standard penalty. Invariant broken: NON-INTERFERENCE — B's liquidation outcome was influenced by A's unrelated cap-occupying borrow, not solely B's own risk. Impact: temporary freezing of funds (denial of B's emergency borrow window that could have avoided or reduced the liquidation). Proof: simnet test showing B's emergency `system-borrow` succeeds when submitted alone near the liquidation threshold, and reverts when A's cap-filling borrow is mined first in the same block, followed by B's position becoming liquidatable.
