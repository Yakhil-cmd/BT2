[Function: system-borrow] Attacker A borrows the exact remaining `(- CAP-DEBT debt)` amount computed by reading `get-debt` immediately before submission, in the same block as victim B's own `system-borrow` call for a modest top-up that would have fit under the cap had A not raced ahead. Invariant broken: ORDERING NEUTRALITY. Impact: temporary freezing of funds for B for that block. Proof: simnet test with A dynamically sizing its borrow from a live `get-debt`/`get-cap-debt` read to consume all headroom, then asserting B's top-up borrow submitted in the same block reverts with ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED, versus succeeding when tested without A's interference.
