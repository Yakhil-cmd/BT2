Precondition: `GithubAuthenticationController` is `ActionController::Base` with no `protect_from_forgery` (unlike `ShipitController#protect_from_forgery with: :exception`). Scoped impact: Critical, authentication/login bypass — victim unknowingly acts under, or is later impersonated as, the attacker's identity for stack deploys. Proof idea: ActionController::TestCase POSTing `:callback` without an authenticity token present and asserting the request is processed (no `ActionController::InvalidAuthenticityToken`), confirming CSRF is unenforced on the session-binding action.
