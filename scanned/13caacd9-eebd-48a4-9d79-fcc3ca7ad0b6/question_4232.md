## Question
Does `liquidate-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1604) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the borrower targeted reach `price-resolve` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:373) in a state where it push a third party's position past a fold bound so every evaluation of it aborts?
