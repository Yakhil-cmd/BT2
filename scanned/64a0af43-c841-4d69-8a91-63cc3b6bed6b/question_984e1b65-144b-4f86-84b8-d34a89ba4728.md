[Function: liquidate] A opens debt in grace-protected asset X against collateral in a DISABLED-collateral asset W whose price is only resolved on-demand in `process-collateral-asset`'s fallback branch. Victim B supplies asset W. During grace, no `liquidate` call can even reach the `process-collateral-asset` disabled-asset pricing path for A's position because the debt-side pause check reverts first. When grace ends and W has cratered further, B's disabled-collateral vault absorbs a larger shortfall than if liquidation had been possible immediately. Invariant: SEIZURE BOUND. Impact: Critical, protocol insolvency. Proof: simnet two-account test freezing W price during grace and comparing final W-vault backing to a no-grace control.
