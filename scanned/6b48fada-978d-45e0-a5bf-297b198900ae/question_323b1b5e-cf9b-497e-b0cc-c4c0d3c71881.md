[Function: system-borrow] Attacker A computes off-chain the exact `total-debt` (using `next-index`/`INDEX-PRECISION`) required to leave 0 headroom under CAP-DEBT and calls borrow(ft, amount=headroom, receiver=A) at the start of a block. Victim B is a borrower with a healthy position who is forced to close/reduce a position because B cannot draw the last unit of available debt capacity B was relying on, e.g., to avoid liquidation by adding synthetic debt-based hedging. Sequence: Block N: A occupies the last unit of CAP-DEBT headroom; Block N: B's borrow(ft, amount=1, receiver=B) reverts on ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED, preventing B from adjusting B's own exposure even by the smallest unit. Invariant broken: ORDERING NEUTRALITY. B loses: the ability to make any incremental borrow adjustment on that asset for as long as A holds the position. Impact: temporary freezing of funds (High). Proof idea: two-account Clarinet test asserting B's 1-unit borrow succeeds when run alone, but fails once A's headroom-filling borrow runs first in the same block.
