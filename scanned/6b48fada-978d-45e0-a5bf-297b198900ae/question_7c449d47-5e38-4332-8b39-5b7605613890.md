Attacker A, an ordinary borrower opening a large position through the market's borrow entrypoint, borrows an amount that leaves `(+ debt amount)` just below `CAP-DEBT`. Victim B is a second ordinary borrower who has an existing loan and wants to draw additional debt for refinancing in the same block; B's call to `system-borrow` reverts with `ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED` purely because A occupied the remaining headroom first. Invariant broken: NON-INTERFERENCE and ORDERING NEUTRALITY (B's outcome depends on transaction order within the block). B loses the ability to draw additional debt until A or another principal repays. Impact: temporary freezing of funds.
