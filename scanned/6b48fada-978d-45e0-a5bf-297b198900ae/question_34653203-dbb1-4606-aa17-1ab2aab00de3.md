[Function: system-borrow] Attacker A (acting through the market's borrow path that calls system-borrow on vault-stx) borrows an amount that pushes `total-borrowed`/`total-debt` to exactly CAP-STX-DEBT. Victim B, an existing borrower who needs to `refinance`/re-borrow additional funds against already-posted collateral in the same block, has the call revert with ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED since `(<= (+ debt amount) CAP-DEBT)` now fails. B cannot draw additional liquidity that block and must wait, and if B's position was approaching a liquidation threshold and needed a top-up to avoid it, the delay causes B to be liquidated when it otherwise would not have been. This breaks ORDERING NEUTRALITY. Impact: temporary freezing of funds (B's borrowing capacity is frozen for the block/blocks A occupies the cap). Proof: simnet test, two borrower accounts A and B with posted collateral, A calls borrow to saturate cap-debt, B's borrow call in the same block asserted to fail with ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED, while B's borrow alone (no A tx) succeeds.
