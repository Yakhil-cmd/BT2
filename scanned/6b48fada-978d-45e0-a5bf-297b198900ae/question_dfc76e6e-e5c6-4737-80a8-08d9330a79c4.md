[Function: system-borrow] Attacker A, an ordinary borrower with legitimate collateral, calls system-borrow (via market borrow) for an amount calculated to land `(+ debt amount)` at exactly CAP-STX-DEBT minus 1. Victim B is a second unprivileged borrower who, in the very next block, needs to draw a small additional amount to avoid being classified unhealthy after an oracle price update; B's borrow reverts with ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED purely because A occupied the last unit of headroom, and B is liquidated in the following block by a third party at the configured penalty, a loss B would have avoided had the cap not been artificially exhausted by A's ordering choice. This breaks ORDERING NEUTRALITY and NON-INTERFERENCE. Impact: temporary freezing of funds (B's ability to top-up/avoid liquidation is frozen for the blocks A occupies the cap). Proof: two-account simnet test simulating a price drop event, run B's top-up borrow in isolation (succeeds, avoids liquidation) versus after A's cap-filling borrow in the same block (reverts), and assert B's health/liquidation outcome differs between runs.
