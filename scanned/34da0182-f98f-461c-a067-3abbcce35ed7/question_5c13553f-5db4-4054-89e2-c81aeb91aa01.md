Can a staker with very small `shares` relative to `PRECISION` structure many small `stake` calls across signers so each call's rounding-down loss is individually negligible but cumulatively the signer's aggregated `stacked_amt` used by `pox_5_make_signer_set` (which sums raw `amount-ustx`, not rewards) is unaffected while the *reward* side under-attributes to smaller stakers, letting a large co-staker's `rpt-paid` snapshot capture rounding remainders that were never actually earned by that staker's own shares? Equality broken: sBTC paid to (signer, staker, cycle) via `claim-staker-rewards-for-signer` == rewards earned by that staker's own `shares`, once, with no cross-staker leakage from `PRECISION` floor rounding. Scoped impact: Critical, this is an sBTC reward theft: a staker collects rewards for STX-shares they did not commit to that reward pool.
