### Title
Chained floor-division precision loss causes premature bad-debt socialization, letting a liquidator write off debt while the borrower still retains uncollected collateral - (File: `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary
In `liquidate`, the decision to socialize a borrower's bad debt across *all* suppliers of the debt vault (`no-collateral-left` → `socialize-debt-asset`) is gated on `other-debt-repayable`, a value derived from a chain of floor-rounding divisions (`div-bps-down`, `mul-div-down`, `mul-div-down`, then a single `mul-div-up`). This mirrors the Paladin `_pledge` bug: a boundary check is performed on a pre-truncation quantity (`other-coll-usd`) while the actually recoverable quantity after rounding (`other-debt-repayable`) can differ and be truncated all the way to zero. When that happens, the contract treats the borrower's other collateral as "nothing recoverable" and immediately socializes the remaining debt onto all vault depositors, even though the borrower still holds a small, non-zero collateral balance in that other asset which was never seized or applied to the debt.

### Finding Description
In `liquidate` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1514-1532`), after seizing the primary collateral asset, the contract checks whether the borrower has any other, unseized collateral that could still repay debt: [1](#0-0) 

```clarity
(target-coll-full-usd (normalize (* user-coll-balance coll-price) coll-decimals false))
(other-coll-usd (if (> total-collateral-usd target-coll-full-usd)
                    (- total-collateral-usd target-coll-full-usd)
                    u0))
(other-debt-repayable
  (if (> other-coll-usd u0)
    (let ((other-adj (div-bps-down other-coll-usd (+ BPS liq-penalty-max)))
          (other-tokens (mul-div-down other-adj (pow u10 debt-decimals) debt-price))
          (other-borrow-idx (get index (unwrap-panic (get-cached-indexes debt-aid))))
          (other-scaled (mul-div-down other-tokens INDEX-PRECISION other-borrow-idx)))
      (mul-div-up other-scaled other-borrow-idx INDEX-PRECISION))
    u0))
(no-collateral-left (and
                      (is-eq coll-removed u0)
                      (or
                        (is-eq (len (get collateral pos-full)) u1)
                        (and
                          (is-eq (len (get collateral pos-full)) (len (get collateral position)))
                          (is-eq other-debt-repayable u0)))))
```

`other-debt-repayable` is derived from `other-coll-usd` (a real, positive USD value representing the borrower's remaining collateral in other assets) through **three consecutive floor divisions** (`div-bps-down`, `mul-div-down`, `mul-div-down`) before a single `mul-div-up` at the very end. This chained truncation, exactly like the `slope`/`bias` truncation in the Paladin report, means the "check quantity" (`other-coll-usd > 0`, guarding entry into the branch) is evaluated on the pre-truncation amount, while the actual decision variable (`other-debt-repayable == u0`) is the post-truncation, doubly/triply-floored amount. For small `other-coll-usd` values (dust-level residual collateral in a second asset, common after a proportional partial liquidation via `scale-debt-for-liquidation` at line ~880-899, or simply a borrower with a tiny secondary collateral position), the successive floor divisions can drive `other-debt-repayable` to exactly `u0` even though `other-coll-usd` is strictly positive.

When `other-debt-repayable` rounds down to `u0`, `no-collateral-left` becomes `true`, and the contract immediately runs `socialize-debt-asset` (via `fold socialize-debt-asset ...`), which calls `vault-socialize-debt` to write down the vault's `lindex`/debt for all depositors of that debt asset: [2](#0-1) 

The borrower's other collateral asset is never touched by this liquidation call (only `coll-aid` is seized), so that dust collateral remains parked on the borrower's position while the debt has already been written off as bad debt and socialized to every supplier of the debt vault.

### Impact Explanation
`vault-socialize-debt` reduces the vault's recoverable debt/index, which is paid for collectively by all liquidity suppliers of that debt-asset vault (their zToken share value / accrued yield is diluted) — this is the explicitly in-scope "socialization charged to all suppliers" impact class, and it is triggered by a liquidator's transaction against a borrower, harming a third, unprivileged party (every other depositor in the vault) rather than only the caller. Because the socialization is executed even though the borrower still has an uncaptured collateral balance, protocol suppliers absorb a loss that the borrower's own remaining assets could otherwise have partially or fully covered — a permanent loss of unclaimed yield/principal socialized onto suppliers, which the report explicitly flags as an in-scope Impact (Temporary/permanent freezing or loss of unclaimed yield for suppliers).

### Likelihood Explanation
Reaching `other-debt-repayable == u0` while `other-coll-usd > 0` requires a specific edge case: a borrower whose secondary/"other" collateral value is small enough that the triple-floor-division chain (`div-bps-down` → `mul-div-down` → `mul-div-down`) drives it to zero before the trailing `mul-div-up`. Any liquidator can engineer or opportunistically encounter this by targeting borrowers with a small residual collateral balance in a second asset (e.g., after repeated partial liquidations, or a borrower who added a tiny amount of a second collateral type). This does not require any privileged access, oracle manipulation, or DAO involvement — only ordinary `liquidate` calls with normal parameters, making it independently triggerable by any liquidator.

### Recommendation
Do not gate the `no-collateral-left` / socialization decision on a rounded-down derived quantity (`other-debt-repayable`) while allowing dust residual collateral (`other-coll-usd`) to persist unseized. Either:
1. Seize/settle the borrower's remaining collateral asset(s) whenever `other-coll-usd > 0`, regardless of whether `other-debt-repayable` rounds to zero, before declaring `no-collateral-left`, or
2. Compare against the pre-rounding quantity consistently (mirroring the Paladin fix of using the rounded, applied value everywhere a bound is checked) — i.e., require `other-coll-usd == u0` (not `other-debt-repayable == u0`) as the condition for having "no collateral left," so socialization only occurs when the borrower is truly out of any collateral value, not merely when the derived repayable-debt figure underflows to zero through compounded floor rounding.

### Proof of Concept
1. Borrower deposits a primary collateral (`coll-aid`, large amount) and a secondary collateral (`other-aid`, a very small dust amount, e.g., a few units in USD terms) plus debt in `debt-aid`, positioning them for liquidation (`current-ltv >= ltv-liq-partial`).
2. Ensure the secondary collateral's dust USD value, once passed through `div-bps-down(other-coll-usd, BPS+liq-penalty-max)` → `mul-div-down(..., 10^debt-decimals, debt-price)` → `mul-div-down(..., INDEX-PRECISION, borrow-index)`, floors to `u0` at the `rem-scaled`/`other-scaled` stage (achievable by choosing decimals/price/index ratios such that the intermediate floor-divisions truncate the small numerator to zero) — this is analogous to picking `amount` values in the Paladin PoC such that `slope = amount / boostDuration` truncates.
3. Call `liquidate` against the borrower's primary collateral asset, driving `coll-removed` down (or to zero via the existing collateral-capping logic) so that `no-collateral-left`'s other branch conditions (`len` checks) are satisfied.
4. Because `other-debt-repayable` computes to `u0` despite `other-coll-usd > 0`, `no-collateral-left` evaluates `true`, and `socialize-debt-asset` runs, calling `vault-socialize-debt` — writing down the debt-vault's liquidity index for all suppliers — while the borrower's dust collateral in `other-aid` remains on their position, never seized nor applied against the written-off debt.
5. Verify post-transaction: (a) `total-debt-usd` for the borrower before vs. after shows debt removed via socialization rather than repayment, (b) the borrower's `other-aid` collateral entry is unchanged/nonzero, and (c) the debt vault's `lindex`/share value has been reduced, confirming the loss was borne by other suppliers rather than recovered from the borrower's remaining collateral.
