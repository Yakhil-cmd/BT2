### Title
Utilization-based interest rate can be manipulated right before `accrue()` locks it in for the entire un-accrued time window, letting a borrower underpay interest owed to LP depositors - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar` and equivalent `v0-vault-*.clar` vaults)

### Summary
The interest-bearing index (`index`/`lindex`) is only updated when `accrue()` runs, and the rate it bakes in is computed from the **current** `utilization()` at the moment `accrue()` executes, then applied retroactively over the *entire* elapsed `time-delta` since `last-update` [1](#0-0) . A borrower can flash-loan or self-fund a large `deposit`/`system-repay` that temporarily inflates `available-assets` (driving `utilization()` toward zero) in a transaction just before triggering `accrue()`, causing the low rate to be locked into `index`/`lindex` for the whole outstanding period rather than just the instant of manipulation, then reverse the deposit. This mirrors the referenced report's pattern of priming a shared rate variable in one call and having a subsequent call consume/lock it in.

### Finding Description
`interest-rate()` is derived purely from the instantaneous `utilization()` value (`get-available-assets` vs `total-debt`) [2](#0-1) . `next-index()`/`next-liquidity-index()` then take that single point-in-time rate and apply it across `time-delta = stacks-block-time - last-update`, which can represent a long elapsed period with no accrual calls in between [3](#0-2) . `accrue()` writes this computed `next`/`nliq` back into the persistent `index`/`lindex` vars and updates `last-update`, permanently baking the momentary rate into the shared debt/liquidity index consumed by every subsequent depositor, borrower, and withdrawer [4](#0-3) . Any state-changing entrypoint (`deposit`, `system-borrow`, `system-repay`, `flashloan`, `redeem`) calls `accrue()` first [5](#0-4) . Because `utilization()` depends on the vault's current asset balance, a caller who inflates `assets`/`available-assets` in one transaction (via a large `deposit`, funded by a flash loan or owned capital) sets up a temporarily distorted utilization that is then consumed by the *next* transaction's `accrue()` call - whether from the same attacker or an unrelated party - locking a below-market borrow rate into `index` for the full elapsed `time-delta`, not just the manipulated instant.

### Impact Explanation
The victims are the vault's LP/depositors (suppliers), whose yield is a function of `debt-delta * (1 - fee-reserve)` computed off the locked `index`/`lindex` update [6](#0-5) . An attacker holding outstanding debt can artificially depress the rate baked into `index` for the entire un-accrued period, then repay reduced interest via `system-repay`, permanently underpaying interest owed for that period. This is theft of unclaimed yield from the supplier pool - a High-impact category. The severity scales with how long `time-delta` (`last-update` staleness) is when the manipulation occurs; the longer accrual has been idle, the larger the underpaid-interest window that gets retroactively discounted.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a state-changing call (deposit/flash loan) to skew `available-assets` immediately followed by a call to `system-repay` (or another accrual-triggering action) before anyone else calls `accrue()` and before `utilization()` reverts to its natural value. On a block-based chain like Stacks, this is achievable within a small number of sequential transactions/blocks by a single actor, similar to the original report's requirement of front-running the hourly funding-rate update; it is opportunistic (requires existing debt and enough vault liquidity headroom) but does not require any privileged role.

### Recommendation
Compute interest using a time-weighted/twap utilization, or accrue interest incrementally per-block rather than lump-summing a point-in-time rate over an arbitrarily large `time-delta`; alternatively, force `accrue()` to be called on every block/transaction that changes `assets`/`total-borrowed` (removing the ability to let `last-update` go stale) and add a same-block/short-window guard preventing large deposits from influencing the rate applied to elapsed history.

### Proof of Concept
1. Let the vault go a long period without any interaction, causing `last-update` to be stale (large pending `time-delta`) while an attacker holds outstanding debt via `system-borrow`.
2. Attacker (with a flash loan or own capital) calls `deposit` with a large amount, inflating `assets`/`get-available-assets`. This transaction itself calls `accrue()` at its own current (pre-deposit) state, but the resulting vault balance is now much larger. [7](#0-6) 
3. Immediately (same or next transaction) attacker calls `system-repay`, which calls `accrue()` again; `utilization()` now reads the inflated `available-assets`, producing a near-zero `interest-rate()` [8](#0-7) , which `next-index`/`next-liquidity-index` apply over the full stale `time-delta` [3](#0-2)  and permanently commit via `var-set index next` / `var-set lindex nliq` [9](#0-8) .
4. Attacker's `system-repay` now computes `debt`/`interest-paid` off this artificially discounted `index`, underpaying interest owed to LP suppliers for the whole elapsed window [5](#0-4) .
5. Attacker withdraws the deposited capital (redeem) and repays the flash loan, having permanently reduced the interest income the LP pool earned for that period.

(define-private (next-index)
  (let ((states (var-get pause-states))
        (idx (var-get index)))
    (if (get accrue states)
        idx
        (let (
            (rate (interest-rate))
            (time-delta (- stacks-block-time (var-get last-update)))
            (multiplier (if (is-eq time-delta u0)
                          INDEX-PRECISION
                          (calc-multiplier-delta rate time-delta true))))
          (calc-index-next idx multiplier)))))

(define-public (system-repay (amount uint))
  (let (
        (states (var-get pause-states))
        (u (try! (accrue)))
        (scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (debt (total-debt))
        (total-borrowed-amount (var-get total-borrowed))
        (capped-amount (if (> amount debt) debt amount))
        (principal-reduction (calc-principal-ratio-reduction capped-amount scaled-principal debt))
        (capped-reduction (if (> principal-reduction scaled-principal) scaled-principal principal-reduction))
        (updated-scaled-principal (- scaled-principal capped-reduction))
        (principal-repaid (mul-div-down capped-amount total-borrowed-amount debt))
        (interest-paid (- capped-amount principal-repaid))
        (total-borrowed-new (if (> total-borrowed-amount principal-repaid) (- total-borrowed-amount principal-repaid) u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (not (get repay states)) ERR-PAUSED)
    (asserts! (> amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (try! (receive-underlying capped-amount tx-sender))
    (var-set principal-scaled updated-scaled-principal)
    (var-set total-borrowed total-borrowed-new)
