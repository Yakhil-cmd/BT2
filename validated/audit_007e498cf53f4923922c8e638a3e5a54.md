### Title
Seller can front-run a position NFT sale by draining the account's collateral/borrowing power before transfer - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs)

### Summary
In XOXNO Lending, `account_id == token_id`: the position NFT *is* the lending account, and collateral plus debt travel with the token. There is no escrow lock, sale-listing state, or transfer cooldown — the holder can freely call `withdraw`, `borrow`, `multiply`, `swap_collateral`, or `repay_debt_with_collateral` up to the very ledger in which the NFT changes hands. This is the same bug class as the Footium finding (assets associated with an NFT are drained between agreement and settlement); only the container differs.

### Finding Description
`process_withdraw` in `contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs` authorizes only the current owner/delegate (`require_owner_or_delegate`) and pays out to an arbitrary `to` recipient after a post-pool solvency check:

```rust
require_owner_or_delegate(env, account_id, caller, &account.owner);
let recipient = to.unwrap_or_else(|| caller.clone());
...
let paid = settle_withdraw(env, &mut account, &recipient, &aggregated, &mut cache);
let _ = enforce_post_pool_solvency(env, &mut cache, &mut account);
```

Likewise `borrow` pays proceeds to a caller-chosen `to`, and `repay_debt_with_collateral`/`swap_collateral`/`multiply` can restructure or lever the position to the seller's benefit. Nothing stamps a "listed" flag on the account or freezes asset movement while a marketplace sale or OTC agreement is pending. Authority is resolved live from `owner_of` on every call, so the seller retains full draining power until the transfer executes.

Two drain shapes exist:

- **Collateral strip (debt-free or lightly levered account):** withdraw all but a dust amount. A full zero-amount withdrawal of every asset empties the account, triggers `cleanup_account_if_empty` → `remove_account_and_burn_nft`, and burns the token — which would abort the sale — but leaving 1 unit of one asset keeps the account and NFT alive while 99.99%+ of the value exits to the seller.
- **Leverage strip:** call `borrow` (or `multiply`) up to the LTV/borrow-cap limit, sending proceeds to the seller, leaving the buyer an NFT carrying near-maximum debt at HF ≈ 1, one price tick from liquidation.

### Impact Explanation
A buyer who inspects `get_account_positions` / `get_health_factor` and signs a purchase reflecting that collateral has no on-chain guarantee of receiving it. Between the buyer's inspection/signing and the settlement ledger, the seller submits `withdraw`/`borrow` and extracts the position's realizable value to a separate address. The buyer receives a live NFT over a dust-value or max-levered account — theft of the buyer's payment, matching the Footium "empty club" outcome.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by a single unprivileged address (the current NFT holder) via `withdraw`/`borrow`, both documented caller-auth entrypoints. Exploitation requires a sale channel where price is negotiated before or alongside settlement (any NFT marketplace listing or OTC deal), and a race window of one ledger — the same conditions the Footium report treated as High. It is partially self-limiting: a fully emptied account burns the NFT, and a healthy position cannot be pushed below HF 1 by `withdraw`/`borrow`, so the attacker leaves dust rather than zero, and must leave at least 1-WAD HF when debt exists.

### Recommendation
Add one of:
- A seller-initiated lock (timelock) that blocks `withdraw`, `borrow`, `multiply`, `flash_position`, `swap_*`, `repay_debt_with_collateral`, and transfers until a ledger delay elapses — mirrors the Footium "timelock" recommendation.
- A protocol-level atomic `transfer_after_terms`/escrow entrypoint that snapshots collateral shares and debt at listing and reverts the sale if `get_account_positions` changed.
- At minimum, document prominently that NFT purchasers must verify positions in the same transaction they acquire the token (e.g., a marketplace contract that checks `get_account_positions` and calls `transfer_from` atomically).

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice supplies 10,000 USDC via `supply(caller=alice, account_id=0, ...)`; NFT `id` is minted to Alice.
2. Alice lists NFT `id` for sale off-chain; Bob verifies `get_account_positions(id)` shows 10,000 USDC collateral and agrees to pay ~10,000 USDC.
3. Before Bob's buy tx lands, Alice submits `withdraw(caller=alice, account_id=id, withdrawals=[(usdc_key, balance-1)], to=alice)`. `require_owner_or_delegate` passes (Alice is still owner), solvency passes (no debt), the pool pays out, and `cleanup_account_if_empty` does not fire because one supply unit remains.
4. Bob's marketplace transaction executes `transfer`/`transfer_from` of `id`; Bob receives a live NFT over an account holding 1 unit of USDC and no longer owns any recourse — the old owner cannot be called to account because the drain was fully authorized at the time.

Variant: Alice's account has collateral; she calls `borrow(caller=alice, account_id=id, borrows=[(eth_key, max_ltv_amount)], to=alice)`, then sells; Bob receives an NFT at HF ≈ 1 carrying the new debt.
