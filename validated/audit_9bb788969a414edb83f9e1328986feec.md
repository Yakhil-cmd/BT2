## Title
Self-liquidation lets a borrower extract their liquidation bonus at the expense of debt-vault depositors via bad-debt socialization - (File: `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

## Summary
`liquidate()` in `v0-4-market.clar` never checks that the caller (`liquidator = contract-caller`) is different from the `borrower` being liquidated. A borrower whose own position has crossed `LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL`/`LTV-LIQ-FULL` can call `liquidate` against themselves, defaulting the `collateral-receiver` to their own address, and thereby unwind their position at the discounted "liquidation-penalty" rate. When the seized-collateral value cannot cover `debt + bonus` (the ordinary under-collateralized case), the shortfall is not paid by the self-liquidator — it is written off through `socialize-debt-asset` → `vault-socialize-debt`, which reduces the debt vault's `lindex`/`assets`, i.e. it is charged to the vault's depositors.

## Finding Description
In `liquidate()` [1](#0-0) , `liquidator` is simply `contract-caller`, and `borrower` is an arbitrary parameter supplied by the caller. There is no `asserts!` anywhere in the function requiring `liquidator != borrower`; the only identity check is `(asserts! (is-eq contract-caller tx-sender) ERR-AUTHORIZATION)` [2](#0-1) , which only rules out delegated calls, not self-targeting.

When collateral is processed, `process-collateral-asset` computes the collateral the liquidator should receive as `debt-repay * (1 + penalty)` and caps it at the borrower's actual balance [3](#0-2) . If the collateral is insufficient to cover the bonus, `calc-final-liquidation-amounts` recomputes the *debt actually repaid* downward (`calc-liq-debt-repay-real`) so the liquidator seizes all remaining collateral while repaying strictly less than the debt that collateral is nominally worth [4](#0-3) .

After execution, if no collateral remains, the outstanding debt difference is socialized via `socialize-debt-asset`, which calls `vault-socialize-debt` on the debt vault, writing down `lindex`/`assets` — i.e., imposing the loss on the vault's suppliers rather than the borrower [5](#0-4)  and [6](#0-5) . The vault-level accounting of this write-down is in `socialize-debt` [7](#0-6) .

Because `collateral-receiver` defaults to `liquidator` [8](#0-7) , a borrower who self-liquidates keeps 100% of the seized collateral in their own wallet while only partially repaying debt, with the unpaid remainder absorbed by the debt vault's depositors.

The only anti-abuse check present, `same-block-check`, only blocks liquidation in the same block a borrow was made [9](#0-8) ; it does nothing to prevent a borrower whose position naturally drifts (or is pushed) past `LTV-LIQ-FULL` from later self-liquidating.

## Impact Explanation
Without and with the attacker's transaction:
- Without: at `LTV=100%` (collateral == debt), a normal unwind requires the borrower to `repay()` the full debt and then `collateral-remove()` the full collateral — dollar-for-dollar, no loss to anyone.
- With the attacker's self-liquidation: they repay only `collateral / (1 + penalty)` of the debt (e.g., ~90.9% at a 10% max penalty) while removing 100% of the collateral to their own wallet. The unpaid ~9.1% of debt is not covered by anyone — it is force-written off through `socialize-debt-asset`/`vault-socialize-debt`, permanently reducing the debt vault's backing (`lindex`) for all of its depositors [10](#0-9) .

This is a direct transfer of value from unprivileged debt-vault suppliers to the self-liquidating borrower, landing in the "theft of user funds at rest / protocol insolvency" impact class, since depositors' claims are diluted to fund the borrower's discounted exit.

## Likelihood Explanation
Any borrower can trivially reach `LTV-LIQ-FULL` (either by holding a position through ordinary market price movement, or by simply not adding collateral/repaying as prices move against them) and then call `liquidate(self, ..., debt-amount, 0, some(self), none)`. No special privilege, flashloan, or oracle manipulation is required — a natural price move that would otherwise let anyone liquidate the position is sufficient, and the borrower is incentivized to front-run third-party liquidators to capture the discount for themselves instead of losing it to a stranger.

## Recommendation
Add `(asserts! (not (is-eq contract-caller borrower)) ERR-SELF-LIQUIDATION)` to `liquidate()` (and the corresponding check in `liquidate-multi`/`liquidate-redeem`), or alternatively require that debt actually repaid always equal the full nominal value of collateral seized (never allowing a shortfall to be socialized) when the borrower is also the receiver of the seized collateral.

## Proof of Concept
1. Alice deposits collateral C and borrows debt D such that, after a price move, `D/C >= LTV-LIQ-FULL` (e.g., C = $100 USD collateral, D = $100 USD debt, `liq-penalty-max` = 10%).
2. Alice waits at least one block past her last borrow (to pass `same-block-check`).
3. Alice calls `market.liquidate(alice, collateral-ft, debt-ft, D, 0, some(alice), none)` from her own account.
4. `process-collateral-asset` computes `coll-usd-expected = D * 1.10 = 110`, capped at `user-coll-balance = 100` → `coll-actual = 100`.
5. `calc-final-liquidation-amounts` recomputes `debt-final-usd = 100 / 1.10 ≈ 90.9`.
6. Alice repays ~90.9 in debt tokens and receives all 100 in collateral back to her own wallet.
7. Since `coll-removed` empties the position, `no-collateral-left` is true and the remaining ~9.1 debt is socialized via `socialize-debt-asset` → `vault-socialize-debt`, permanently reducing the debt vault's backing for all depositors [5](#0-4) .
8. Net effect: Alice exits her position paying ~9% less than her actual debt, funded entirely by the debt vault's other depositors.

(define-private (socialize-debt-asset
                (debt-entry { aid: uint, scaled: uint })
                (acc { borrower: principal, success: bool }))

  (if (not (get success acc))
      acc

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))
