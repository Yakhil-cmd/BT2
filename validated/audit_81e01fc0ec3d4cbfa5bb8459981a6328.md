### Title
`roll-sbtc` in pox-5.clar credits `total-sbtc-staked` and per-staker sats entitlement using the nominal transfer amount instead of the actually-received balance delta - (File: `stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar`)

### Summary
`pox-5.clar`'s `roll-sbtc` private function moves a staker's custodied sBTC into or out of the contract and then unconditionally updates the global `total-sbtc-staked` accounting variable by the nominal `delta` requested, without verifying that the contract's actual `sbtc-token` balance changed by that same amount.

### Finding Description
When a staker increases their sBTC commitment, `roll-sbtc` computes `delta = new-sbtc - old-sbtc`, calls `contract-call? 'SM3VDXK3WZZSA84XXFKAFAF15NNZX32CTSG82JFQ4.sbtc-token transfer delta tx-sender current-contract none`, and then does `(var-set total-sbtc-staked (+ (var-get total-sbtc-staked) delta))` regardless of whether the contract actually received `delta` sats [1](#0-0) . The comment at the top of the function explicitly documents this "transfer, then blindly credit the nominal amount" pattern [2](#0-1) . Symmetrically, on a decrease it refunds `delta` out of the contract via `as-contract? ((with-ft ... "sbtc-token" delta)) ... transfer delta tx-sender staker none` and decrements `total-sbtc-staked` by the same nominal `delta`, again without checking the contract's actual resulting balance [3](#0-2) .

This mirrors the reported bug class exactly: the contract assumes `amount requested to move == amount actually moved`, exactly the assumption `ShortCollateral._addCollateral` made about ERC20 `safeTransferFrom` before crediting `collateralAmount`. Here the equivalent unvalidated equality is `total-sbtc-staked` (and the staker's/`accumulator`'s recorded `amount-sats` entitlement, which drives future `unstake-sbtc` payouts and reward-per-token calculations) versus the sBTC token contract's actual balance held by pox-5. `roll-sbtc` is invoked from the bond-registration/rollover path (`register-for-bond`/`update-bond-registration`) each time a staker's committed sats change, so this bookkeeping assumption is exercised on every L1-BTC-backed bond operation, not just a rare edge case [4](#0-3) .

The `sbtc-token` contract address is hardcoded (`'SM3VDXK3WZZSA84XXFKAFAF15NNZX32CTSG82JFQ4.sbtc-token`) throughout pox-5.clar, including in `roll-sbtc`, `claim-staker-rewards-for-signer`, and `transfer-from-reserve` [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) . There is no mechanism in pox-5 to swap out or disable this token if its `transfer` semantics ever changed (e.g. began deducting a fee, similar to how USDT could later add a fee-on-transfer switch in the original finding) — the same "no way to remove/adjust for a misbehaving collateral asset" gap flagged in the source report.

### Impact Explanation
If the actual sBTC amount received/paid by the contract ever diverges from the nominal `delta` (e.g., due to a future sbtc-token upgrade that takes a protocol fee, pauses partial transfers, or otherwise doesn't move the full requested amount), `total-sbtc-staked` and staker `amount-sats` entitlements become inflated relative to the sBTC actually custodied by pox-5. Because `unstake-sbtc`/bond-exit logic pays stakers out of the recorded `amount-sats` entitlement rather than a balance-checked amount, later stakers attempting to redeem their BTC-backed entitlement could find the contract under-collateralized, i.e., permanent freezing of some stakers' locked sBTC — a High-severity outcome under the stated impact classes (permanent freezing of staked sBTC, or signing/reward slots exceeding locked value, since reward-per-token calculations also use the inflated `total-sbtc-staked`/`amount-sats`).

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is low under sBTC's current SIP-010 `transfer` semantics (no fee-on-transfer today), but the root-cause pattern — crediting internal accounting by the requested/nominal amount instead of the verified received amount, with no way to adjust behavior if the token contract's transfer semantics change — is identical to the reported issue. This is a latent accounting-equality risk rather than an immediately exploitable bug against the current, unmodified `sbtc-token` contract, since exploiting it requires the referenced sBTC token contract's `transfer` function to actually behave in a lossy/fee-taking way, which is outside pox-5's control.

### Recommendation
In `roll-sbtc` (and any other pox-5 function that moves sBTC), verify the contract's `sbtc-token` balance before and after the transfer and credit/debit `total-sbtc-staked` and staker `amount-sats` by the observed balance delta rather than the nominal requested `delta`. This makes pox-5's internal accounting resilient to any future change in `sbtc-token` transfer semantics rather than trusting the requested transfer amount unconditionally.

### Proof of Concept
1. Staker calls `register-for-bond`/`update-bond-registration`, which invokes `roll-sbtc(staker, old_sbtc, new_sbtc)` with `new_sbtc > old_sbtc` [1](#0-0) .
2. `roll-sbtc` calls `sbtc-token transfer delta tx-sender current-contract none`. Assume (hypothetically, matching the report's premise) the token contract deducts a fee and only `delta - fee` sats actually reach pox-5.
3. `roll-sbtc` still executes `(var-set total-sbtc-staked (+ (var-get total-sbtc-staked) delta))`, crediting the full nominal `delta`, and the caller's bond-membership record stores `amount-sats` as the full nominal amount as well (per the surrounding bond-registration logic).
4. Repeat for multiple stakers; the sum of recorded `amount-sats` entitlements across all stakers now exceeds the sBTC actually held by pox-5.
5. When later stakers call the sBTC-refund branch of `roll-sbtc` (decreasing their commitment) or the bond's exit/unstake-sbtc path, the contract attempts to pay out `delta` sats it does not fully hold, causing some stakers' entitled sBTC to be permanently frozen/unredeemable [3](#0-2) .

;; Move a staker's custodied sBTC from `old-sbtc` to `new-sbtc`, transferring
;; only the net difference: pull the increase from the staker, or refund the
;; decrease. `total-sbtc-staked` is updated by the net change. A registration
;; with no rollover passes `old-sbtc` of `u0`, which transfers the full amount.
;; A no-op when the two are equal.
(define-private (roll-sbtc
        (staker principal)
        (old-sbtc uint)
        (new-sbtc uint)
    )
    (begin
        (if (> new-sbtc old-sbtc)
            (let ((delta (- new-sbtc old-sbtc)))
                (try! (contract-call?
                    'SM3VDXK3WZZSA84XXFKAFAF15NNZX32CTSG82JFQ4.sbtc-token
                    transfer delta tx-sender current-contract none
                ))
                (var-set total-sbtc-staked (+ (var-get total-sbtc-staked) delta))
            )
            (if (< new-sbtc old-sbtc)
                (let ((delta (- old-sbtc new-sbtc)))
                    (try! (as-contract?
                        ((with-ft
                            'SM3VDXK3WZZSA84XXFKAFAF15NNZX32CTSG82JFQ4.sbtc-token
                            "sbtc-token" delta
                        ))
                        (try! (contract-call?
                            'SM3VDXK3WZZSA84XXFKAFAF15NNZX32CTSG82JFQ4.sbtc-token
                            transfer delta tx-sender staker none
                        ))
                    ))
                    (var-set total-sbtc-staked
                        (- (var-get total-sbtc-staked) delta)
                    )
                )
                ;; new-sbtc == old-sbtc, no transfer needed
                true
            )
        )
        (ok true)
    )
