### Title
Unprivileged borrower can shield an underwater account from liquidation and bad-debt cleanup by planting a dust collateral leg whose price feed fails closed — (File: contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs)

### Summary
`calculate_account_risk_totals` prices **every** supply position on the account through the fail-closed strict oracle path (`Context::cached_price` → `panic_with_error!` when no valid price). Both `liquidate` (via `build_liquidation_plan`) and `clean_bad_debt` must compute these totals, so one unpriceable collateral leg on the borrower's book reverts both entrypoints. Supply requires no price, so an indebted borrower can add a dust leg of any listed asset whose feed is already failing — or, for Aquarius-LP collateral, force the failure themselves by draining the pool below `min_pool_value_wad` (`InsufficientAquariusLiquidity`). The codebase's own threat model records this as DoS.1, and `audit_liquidate_and_clean_bricked_by_unpriceable_dust_leg` reproduces it end-to-end.

### Finding Description
- `calculate_account_risk_totals` iterates all `supply_positions` and calls `cache.cached_price(&hub_asset.asset)` for each; `cached_price` panics with `OracleError` when the strict fetch produced no valid feed (`contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs:171-180`, `contracts/controller/src/context.rs:153-160`).
- `build_liquidation_plan` runs this totals computation before any repayment can be applied (`contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/plan.rs:34-44`), so `liquidate` reverts regardless of the payment legs chosen.
- `clean_bad_debt` also reverts, confirmed by the harness test asserting `PRICE_FEED_STALE` on both paths (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs:36-40`).
- Supply does not need the price, so the fragile leg can be planted cheaply (`tests/.../audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs:26-30`).
- For `PriceSource::AquariusLp`/`AquariusStableLp` collateral, `read` returns `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` when `pool_value_wad < min_pool_value_wad` (`contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs:118-122`). A large LP holder can push a thin Aquarius pool under that floor by withdrawing, converting a passive oracle-outage shield into an attacker-triggered one. Mainnet config lists such LP assets as collateral (e.g. `XLMUSDC_LP`, `configs/mainnet/markets.json:1198-1220`).
- Threat model DoS.1 documents the same mechanism (`docs/explanation/threat-model.md:364`).

### Impact Explanation
While the fragile feed is invalid, the borrower's account cannot be liquidated and `clean_bad_debt` cannot socialize the debt. If the shielded account's real collateral depreciates during the outage (or the outage is induced at a chosen moment via the LP-liquidity floor), debt accrues interest unbacked: liquidation that would have recovered collateral is impossible, and cleanup is blocked, producing permanent bad debt / protocol insolvency borne by suppliers. For the Aquarius variant the attacker controls the trigger timing, and can restore the feed after the position deteriorates (or after extracting remaining value), making the insolvency permanent rather than transient.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an open borrow and either (a) a listed asset whose feed is currently stale/failing — reachable at zero marginal cost with a dust supply — or (b) being an LP in an Aquarius pool backing a listed collateral with value near `min_pool_value_wad`. Both are reachable by a single unprivileged address through `supply`. The attacker forfeits only a dust amount of collateral. Severity is Medium: impact is conditional on a price-outage window and on the account actually being or becoming liquidatable during it, and the outage is externally observable and ends when the feed recovers.

### Recommendation
Decouple liquidation/cleanup viability from collateral legs that cannot be priced. Options: in `build_liquidation_plan` and `clean_bad_debt`, treat unpriceable supply legs as zero-value (exclude them from both `total_collateral`/`weighted_collateral` and from seizure legs) instead of aborting the whole valuation; or allow `liquidate`/`clean_bad_debt` to drop such legs. A less invasive mitigation: let `clean_bad_debt` proceed when collateral value is provably zero-or-unpriceable after a grace period. At minimum, gate admission of Aquarius-LP collateral on `min_pool_value_wad` being far above plausible withdrawal-driven floor breaches, and monitor LP depth as a listing-health metric.

### Proof of Concept
Existing in-repo reproduction: `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs:4-52`. The borrower borrows ETH against USDC, the WBTC feed is set stale, the borrower supplies `0.001` WBTC (accepted), USDC price drops making the account liquidatable, then `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` both revert with `PRICE_FEED_STALE` until the WBTC feed recovers. For the attacker-controlled variant: hold Aquarius LP shares in a listed LP-collateral pool, call `supply` with a dust amount of the LP token onto the underwater account, withdraw pool liquidity to push `pool_value_wad` below `min_pool_value_wad` so `aquarius::read` returns `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` (`aquarius.rs:120-122`), then every `liquidate`/`clean_bad_debt` on the account reverts while debt accrues.
