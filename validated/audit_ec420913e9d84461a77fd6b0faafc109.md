### Title
Donation-based inflation of Aquarius constant-product LP fair-value oracle enables over-borrowing and protocol bad debt - (File: common/src/oracle/lp.rs)

### Summary
The UsualMoney exploit priced vault shares off a manipulable liquidity pool. The direct analog in XOXNO Lending is the `AquariusLp` price source: the constant-product fair-value formula `2 * sqrt(value_a * value_b) / total_shares` is invariant to *swaps* (reserve product is constant) but is **not** invariant to *direct donations* of either underlying token to the pool contract. An unprivileged attacker can transfer tokens straight into an Aquarius pool, inflating `get_reserves` without minting shares, multiply their LP collateral's oracle price by `sqrt(1 + D/V)`, borrow against the inflated valuation, then redeem their LP shares to recover the donation — leaving the lending pool with bad debt. LP share tokens (e.g. `XAUMUSDC_LP`, `AQUAUSDC_LP`, `PYUSDUSDC_LP`) are listed collateral markets in hub 3.

### Finding Description
`fair_lp_price_wad` computes the LP share price as `2 * sqrt(Va * Vb) / share_supply_wad`, where `Va`/`Vb` are oracle-priced reserve values [1](#0-0) . The reserves come from a live `get_reserves` call on the pool [2](#0-1) . A plain token `transfer` to the pool address raises one reserve and therefore raises the reported LP price by `sqrt(1 + D/V_leg)` — the geometric mean dampens but does not prevent the manipulation, unlike the stable-swap path which solves `D` from both reserves (also donation-sensitive, but the constant-product case is the cleaner analog).

Mitigations exist but are bounded:
- `min_pool_value_wad` only *floors* pool value; it does not cap upward manipulation [3](#0-2) .
- Sanity bands cap the accepted price — e.g. `AQUAUSDC_LP` accepts a ~10x range (`min_sanity_price_wad` 5.36e15 to `max_sanity_price_wad` 5.36e16), permitting up to ~3.1x real manipulation headroom [4](#0-3) .
- Each LP market uses a single `AquariusLp` source, so dual-leg tolerance/`RequireDisjoint` cross-checks do not apply to reserve manipulation.

Attack path for an unprivileged caller:
1. Acquire a large LP position in the target Aquarius pool (or mint LP by adding liquidity).
2. `supply` the LP shares as collateral in hub 3 via `controller.supply`.
3. Atomically donate underlying tokens to the pool (capital obtainable via `pool.flash_loan` or `flash_position` within the same transaction, since the donation only needs to persist during the borrow).
4. `borrow` against the inflated collateral value — `Context` prices the LP collateral through the manipulated source and the position passes the HF gate.
5. Remove liquidity to recover the donation (the donated reserve is shared pro-rata; the attacker loses only the fraction backing the collateral now locked in the lending pool).
6. Walk away from the underwater position; liquidation cannot recover the shortfall.

Profit condition: borrowed value (`LTV × inflated collateral`) exceeds `true collateral value + unrecovered donation share`. With ~3x inflation headroom inside the sanity band and a ~50–70% LTV this is achievable for pools whose `min_pool_value_wad` (200k–500k USD) is the dominant capital requirement.

### Impact Explanation
Theft of user funds / protocol insolvency: borrows are repaid from real supplier assets in the physical pool while the collateral is worth a fraction of the debt, creating bad debt that `clean_bad_debt`/`recapitalize` must socialize to suppliers.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium-to-high for shallow pools. Requires capital on the order of `V_pool × (k²−1)` for k-fold inflation (e.g. ~9x a 200k-USD pool for the band limit), but the donation is largely recoverable through LP redemption, and the protocol's own `flash_loan`/`flash_position` can supply the transient capital inside one transaction. Cost scales with pool TVL, which `min_pool_value_wad` deliberately keeps low (200k WAD floor).

### Recommendation
- Cap LP price deviation from the oracle-implied balanced value: compute fair value as `2 * sqrt(Va * Vb)` **and** reject when `max(Va,Vb)/min(Va,Vb)` exceeds a tight skew bound (donations skew this ratio even though swaps do not change the product proportionally... note swaps also skew it — so instead bound the *change* by comparing against the balanced reserves implied by the two oracle prices and total value).
- Stronger: value the LP at `min(virtual_value)` using oracle-priced balanced reserves — i.e., derive reserves as `sqrt`-consistent with oracle prices rather than trusting raw `get_reserves`.
- Tighten `min_sanity_price_wad`/`max_sanity_price_wad` bands for LP markets to a narrow window (e.g. ±20–30%) around the reference virtual price, since legitimate LP fair value is swap-invariant and should drift slowly.

### Proof of Concept
1. Call `pool.flash_loan` for `D` units of token A (any listed flashloanable asset swappable to A, or A itself if listed).
2. `controller.supply(account, XAUMUSDC_LP, shares)` with previously acquired LP shares.
3. `token_a.transfer(attacker → aquarius_pool, D)` — `get_reserves` now reports `reserve_a + D`.
4. `controller.borrow(...)` — `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` inside the LP source read returns the inflated reserve, `fair_lp_price_wad` reports `sqrt(1+D/Va)×` the true price within the sanity band, HF check passes.
5. `withdraw` borrowed assets; repay flash loan after redeeming part of the LP position.
