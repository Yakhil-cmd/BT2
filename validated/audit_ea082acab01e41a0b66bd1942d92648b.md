### Title
Vault-wide permanent DoS via unguarded subtraction in treasury-fee accrual after bad-debt socialization desynchronizes `total-borrowed` from `principal-scaled` - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar` and equivalent vault contracts)

### Summary
The vault's `accrue()` function — invoked unconditionally at the top of every `deposit`, `redeem`, `system-borrow`, and `system-repay` call — computes a treasury fee share (`reserve-inc`) and then performs `(- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc)` with no floor/guard. The equivalent read-only helper `calc-treasury-lp-preview` (used by `convert-to-shares-preview`/`convert-to-assets-preview`) performs the identical unguarded subtraction `(- ta-preview reserve-inc)`. `reserve-inc` is derived from `debt-delta = new-debt - old-debt`, where `old-debt` is computed from `principal-scaled * index`, while the "unrealized interest" component of `total-assets-preview` is computed as `max(debt-preview - total-borrowed, 0)`. These two quantities — `old-debt` (indexed on `principal-scaled`/`index`) and `total-borrowed` (a separately, ratio-reduced running total) — are supposed to stay in lockstep, but `socialize-debt` and `system-repay` reduce them using two *different* proportional formulas (`calc-principal-ratio-reduction` vs. `mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal`), each independently rounded. Repeated liquidations that trigger bad-debt socialization (an action performed by liquidators, i.e., unprivileged third parties) can push `total-borrowed` above the value implied by `principal-scaled * index`, causing the "interest" component of `total-assets-preview` to be smaller than `reserve-inc`. Once that happens, `(- ta-preview reserve-inc)` underflows and Clarity aborts the transaction — and because `accrue()` runs first in every vault entrypoint, this becomes a **permanent revert on every subsequent deposit, redeem, borrow, and repay for every user of that vault**, not just the caller who triggered the drift. [1](#0-0) 

### Finding Description
`total-assets-preview` treats `interest = max(debt-preview - total-borrowed, 0)` as the outstanding unrealized interest: [2](#0-1) 

`calc-treasury-lp-preview` computes `debt-delta` from a *different* baseline (`principal-scaled * index`, not `total-borrowed`) and then subtracts the resulting `reserve-inc` from `ta-preview` with no protective check: [3](#0-2) 

The same unguarded pattern is inlined in `accrue()` itself, which every state-changing vault function calls first via `(u (try! (accrue)))`: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

`total-borrowed` and `principal-scaled` are reduced by two independently-rounded ratios in `system-repay`: [6](#0-5) 

and again in `socialize-debt`, which is invoked by the market during liquidation-driven bad-debt write-downs — a path controlled by an unprivileged liquidator, not by the depositors/borrowers who will later be harmed: [7](#0-6) 

Because `principal-scaled` (used to derive `old-debt`/`debt-delta`) and `total-borrowed` (used to derive `interest`) are decremented via two separate proportional formulas at separate rounding boundaries, repeated liquidation/socialization events (each triggered by a different liquidator acting on a different borrower's position) can drive `total-borrowed` above the value implied by `principal-scaled * index`. When that happens, the "interest" term used inside `total-assets-preview` collapses toward/at `u0` or under-reports relative to `debt-delta`, while `reserve-inc` (bounded only by `debt-delta * fee-reserve / BPS ≤ debt-delta`) can exceed `ta-preview`. The subtraction `(- ta-preview reserve-inc)` then underflows, which in Clarity causes the transaction to abort with a runtime arithmetic error.

### Impact Explanation
Since `accrue()` unconditionally runs at the start of `deposit`, `redeem`, `system-borrow`, and `system-repay` for the affected vault, once this underflow condition is reached it reverts on **every future call** — there is no recovery path, no admin function to reset `assets`/`total-borrowed`/`principal-scaled` back into consistency, and the condition is not self-correcting (analogous to `fundAppeal` becoming permanently unusable once `totalCost - paidFees` underflows). This freezes:
- All depositors' ability to `redeem` their supplied funds.
- All borrowers' ability to `repay` debt (worsening their own liquidation risk) or `borrow` more.
- The victims are entirely different, unprivileged principals from the liquidator(s) whose socialize-debt calls caused the drift — a clear third-party harm, not "only the caller."

This lands on the in-scope **temporary/permanent freezing of funds** impact class, since the entire vault's liquidity and every user's position becomes stuck.

### Likelihood Explanation
The exact drift requires accumulation of rounding divergence between `principal-scaled`'s ratio-reduction and `total-borrowed`'s ratio-reduction across multiple `system-repay`/`socialize-debt` calls (each using independent `mul-div-down`/`calc-principal-ratio-reduction` roundings), which is plausible in a vault that undergoes several partial liquidations/bad-debt socializations over its life, but the precise number of operations and rounding pattern needed to flip the inequality was not verified end-to-end with a concrete numeric trace in this review — this should be validated with a fuzz/property test that repeatedly liquidates varied-size positions to confirm `total-borrowed` can exceed `principal-scaled * index / INDEX_PRECISION`.

### Recommendation
- Guard both subtractions with a floor, e.g. `(if (> ta-preview reserve-inc) (- ta-preview reserve-inc) u0)` in `calc-treasury-lp-preview` and the equivalent inline computation in `accrue()`.
- Unify the two independent proportional-reduction formulas used for `principal-scaled` and `total-borrowed` (in `system-repay` and `socialize-debt`) so they cannot diverge, or recompute `total-borrowed` derivatively from `principal-scaled * index` instead of maintaining it as a separately-rounded running total.
- Add an invariant check/test asserting `total-borrowed <= principal-scaled * index / INDEX_PRECISION` continuously holds across repeated liquidation/socialization sequences.

### Proof of Concept
Not independently reproduced with a numeric trace in this review (would require a Clarinet/vitest simulation performing successive partial liquidations that each invoke `socialize-debt` on different borrowers to accumulate rounding drift between `total-borrowed` and `principal-scaled`, then observing the abort in a subsequent `deposit`/`redeem` call). The unguarded-subtraction code path itself is directly verifiable in `calc-treasury-lp-preview` and in the inline `accrue()` logic cited above.
