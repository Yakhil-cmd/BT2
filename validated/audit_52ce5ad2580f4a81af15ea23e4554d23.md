### Title
Permissionless `flash_loan` with attacker-chosen `receiver` drains third-party token allowances to the pool - ([File: contracts/pool/src/ops/flash.rs])

### Summary
`Controller::flash_loan` accepts an arbitrary `receiver` contract address and the pool repays itself via `token.transfer_from(pool, receiver, pool, amount + fee)` against whatever allowance `receiver` has granted the pool. Any contract holding a standing (or residual, not-yet-expired) allowance to the pool and implementing `execute_flash_loan` can be named as `receiver` by an unprivileged caller, forcing it to fund the flash-loan fee without ever initiating a loan.

### Finding Description
The vulnerable flow is in `flash::apply`:

1. `flash_loan` is a permissionless controller entrypoint (`require_authorized_caller` only checks the caller, not the receiver) that forwards a user-supplied `receiver` to the pool. [1](#0-0) 
2. The pool transfers `amount` to `receiver` and invokes its `execute_flash_loan` callback. [2](#0-1) 
3. Repayment is pulled with `transfer_from` where `from = receiver` — an address chosen by the caller — requiring only that `allowance(receiver, pool) >= amount + fee`. `receiver` itself never signs anything. [3](#0-2) 

Net effect on a forced receiver: `+amount` then `-(amount + fee)`, i.e. a loss of exactly `fee`, booked as protocol revenue. This mirrors the reported bug class: a `transfer_from` whose `from` is caller-controlled rather than the authorizing party.

### Impact Explanation
A victim contract that has granted the pool allowance ≥ `amount + fee` (e.g., a flash receiver that approves inside its callback with a generous `expiration_ledger`, leaving residual allowance after its loan completes — Soroban allowances persist until their expiration ledger) loses the flash-loan fee every time an attacker routes a loan through it. Repeatable while allowance remains.

### Likelihood Explanation
Constrained but reachable:

- `receiver` must be a WASM contract (`require_wasm_receiver`) implementing `execute_flash_loan` that returns without reverting — receivers that validate `initiator` or `pool` would revert, limiting targets to permissive implementations. [4](#0-3) 
- The victim must hold live allowance ≥ `amount + fee` to the pool; flash receivers that approve-and-clear inside the callback with a short expiration leave little residual, but any standing or long-expiry approval qualifies.
- The attacker gains no direct profit — the fee accrues to protocol revenue — so this is a griefing/forced-loss vector rather than a theft-for-profit, which caps severity at Medium.

Caveat: I could not fully verify the internals of `require_wasm_receiver` (in `common/src/validation.rs`) or whether any shipped flash receiver clears its allowance; if receivers are required to self-approve only transiently within the callback, the practical attack surface shrinks substantially.

### Recommendation
Tie repayment to the actual loan recipient's consent:

- Pull repayment from the receiver only for allowance it creates during the same transaction: snapshot `allowance(receiver, pool)` before the payout and require the post-callback allowance increase to cover `total_repayment`, or
- Require `receiver` to authorize the loan (e.g., a signed payload or `receiver.require_auth()` in the controller entrypoint), or
- Restrict repayment to a plain balance check (`require_balance`) and let the receiver `transfer` back itself, removing `transfer_from` on an arbitrary `from`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Victim contract `V` implements `execute_flash_loan` returning normally and has previously approved the pool for ≥ `amount + fee` (residual allowance from a prior legitimate flash loan).
2. Attacker calls `controller.flash_loan(hub_asset, amount, receiver = V, data = arbitrary)`; `process_flash_loan` authenticates only the attacker and forwards `V` to the pool.
3. Pool transfers `amount` to `V`, invokes `V.execute_flash_loan` (returns), then `transfer_from(pool, V, pool, amount + fee)` succeeds using `V`'s standing allowance.
4. `V` ends down `fee`; the attacker pays nothing beyond gas and may repeat until `V`'s allowance or balance is exhausted.

```rust
/// Pulls principal + fee via `transfer_from` after verifying allowance.
fn collect_repayment(
    env: &Env,
    asset: &token::Client,
    pool: &Address,
    receiver: &Address,
    terms: &FlashTerms,
) {
    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        asset.allowance(receiver, pool) >= terms.total_repayment,
        FlashLoanError::InvalidFlashloanRepay
    );
    asset.transfer_from(pool, receiver, pool, &terms.total_repayment);
    require_balance(env, asset, pool, terms.balance_after_repayment);
```
