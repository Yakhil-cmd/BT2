The bug class is "recipient can extend the expiration of a share they received" — in this codebase the analog is NFT approval expiry. Let me check the `approve`/`is_approved_for_all` implementation in the pinned OpenZeppelin dependency.### Title
NFT operator can mint a per-token approval that outlives its own `approve_for_all` expiry, retaining transfer power over the entire lending position - (File: contracts/position-nft/src/contract.rs)

### Summary
CVE-2020-8122's class — a recipient extending the expiration of a share it received — maps onto the position NFT's two-tier approval scheme. `approve_for_all` grants an operator time-bounded authority over every token of an owner, stored under `NFTStorageKey::ApprovalForAll(owner, operator)` and expiring at `live_until_ledger`. The stock OpenZeppelin `approve` authorizes any caller that is the token owner **or a current operator**, and writes a separate `NFTStorageKey::Approval(token_id)` entry whose expiry is a caller-chosen `live_until_ledger` with no relationship to the operator grant that authorized the call. An operator whose blanket grant expires at ledger T can therefore approve an accomplice (or itself) on a specific token until an arbitrary later ledger, and that approval remains valid after the operator grant lapses.

### Finding Description
Per the in-repo contract and endpoint tables, `approve(approver, approved, token_id, live_until_ledger)` requires only that `approver` authorize and pass the stock owner-or-operator check; the approval "expires at `live_until_ledger`" chosen by the caller, with no check bounding it by the expiry of the `ApprovalForAll` entry that conferred the approver's authority [1](#0-0) . `Approval(token_id)` and `ApprovalForAll(owner, operator)` are independent temporary keys, each expiring on its own ledger [2](#0-1) . `transfer_from` accepts a spender that is "the token's live approved address, or an operator," and the per-token approval is only cleared when a transfer actually consumes it [3](#0-2) . Because `account_id == token_id` and moving the token moves the whole position — collateral and debt — the residual approval is control of a lending account, not of a collectible [4](#0-3) .

### Impact Explanation
A time-bounded operator grant is a deliberate UX choice (e.g., delegating account management for a window). The owner reasonably expects all derived authority to end at `live_until_ledger`. Instead, the operator can perpetuate transfer authority past that ledger and later `transfer_from` the position to itself, draining withdrawable collateral, borrowing against it, or holding it for ransom. This is theft of user funds reachable by a single unprivileged address, exactly the "extend the expiration of a received share" bug class.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires the victim to have granted the attacker `approve_for_all` — a plausible, legitimate flow (position managers, marketplaces). Once granted, the extension needs one `approve` call before expiry and one `transfer_from` after; no privileged role, no timing race. The gap is structural: nothing binds a delegated approval's lifetime to the delegating grant.

### Recommendation
In `approve`, when the approver is an operator rather than the owner, read the `ApprovalForAll(owner, approver)` expiry and require the requested `live_until_ledger` to be at or before it (or simply forbid operators from creating approvals that outlive their own grant). Alternatively, key per-token approvals under the granting identity so they die with the operator grant.

### Proof of Concept
```rust
let env = Env::default();
env.mock_all_auths();
let (_controller, client) = setup(&env);
let owner = Address::generate(&env);
let operator = Address::generate(&env);
let accomplice = Address::generate(&env);
let token_id = client.mint(&owner);

// Owner grants operator authority until ledger T.
let t = env.ledger().sequence() + 1_000;
client.approve_for_all(&owner, &operator, &t);

// Operator approves an accomplice on this token far beyond T.
let far = t + 10_000_000;
client.approve(&operator, &accomplice, &token_id, &far);

// Advance past the operator grant expiry.
env.ledger().with_mut(|l| l.sequence_number = t + 1);
assert!(!client.is_approved_for_all(&owner, &operator));

// The stale per-token approval still transfers the whole position.
client.transfer_from(&accomplice, &owner, &accomplice, &token_id);
assert_eq!(client.owner_of(&token_id), accomplice);
```
