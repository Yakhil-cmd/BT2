### No vulnerability found for this question.

The premise that `transfer` (`mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:752`) can reach `collateral-remove` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-market-vault.clar:406`) is false. `transfer` only calls `accrue` and `ft-transfer?` on the vault's own `zft` share token — it contains no call into the market contract at all [1](#0-0) .

Separately, `collateral-remove` is gated by `check-impl-auth`, meaning only the registered market implementation contract can invoke it — not an arbitrary unprivileged caller reachable through the vault's `transfer` entrypoint [2](#0-1) .

Since there is no call path from `transfer` to `collateral-remove`, and `collateral-remove` cannot be triggered by an unprivileged principal in the first place, the described exploit chain does not exist in this codebase.

(define-public (collateral-remove (account principal) (amount uint) (ft <ft-trait>) (asset-id uint) (recipient principal))
  (let ((states (var-get pause-states))
        (entry (resolve account))
        (user-id (get id entry))
        (mask (get mask entry))
        (remaining (try! (remove-user-collateral user-id asset-id amount)))
        (updated-mask (if (is-eq remaining u0)
                        (mask-update mask asset-id true false) ;; collateral, remove
                        mask))
        (updated-entry (merge entry (refresh updated-mask))))

    (try! (check-impl-auth))
    (asserts! (not (get collateral-remove states)) ERR-PAUSED)
    (asserts! (> amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (try! (send-tokens ft amount recipient))
