### No vulnerability found for this question.

**Reasoning:** The claimed mechanism does not match the actual code. `deposit` (`mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:763-795`) only performs `receive-underlying` (a `transfer` call *from* `contract-caller`, i.e., pulled from the depositor themselves) and `ft-mint?` to the caller-chosen `recipient`. [1](#0-0) 

There is no "mandatory payout" step in `deposit` that is forced onto a third-party victim: the `recipient` is a parameter freely chosen by the caller for their own mint, not a value imposed by or on behalf of another unprivileged party. `ft-mint?` is a native SIP-010 token-supply credit against an internal map — it does not invoke any external contract logic on the recipient and cannot "reject delivery" the way an STX/token `transfer` to a hostile contract can. That failure mode (transfer to a principal that can revert) only exists in `send-underlying`, used by `redeem`, `system-borrow`, etc., not in `deposit`. [2](#0-1) 

`total-debt` (`mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:328-329`) is a private read helper over `principal-scaled` and `index`; it is invoked inside `deposit`'s call chain only indirectly via `accrue`/`total-assets`-style previews, and it does not select or force any transfer recipient at all — it purely returns a debt figure used for share/asset conversion math. [3](#0-2) 

Since `deposit` has no code path where a victim's funds are pushed to an attacker-chosen or attacker-influenced principal that can revert delivery, the "two unprivileged principals, victim's payout routed through a rejecting principal" scenario required by the rules cannot be constructed from this function. There is also no shared-state interleaving here that produces an outcome difference for a victim B based on whether attacker A transacted first — `deposit`'s effect on shared vars (`assets`, `zft` supply via mint) only affects the depositor's own conversion rate at the time of their own call, which is the normal, in-scope share-pricing behavior of an ERC4626-style vault, not a defect. [4](#0-3)

(define-private (total-debt)
  (calc-cumulative-debt (var-get principal-scaled) (var-get index)))

(define-private (debt-preview)
  (calc-cumulative-debt (var-get principal-scaled) (next-index)))

(define-private (total-assets)
  (let ((current-assets (var-get assets))
        (debt (total-debt))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (interest (if (> debt borrowed) (- debt borrowed) u0)))
    (+ current-assets interest)))
