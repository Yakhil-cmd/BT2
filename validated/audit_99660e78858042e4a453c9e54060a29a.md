### Title
Flash-loan-assisted reserve donation inflates Aquarius LP collateral price up to the sanity band, enabling undercollateralized borrows that drain pool liquidity - (File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs)

### Summary
The `AquariusLp` / `AquariusStableLp` price source derives the LP share price from the pool's *live* reserves (`get_reserves`) and total share supply, resolved fresh on every read. Because LP oracles are sole-source by construction and exempt from tolerance/smoothing checks, the `min_sanity_price_wad`/`max_sanity_price_wad` band is the only bound on a manipulated reading. An unprivileged attacker can flash-mint a dominant LP position, donate reserves into the pool (raising `reserve_a`/`reserve_b` while `total_shares` is unchanged), and borrow against the inflated collateral valuation — the same class as the DEUS Finance AMM-oracle flash-loan manipulation.

### Finding Description
`providers::aquarius::read` fetches `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` and `aquarius_total_shares_call` directly from the pool contract during every price resolution, then computes the fair price via `fair_lp_price_wad` (`2 * sqrt(value_a * value_b) / supply`) or `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` (`D * min(price_a, price_b) / supply`) [1](#0-0) . Both formulas are proportional to pool reserves but `total_shares` only changes on liquidity mint/burn — not on token donations. A direct token transfer to the pool followed by its permissionless reserve sync raises the reported reserves, raising the LP share price without any new shares minted.

`validate_asset_oracle` requires an LP source to be the sole source (`sources.len() == 1`) and waives tolerance and smoothing validation for it, noting "the band is the only backstop" [2](#0-1) . `aquarius::read` enforces only a lower liquidity floor (`min_pool_value_wad`); there is no upper bound on reserve growth relative to supply [3](#0-2) . Deployed mainnet configs admit wide bands — e.g. `min_sanity_price_wad = 6 USD` vs `max_sanity_price_wad = 25 USD` for one collateral [4](#0-3)  — a >4x permitted deviation.

The controller consumes this price as the strict Context-cached feed for `calculate_account_risk_totals`, where `ltv_collateral` (the borrow gate) is `LTV × position_value_floor(shares, supply_index, price)` [5](#0-4) . Inflating `price` linearly inflates borrow capacity for the same LP collateral.

### Impact Explanation
Wherever `band_top / true_price > 1 / LTV`, an attacker borrows more USD value than the true value of their LP collateral, withdraws the borrowed assets, and abandons the position. The shortfall is socialized across suppliers via bad-debt write-down of the supply index or is outright stolen liquidity — theft of user funds / protocol insolvency. The donation cost is largely recoverable because the attacker redeems their dominant LP share pro-rata over the inflated reserves; net cost is only the fraction of the pool they do not own.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requirements: (a) a market accepts an Aquarius LP share as collateral (multiple AquariusLp oracles are configured on mainnet, implying LP collateral support; specific controller listings were not verified in this pass), (b) the sanity band top exceeds `1/LTV` of true price — plausible given the observed wide bands, and (c) the pool's `get_reserves` reflects donated balances (standard for AMMs that sync reserves to token balances). Capital cost scales with the target inflation (sqrt-scaling for constant-product, near-linear for stable pools) and is partially self-financed through LP redemption; flash liquidity via `flash_loan`/`flash_position` covers the transient capital. Medium likelihood, scaling with band width.

Caveats: if Aquarius caches reserves and ignores unsolicited transfers without an explicit sync, or if governance bands are kept tighter than `1/LTV`, the attack is infeasible. Neither could be fully confirmed from the in-scope code.

### Recommendation
- Tighten `validate_lp_sanity_band` enforcement so the configured `max_sanity_price_wad / min_sanity_price_wad` ratio is capped near `1` (e.g. within the liquidation buffer), since the band is the sole backstop for LP sources.
- For constant-product pools, the geometric-mean formula is already skew-resistant; for stable pools prefer `min(D, xa + xb)` with reserves cross-checked against a manipulation-resistant invariant, and cap accepted per-read reserve growth relative to a stored baseline.
- Consider a lower bound on `min_pool_value_wad` combined with an upper sanity on implied `reserve/supply` growth, so donations cannot move the price to the band top without tripping a rejection.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker acquires >90% of an Aquarius pool's LP shares (mint liquidity with their own/flash-borrowed funds); the share token is listed as lending collateral.
2. `controller.supply` the LP shares into a position.
3. Donate (direct `token.transfer` to the pool address + pool sync) enough of one reserve leg to push `fair_lp_price_wad`/`fair_stable_lp_price_wad` to `max_sanity_price_wad` — reserves grow, `total_shares` unchanged.
4. `controller.borrow` up to `LTV × inflated_price × shares` in XLM/USDC, exceeding the true collateral value.
5. Redeem LP shares pro-rata to recover the donation; default on the loan. Suppliers absorb the deficit via bad-debt socialization.
