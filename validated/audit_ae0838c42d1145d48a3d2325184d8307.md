### Title
Aquarius gauge rewards accruing to LP collateral are permanently unclaimable — the pool holds the LP position but has no entrypoint to authorize the claim - (File: contracts/pool/src/lib.rs)

### Summary
The pool is the on-chain holder of Aquarius LP tokens supplied as collateral, so all venue rewards on that liquidity accrue to the pool address. Aquarius gauge rewards require the holder's own authorization to claim, and the pool contract exposes no entrypoint that performs or forwards such a claim. This mirrors the reported `BondRewards` dead code: a privileged-only external action whose sole possible caller can never invoke it, leaving the yield permanently frozen.

### Finding Description
When users supply Aquarius LP tokens as collateral, the physical LP position sits in the pool contract's balance. The threat model documents the consequence directly: "Aquarius LP collateral earns venue rewards for its holder, which is the pool. The pool has no entrypoint to claim or forward them... Gauge rewards need the pool's authorization and therefore cannot be claimed at all." [1](#0-0) 

The pool's mutator surface covers market operations (create, params, supply/mint/burn flows, debt, revenue, strategies, upgrades) but contains no gauge/venue reward claim path — the only "claim" logic is `claim_revenue` for protocol fee shares in `ops/revenue.rs`, which operates on internal pool revenue, not external venue rewards. There is also no generic `execute`/`call` escape hatch that governance or the controller could use to make the pool authorize an external claim. Unlike the non-gauge venue claim (which can be pushed into the pool as an unbooked donation by anyone), gauge rewards revert without the pool address's `require_auth`, and the pool never emits that auth.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of unclaimed yield: every epoch of gauge rewards attributable to LP collateral held by the pool accrues to an address that can never authorize the claim. The loss scales with the amount of LP collateral supplied and the gauge emission rate, and is unrecoverable absent a contract upgrade. This is the same impact class as the report — yield owed to the protocol's stakeholders is structurally unreachable.

### Likelihood Explanation
Deterministic, not probabilistic: the moment any Aquarius LP collateral is listed and supplied, gauge rewards begin accruing to the pool and none can ever be claimed. No attacker action is needed; the functionality is unavailable by construction, exactly as in the `BondRewards` report.

### Recommendation
Add a pool entrypoint that claims gauge/venue rewards and routes the proceeds to a defined destination (e.g., revenue shares or the accumulator), or a governance-gated generic call that lets the pool authorize a specific claim. If the intended design is to forgo gauge yield, keep the documentation but note that the frozen rewards are also unrecoverable for the protocol itself, not just suppliers.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lists an Aquarius LP token as collateral; a user calls `controller::supply` to deposit LP tokens — the pool becomes the LP holder.
2. Gauge rewards accrue to the pool address each epoch.
3. Any attempt to claim gauge rewards for the pool fails: the venue requires `require_auth` from the pool address, and no pool, controller, or governance entrypoint produces that authorization. Scanning `contracts/pool/src/lib.rs` and `contracts/pool/src/ops/` shows no claim path for external venue rewards; `claim_revenue` only burns internal revenue shares. [1](#0-0)
