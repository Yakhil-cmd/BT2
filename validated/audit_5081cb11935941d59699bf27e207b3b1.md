### Title
A borrower can dilute protocol liquidation fees by padding collateral with low-fee assets - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs)

### Summary
**Medium — loss of protocol revenue.** The liquidation engine allocates a single USD-denominated seizure pro rata across every supplied collateral leg, but calculates the protocol fee using each leg’s own `liquidation_fees` rate. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

An unhealthy borrower can therefore add a large amount of collateral carrying a low liquidation-fee rate, reducing the fraction seized from a high-fee collateral leg and converting part of the expected protocol fee into liquidator proceeds. [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

### Finding Description
`calculate_seized_collateral` first computes `total_seizure_usd` from the normalized repayment and one account-wide liquidation bonus. [5](#0-4) 

For each supply position, it computes the position’s USD share of `total_collateral` and assigns that fraction of the total seizure to the leg. [6](#0-5) 

The leg’s principal is derived by dividing its uncapped seizure by `1 + bonus`, and the fee-bearing bonus is the amount seized above that principal. [7](#0-6) 

The protocol fee is then calculated with the individual position’s stamped `liquidation_fees` rate. [8](#0-7) [9](#0-8) 

Consequently, adding another collateral position does not merely add collateral that can be seized: it changes the denominator `total_collateral`, reduces the seizure allocated to existing collateral, and can replace a high-fee leg’s bonus with a low-fee leg’s bonus. [10](#0-9) 

In `SeizeMode::Transfer`, each `SeizeEntry.protocol_fee` is passed to the pool withdrawal leg and withheld from the liquidator’s payout. [11](#0-10) [12](#0-11) 

In `SeizeMode::Credit`, `split_seized_shares` similarly applies the leg-specific fee rate only to that leg’s `bonus_scaled` shares. [13](#0-12) [14](#0-13) 

The public `liquidate` entrypoint is permissionless and explicitly permits self-liquidation, requiring only the liquidator’s authorization. [15](#0-14) 

### Impact Explanation
The protocol receives less liquidation revenue whenever an account can concentrate most of its collateral value in assets with lower `liquidation_fees` rates while retaining some exposure to a higher-fee asset. [16](#0-15) 

Because the withheld amount is booked as protocol revenue, the avoided fee is a direct loss of protocol yield rather than a change in liquidation pricing borne by another user. [12](#0-11) [17](#0-16) 

A borrower who controls or cooperates with the liquidator can capture the avoided fee as additional net collateral proceeds; self-liquidation is an explicitly supported path. [18](#0-17) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires only an unhealthy account, at least one high-fee collateral position, and sufficient collateral in a listed lower-fee asset to dominate total collateral value. [10](#0-9) [8](#0-7) 

The borrower can reach those conditions through ordinary supply, borrow, and liquidation interactions, then call `liquidate` with `SeizeMode::Transfer` or a controlled `SeizeMode::Credit` receiver. [15](#0-14) [19](#0-18) 

No privileged action, oracle manipulation, leaked key, or third-party victim is required because liquidation is permissionless and self-liquidation is supported. [20](#0-19) 

### Recommendation
Do not allow heterogeneous per-asset liquidation-fee rates to be blended through account-wide pro-rata seizure.

Prefer processing collateral legs independently with an explicit liquidation allocation, or charge the protocol fee using a rate that cannot be diluted by adding lower-fee collateral, such as the highest applicable `liquidation_fees` rate among the seized positions. [21](#0-20) 

If per-asset selection is introduced, ensure repayment and seizure remain coupled, preserve the same bounds checks in `LiquidationPlan::validate`, and prevent a liquidator from choosing only zero-fee collateral when higher-fee collateral backs the same debt. [22](#0-21) 

### Proof of Concept
Assume two collateral assets have identical prices, liquidation bonuses, and thresholds, but `H` has a 50% `liquidation_fees` rate and `L` has a 0% rate. [8](#0-7) [23](#0-22) 

1. An account holds `$1,000` of `H`, becomes liquidatable, and receives a liquidation plan that repays `$100` of debt at a 10% bonus; the planned seizure is `$110`. [5](#0-4) [15](#0-14) 

2. With only `H` supplied, the entire `$110` seizure is allocated to `H`; its principal is `$100`, its bonus is `$10`, and the 50% fee books `$5` of protocol revenue. [2](#0-1) [24](#0-23) 

3. Before liquidation, the borrower supplies `$9,000` of `L`, so `H` represents only 10% of total collateral value and `L` represents 90%. [6](#0-5) 

4. For the same `$100` repayment and 10% bonus, the pro-rata split allocates `$11` to `H` and `$99` to `L`. [1](#0-0) 

5. The `H` leg now has `$10` of principal and `$1` of bonus, producing only `$0.50` of protocol fee, while the `L` leg has `$90` of principal and `$9` of bonus at a 0% fee. [2](#0-1) 

6. The borrower or cooperating liquidator calls `liquidate(liquidator, account_id, debt_payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)` with `$100` of debt payments; total gross seizure remains `$110`, but protocol revenue falls from `$5` to `$0.50`, and the liquidator receives the avoided `$4.50`. /// Subtracts a liquidation protocol fee from the gross amount and books it as revenue.
///
/// Non-liquidation paths or zero fee return `gross_amount` unchanged. Panics
/// if the fee exceeds the gross withdrawal.
pub(crate) fn withhold_liquidation_fee(
    env: &Env,
    cache: &mut Cache,
    gross_amount: i128,
    is_liquidation: bool,
    protocol_fee: i128,
) -> i128 {
    if !is_liquidation || protocol_fee == 0 {
        return gross_amount;
    }
    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        gross_amount >= protocol_fee,
        CollateralError::WithdrawLessThanFee
    );

    let fee = Ray::from_asset(env, protocol_fee, cache.params().asset_decimals);
    interest::add_protocol_revenue(cache, fee);
    gross_amount
        .checked_sub(protocol_fee)
        .unwrap_or_else(|| panic_with_error!(env, GenericError::MathOverflow))
