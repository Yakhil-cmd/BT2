### Title
Same-transaction deposit/withdraw allows JIT-liquidity fee extraction from other LPs due to missing time/queue-based sandwich protection - (File: programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/deposit.rs, withdraw.rs, swap_base_input.rs)

### Summary
The external report flags `msg.sender != tx.origin` in `RioLRTCoordinator.rebalance()` as a fragile EOA check meant to stop flash-loan sandwich attacks around reward distribution. Raydium CP-Swap has no analog of a `tx.origin`/EOA check anywhere in `deposit`, `withdraw`, or `swap_base_input`/`swap_base_output`, and more importantly it has **no substitute protection at all** (no minimum holding period, no per-epoch/per-block queueing, no accrual-vesting) against the underlying bug class the report describes: a single actor depositing liquidity immediately before a fee-generating event and withdrawing immediately after, in order to capture a disproportionate share of fees relative to actual capital-at-risk time.

### Finding Description
In `deposit()` and `withdraw()`, LP share pricing is computed purely from the current vault balances via `PoolState::vault_amount_without_fee`, which returns `vault_amount - (protocol_fees + fund_fees + creator_fees)` [1](#0-0) . Any portion of the trade fee that is *not* routed to protocol/fund/creator fee ledgers remains in the vault and is folded directly into the LP-redeemable reserves, i.e., it accrues instantly and proportionally to whoever holds LP shares at redemption time, not to whoever held them while the trade occurred. `swap_base_input()` computes `trade_fee`, `protocol_fee`, `fund_fee`, `creator_fee` and only removes protocol/fund/creator amounts from the LP-visible pool via `update_fees`, leaving the LP-side trading fee remainder inside `total_output_token_amount`/vault balances that back `lp_supply` [2](#0-1) .

`deposit()` mints LP tokens based on the current ratio of `lp_supply` to `vault_amount_without_fee` [3](#0-2) , and `withdraw()` burns LP tokens and pays out based on the same live ratio [4](#0-3) . Neither instruction checks a lock-up timestamp, a minimum-slot delay, an EOA/CPI-depth restriction, or any other mechanism to prevent a caller from depositing and withdrawing across a large fee-generating swap within the same atomic transaction (Solana transactions can freely compose multiple instructions from the same or different programs in one atomic unit, so this does not require any external flash-loan facility — it only requires the same signer issuing `deposit`, then (via any composed instruction sequence) a large `swap`, then `withdraw`, or coordinating with another party's swap in the same transaction/bundle).

This mirrors the class of bug in the report: the target system relies on the absence of a specific technical barrier (there, "not being a contract"; here, "no one deposits and withdraws around the same swap") rather than an explicit, enforced economic/time-based safeguard, so it is fragile to any actor willing/able to combine the operations atomically or across adjacent transactions with no risk of capital loss in between.

### Impact Explanation
An attacker (or a briefly cooperating pair of transactions in the same slot) can:
1. `deposit()` a large amount of liquidity into a pool right before a large `swap_base_input`/`swap_base_output` call that generates substantial LP-side trading fees.
2. Let the swap execute (their own or someone else's), which increases `vault_amount_without_fee` relative to `lp_supply`.
3. `withdraw()` immediately, redeeming a share of the newly-added fee value.

This extracts fee income from long-term LPs without bearing the price/impermanent-loss risk they take on, and can be repeated per swap. This directly harms other liquidity providers' economic entitlement to trading fees — a fee-ledger/accounting fairness issue causing real fund transfer from long-term LPs to the JIT actor.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by any unprivileged actor with `deposit` and `withdraw` permissions on the pool and no special capital-lockup requirement, using a single atomic transaction (or closely sequenced transactions) — no admin/validator/leaked-key assumptions required. The likelihood is highest on pools with large discrete trades and thin `lp_supply`, but the mechanism is generally available on every pool since there is no mitigating check in `Deposit`/`Withdraw` account constraints or instruction logic [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) .

### Recommendation
Introduce an explicit safeguard rather than relying on the absence of composability, analogous to the report's recommendation of a delay/reentrancy guard: e.g., track a `last_deposit_slot`/`last_deposit_timestamp` per LP-holder (or per-pool minimum holding duration) and reject `withdraw` if it occurs within the same slot/transaction or before a minimum elapsed time since the account's last `deposit`; alternatively, vest newly accrued LP-side trading fees over time rather than crediting them instantly and fully into the redeemable vault balance used by `deposit`/`withdraw`.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual sequence within one atomic Solana transaction (or immediately sequential transactions with no intervening price risk):
1. Instruction 1: `deposit(pool, lp_token_amount = X, ...)` — attacker obtains LP shares priced off current `vault_amount_without_fee` [3](#0-2) .
2. Instruction 2: `swap_base_input(pool, large_amount_in, ...)` — trade fee accrues into vault balances beyond the portion siphoned to protocol/fund/creator ledgers [7](#0-6) .
3. Instruction 3: `withdraw(pool, lp_token_amount = X, ...)` — attacker redeems shares at the now-higher `vault_amount_without_fee`/`lp_supply` ratio, capturing a slice of the just-generated fee [4](#0-3) .

No check in the `Deposit`/`Withdraw` account structs or instruction bodies rejects this sequence (only pool-status bits `Deposit`/`Withdraw` are checked, not timing) [8](#0-7) [9](#0-8) .

    pub fn vault_amount_without_fee(&self, vault_0: u64, vault_1: u64) -> Result<(u64, u64)> {
        let fees_token_0 = self
            .protocol_fees_token_0
            .checked_add(self.fund_fees_token_0)
            .ok_or(ErrorCode::MathOverflow)?
            .checked_add(self.creator_fees_token_0)
            .ok_or(ErrorCode::MathOverflow)?;
        let fees_token_1 = self
            .protocol_fees_token_1
            .checked_add(self.fund_fees_token_1)
            .ok_or(ErrorCode::MathOverflow)?
            .checked_add(self.creator_fees_token_1)
            .ok_or(ErrorCode::MathOverflow)?;
        Ok((
            vault_0
                .checked_sub(fees_token_0)
                .ok_or(ErrorCode::InsufficientVault)?,
            vault_1
                .checked_sub(fees_token_1)
                .ok_or(ErrorCode::InsufficientVault)?,
        ))
    }
