### Title
JIT liquidity sandwich lets an attacker steal LP trading fees via front-run `deposit` / back-run `withdraw` around a swap - (File: `programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/deposit.rs`, `programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/withdraw.rs`, `programs/cp-swap/src/states/pool.rs`)

### Summary
The pool's redeemable value per LP token is computed from `vault_amount_without_fee`, which only excludes `protocol_fees`, `fund_fees`, and `creator_fees` from the vault balance — the LP trading fee (`trade_fee`) that accrues to liquidity providers is left inside the vault and immediately increases the token amounts backing each LP share. Because `deposit` and `withdraw` have no cooldown, lockup, or time-weighting, an attacker can front-run a pending large swap with a `deposit`, let the swap's `trade_fee` inflate the redemption value, then immediately `withdraw` to capture a pro-rata share of that fee — diluting the fee income that should have accrued entirely to pre-existing LPs. This is the same "instantaneous exchange-rate inflation via reward/fee event → sandwich to extract value" bug class described in the reference report for `OperationalStaking::rewardValidators`, adapted to Raydium CP-swap's constant-product fee accrual model.

### Finding Description
`vault_amount_without_fee` subtracts only `protocol_fees_token_{0,1}`, `fund_fees_token_{0,1}`, and `creator_fees_token_{0,1}` from the raw vault balances: [1](#0-0) 

The LP-side `trade_fee` computed in `CurveCalculator::swap_base_input` is *not* subtracted out — it stays in the vault and is folded directly into `total_input_token_amount`/`total_output_token_amount`, which is what `deposit`/`withdraw` use via `lp_tokens_to_trading_tokens` to price LP shares: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Because `deposit` and `withdraw` have no cooldown or minimum holding period, and both compute the exchange price from the *current* vault balances at call time, an attacker (or a bundled set of instructions) can:
1. Front-run a large incoming `swap_base_input`/`swap_base_output` by calling `deposit` with a large amount of liquidity, minted at the pre-swap price.
2. Let the victim's swap execute, which adds `trade_fee` into the vault, raising the redeemable value of every outstanding LP token (including the attacker's freshly minted ones).
3. Back-run by calling `withdraw` immediately, redeeming LP tokens at the now-inflated rate, and receiving a pro-rata share of the trade fee proportional to the attacker's now-large share of `lp_supply` — despite having supplied capital to the pool for only the duration of one swap.

This directly mirrors the reported pattern: `rewardValidators` inflates `exchangeRate` right before delegators can mint shares at the old price and immediately unwind, capturing rewards without bearing risk; here, `swap_base_input`/`swap_base_output` inflate the vault (and thus the LP redemption rate) right after a `deposit`, and `withdraw` has no lockup analogous to the staking cooldown to prevent immediate extraction.

### Impact Explanation
Pre-existing LPs are diluted out of the fee income generated by swaps that occur while the attacker's JIT liquidity is present, since the fee is split pro-rata by `lp_supply` share at redemption time rather than by time-weighted contribution. An attacker with capital much larger than the existing pool's TVL can deposit right before a large swap and capture close to 100% of that swap's `trade_fee`, which otherwise would have accrued entirely to genuine, longer-term LPs — a concrete transfer of LP fee funds from honest LPs to the attacker.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack is deterministic and riskless: `deposit` and `withdraw` can be issued in the same transaction (or as tightly bundled front-run/back-run transactions), require no privileged role, and only need a mempool-visible/known pending large swap. There is no cooldown analogous to `OperationalStaking`'s 28-day unstake delay that would expose the attacker to price risk, and slippage parameters (`maximum_token_*_amount` / `minimum_token_*_amount`) can be set precisely since the attacker fully controls both the deposit and withdrawal instructions.

### Recommendation
Consider one or more of the following mitigations:
- Introduce a minimum holding period or lockup for newly deposited LP shares before they are eligible to redeem accrued trade fees (analogous to preventing `transferUnstakedOut` for frozen/just-delegated stake in the referenced fix).
- Track and distribute `trade_fee` using a time-weighted or checkpointed accounting mechanism (e.g., fee-per-share accumulator snapshotted at deposit time) rather than instantaneously folding it into the shared vault balance used for share pricing.
- Consider a withdrawal fee or same-block deposit/withdraw restriction to discourage atomic JIT liquidity sandwiching.

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool `P` holds LP supply `S` backed by `total_token_0`, `total_token_1` (net of protocol/fund/creator fees) per `vault_amount_without_fee` (`programs/cp-swap/src/states/pool.rs:200-221`).
2. Attacker observes a large pending `swap_base_input` transaction that will generate a large `trade_fee` (`programs/cp-swap/src/curve/calculator.rs:104-124`).
3. Attacker submits `deposit` with a large `lp_token_amount`, minted at the pre-swap ratio via `CurveCalculator::lp_tokens_to_trading_tokens` (`programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/deposit.rs:99-110, 192-201`), immediately before the victim swap.
4. Victim's `swap_base_input` executes; `pool_state.update_fees` records `protocol_fee`/`fund_fee`/`creator_fee` separately, but `trade_fee` remains in the vault, raising `total_token_0_amount`/`total_token_1_amount` used for LP pricing (`programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/swap_base_input.rs:158-163`, `programs/cp-swap/src/states/pool.rs:200-221`).
5. Attacker immediately calls `withdraw` for the same `lp_token_amount`, redeeming at the now-inflated rate via `CurveCalculator::lp_tokens_to_trading_tokens` (`programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/withdraw.rs:112-123`), extracting a pro-rata share of the swap's `trade_fee` without having provided liquidity for any meaningful duration or bearing market risk.

    pub fn vault_amount_without_fee(&self, vault_0: u64, vault_1: u64) -> Result<(u64, u64)> {
        let fees_token_0 = self
            .protocol_fees_token_0
            .checked_add(self.fund_fees_token_0)
            .ok_or(ErrorCode::MathOverflow)?
            .checked_add(self.creator_fees_token_0)
            .ok_or(ErrorCode::MathOverflow)?;
        let fees_token_1 = self
            .protocol_fees_token_1
            .checked_add(self.fund_fees_token_1)
            .ok_or(ErrorCode::MathOverflow)?
            .checked_add(self.creator_fees_token_1)
            .ok_or(ErrorCode::MathOverflow)?;
        Ok((
            vault_0
                .checked_sub(fees_token_0)
                .ok_or(ErrorCode::InsufficientVault)?,
            vault_1
                .checked_sub(fees_token_1)
                .ok_or(ErrorCode::InsufficientVault)?,
        ))
    }
