### No vulnerability found for this question.

**Basis for this conclusion:**

`debt-remove-scaled` in `market-vault.clar` only updates the borrower's scaled-debt bookkeeping [1](#0-0) ; it has no relationship to zToken share pricing or `vault-redeem`'s underlying conversion, so it cannot be the vector for the described attack.

The actual mechanism in question is `vault-redeem` → the vault's `redeem` function, which converts `amount` (zToken shares seized from B, held by the market) to underlying via `convert-to-assets-preview`, computed as `amount * total-assets / total-supply` at redemption time [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) .

The `deposit` function mints shares proportionally to the existing `total-assets`/`total-supply` ratio: `inkind = convert-to-shares-preview(amount) = amount * ts / ta` [4](#0-3) . Because both `assets` and `zft` supply increase together, the price-per-share ratio is preserved by construction (up to integer-division rounding of at most 1 unit), unlike a "donation" attack where assets are added without minting shares. A's same-block deposit before `liquidate-redeem` therefore cannot meaningfully inflate or deflate the share price that applies to B's seized collateral being redeemed — the shift is bounded by negligible rounding, not an economically exploitable difference.

Additionally, `collateral-seized` (the number of zToken shares taken from B) is fixed by `liquidate`'s own oracle-based USD valuation logic before `vault-redeem` is ever called [5](#0-4) , and `vault-redeem` simply burns that fixed share amount from the market's own zToken balance and pays out based on the vault's own consistent internal accounting — a value that applies identically regardless of whether A deposited beforehand.

Since A's deposit doesn't materially move the share-price ratio B's shares get redeemed at, there is no demonstrable path for A to extract value beyond the `SEIZURE BOUND` established by `liquidate`. This does not meet the bar of a reproducible, economically significant fund-theft PoC as required by the rules.

(define-private (convert-to-assets-preview (amount uint))
  (let ((ta (total-assets-preview))
        (ts (total-supply-preview)))
    (if (is-eq ta u0)
        u0
        (if (is-eq ts u0)
            u0
            (mul-div-down amount ta ts)))))

(define-public (redeem (amount uint) (min-out uint) (recipient principal))
  (let (
    (states (var-get pause-states))
    (u (try! (accrue)))

    (balance (get-balance-internal account))

    (available-assets (get-available-assets))

  (asserts! (> amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)
