### Title
Borrower can self-liquidate to capture the liquidator bonus while socializing the shortfall onto lenders - (File: `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary
`liquidate` in `v0-4-market.clar` never checks that the liquidator is different from the position owner. Any principal, including the borrower itself, can call `liquidate` on `borrower`, repay their own debt from their own wallet, and pull back their own collateral plus the liquidator bonus (`liq-penalty`). When the seized amount empties the position's collateral while debt remains, the remainder is written off via the bad-debt socialization path, which is funded by all lenders of that debt asset. This is the same bug class as the Licredity "proxy-based self-liquidation" report — a position owner captures the liquidator's incentive and shifts the resulting shortfall to depositors — except in Zest it does not even require a proxy contract, because no owner/self check exists at all.

### Finding Description
`liquidate` computes the liquidation parameters purely from LTV thresholds and never compares `borrower` to `contract-caller`/`liquidator`: [1](#0-0) 

The only sender-related check enforced is that `contract-caller` equals `tx-sender` (i.e., the call is not made through an intermediary contract), and health/pause checks — there is no `borrower != contract-caller` or `borrower != liquidator` assertion anywhere in the function: [2](#0-1) 

Collateral seized always includes the liquidator bonus on top of the debt repaid: [3](#0-2) 

When the seizure exhausts collateral while debt remains, the shortfall is not absorbed by the liquidator — it is socialized across the lenders of the debt asset via `socialize-debt-asset`: [4](#0-3) 

Because the borrower can be the `liquidator`, they can:
1. Take on debt until the position crosses `ltv-liq-partial`/`ltv-liq-full` (organically via interest accrual, or by borrowing right up to the liquidation boundary).
2. Wait one block to clear the `last-borrow-block` same-block guard: [5](#0-4) 
3. Call `liquidate` on themselves, repaying debt from their own wallet and receiving back their own collateral plus the bonus, while any residual debt that can no longer be covered by remaining collateral is pushed into `socialize-debt-asset` and absorbed by the shared lender pool.

The `is-eq contract-caller tx-sender` check blocks the exact "wrapper contract" indirection used in the Licredity PoC, but it does nothing to stop a direct self-call — which is the simpler and more direct version of the same exploit.

### Impact Explanation
This lets a borrower extract the liquidator bonus that is meant to compensate third-party risk-taking, and dump any resulting bad debt onto lenders of the debt-asset vault instead of bearing it themselves. This is theft of protocol/lender-owned value (the bonus effectively paid out of the shared vault) and, when bad-debt socialization triggers, a direct loss of principal to all lenders of that asset — protocol insolvency risk if repeated ("farmed") across positions and assets, matching the Critical impact class (theft of funds / protocol insolvency).

### Likelihood Explanation
No price manipulation or oracle compromise is required. The only friction is the one-block delay from `last-borrow-block`, which is trivially satisfied by splitting the borrow and self-liquidation into two consecutive blocks. The attack is fully permissionless, repeatable, and scales with available liquidity — high likelihood given the missing check is a single, obvious `asserts!` omission.

### Recommendation
Add an explicit check in `liquidate` (and `liquidate-redeem`) that `contract-caller`/the resolved `liquidator` is not equal to `borrower`, mirroring Licredity's `CannotSeizeOwnPosition` guard. Additionally, since the `is-eq contract-caller tx-sender` check already blocks proxy-contract indirection, ensure this direct self-liquidation path is closed with the same rigor — e.g., also reject cases where `collateral-receiver` resolves to the borrower, to prevent routing the bonus back to the borrower through a receiver argument.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice deposits collateral and borrows up to just under `ltv-liq-partial`.
2. Wait until debt accrues (or she borrows more later) so `current-ltv >= ltv-liq-partial`, in a block different from her last borrow (`last-borrow-block` check): [6](#0-5) 
3. Alice calls `market.liquidate(alice, collateral-ft, debt-ft, debt-amount, 0, some(alice), none)` directly (not via a wrapper contract, satisfying `is-eq contract-caller tx-sender`).
4. She repays `debt-to-repay` from her own funds and receives `coll-final = debt-to-repay*(1+liq-penalty)` of her own collateral back to herself, per: [7](#0-6) 
5. If this exhausts her collateral (`no-collateral-left`), remaining debt is socialized onto lenders per the `bad-debt-socialized` block, while she has already pocketed the bonus: [4](#0-3)
