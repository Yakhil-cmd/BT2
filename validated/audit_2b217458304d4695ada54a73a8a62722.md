This confirms the conclusion: `CommittedTransaction` events flow into `use_case_history` exclusively from the on-chain execution/commit path, not from local mempool eviction. Specifically:

- `check_is_full_after_eviction` in `mempool/src/core_mempool/transaction_store.rs` only evicts transactions from the local `parking_lot_index` to make room in the local in-memory `TransactionStore`.
