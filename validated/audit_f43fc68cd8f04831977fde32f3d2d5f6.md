### Title
Suppliers can frontrun the bad-debt supply-index write-down and exit at the pre-loss index, concentrating the entire loss onto remaining suppliers - (File: contracts/pool/src/interest.rs)

### Summary
`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` socializes eligible bad debt by lowering the affected market's `supply_index`, but the write-down is only applied inside `seize_positions`, which runs when a liquidation or `clean_bad_debt` call executes. Between the moment an account becomes provably insolvent (public on-chain state: price crash makes debt > collateral) and the moment anyone calls `liquidate`/`clean_bad_debt`, suppliers can call `withdraw` and redeem at the un-written-down index. Withdrawal is not gated by the backing check — `require_backed_market`/`backing_shortfall` is only enforced on `supply` — and `backing_shortfall` still values the unrecoverable debt at face value, so the pool pays out the fleeing supplier in full. The repo's own harness test `supplier_can_exit_ahead_of_bad_debt_writedown` (tests/test-harness/tests/controller/bad_debt_index.rs:396-473) demonstrates Carol's loss amplifying ~4x when Bob withdraws first.

### Finding Description
- Loss is applied lazily: `interest::apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` reduces `supply_index` pro-rata (contracts/pool/src/interest.rs:73-89), but it is invoked only from `ops/seize.rs::seize_positions`, reached via controller `liquidate` or `clean_bad_debt` (contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/apply.rs, bad_debt.rs).
- `backing_shortfall` counts outstanding debt at ceiled face value (contracts/pool/src/guards.rs:61-66), so a market with unseizable-yet-uncollateralized debt still reads "backed" until the write-down executes.
- `require_backed_market` (guards.rs:52-58) is invoked only on the supply path (ops/supply.rs); `withdraw` burns shares at the current supply index with no solvency gate.
- Any unprivileged supplier can therefore submit `withdraw(receiver, to_pool_share=false, scaled_amount=MAX)` in the same ledger window before the liquidation/cleanup transaction, receiving full pre-loss value paid from cash that economically belongs to the socialized loss.

### Impact Explanation
The loss that should be distributed pro-rata over all suppliers is shifted entirely onto whoever does not frontrun. With one large supplier exiting ahead of the write-down, remaining suppliers absorb up to the full bad debt — the harness test shows ~4x amplification for a 25% supplier. This is theft-by-dodging of user funds: passive suppliers' claims are written down while the exiting supplier's are paid in full. Severity Medium: requires an insolvent position to exist and be publicly observable before cleanup, which is a routine state after price moves, and any holder can execute the exit permissionlessly.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. Preconditions: (1) an account's ceil risk debt exceeds its collateral (routine after a price crash or via the dust-cleanup path), (2) pool cash is sufficient to pay the withdrawing supplier. The insolvency is public on-chain state; no privileged access, timing oracle, or coordination is needed — a single `withdraw` transaction before the first `liquidate`/`clean_bad_debt`. Bots monitoring prices can automate this.

### Recommendation
Block or haircut exits while a market carries pending un-socialized loss. Options: (a) run `require_backed_market`-style shortfall accounting on `withdraw` that treats known-unrecoverable debt at zero rather than face value; (b) apply the write-down eagerly at index-update time (`update_indexes`/`global_sync`) using a marked-bad-debt book, so no profitable exit window exists; (c) a short withdrawal delay or a write-down checkpoint that reprices queued withdrawals at the post-loss index. At minimum, document that suppliers bear frontrunning-concentrated losses, matching the InfiniFi-style fix of reverting redemptions while losses are pending.

### Proof of Concept
Reproduced by the in-repo test `supplier_can_exit_ahead_of_bad_debt_writedown` (tests/test-harness/tests/controller/bad_debt_index.rs:402-473):

```text
1. Bob supplies 75 ETH, Carol supplies 25 ETH, Alice supplies 10 USDC, borrows 0.003 ETH.
2. set_price("USDC", 10 cents) → Alice's debt exceeds collateral (insolvent, public state).
3. No write-down applied yet: supply_index unchanged, backing_shortfall counts debt at face.
4. Bob calls withdraw(all) → exits at pre-loss index, recovers ≥ his full deposit.
5. Liquidator calls liquidate(ALICE, ETH, 0.001) → seize_positions →
   apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index → supply_index drops; Carol alone bears the write-down.
6. carol_loss_after_dodge > 3x carol_loss_passive.
```

vs. Scenario A where Bob stays passive and the loss is split pro-rata.
