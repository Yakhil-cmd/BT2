### Title
Dust top-up griefing keeps insolvent accounts above the $5 collateral cap, permanently blocking permissionless `clean_bad_debt` - ([File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/curve.rs](contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/curve.rs))

### Summary
Permissionless bad-debt socialization is gated on `total_collateral <= BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` ($5). Because any third party may top up an existing supply leg of a foreign account, an attacker can push an insolvent account's collateral just above the $5 gate with a token-cost donation, making `clean_bad_debt` revert indefinitely and forcing reliance on the owner-gated `force_socialize_bad_debt` timelock path.

### Finding Description
`is_socializable_bad_debt` admits cleanup only when `total_debt > total_collateral && total_collateral <= BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` [1](#0-0) . The standalone entrypoint `clean_bad_debt` calls `socialize_bad_debt` with `BadDebtGate::DustCapped` and reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` when the gate fails [2](#0-1) . Meanwhile `supply` permits any caller to add funds to a foreign account as long as every supplied `HubAssetKey` already exists in `account.supply_positions` [3](#0-2) . Collateral is valued half-up in USD WAD, so the donation needed is only `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD + 1 - total_collateral` worth of an already-held supply asset — often cents.

### Impact Explanation
The only unprivileged route that writes off insolvent-account debt against the supply index (`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`) is permanently shut while the straddle holds: every `clean_bad_debt` reverts, and the in-liquidation hook `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation` also refuses to socialize [4](#0-3) . Bad debt stays on the book, supplier value in the affected market remains trapped behind unbacked claims, and resolution requires the privileged `force_socialize_bad_debt` governance/timelock path [5](#0-4) . This maps to temporary freezing of funds / prolonged protocol insolvency. The codebase itself acknowledges the straddle (`bad_debt_straddle_blocks_dust_gate`), but the workaround is privileged and time-delayed, so the griefing vector on the permissionless path still stands as a Medium availability issue.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requirements: an insolvent account whose remaining collateral sits at or below $5 (the common terminal state after partial liquidations drain collateral), and at least one of its supply assets being cheap enough to donate a few cents of. The attacker needs no ownership, delegation, flash loan, or oracle manipulation — a single `supply(caller, account_id, spoke_id, [(hub_asset, dust)])` suffices, and can be repeated each time liquidation shrinks collateral back under the threshold. Cost per re-arm is bounded by the $5 cap.

### Recommendation
Exclude post-insolvency inbound supply, or let `clean_bad_debt` treat collateral added after the debt became socializable as forfeitable regardless of the dust cap. Concretely: (a) reject third-party top-ups on accounts where `is_socializable_bad_debt`-style insolvency already holds, or (b) record a per-account `collateral_below_threshold` flag/block-height once the dust gate is first satisfied and let cleanup proceed while debt exceeds the originally measured collateral, so later donations cannot reopen the gate. Alternatively, have `clean_bad_debt` count only collateral up to `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` toward the gate (cap-and-clean), converting any excess into revenue in the same call.

### Proof of Concept
1. ALICE holds supply position `USDC` and debt `ETH`; price moves make her insolvent with `total_collateral ≈ $4`, `total_debt ≈ $50` (e.g. after a partial liquidation, per `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation` [6](#0-5) ).
2. Any caller can now legally socialise her debt: `clean_bad_debt(MALLORY, alice_id)` would pass `is_socializable_bad_debt` since `$4 <= $5` and `D > C`.
3. MALLORY instead calls `supply(mallory, alice_id, spoke_id, [({hub_id, USDC}, ~$1.10)])`. `require_third_party_existing_supply` passes because `USDC` is already in `account.supply_positions` [3](#0-2) .
4. `total_collateral` becomes ≈ $5.10 > `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD`. Every subsequent `clean_bad_debt` reverts `CannotCleanBadDebt`, and residual liquidation tails also refuse socialization.
5. If a liquidator's pro-rata seizure pushes collateral back under $5, MALLORY re-arms for at most ~$5; otherwise cleanup stays blocked until governance executes `force_socialize_bad_debt`, leaving the market's write-down (and trapped supplier backing) outstanding. This is exactly the state pinned by `bad_debt_straddle_blocks_dust_gate` [7](#0-6) .

/// Straddle, first half. An attacker who raises `total_collateral` strictly above
/// `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` (`BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD + 1` is the cheapest such state) blocks
/// the permissionless dust-gated socialization path while staying insolvent. The gate is
/// value-based, not count-based, so one unit of a second collateral cannot block it: the
/// attacker must post value above the threshold and keep it there.
#[rule]
fn bad_debt_straddle_blocks_dust_gate(e: Env, debt_wad: i128, collateral_wad: i128) {
    let _ = e;
    cvlr_assume!(collateral_wad >= BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD + 1);
    cvlr_assume!(collateral_wad <= 1_000_000 * WAD);
    cvlr_assume!(debt_wad > collateral_wad && debt_wad <= 2_000_000 * WAD);

    // Symbolic straddle: anywhere strictly above the cap, the dust gate is shut.
    cvlr_assert!(!is_socializable_bad_debt(
        Wad::from(debt_wad),
        Wad::from(collateral_wad)
    ));
