The Symmetrical bug's root cause is a protocol fee computed from a stale, user-chosen LIMIT price rather than the actual execution price, with no correction step afterward. I searched polkadot-sdk for analogous patterns — AMM swaps, transaction-payment fee handling, and order/bid mechanisms — and found no structural analog:

- `pallet-asset-conversion`'s `get_amount_in`/`get_amount_out` and `quote_price_*` functions always compute swap amounts from live pool reserves at the moment of execution, not from a stale user-supplied price; `amount_in_max`/`amount_out_min` serve only as slippage guards, not as the fee basis [1](#0-0) .
- `pallet-transaction-payment` and `pallet-asset-conversion-tx-payment` charge a preliminary fee at `prepare`/`validate` time but always correct it in `post_dispatch_details` using `compute_actual_fee`, which is based on the actual post-dispatch weight, not a stale declared value [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) .
- The on-demand coretime market's `place_order` and coretime `place_order`/`adjust_bid` mechanisms use a `price_limit` as a ceiling the buyer is willing to pay, with actual charge computed from the current spot price, not from the user's limit [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) .
- `pallet-uniques`'s `buy_item` deducts exactly the seller-set listed price regardless of the buyer's bid, so there's no fee/price mismatch to exploit [6](#0-5) .

None of these constructs allow a user to lock in a fee/cost based on a self-chosen price that is later executed at a different market price without a corrective step, so there is no reachable FRAME/XCM analog to this Symmetrical finding.
