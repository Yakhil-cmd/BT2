### Title
Accrual `i128` overflow permanently freezes a whale market — every pool verb panics before the borrow-index cap can engage - ([File: common/src/rates/index.rs])

### Summary
CVE-2024-21053 is a hang/crash (availability) bug class. The analog in XOXNO Lending is the RAY-value overflow inside interest accrual: once `borrowed * borrow_index` exceeds `i128::MAX`, `accrue_step` panics inside `Ray::mul`, and because `global_sync` runs at the head of every pool mutator, the entire market — repay, withdraw, borrow, liquidation, bad-debt cleanup — is permanently bricked. The `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` cap bounds the index ratio but never the `borrowed × index` product, so it does not prevent the freeze.

### Finding Description
`global_sync` in `contracts/pool/src/interest.rs` (lines 20-33) unconditionally chunks elapsed time and calls `accrue_chunk` → `accrue_step` for every market interaction. Inside `common/src/rates/index.rs`, `calculate_supplier_rewards` computes `borrowed.mul(env, new_borrow_index)` and `borrowed.mul(env, old_borrow_index)` (lines 80-83), and `scaled_to_original` in `common/src/rates/scaling.rs:14-16` multiplies scaled positions by the index. `Ray::mul` panics on `i128` overflow (`GenericError::MathOverflow`).

`update_borrow_index` clamps `new_index` at `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` (lines 13-19), but that only bounds the *index*, not the *debt value* `borrowed * index`. On a whale market (huge `borrowed` at ~98% utilization on a steep rate curve), the debt value crosses `i128::MAX` while `borrow_index` is still far below its cap — the test `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:316-360`) demonstrates exactly this: accrual reverts with `MATH_OVERFLOW` at index ~170x while the cap never engages, and `try_withdraw_raw` and `try_repay` revert identically because every verb accrues first.

No recovery path exists: `update_indexes`, `repay`, `withdraw`, `liquidate`, and `clean_bad_debt` all route through accrual of the same market, so the panic is self-perpetuating. Even owner-gated `force_socialize_bad_debt` accrues first. This is a deterministic on-chain panic, not a budget/gas issue.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of all user funds in the affected market: suppliers can never withdraw, borrowers can never repay, liquidators can never seize, and `clean_bad_debt`/`recapitalize` cannot run because accrual panics first. All pool cash backing that market's supply positions is locked forever — this meets the "permanent freezing of funds" acceptance criterion.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by a single unprivileged address via `supply` + `borrow` (attacker supplies collateral in one market and borrows in the whale market, or simply supplies the liquidity that lets utilization sit high), plus `update_indexes` to drive accrual. The attack requires a very large market principal (the test uses ~1 billion 18-decimal tokens at 98% utilization) and sustained high borrow rates over a long horizon — accrual is in millisecond chunks capped by `max_borrow_rate`, so the index must compound for years before `borrowed × index` saturates. Capital-intensive and slow, hence Medium rather than High; no privilege, parameter misconfiguration, or third-party honesty assumption is needed.

### Recommendation
Saturate instead of panicking on the debt-value product: replace `borrowed.mul(env, index)` in `calculate_supplier_rewards` (and `scaled_to_original` callers on accrual paths) with a saturating `mul_div` (e.g. `fp_core::mul_div_floor_saturating` already used for `protocol_fee_shares` and cap conversion in `common/src/rates/scaling.rs:26-33` and `index.rs:94-99`), or gate accrual by clamping `borrowed`/`index` growth once the product nears `i128::MAX`. At minimum, make `update_borrow_index`'s cap account for position size, or add an emergency path that burns debt/socializes at the last committed index without re-accruing.

### Proof of Concept
`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:316-360` is an executable PoC:
1. Create an 18-decimal market on the XLM steep interest curve; lift caps.
2. BOB supplies `10^9` whole tokens; ALICE supplies collateral and borrows 98% of it.
3. Loop `update_indexes` advancing one year per step: at the year where `borrowed × borrow_index > i128::MAX` (index ≈ 170x, still `< MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`), accrual reverts with `MATH_OVERFLOW`.
4. Subsequent `withdraw` (even amount 1) and `repay` revert with the same `MATH_OVERFLOW`, confirming the market — and all funds in it — is permanently frozen.
