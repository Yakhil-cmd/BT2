### Title
Rounding cascade in remaining-collateral liquidation math lets a liquidator seize 100% of a borrower's collateral for a partial debt repayment - (File: `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary
`liquidate()` computes `coll-final-raw`/`debt-to-repay` for the capped/partial liquidation, then re-derives `remaining-debt-to-repay` from the *leftover* collateral (`coll-remaining`) through a chain of four divisions (`normalize` → `div-bps-down` → `mul-div-down` → `mul-div-down` → `mul-div-up`). If that chain rounds the nonzero leftover collateral value down to `0`, the code treats the remainder as "unliquidatable dust" and sets `coll-final` to the borrower's *entire* collateral balance for that asset — while `debt-to-repay` stays pinned to the (small) partial amount computed earlier. A liquidator who chooses `debt-amount` to land exactly in this rounding gap pays for only a small partial liquidation but receives all of the borrower's collateral in that asset.

### Finding Description
In `liquidate()` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1471-1486`):

```
(scaled-info (scale-debt-for-liquidation debt-final coll-actual curr-scaled debt-aid))
(coll-final-raw (get coll-final scaled-info))
(coll-remaining (- user-coll-balance coll-final-raw))
(remaining-debt-to-repay
  (if (> coll-remaining u0)
    (let ((rem-coll-usd (normalize (* coll-remaining coll-price) coll-decimals false))
          (rem-debt-usd (div-bps-down rem-coll-usd (+ BPS liq-penalty-max)))
          (rem-debt-tokens (mul-div-down rem-debt-usd (pow u10 debt-decimals) debt-price))
          (rem-borrow-index (get index (unwrap-panic (get-cached-indexes debt-aid))))
          (rem-scaled (mul-div-down rem-debt-tokens INDEX-PRECISION rem-borrow-index)))
      (mul-div-up rem-scaled rem-borrow-index INDEX-PRECISION))
    u1))
(coll-final (if (is-eq remaining-debt-to-repay u0) user-coll-balance coll-final-raw)))
```

`debt-to-repay` (the amount the liquidator actually pays into the vault) is fixed earlier by `scale-debt-for-liquidation` at `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:858-877`, based on the partial-liquidation factor (`liq-pct-scaled`) and is capped at `curr-scaled`. It is **not** recomputed when `coll-final` is bumped up to `user-coll-balance`.

`remaining-debt-to-repay` goes through four successive rounding-down divisions (`div-bps-down`, two `mul-div-down`) before one rounding-up step. Because `coll-remaining` can be a nonzero but small token amount (relative to `debt-decimals`/`debt-price`/`rem-borrow-index` scale), this whole chain can legitimately evaluate to `0` even though `coll-remaining > 0`. When that happens, the branch `(is-eq remaining-debt-to-repay u0)` fires and `coll-final` is set to the *full* `user-coll-balance`, silently discarding the earlier proportional cap (`coll-final-raw`) — yet the corresponding `debt-to-repay`, `scaled-to-remove`, and `debt-final-usd` used for the vault repay and the emitted event are all still the small partial amounts.

This is the same bug class as the referenced report: a value derived through a *different* chain of lossy integer divisions than the value it is compared against (`quote2size`/`size2quote` mismatch in the report vs. the USD→BPS→token→scaled→token round-trip here) can diverge from the "true" value by design-invisible rounding, and the code branches on that divergence in a way that produces an unintended, unbounded state transition (an extra fill order in the report; seizing 100% instead of the proportional share here).

`current-ltv`, `liq-pct-scaled`, and hence `debt-final`/`coll-final-raw`/`coll-remaining` are all functions of the liquidator-supplied `debt-amount` argument, along with the borrower's live oracle-priced collateral/debt. A liquidator can pick `debt-amount` (and, since price feeds can also be supplied via `price-feeds`, effectively control most inputs to the chain) to land `coll-remaining` in the exact window where the multi-step division rounds `remaining-debt-to-repay` to `0`, while keeping `debt-to-repay` itself as small as the liquidation-factor curve allows (e.g., just crossing `ltv-liq-partial`, which yields a small `liq-pct-scaled`).

### Impact Explanation
The victim is the liquidated borrower, whose entire collateral position in the chosen asset (`coll-aid`) is seized (`coll-final = user-coll-balance`) via `collateral-remove` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1506-1512`) even though only a partial, capped debt amount (`debt-to-repay`) was ever repaid on their behalf. The shared state harmed is the borrower's own position (a stranger's position from the liquidator's perspective) — the liquidator (attacker) extracts collateral value far beyond the bound the graduated-liquidation/penalty formula intended, at the cost of a disproportionately small `debt-to-repay`. This is a direct, uncompensated transfer of value from one unprivileged user (the borrower) to another (the liquidator), i.e. theft of user funds at rest — Critical impact under "seizure exceeding its bound."

### Likelihood Explanation
Exploitability depends on being able to steer `debt-amount` (a caller-controlled, unbounded parameter) so that the multi-hop division chain (`normalize` → `div-bps-down` → `mul-div-down` ×2 → `mul-div-up`) rounds `remaining-debt-to-repay` to exactly `0` for a nonzero `coll-remaining`. Given four chained integer divisions, this rounding window is reachable by brute-force search or direct calculation offline (liquidator can simulate the exact on-chain formula before submitting the transaction), and requires no special privilege — only a liquidatable position and a liquidator willing to craft the specific `debt-amount`.

### Recommendation
Do not silently promote `coll-final` to `user-coll-balance` based on a value (`remaining-debt-to-repay`) computed through an independent lossy conversion chain from the one used to determine `debt-to-repay`. Either:
- Recompute `debt-to-repay`/`scaled-to-remove` proportionally whenever `coll-final` is bumped to `user-coll-balance` so the liquidator pays for exactly what they receive, or
- Only sweep the residual dust when `coll-remaining` itself (in raw token units, or its USD value) is below an explicit, fixed minimum dust threshold, instead of relying on a rounding-to-zero result from a multi-step division chain, mirroring the report's recommendation of zeroing out limits deterministically rather than depending on incidental precision loss.

### Proof of Concept
1. Borrower has collateral `C` in `coll-aid` (large balance, e.g. 100 sBTC) and debt in `debt-aid` such that `current-ltv` is just above `ltv-liq-partial` (minimal partial-liquidation factor).
2. Liquidator computes off-chain, using the exact on-chain formulas (`calc-liquidation-params`, `process-debt-asset`, `process-collateral-asset`, `calc-final-liquidation-amounts`, `scale-debt-for-liquidation`), a `debt-amount` such that:
   - `liq-pct-scaled` is minimal (small `debt-final`, hence small `coll-final-raw`),
   - `coll-remaining = user-coll-balance - coll-final-raw` is nonzero but small enough that, after `normalize`/`div-bps-down`/`mul-div-down`/`mul-div-down`, `rem-scaled` truncates to `0`, making `remaining-debt-to-repay = 0` at line `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1484`.
3. Liquidator calls `liquidate(borrower, collateral-ft, debt-ft, debt-amount, 0, none, price-feeds)`.
4. On-chain: `coll-final` becomes `user-coll-balance` (line 1486) while `debt-to-repay` remains the small partial amount from step 2 — the liquidator repays a small debt but receives the borrower's entire collateral balance in that asset via `collateral-remove` (line 1506-1512), confirmed by the `collateral-seized: coll-final` field in the emitted `liquidate` event (line 1575). ;; Converts to scaled units, caps at current debt, calculates final collateral
```text
;; Returns: { scaled-to-remove: uint, debt-to-repay: uint, coll-final: uint }
(define-private (scale-debt-for-liquidation
  (debt-final uint)
  (coll-actual uint)
  (curr-scaled uint)
  (asset-id uint))
  (let (;; convert debt amount to scaled units for storage
        (borrow-index (get index (unwrap-panic (get-cached-indexes asset-id))))
        (scaled-debt (mul-div-down debt-final INDEX-PRECISION borrow-index))
        ;; cap at current debt (prevent over-repayment)
        (scaled-to-remove (if (> scaled-debt curr-scaled) curr-scaled scaled-debt))
        (debt-to-repay (mul-div-up scaled-to-remove borrow-index INDEX-PRECISION))
        ;; If debt was capped, scale collateral proportionally
        (coll-final (if (< scaled-to-remove scaled-debt)
                        (mul-div-down coll-actual scaled-to-remove scaled-debt)
                        coll-actual)))
    {
      scaled-to-remove: scaled-to-remove,
      debt-to-repay: debt-to-repay,
      coll-final: coll-final
    }))
```
