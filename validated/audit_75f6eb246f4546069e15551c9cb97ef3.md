### Title
Late stake/unstake before `calculate-rewards` snapshot lets a staker capture sBTC rewards accrued before they staked - (File: `stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar`)

### Summary
`calculate-rewards` in `pox-5.clar` distributes sBTC rewards that accrued over an entire elapsed half-cycle window using the *current* value of `get-total-shares-staked-for-cycle` at call time, rather than a value frozen at the close of that window. This mirrors the `UniV3Vault.collectEarnings()` front-run: pending yield is not "locked in" against a snapshot before an account can change its share of the pool, so a staker can insert (or remove) a position immediately before the permissionless `calculate-rewards` call and receive a slice of rewards it did not economically earn.

### Finding Description
`calculate-rewards` computes `calculation-height` as `(- (distribution-cycle-to-burn-height (current-distribution-cycle)) u1)` — i.e., the boundary of the *already-closed* half-cycle — and then reads `gross-accrued-rewards` via `get-new-rewards`, which is all sBTC that accumulated in the contract since the last computation [1](#0-0) . It then divides `stx-staker-rewards` by `cycle-staked-ustx`, obtained from `(get-total-shares-staked-for-cycle stx-cycle none)` [2](#0-1) .

The staking-per-token accumulator (`rewards-per-token-for-cycle`) is only updated at the moment `calculate-rewards` executes, and shares (`staker-shares-staked-for-cycle`/`signer-shares-staked-for-cycle`) are settled/adjusted whenever a staker's action (stake, delegate, etc.) touches that cycle's entry, via `settle-rewards`/`settle-staker-rewards`, which crystallize *earned-so-far* into `rewards-per-token`/`unclaimed` snapshots before mutating shares [3](#0-2) , [4](#0-3) .

Because `calculate-rewards` is permissionless and can be called by "anyone" at any time after the half-cycle boundary has passed [5](#0-4) , there is a window between the close of the half-cycle (`calculation-height`) and the actual on-chain execution of `calculate-rewards` during which `cycle-staked-ustx` can still be mutated by new stake/unstake actions for that same `stx-cycle`. Any staker who adds shares in that window is added to the denominator used to split rewards that fully accrued *before* their stake existed, exactly as in the reported `UniV3Vault` bug where an attacker deposits right before `collectEarnings()`/rebalance to capture already-accrued LP fees.

The `compute-earned-rewards` formula (`earned = shares * (rpt-current - rpt-paid) / PRECISION + pending`) further compounds this: a newly-added staker's `rpt-paid` is settled to the *pre-calculation* accumulator value, so once `calculate-rewards` bumps `rpt-current` using the diluted `cycle-staked-ustx`, the new staker immediately becomes eligible for a proportional share of the freshly added `accrued-rewards-per-ustx` [6](#0-5) .

### Impact Explanation
This breaks the equality that sBTC rewards paid to a staker must correspond to STX actually locked for the reward period during which the rewards were earned. An attacker (or MEV bot) can front-run the `calculate-rewards` transaction, stake immediately before it lands, receive a slice of `stx-staker-rewards` for the entire elapsed window, then withdraw. This is theft of sBTC rewards from legitimate long-term stakers who diluted their share unknowingly — a High-severity impact per the rubric ("theft ... of reserve or fees", "reward slots ... exceeding locked value").

### Likelihood Explanation
`calculate-rewards` is explicitly documented and tested as callable by anyone, and its execution height can trail the true `calculation-height` boundary by an arbitrary number of blocks (it only requires `calculation-height > last-calc`) [7](#0-6) . This gives attackers/bots a practical window to observe pending rewards (`get-new-rewards`) and time a stake transaction just ahead of the distribution call, similar to the one-block front-run demonstrated in the original report.

### Recommendation
Snapshot `cycle-staked-ustx` (and any bond `total-sats`) at the half-cycle boundary height itself (e.g., via a historical/frozen read at `calculation-height`, or by having stake/unstake changes only affect the *next* not-yet-closed cycle's totals) rather than reading the live, mutable total at the time `calculate-rewards` executes. This ensures rewards for an already-elapsed window are split only among shares that existed for that window, closing the front-run vector.

### Proof of Concept
1. A half-cycle window closes at burn height `H` with `10` staked STX earning `X` sBTC in rewards, all belonging to staker A.
2. `calculate-rewards` has not yet been called (it is permissionless and can lag).
3. Attacker B observes `get-new-rewards()` is non-zero and submits a stake transaction (increasing `staker-shares-staked-for-cycle` for the just-closed `stx-cycle`) with high stake, then triggers/waits for `calculate-rewards`.
4. `calculate-rewards` executes, reading `cycle-staked-ustx` as `(get-total-shares-staked-for-cycle stx-cycle none)`, now inflated by B's stake, and computes `accrued-rewards-per-ustx = stx-staker-rewards * PRECISION / cycle-staked-ustx` [2](#0-1) .
5. B calls `claim-staker-rewards`/`get-earned-staker-rewards`, receiving a proportional share of `X` despite having staked for zero blocks during the actual reward-accrual window [4](#0-3) .
