### Title
Donation-inflated Aquarius LP fair price lets an attacker over-borrow against LP collateral - (File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs)

### Summary
The DKP incident used a live pair-balance ratio as the exchange price and moved it with a flash loan. XOXNO Lending's analog is the `AquariusLp` / `AquariusStableLp` price source: `read()` resolves the LP share price from the Aquarius pool's *current* reserves and total shares via `fair_lp_price_wad` / `fair_stable_lp_price_wad`. These formulas are resistant to *swaps* (constant-product k, stable invariant D), but not to *donations*: an unprivileged direct token transfer into the Aquarius pool raises the reserve input without minting shares, inflating the LP collateral price used by the controller's health/LTV checks. The attacker then over-borrows and abandons the position, socializing the loss.

### Finding Description
`providers/aquarius.rs::read` calls `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` and `aquarius_total_shares_call`, builds `LpLeg`s priced by external feeds, and computes `price_wad = fair_lp_price_wad(...)` for constant-product pools (contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs:90-114). In `common/src/oracle/lp.rs:72-86`, `fair_lp_price_wad = 2*sqrt(value_a * value_b) / share_supply_wad`, where `value_i = reserve_i * price_wad_i` — the reserve is a raw balance input, not an invariant-consistent quantity.

For constant-product pools the inflation is sqrt-damped: donating `D` of token A multiplies the fair price by `sqrt(1 + D/reserve_a)` — e.g. `D = 3*reserve_a` doubles the price. For `AquariusStableLp`, `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` derives the share price from the StableSwap invariant `D`/virtual price, which grows roughly *linearly* with a donated leg — the same donation is materially cheaper per unit of price inflation.

The donation is not fully burned: the attacker can hold the majority of that pool's share supply (anyone can mint Aquarius LP shares), so on pool exit they recover ~their share fraction of the donation; net cost is only `(1 - share_fraction) * D`, and the inflation can be funded inside one transaction by the controller's own `flash_loan`/`flash_position` (borrow token A → transfer to the Aquarius pool → `supply`/`borrow` against the repriced LP collateral → burn/withdraw Aquarius liquidity → repay the flash loan).

Nothing else bounds this: `min_pool_value_wad` only floors pool value (inflation helps pass it), `tolerance`/`RequireDisjoint` are no-ops for a single-source oracle, and the only cap is the configured `min_sanity_price_wad`/`max_sanity_price_wad` band — which mainnet configs set widely (e.g. ~5-10x spread in `configs/ops/mainnet/*.json`), leaving ample room above the honest price.

### Impact Explanation
The inflated LP price flows into `Context` collateral valuation, so `borrow` (and `multiply`/`swap_debt`) pass LTV and `health_factor >= 1 WAD` checks against collateral worth far less than accounted. The attacker extracts borrowable assets, the position is immediately underwater, and cleanup via `clean_bad_debt`/`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` writes the loss into the supply index — theft of supplier funds / protocol insolvency. Severity: **High** — unprivileged, repeatable per listed LP market, capped only by the sanity band and pool share dominance.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires: (a) a listed market whose oracle is `AquariusLp`/`AquariusStableLp` — mainnet configs show several (e.g. `XLMAQUA_LP`); (b) the attacker holding a large share of that Aquarius pool's shares to recover most of the donation (or accepting the full donation cost if the borrowable payout exceeds it — plausible when the sanity band allows a multi-x inflation and LTV is high); (c) the fair price not already sitting at `max_sanity_price_wad`. All steps are unprivileged entrypoints. Uncertainty: whether Aquarius `get_reserves` reflects raw `transfer` donations immediately or only after a sync/deposit — if the latter, the attacker additionally triggers the pool's own sync, which does not change feasibility.

### Recommendation
Do not price LP shares from manipulable reserve inputs. Prefer `total_supply`-denominated valuations using oracle-priced constituent claims only when reserves are provably invariant-consistent; otherwise, tighten `min/max_sanity_price_wad` per LP asset to a narrow band around the feed-implied price, require multi-source `independence` for LP collateral markets, and/or snapshot reserves via a manipulation-resistant read (e.g. TWAP or min-of-recent-observations) rather than the live balance.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker supplies liquidity into the Aquarius pool behind listed market `M` until they hold share fraction `s` of `total_shares`; deposits LP shares as collateral via controller `supply`.
2. In one transaction: `flash_loan` token A → `token.transfer(attacker → aquarius_pool, D)` → `borrow(market B, amount)` — the aggregator reads inflated `reserve_a`, `fair_lp_price_wad`/`fair_stable_lp_price_wad` rises (up to `max_sanity_price_wad`), so HF/LTV pass for an amount exceeding true collateral value → withdraw Aquarius liquidity to recover ≈`s*D` → repay flash loan.
3. Position is underwater; liquidators cannot recover the deficit; `clean_bad_debt` socializes the loss into `supply_index`, draining suppliers of market B.
