### Title
Aquarius LP rewards accruing to the pool are permanently unrecoverable - (File: contracts/pool/src/lib.rs)

### Summary
XOXNO Lending accepts Aquarius LP tokens as collateral, but the liquidity pool that custodyes them has no generic reward-claim or stranded-asset withdrawal method. [1](#0-0)  Aquarius rewards attributable to pool-held LP collateral therefore either remain unclaimed at the venue or are pushed to the pool as unbooked token balances with no recovery path. [2](#0-1) 

### Finding Description
An unprivileged user can call controller `supply` with an Aquarius LP market asset and a positive amount, causing the controller to transfer the measured LP balance to the pool and the pool to mint supply shares and credit market cash. [3](#0-2)  Mainnet configuration includes Aquarius LP markets and collateral-enabled LP markets. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

The pool owns the deposited LP position and is therefore the venue-reward beneficiary, but its public interface contains only market accounting operations and `claim_revenue`; it has no arbitrary token `sweep`, `recover`, or venue-specific reward claim. [6](#0-5)  `claim_revenue` only burns internally tracked revenue shares and transfers the same market asset to the owner, so it cannot recover unrelated reward tokens or unbooked balances. [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7) 

### Impact Explanation
Venue rewards generated by user-supplied LP collateral are permanently frozen or forfeited rather than distributed to suppliers or protocol revenue. [2](#0-1)  If an external caller permissionlessly pushes claimable rewards to the pool, those tokens become unbooked donations because direct token transfers do not update tracked cash. [9](#0-8)  Gauge rewards are worse: they require pool authorization and cannot be claimed at all because the pool exposes no method that performs that authorized claim. [2](#0-1) 

### Likelihood Explanation
Any active Aquarius LP collateral position can accrue venue rewards, and mainnet configuration enables multiple Aquarius LP assets for collateral use. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4)  Triggering the loss requires only an ordinary unprivileged `supply` call and the passage of reward accrual; no privileged action or attacker manipulation is required. [10](#0-9) 

### Recommendation
Add an owner-gated recovery path that can claim supported Aquarius rewards and transfer only unbooked balances to the accumulator or another governance-controlled recipient. [11](#0-10)  The recovery amount should be measured as `token.balance(pool) - tracked_cash_and_reserved_obligations` for reward assets rather than debiting market cash or minting revenue shares. [12](#0-11) 

### Proof of Concept
1. A user calls controller `supply(caller=user, account_id=0, spoke_id=<LP collateral spoke>, assets=[(HubAssetKey { hub_id: 3, asset: <Aquarius LP token> }, amount)])`. [10](#0-9) 
2. The controller transfers the LP tokens to the pool, and the pool credits cash and mints supply shares. [13](#0-12) 
3. Aquarius accrues rewards for the pool as LP holder; push-style rewards can be sent to the pool without holder authorization, while gauge rewards require authorization the pool cannot provide. [2](#0-1) 
4. No pool ABI method can claim venue rewards or transfer an arbitrary stranded reward token; `claim_revenue` is restricted to booked market revenue and `transfer_out` uses only the market's `asset_id`. [6](#0-5) [8](#0-7) 
5. The rewards remain permanently inaccessible to suppliers and the protocol accumulator. [2](#0-1)
