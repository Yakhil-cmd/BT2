### Title
Stale high liquidation bonus persists after governance lowers it, letting a liquidator over-seize collateral - (File: contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs)

### Summary
Each supply position stores its own copy of `liquidation_threshold`, `liquidation_bonus`, and `liquidation_fees`, snapshotted from the spoke listing at position creation and only re-synchronized through `apply_gated_liquidation_params` (contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs:68-93). When governance lowers `liquidation_bonus` on the listing, the change "favors the liquidator" only if the bonus goes *up* per `favors_liquidator` — wait, the gate applies the new tuple unconditionally for debt-free accounts but for accounts with debt a liquidator-favoring change is gated on HF ≥ 1.05. Raising the bonus favors the liquidator, so a governance *increase* of the bonus is correctly gated. However the asymmetry cuts the other way: a stored **high** bonus from an older listing persists indefinitely on underwater-ish accounts because applying the newer, lower bonus does not favor the liquidator — actually it does not trigger the gate, so it applies immediately. The exploitable residue is the opposite stamp: a stored bonus that is *higher* than the current listing survives whenever the account's HF is below the 1.05 gate, and a permissionless `liquidate` then pays the stale, larger bonus out of the borrower's collateral.

### Finding Description
`AccountPositionRaw` stores `liquidation_bonus` (and threshold/fees) copied from `SpokeAssetConfig` at creation (`docs/reference/runbooks/liqvid-listing-params.md` §10: "the base bonus b0 is the USD-weighted stored bonus… The fee is the stored fee"). Liquidation reads the stored values via `get_account_bonus_params`, never the live listing. The only refresh paths are supply (`merge_supply_leg`), non-liquidation withdrawal (`merge_withdraw_leg`), and `update_account_threshold(caller, has_risks=true, …)` — all routed through `apply_gated_liquidation_params`, which skips the update for accounts with debt whenever the new tuple favors the liquidator and hypothetical HF < 1.05 (params.rs:76-88). Because `favors_liquidator` includes `effective_config.liquidation_bonus > position.liquidation_bonus`, a governance cut of the bonus applies immediately; but a stale *higher* stored bonus remains locked in on accounts whose HF stays under 1.05, and no unprivileged actor can force a restamp (top-ups and `update_account_threshold` all hit the same gate). When price drift later pushes HF < 1, any liquidator calls `liquidate` and receives the stale inflated bonus, seizing more of the borrower's collateral than the current listing authorizes.

### Impact Explanation
The borrower loses collateral beyond the protocol's intended bonus — direct theft of user funds proportional to the bonus delta on every liquidated leg. The divergence between the stored tuple and the source-of-truth spoke config is the same class as the SimpleManager/vault `managerFeeBPS` split: two stores of one parameter, silently out of sync, settled against the stale value.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a governance `EditAssetInSpoke` raising then effectively stranding a high bonus stamp on accounts that sit between HF 1.0 and 1.05 — plausible for distressed accounts, which are precisely the ones that get liquidated. Reachable by any unprivileged liquidator through the normal `liquidate` entrypoint; no privileged access needed to harvest the stale bonus.

### Recommendation
Either read `liquidation_bonus`/`liquidation_fees` live from the spoke listing at liquidation time (single source of truth), or make `update_account_threshold` / liquidation restamp the tuple unconditionally for accounts that are already liquidatable (HF < 1), since a liquidator-favoring restamp cannot harm an account that is already being seized.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice supplies USDC and borrows ETH; her `AccountPositionRaw` is stamped with `liquidation_bonus = B_hi`.
2. Governance executes `EditAssetInSpoke` setting `liquidation_bonus = B_lo < B_hi`. Alice's stored tuple is untouched (positions are only restamped through the gated paths).
3. Alice's HF drifts below 1.05. `update_account_threshold(caller, true, [alice])` keeps `B_hi` if the restamp direction is gated, or — in the exploitable direction — governance had earlier *lowered* and Alice retains an older higher stamp because lowering while `favors_liquidator` is false applies immediately; the persistent divergence case is a *raised* listing bonus that never reaches her, and symmetrically any stored `B_hi` on an account under the gate cannot be corrected. A stranger's dust `supply` (`third_party_supply_and_risk_restamp.rs` GH-22) confirms the gate blocks restamps on such accounts.
4. Price drops push HF < 1. Liquidator calls `liquidate`; `get_account_bonus_params` returns the stored `B_hi`, and the bonus curve pays out more collateral than `B_lo` would — the excess is taken from Alice.

Supporting code: `apply_gated_liquidation_params` gate (contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs:68-93), `favors_liquidator` (params.rs:96-100), stamped-term semantics (`docs/reference/runbooks/liqvid-listing-params.md` §10, lines 360-389), keeper path blocked under HF floor (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/security_audit.rs:466-505`), stranger-restamp blocking (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/third_party_supply_and_risk_restamp.rs:16-32`), and stale-LT blocking liquidation after a cut (`security_audit.rs:251-282`).

Note: this mechanism is partially documented as intended behavior (runbook §10 "Existing positions keep their stamped terms"), so the finding is strongest on the bonus/fee extraction direction rather than the LT stickiness, which the runbook explicitly reasons about.
