### Title
Whale-supplied market at sustained high utilization overflows the RAY i128 value domain during accrual, permanently freezing the market - (File: tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs)

### Summary
The DHCP CVE class — a counter/value that overflows after processing an enormous accumulated volume — maps onto the pool's `i128` RAY-domain value arithmetic. A single unprivileged address can supply a very large amount of a high-decimal asset, keep utilization high, and let interest accrual push `scaled_to_original` (`borrowed × borrow_index / RAY`) past `i128::MAX`. Because every pool verb calls `global_sync`/`accrue_step` first, the resulting `MathOverflow` panic bricks the market: no withdraw, repay, borrow, or liquidation ever succeeds again. The `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` cap cannot engage first because value overflow occurs before the index ceiling.

### Finding Description
`accrue_step` in `common/src/rates/simulate.rs:60-61` computes `scaled_to_original(env, borrowed, borrow_index)` and `scaled_to_original(env, supplied, supply_index)` unconditionally each chunk. `update_borrow_index` (`common/src/rates/index.rs:13-19`) multiplies the index before clamping to `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY = 1e36`, so the borrowed-share total can cross the `i128` value ceiling while the index is still far below its cap. `MAX_BORROW_RATE_RAY = 2 * RAY` (`common/src/constants/pool.rs:12`) permits a 200% APR curve, under which index growth is steep enough that a ~1e9-token 18-decimal market at ~98% utilization reaches the value cliff. `docs/reference/formulas.md` numeric-limits section itself acknowledges: "Value overflow can occur before the index ceiling and block repayment/withdrawal because those operations accrue first." The harness test `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` demonstrates the freeze concretely: `try_update_indexes_for` returns `MathOverflow`, and `try_withdraw_raw` / `try_repay` subsequently fail with the same error — the index cap never engages and no recovery path exists.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of funds. Once `borrowed × borrow_index` exceeds the i128 domain, every entrypoint that touches the market (`update_indexes`, `supply`, `borrow`, `withdraw`, `repay`, `liquidate`, `flash_loan`, `claim_revenue`) panics in accrual before any state change. All suppliers' deposits, the borrower's collateral backing that debt, and accrued protocol revenue in that market are locked forever; liquidation of the bad debt becomes impossible, dragging the whole hub toward insolvency as prices move.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a whale-scale position (on the order of a billion whole tokens of an 18-decimal asset, ~98% utilized) and years of compounding at a steep rate segment — high capital cost and long horizon, but reachable by a single unprivileged address via `supply`/`borrow`/`update_indexes` with no privileged parameters; `max_utilization` can be disabled or bypassed by governance settings and the borrow cap saturation behavior described in `formulas.md` does not prevent it. Medium likelihood, High severity.

### Recommendation
Cap accrual when the pre-clamp index multiplication would overflow (clamp `borrow_index`/`supply_index` at their ceilings *before* computing interest, and skip interest once capped), or bound the accrual step so `scaled_to_original` cannot overflow — e.g., treat `borrow_index == MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` as "accrue nothing" (already true at the cap per `borrow_index_at_the_ceiling_is_sticky_and_accrues_nothing`, but the panic fires before the cap is reached). Alternatively add a monitored per-market total-value ceiling that rejects `supply`/`borrow` once `supplied × index` approaches `i128::MAX / factor_headroom`.

### Proof of Concept
`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:320-361` is an executable PoC: supply `1e9 * 10^18` BIG18, borrow 98% of it at max utilization, advance time year-by-year; `try_update_indexes_for("BIG18")` fails with `MATH_OVERFLOW` within 40 simulated years while `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`, after which `try_withdraw_raw` and `try_repay` fail identically — the market is frozen with all funds locked.
