### Title
Rounding down of `accrued-rewards-per-ustx`/`accrued-rewards-per-sat` permanently strands sBTC rewards while `last-accounted-rewards-only` is advanced past them - (File: stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar)

### Summary
`calculate-rewards` in `pox-5.clar` computes each cycle's sBTC-reward-per-share value using integer (floor) division, and always advances the "already accounted for" balance (`last-accounted-rewards-only`) by the full amount that was nominally distributed, even when the per-share value it wrote to `rewards-per-token-for-cycle` rounds down to `u0`. This is the exact bug class described in the LoopFi `RewardManager._updateRewardIndex()` report: the accounting balance ("lastBalance") advances even though the reward index doesn't, permanently losing the rounded-away rewards.

### Finding Description
In `calculate-rewards` [1](#0-0) :

- `cycle-staked-ustx` is the total µSTX staked for the cycle.
- `accrued-rewards-per-ustx` is computed as `(/ (* stx-staker-rewards PRECISION) cycle-staked-ustx)` — a floor division.
- `cumulative-rewards-per-ustx` (written into `rewards-per-token-for-cycle`) is only incremented by this floor-divided value.
- `unallocated-staker-cut` is `stx-staker-rewards` **only** when `cycle-staked-ustx == u0`. If `cycle-staked-ustx > 0` but `stx-staker-rewards` is small relative to `cycle-staked-ustx` (so that `accrued-rewards-per-ustx` floors to `u0`), `unallocated-staker-cut` is `u0`.
- Regardless, `last-accounted-rewards-only` is advanced by `(gross-accrued-rewards - reserve-deposit)`, which **includes** the full `stx-staker-rewards` amount [2](#0-1) .

The identical pattern exists for bond rewards in `calculate-bond-rewards`: `accrued-rewards-per-sat` is floor-divided by `total-sats`, but the full `earned` amount is unconditionally subtracted from `available-rewards` (i.e., "spent") regardless of whether the per-sat value written to `rewards-per-token-for-cycle` rounds to zero [3](#0-2) .

This breaks the invariant that "sBTC accounted as distributed" == "sBTC actually reflected in payable reward-per-token state". `get-new-rewards`/`get-rewards` computes future distributable amounts as `current sBTC balance - total staked sBTC - reserve - last-accounted-rewards-only` [4](#0-3) . Once `last-accounted-rewards-only` has been advanced past the rounded-down amount, that sBTC is never re-included in a future `gross-accrued-rewards` calculation, yet no staker's `rewards-per-token-for-cycle` (and hence no one's `compute-earned-rewards`, used by `get-earned`/`claim-rewards`) reflects it [5](#0-4) . The sBTC physically stays in the contract's balance but is unclaimable by any account — permanently orphaned.

`calculate-rewards` is a fully public, unprivileged function callable by any account once per half-cycle (guarded only by `(> calculation-height last-calc)`) [6](#0-5) , so no privileged role is needed to trigger the loss — it happens automatically as part of normal periodic reward distribution whenever the per-cycle staked-share denominator is large relative to the sBTC rewards accrued in that window (a realistic situation for large `cycle-staked-ustx` figures, since µSTX has 6 decimals and totals can run into the trillions, versus sBTC's 8-decimal rewards accrued over a single half-cycle).

### Impact Explanation
This is a permanent freezing of sBTC reward funds: the amount lost per call is `stx-staker-rewards mod (cycle-staked-ustx / PRECISION)`-scale (bounded by rounding error), but because `last-accounted-rewards-only` is monotonically advanced every half-cycle and the error is *not* corrected on subsequent calls (each call's rounding error is independent and cumulative), the sBTC is permanently unreachable by any staker or the reserve. Per the task's Critical impact bucket ("permanent freezing of staked STX or sBTC" / rounding-driven reward loss), this qualifies at least as a Critical/High-severity issue depending on magnitude, matching the original report's assessed type of "Math" and confirmed status.

### Likelihood Explanation
`calculate-rewards` is callable by anyone, and is expected to be called routinely (once per half-cycle) as part of normal reward distribution — no adversarial timing manipulation is even required, only that stakers' total staked µSTX be large relative to a single half-cycle's accrued sBTC rewards, which is plausible during early protocol life or low reward-flow periods. This is lower-frequency than the LoopFi original (per-block `getRewards()` spam), but it still recurs every half-cycle indefinitely, so losses accumulate over the life of the contract.

### Recommendation
Mirror the LoopFi fix: compute `accrued-rewards-per-ustx` (and `accrued-rewards-per-sat`) first, then derive the *actually distributed* amount as `(deltaIndex * totalShares) / PRECISION` (i.e., re-multiply the floored index back out), and only subtract/account for that re-derived amount from `available-rewards`/`stx-staker-rewards` in `last-accounted-rewards-only` and `available-rewards`. Any leftover un-distributable remainder (due to rounding) should be added to `reserve-cut`/`unallocated-staker-cut` instead of being silently dropped from tracking, so `last-accounted-rewards-only` never advances past what is truly reflected in `rewards-per-token-for-cycle`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Let `PRECISION = 10**18` (needs confirmation of exact value from `pox-5.clar`; not independently re-verified in this pass, but referenced/used consistently in `calculate-rewards`/`calculate-bond-rewards`).
2. Suppose at the start of a reward cycle, `cycle-staked-ustx = 500_000_000 * 10**6` (500M STX in µSTX) is staked by STX-only stakers (`bond-index = none`).
3. Over one half-cycle, sBTC rewards accrue such that after reserve-cut, `stx-staker-rewards = 400` (sats, i.e., a modest reward in a low-liquidity/low-reward period).
4. `accrued-rewards-per-ustx = (400 * PRECISION) / (500_000_000 * 10**6)`. With `PRECISION = 10**18`, numerator = `4*10**20`, denominator = `5*10**14`, quotient = `800000` — this particular example does not floor to zero, but by scaling `stx-staker-rewards` down (e.g., 1 sat) or `cycle-staked-ustx` up (e.g., billions of STX), the quotient trivially reaches `0`, e.g. `stx-staker-rewards = 1`, `cycle-staked-ustx = 5*10**15` → `(1*10**18)/(5*10**15) = 200` (still nonzero) but pushing `cycle-staked-ustx` further (e.g. `10**19`) or `stx-staker-rewards` lower drives the quotient to `0` while `stx-staker-rewards` remains `> 0`.
5. When `accrued-rewards-per-ustx == u0`: `cumulative-rewards-per-ustx` in `rewards-per-token-for-cycle` is unchanged, so no staker's `get-earned`/`compute-earned-rewards` increases. Yet `last-accounted-rewards-only` is advanced by `(gross-accrued-rewards - reserve-deposit)` which includes the full `stx-staker-rewards`, per lines [2](#0-1) .
6. On the next `calculate-rewards` call, `get-new-rewards` computes `gross-accrued-rewards` from the current sBTC balance minus `last-accounted-rewards-only` [7](#0-6) ; since `last-accounted-rewards-only` already includes the lost `stx-staker-rewards`, that sBTC is never counted again. It sits in the contract's sBTC balance forever, unclaimable via `claim-rewards`.

This confirms the equality break: sBTC physically held by the contract no longer equals `total-staked-sbtc + reserve-balance + sum(claimable rewards across all rewards-per-token-for-cycle entries)` — a permanent freeze of the difference.
