The symbol `ConcurrentBTreeInterner::intern_deferred` referenced in the question does not exist in this codebase. The actual `ConcurrentBTreeInterner` implementation in `third_party/move/move-vm/types/src/interner.rs` only exposes `intern`, `intern_by_ref`, `lookup`, `get`, `len`, `is_empty`, and `flush` [1](#0-0) , as also confirmed by the test suite exercising these exact methods [2](#0-1) .

More importantly, this interner has nothing to do with the transaction-admission boundary defined by the review scope (REST → mempool → vm-validator → VM validation convergence on sender/signer/sequence/chain-id/expiry binding). `InternedModuleIdPool`, which wraps `ConcurrentBTreeInterner<ModuleId>`, is used purely internally by the Move VM's module loader/runtime environment to deduplicate `ModuleId` allocations during module loading and execution [3](#0-2) , and is wired into `RuntimeEnvironment` for code-cache/loader bookkeeping [4](#0-3) . It is not part of any admission decision (sender authentication, sequence number checks, chain-id, expiry, or replay binding) made by mempool, vm-validator, or the REST API — it only affects how already-admitted, already-validated transactions' referenced modules are identified internally during execution. Even a hypothetical race causing a duplicate `ModuleId` entry would be a VM-internal correctness/performance concern (e.g., duplicate cache entries), not a mechanism by which an unprivileged caller could get a transaction admitted under the wrong sender/signer, bypass sequence/expiry/chain-id checks, or cause admission-stack disagreement as required by the Decision Standard.
