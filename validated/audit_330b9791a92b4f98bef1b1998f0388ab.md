### Title
Self-liquidating an underwater account lets the borrower capture discounted collateral while suppliers absorb the unpaid debt - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/curve.rs)

### Summary
`liquidate` is permissionless and explicitly permits self-liquidation; `SeizeMode::Transfer` returns `None` as the receiver without checking whether `liquidator` owns the target account, while only `Credit(account_id)` rejects same-account seizure. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1)  When collateral value is below debt, the repayment quote is capped at `floor(collateral / (1 + base_bonus))`, rather than the full debt. [3](#0-2)  The borrower can therefore pay the discounted quote, receive the seized collateral back in `Transfer` mode, and leave the residual debt to be burned through bad-debt socialization. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

### Finding Description
`Controller::liquidate(liquidator, account_id, debt_payments, seize_mode)` requires only the liquidator’s authorization and does not authorize or exclude the target account owner. [6](#0-5)  The liquidation plan requires an unhealthy account, but it does not distinguish a third-party liquidation from one submitted by the account owner. [7](#0-6) 

For an insolvent account, `estimate_liquidation_amount` returns the collateral-backed amount `min(total_debt, floor(total_collateral / (1 + base_bonus)))` and uses the base bonus. [3](#0-2)  A larger offered repayment is trimmed to that quote when the plan is not a full close, so the borrower cannot be forced to pay the unbacked shortfall through liquidation. [8](#0-7) 

Execution then transfers the planned repayment from `liquidator` to the pool and repays the corresponding debt legs. [9](#0-8)  In `Transfer` mode, the same `liquidator` address is passed as the withdrawal receiver, and the pool transfers the collateral proceeds to that receiver after withholding any configured liquidation fee. [4](#0-3) [10](#0-9) 

After repayment and seizure, the controller recalculates account totals and calls bad-debt cleanup when remaining debt exceeds remaining collateral and collateral is at or below the dust threshold. [11](#0-10) [12](#0-11)  Cleanup sends the remaining borrow position to the pool, where it is burned and its value is subtracted from supplier claims by reducing the supply index. [5](#0-4) [13](#0-12) 

### Impact Explanation
This is theft of supplier funds because the borrower avoids paying the underwater shortfall, while unrelated suppliers’ claims are permanently written down through the supply-index reduction. [14](#0-13)  The loss can be as large as the account’s unbacked debt, bounded by the affected market’s total supplied value. [15](#0-14) 

For example, with collateral worth `100`, debt worth `110`, and a 5% base bonus, the borrower’s liquidation payment is approximately `95.24`; the borrower receives the collateral proceeds net of the liquidation fee, while approximately `14.76` of debt remains unbacked and is socialized. [3](#0-2) [5](#0-4)  By comparison, ordinary repayment would require paying the full `110` debt while retaining the `100` collateral. [16](#0-15) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack is reachable by any borrower whose account becomes unhealthy and insolvent through price movement or interest accrual, and it requires no privileged authorization or leaked key. [7](#0-6) [6](#0-5)  The borrower needs only enough debt tokens to fund the collateral-backed quote, which is lower than the full debt, making the attack easier to fund than ordinary repayment. [3](#0-2)  Automatic socialization is conditional on the remaining collateral being no more than the `$5` dust threshold, so the simplest profitable case is one where the liquidation seizes all or nearly all collateral. [17](#0-16) [12](#0-11) 

### Recommendation
Reject self-liquidation for both seizure modes by comparing `liquidator` with the target account’s owner and any authorized delegates, not only by rejecting `Credit(account_id)`. [2](#0-1)  Alternatively, restrict liquidation of accounts with `total_collateral < total_debt` to privileged keepers, or require self-liquidations to repay the full debt without a bonus or subsequent bad-debt cleanup. [3](#0-2) [12](#0-11) 

### Proof of Concept
1. A supplier deposits the debt asset so the market has supplier claims that can absorb bad debt. [18](#0-17) 
2. A borrower supplies collateral and borrows another asset while solvent; a subsequent collateral-price decrease makes `health_factor < 1` and `total_collateral < total_debt`. [7](#0-6) [3](#0-2) 
3. The borrower calls:
   ```rust
   controller.liquidate(
       borrower_address,
       borrower_account_id,
       vec![(HubAssetKey { hub_id, asset: debt_asset }, full_debt_amount)],
       SeizeMode::Transfer,
   );
   ```
   `debt_payments` is the debt leg for the borrowed asset, and `SeizeMode::Transfer` sends the collateral proceeds back to `borrower_address`. [19](#0-18) [10](#0-9) 
4. The plan trims the offered full-debt payment to `floor(C / (1 + base_bonus))`, pulls only that amount from the borrower, repays that portion of the debt, and withdraws the seized collateral to the same borrower address. [8](#0-7) [9](#0-8) [4](#0-3) 
5. The post-liquidation account has residual debt and zero or dust collateral, so `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation` invokes cleanup; the pool burns the remaining borrow shares and lowers the market supply index. [12](#0-11) [5](#0-4) 
6. For `C = 100`, `D = 110`, and `base_bonus = 5%`, the borrower pays about `95.24`, receives the collateral proceeds net of the fee, and suppliers absorb the remaining `D - 95.24 ≈ 14.76`. [3](#0-2) [13](#0-12)

/// Resolves an authorized credit receiver, or `None` for underlying transfer.
/// `Credit(0)` creates an account owned by the liquidator.
///
/// Credit requires a different account in the same spoke, in `Normal` mode.
fn resolve_seize_receiver(
    env: &Env,
    liquidator: &Address,
    account_id: u64,
    account: &Account,
    seize_mode: SeizeMode,
    cache: &mut Context,
) -> Option<(u64, Account)> {
    let requested = match seize_mode {
        SeizeMode::Transfer => return None,
        SeizeMode::Credit(id) => id,
    };

    if requested == 0 {
        return Some(account::create_account_with(
            env,
            liquidator,
            account.spoke_id,
            PositionMode::Normal,
            cache,
            SpokeAdmission::AllowDeprecated,
        ));
    }

    // Crediting the same account would undo the seizure.
    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        requested != account_id,
        CollateralError::SelfLiquidationNotAllowed
    );

/// Executes transfer-mode seizure: burns shares, debits pool cash, and pays
/// underlying to the liquidator after withholding the protocol fee.
pub(crate) fn apply_liquidation_seizures(
    env: &Env,
    liquidator: &Address,
    account: &mut Account,
    seized: &Vec<SeizeEntry>,
    cache: &mut Context,
) {
    let mut entries: Vec<PoolWithdrawEntry> = Vec::new(env);
    for entry in seized.iter() {
        enforce_spoke_asset_flags(
            env,
            cache,
            account.spoke_id,
            &entry.hub_asset,
            FreezePolicy::SeizureLeg,
        );

        let position: AccountPosition =
            (&expect_invariant(env, account.supply_positions.get(entry.hub_asset.clone()))).into();
        entries.push_back(PoolWithdrawEntry {
            action: make_pool_action(&position, entry.amount, entry.hub_asset),
            protocol_fee: entry.protocol_fee,
        });
    }
    apply_withdraw_batch(
        env,
        account,
        liquidator,
        WithdrawKind::Liquidation,
        events::PositionAction::LiqSeize,
        &entries,
        cache,
    );

/// Socializes `bad_debt` by reducing the supply index (capped at total supply value).
///
/// Used when seizing unpaid debt: remaining supplier claims shrink pro-rata.
/// No-op when total supplied value is zero. Floors the resulting index at
/// [`SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW`] to avoid a zero index.
pub(crate) fn apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(cache: &mut Cache, bad_debt: Ray) {
    let total_supplied_value = cache.supplied().mul(cache.env(), cache.supply_index());

    if total_supplied_value == Ray::ZERO {
        return;
    }

    let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
    let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);

    let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
    let new_supply_index = cache
        .supply_index()
        .mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);

    cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));
