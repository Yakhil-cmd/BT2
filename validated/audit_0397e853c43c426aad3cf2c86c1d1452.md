### Title

Transient Aquarius stable-pool imbalance can falsify LP collateral health and enable liquidation - (File: `common/src/oracle/lp_stable.rs`)

### Summary

An unprivileged trader can temporarily imbalance an Aquarius stable pool before invoking liquidation. `AquariusStableLp` pricing derives the LP-token price from the pool’s current reserves and share supply rather than a time-smoothed or independently anchored valuation. Because the controller recalculates health factor during `liquidate`, the transient reserve state can force an otherwise solvent borrower below `HF < 1` and let the attacker seize LP collateral at a liquidation bonus.

### Finding Description

`PriceSource::AquariusStableLp` is a supported production pricing source for LP collateral [1](#0-0) . The provider calls the pool’s live `get_reserves` and `get_total_shares`, resolves the two underlying prices, and passes those current values into `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` [2](#0-1) .

For stable pools, `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` normalizes the presently returned reserves, solves the StableSwap invariant `D`, multiplies it by the cheaper leg’s external price, and divides by current LP supply [3](#0-2) . A swap that leaves the pool sufficiently imbalanced therefore changes `D` and the resulting LP price; no reserve-ratio bound, smoothing window, or comparison with a historical invariant is applied in this path [4](#0-3) .

`liquidate` is permissionless and builds its plan from the invocation’s current risk totals [5](#0-4) . The plan admits the account only when the recalculated `health_factor` is below one WAD [6](#0-5) . Collateral valuation uses the invocation-cached feed price and supply index for each supplied asset [7](#0-6) .

### Impact Explanation

The attacker can force a temporary downward revaluation of a victim’s Aquarius stable-LP collateral, make a previously non-liquidatable account satisfy `health_factor < Wad::ONE`, and seize its LP collateral with the configured liquidation bonus. After the liquidation, the attacker can restore the pool balance through the reverse trade, restoring the LP valuation while retaining the seized collateral. This is theft of borrower collateral when the manipulation cost is lower than the bonus and subsequent value recovery [8](#0-7) .

The affected funds are user collateral rather than idle protocol funds. `SeizeMode::Transfer` pays the seized LP tokens from pool custody to the liquidator, while `SeizeMode::Credit` can credit equivalent supply shares to an attacker-controlled receiving account [9](#0-8) .

### Likelihood Explanation

The attack requires a listed `AquariusStableLp` collateral market, enough capital to create a significant reserve imbalance, and a victim whose health factor is close enough to one for the transient valuation change to cross the liquidation boundary. Those preconditions and Aquarius swap slippage make the attack conditional rather than universally profitable, supporting Medium severity.

No privileged role, leaked key, malicious underlying feed, or controller callback is required. The attacker only submits their own Aquarius trade and then calls the public `liquidate` entrypoint [10](#0-9) .

### Recommendation

Do not derive liquidation-sensitive LP collateral value solely from a pool’s instantaneous reserve state. Prefer a bounded, time-smoothed pool NAV or invariant, enforce a maximum reserve-imbalance deviation before accepting the stable-LP reading, and pair the LP valuation with an independent source where economically feasible. If instantaneous reserves remain necessary, reject reserve ratios outside a configured safety envelope rather than valuing the manipulated reserve state directly.

### Proof of Concept

1. Victim creates a leveraged position with Aquarius stable-LP collateral using `supply(victim, 0, spoke_id, [(lp_hub_asset, lp_amount)])`, followed by `borrow(victim, victim_account_id, [(debt_hub_asset, debt_amount)], None)` [11](#0-10) .
2. Attacker submits an Aquarius swap that strongly depletes one reserve leg of the configured pool. The next price read calls `get_reserves` and uses those altered reserves directly [12](#0-11) .
3. `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` recalculates `D` from the depleted reserves, lowering the reported LP price relative to its pre-trade value [3](#0-2) .
4. Attacker invokes `liquidate(attacker, victim_account_id, [(debt_hub_asset, offered_debt)], SeizeMode::Transfer)`. The controller reloads prices and market indexes, recalculates health from the manipulated LP price, and admits the liquidation once the result is below `Wad::ONE` [6](#0-5) .
5. The planned seizure transfers the victim’s LP collateral to the attacker at the liquidation bonus [8](#0-7) .
6. Attacker reverses the Aquarius trade, restoring the pool’s reserves and the LP price while retaining the collateral obtained during the transient liquidation window.
