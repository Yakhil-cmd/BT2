### Title
Whole-unit collateral rounding can make every liquidation offer revert until debt is donated or external state changes - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs)

### Summary
A solvent but unhealthy account whose only collateral position uses fewer than three decimals can enter a narrow debt band where liquidation planning cannot produce any seizure. Any offered repayment is normalized back to the curve quote; that quote is then rounded down to zero whole collateral units, causing the repayment to be released and `liquidate` to revert. The account remains live but cannot be liquidated until interest or prices change, the borrower repays, or someone donates repayment/collateral.

### Finding Description
`Controller::liquidate` is permissionless and accepts `debt_payments` plus a `SeizeMode`, then calls `process_liquidation`. [1](#0-0) 

For a solvent account, `normalize_repayment_plan` replaces the curve quote through `whole_unit_repayment` when the account has exactly one supply position with `asset_decimals < MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS`. [2](#0-1) 

That helper keeps the original curve quote in the problematic band: the held collateral contains at least one whole unit, the quoted seizure does not reach one unit plus the configured margin, but raising the quote to one unit would cost at least the entire debt. [3](#0-2) 

A larger liquidation offer cannot bypass this condition. Unless the normalized ideal repayment reaches the full debt, excess offered value is trimmed back to the ideal quote. [4](#0-3) 

`calculate_seized_collateral` then floors a partial sub-unit seizure to zero for a collateral asset with fewer than `MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS`, and records the quoted value as unbacked. [5](#0-4) 

`build_liquidation_plan` releases the unbacked repayment; when the single planned seizure is empty, it releases all remaining repayment as well. [6](#0-5) 

Execution subsequently calls `require_non_empty_payments` on `result.repaid`, so both partial and full-sized offers revert with an invalid-payment error rather than liquidating the position. [7](#0-6) 

The analogous recovery is externally funded: anyone may call `repay`, while third-party `supply` may add funds only to an existing supply position, so a rescuer can donate debt repayment or additional collateral to move the account out of the stuck band. [8](#0-7) [9](#0-8) 

### Impact Explanation
This can temporarily freeze the unhealthy position's collateral and prevent the protocol from clearing debt through the intended liquidation path. The borrower cannot withdraw while the post-operation solvency checks fail, and liquidators cannot reduce the debt even when they are willing to repay it. [10](#0-9) 

If the borrower lacks funds or refuses to repay, an external resolution requires either waiting for price/index movement or donating value through `repay` or third-party `supply`; donated value benefits the borrower rather than compensating the rescuer. Continued debt accrual while liquidation is blocked can also move the account toward insolvency.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate to low because the state requires a specific combination:

1. A sole collateral position.
2. Collateral decimals below `MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS`.
3. At least one whole collateral unit.
4. Health factor below `1 WAD`.
5. A curve quote that cannot back one whole unit, while the repayment needed to back one unit is at least the remaining debt.

Those bounds are enforced directly in `whole_unit_repayment` and the whole-unit seizure branch. [11](#0-10) [12](#0-11) 

However, no privileged action, oracle manipulation, token defect, or special route is required; an ordinary borrower can reach the state through normal `supply` and `borrow`, followed by ordinary price movement or interest accrual.

### Recommendation
When the sole collateral is a whole-unit asset and the curve quote rounds to zero, choose an executable liquidation outcome deterministically:

- Promote the quote to the minimum repayment that backs one whole unit when that repayment is below the whole debt.
- If that minimum is at least the whole debt, promote the quote to a full close and seize the appropriate whole unit.
- Alternatively, allow a fractional share credit in `SeizeMode::Credit` where shares can represent the seizure even though the token-unit representation rounds to zero.

The resulting quote should remain bounded by collateral and preserve the intended bonus cap. Add regression coverage that tries offers below, equal to, and above the curve quote while the account is inside this band.

### Proof of Concept
Let a borrower have:

- One supply position in asset `C`, with `C.decimals < MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS`.
- `held_units(C) >= 1`.
- Debt `D`, priced in WAD USD.
- `HF < 1 WAD` and `C >= D`, so the account is unhealthy but solvent.
- Liquidation bonus `b`.

Define:

```text
U             = USD value of one whole unit of C
unit_at_bonus = floor(U / (1 + b))
unit_quote    = ceil((U + U / 1_000_000) / (1 + b))
Q             = liquidation curve quote
```

Enter the stuck band when:

```text
Q * (1 + b) < U + U / 1_000_000
unit_at_bonus < D + one_base_unit_of_each_debt_leg
unit_quote >= D
```

The first condition keeps `Q` from reaching one whole seized unit, while the second and third prevent promotion either to full debt or to the one-unit quote. [3](#0-2) 

An unprivileged liquidator can then reproduce the failure with:

```rust
controller.liquidate(
    liquidator,
    account_id,
    vec![(debt_hub_asset, offered_amount)],
    SeizeMode::Credit(0),
);
```

For `offered_amount < D`, normalization accepts at most `Q`; for `offered_amount >= D`, the excess is trimmed to `Q` because `full_close` is false. [13](#0-12) 

The planned `Q * (1 + b)` collateral value converts to less than one whole `C` unit, so the partial whole-unit seizure floors to zero and is omitted. [5](#0-4) 

With no collateral leg left, `build_liquidation_plan` releases the entire repayment, leaving `result.repaid` empty; `process_liquidation` therefore reverts before token repayment or share seizure. Repeating the call with any positive offer hits the same normalized state, while `SeizeMode::Transfer` follows the same empty-seizure path. [6](#0-5) [14](#0-13)
