### Title
Unprivileged dust top-up permanently blocks `clean_bad_debt` by pushing collateral above the $5 socialization gate - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs)

### Summary
The permissionless `clean_bad_debt` entrypoint only admits an insolvent account when its total collateral is at or below `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` ($5). Because any third party may top up an existing supply leg of any account with an arbitrarily small amount, an attacker can keep a bad-debt account's collateral above the dust threshold indefinitely, forcing every `clean_bad_debt` call to revert with `CannotCleanBadDebt` and leaving the bad debt unsocialized until governance executes the timelocked `force_socialize_bad_debt` path.

### Finding Description
The report's bug class — a permissionless, repeatable call that flips state so an important operation fails — maps onto the controller's bad-debt cleanup.

The admission predicate for the permissionless gate:

```rust
// contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/curve.rs:25-27
pub(crate) fn is_socializable_bad_debt(total_debt: Wad, total_collateral: Wad) -> bool {
    total_debt > total_collateral && total_collateral <= Wad::from(BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD)
}
```

`process_clean_bad_debt` authorizes only `caller.require_auth()` — any address may call it — and routes through `BadDebtGate::DustCapped`, which reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` when the predicate fails [1](#0-0) .

Meanwhile, `supply` explicitly permits third parties to top up any supply leg the account already holds:

```rust
// contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs:86-96
if account_id != 0
    && !account::is_owner_or_delegate(env, resolved_account_id, caller, &account.owner)
{
    for (hub_asset, _) in aggregated.iter() {
        assert_with_error!(
            env,
            account.supply_positions.contains_key(hub_asset.clone()),
            GenericError::NotAuthorized
        );
    }
}
```

Supply applies no post-pool solvency gate that would reject deposits onto insolvent accounts (INV-RISK-01: "Supply, repayment and liquidation do not universally apply this final gate") [2](#0-1) . So a griefer deposits ~$5.01 of an asset the insolvent account already holds; `total_collateral` crosses the dust cap; `clean_bad_debt` now always reverts. If later price movement or a partial liquidation drags collateral back below $5, the griefer repeats the top-up — the attack is repeatable exactly like the report's re-syncable `start_balance_sync`. The account owner cannot remove the donated collateral either: `withdraw` requires `ltv_collateral >= total_debt` and `HF >= 1` after the withdrawal [3](#0-2) , which an account with `debt > collateral` can never satisfy, so the dust wedge cannot be withdrawn by the victim.

### Impact Explanation
The permissionless cleanup path is bricked for as long as the griefer maintains the wedge. Bad debt remains on the market's books instead of being written down via the supply index and having residual collateral reclassified as revenue [4](#0-3) . Recovery requires governance to schedule and execute `force_socialize_bad_debt` (owner-only `BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly`, Sensitive delay tier) [5](#0-4)  — and a griefer can repeatedly re-block the permissionless path while ordinary liquidations remain unprofitable (`D > C`). This is a temporary freezing of cleanup/settlement and a delay in loss recognition borne by suppliers; severity is bounded by the governance fallback and by the fact that the griefer's donations become protocol revenue once cleanup eventually lands. Medium.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires no privilege: `supply` and `clean_bad_debt` are both callable by anyone, the wedge costs on the order of the $5 threshold (plus gas), and insolvent accounts near the dust boundary are a recurring state in any lending market. The only requirements are that the target account already holds a supply position in some asset (nearly always true, since it is being liquidated/cleaned) and that collateral sits near the gate.

### Recommendation
Exclude third-party donations from the dust-gate valuation, or gate third-party top-ups onto insolvent accounts. Concretely: in `require_third_party_existing_supply` (contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs:78-97), reject top-ups when `total_debt > total_collateral` for the target account, or compute `is_socializable_bad_debt` on collateral that existed before the current ledger sequence's first top-up. A simpler alternative is to let `clean_bad_debt` sweep any donation received after the account became insolvent into revenue as part of cleanup, removing the griefer's leverage.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice supplies 100 USDC and borrows ETH; the USDC price crashes so `total_debt = $50 > total_collateral = $3 ≤ $5`. The account is inside the dust gate — `clean_bad_debt` would succeed (mirroring `policy_zero_threshold_account_is_still_socializable_as_bad_debt` in `contracts/controller/tests/positions/liquidation_zero_threshold.rs:477-494`).
2. Eve calls `supply(caller: eve, account_id: alice_id, spoke_id, [(usdc_hub_key, ~5.01 USDC)])`. `require_third_party_existing_supply` passes because Alice already holds a USDC supply leg; no solvency gate applies to pure supply.
3. `total_collateral ≈ $8.01 > $5`. Any subsequent `clean_bad_debt(caller, alice_id)` reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` (contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs:229-235).
4. Alice cannot withdraw the wedge (`InsufficientCollateral`), and every time accrual or a small liquidation pulls collateral back under $5, Eve repeats step 2. Cleanup remains blocked until governance schedules `ForceSocializeBadDebt`.

/// Authorizes permissionless dust-gated cleanup outside flash loans.
pub(crate) fn process_clean_bad_debt(env: &Env, caller: &Address, account_id: u64) {
    caller.require_auth();
    validation::require_not_flash_loaning(env);
    clean_bad_debt_standalone(env, account_id);
}

/// Admission condition for bad-debt socialization.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
enum BadDebtGate {
    /// Permissionless: insolvent *and* collateral at or below the dust threshold.
    DustCapped,
    /// Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind.
    InsolventOnly,
}

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);
}

/// Socializes insolvent debt when remaining collateral is at or below the dust cap.
pub(crate) fn clean_bad_debt_standalone(env: &Env, account_id: u64) {
    socialize_bad_debt(env, account_id, BadDebtGate::DustCapped);
}
```

### INV-LIQ-04 — Bad-debt socialization is explicit and total

Permissionless cleanup requires ceil risk debt greater than half-up unweighted
collateral and collateral at or below the fixed $5 dust threshold. Owner-only
forced cleanup omits the dust cap. Both require debt, readable account and NFT
state, valid required prices and no active flash guard. Listing flags and
global pause do not block standalone cleanup.

Cleanup reclassifies all remaining collateral shares as revenue and writes off
all remaining debt against each debt's market. It releases spoke usage and
atomically removes account entries and the NFT. It does not net same-market
supply against debt. Standalone cleanup emits `CleanBadDebtEvent` with
pre-cleanup USD totals, without a controller position-update batch.

Ordinary liquidation and cleanup apply no final account-health or full-backing
assertion. The index floor can leave a shortfall. Recapitalization fills that
shortfall without restoring the lost index or deleted account.
```

# Force-socialize bad debt

This runbook covers governance-authorized removal of an insolvent account.
Use it when collateral exceeds the fixed $5 dust limit of permissionless
`clean_bad_debt`. Cleanup can also proceed when `no_seize` blocks ordinary
liquidation.

The account must hold debt, with ceil risk debt strictly greater than half-up
unweighted collateral (`D > C`). An unhealthy health factor alone does not
qualify. The forced path has no collateral dust cap.

**Cleanup is irreversible.** All remaining collateral becomes protocol revenue.
All remaining debt is written off against its own markets' supply indexes,
including when collateral and debt share a market. There is no automatic netting
or insurance payment. The account is deleted and its position NFT burns.

## Before scheduling

1. Confirm the network, target controller, governance contract, account id,
   NFT owner, positions and spoke. `force_socialize_bad_debt` is owner-only.
   Governance, the controller owner, schedules `ForceSocializeBadDebt` on the
   Sensitive delay tier; follow the
   [governance interface](../endpoints.md#governance) for proposal and execution.
