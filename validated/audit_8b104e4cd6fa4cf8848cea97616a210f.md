### Title
Insolvent borrowers can self-liquidate to retain the liquidation bonus and socialize additional debt - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/curve.rs)

### Summary

An underwater account owner can call `liquidate` on their own account using `SeizeMode::Transfer`, repay only the collateral-backed amount `floor(C / (1 + base_bonus))`, receive substantially all collateral, and trigger automatic bad-debt cleanup for the remainder. The liquidation bonus therefore lets the borrower keep value that should have reduced protocol bad debt.

### Finding Description

`process_liquidation` authenticates only the caller as `liquidator`; it does not require the liquidated account’s owner to be a different party, and explicitly permits owner self-liquidation in `Transfer` mode. [1](#0-0) 

For an insolvent account, `estimate_liquidation_amount` quotes `min(D, floor(C / (1 + base_bonus)))`, where `C` is unweighted collateral and `D` is total debt. [2](#0-1) 

If the liquidator offers enough debt assets, `normalize_repayment_plan` trims the accepted repayment to that quote and marks the plan `seize_all` when it reaches the quote within one unit per debt leg. [3](#0-2) 

For `seize_all`, `calculate_seized_collateral` ignores the ordinary proportional cap and uses the entire collateral balance for every seized leg. [4](#0-3) 

`SeizeMode::Transfer` resolves to no separate receiver, so the collateral withdrawal is paid directly to the same authenticated liquidator. [5](#0-4) 

After execution, any residual debt with at most five USD of remaining collateral is automatically socialized through `execute_bad_debt_cleanup`. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

### Impact Explanation

This is protocol insolvency and indirect theft from suppliers: the borrower pays less than the collateral’s value while withdrawing nearly all collateral, and the unpaid residual is written down against the debt market’s suppliers. [8](#0-7) 

The incremental loss caused by the bonus is approximately `C - C / (1 + base_bonus)`, less any bounded liquidation fee; with a 5% base bonus and `C = 100`, roughly 4.76 USD of collateral value is diverted from debt recovery to the borrower. [9](#0-8) 

### Likelihood Explanation

Any account that becomes insolvent and has collateral with a positive stamped liquidation bonus can execute this path without special privileges, oracle manipulation, or ownership of another account. [10](#0-9) 

The borrower can use the originally borrowed assets or acquire the debt asset externally, then self-liquidate before an independent liquidator captures the discounted collateral. [11](#0-10) 

The vulnerability requires the account to satisfy `C < D`, `HF < 1`, and have sufficient collateral value for the quote to produce a nonzero seizure. [12](#0-11) 

### Recommendation

For an insolvent liquidation initiated by the account owner or an authorized delegate, either require repayment of the full debt or remove the liquidation bonus and liquidation-fee benefit from the seized collateral.

If full ownership-based prevention is considered insufficient because borrowers can use a second address, reduce the economic incentive by charging a liquidation penalty or reserving a larger portion of the seizure for debt recovery rather than paying the entire collateral-backed discount to the liquidator.

### Proof of Concept

Assume one collateral market and one debt market, with prices normalized so token amounts equal USD values, a 5% base liquidation bonus, and an account with `C = 100` collateral, `D = 110` debt, and `W < D`, giving `HF < 1`. [13](#0-12) 

1. The account owner calls controller `liquidate(liquidator, account_id, debt_payments, seize_mode)` with their own address as `liquidator`, `account_id` set to their underwater account, a debt payment covering the debt leg, and `SeizeMode::Transfer`.
2. The plan selects the insolvency quote `floor(100 / 1.05) = 95.238` instead of requiring repayment of the 110 USD debt. [14](#0-13) 
3. The controller pulls about 95.238 debt tokens from the borrower/liquidator and repays that amount to the pool. [11](#0-10) 
4. `seize_all` causes the plan to seize the full 100 USD collateral balance, while the transfer-mode payout sends it to the same caller net of any bounded bonus fee. [15](#0-14) [16](#0-15) 
5. The account retains approximately 14.762 USD of unpaid debt and no meaningful collateral.
6. `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation` sees `total_debt > total_collateral` and zero or dust collateral, then socializes the remaining debt and removes the account. [6](#0-5) 

Compared with honestly repaying 110 and withdrawing 100, the borrower pays only about 95.238, receives nearly 100 collateral, and leaves the bonus-adjusted shortfall to suppliers.
