### Title
Bad-debt write-down resurrects wiped supply claims that can drain recapitalization - ([File: contracts/pool/src/interest.rs](contracts/pool/src/interest.rs))

### Summary
When bad debt reduces supplier claims to zero or below `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW`, `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` clamps the new supply index upward instead of preserving the wipeout, leaving every old share with a nonzero residual claim. [1](#0-0)  Those resurrected claims can later withdraw cash added by `recapitalize`, transferring recapitalization funds to holders whose positions were meant to be effectively destroyed. [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

### Finding Description
Permissionless `clean_bad_debt` authenticates only the caller and admits an insolvent account when its remaining collateral is at or below the socialization dust threshold. [4](#0-3)  Cleanup serializes the victim's deposit and borrow positions into `PoolSeizeEntry` records and sends them to the pool. [5](#0-4) 

For a borrow-side entry, the pool unscales the bad debt, applies it to the supply index, and burns the debt shares. [6](#0-5)  The write-down caps the loss at total supplied value and computes the surviving index proportionately, but then unconditionally raises the result to `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW`. [7](#0-6) 

This is a logical use-after-free: aggregate supply shares survive as positive claims after the socialization calculation has reduced their backing to less than the floor, including complete wipeouts where `remaining == 0`. [1](#0-0)  Withdrawal unscales those surviving shares against the floored index, checks only available reserves before debiting cash, and permits a full close through `i128::MAX`. [8](#0-7) [3](#0-2) 

### Impact Explanation
A near-total or total bad-debt write-down inflates the intended residual value of all remaining supply shares by up to the floor ratio. [9](#0-8)  After a third party recapitalizes the damaged market, holders of those wiped shares can withdraw the newly credited cash even though the socialization formula had assigned them little or no surviving claim. [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

The result is theft of recapitalization funds and can leave the market insolvent again after the phantom claims are paid. [10](#0-9) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger is permissionless once an account is insolvent with collateral at or below the bad-debt dust threshold. [11](#0-10)  An attacker can establish a normally collateralized borrow, wait for collateral depreciation to make the debt a near-total claim on the borrowed market, and then call `clean_bad_debt`; alternatively, an ordinary liquidation can reach the same cleanup path when its post-liquidation totals satisfy the socialization gate. [12](#0-11) 

The attack requires the bad debt to consume almost all supplier value and a later recapitalization or cash credit before the residual claim is valuable, so the conditions are meaningful but not universally present. [1](#0-0) 

### Recommendation
Do not clamp a positive wipeout result upward to `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW`; preserve the exact nonzero `new_supply_index`, and handle complete wipeouts through an explicit market-reset path that clears aggregate supply/revenue state and prevents stale controller positions from remaining withdrawable. [9](#0-8)  If a nonzero floor is required for subsequent division, mark the market as wiped or closed and reject withdrawals, liquidation share credits, and recapitalization until all old supply shares are explicitly settled or burned. [2](#0-1) 

### Proof of Concept
1. An attacker holds all or most scaled supply `S` in market `M`, while a borrower has debt `D` in `M` backed by other collateral.
2. The collateral price falls until the borrower is insolvent and `D >= supplied_value(M)`, while remaining collateral is below the dust threshold.
3. Any address calls controller `clean_bad_debt(caller, account_id)` for the borrower. [13](#0-12) 
4. The pool receives a borrow-side `PoolSeizeEntry`, computes `bad_debt >= total_supplied_value`, and obtains `remaining == 0`, so the proportional `new_supply_index` is zero. [6](#0-5) [14](#0-13) 
5. Line 88 raises that zero index to `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW`, leaving `S` with value `S * SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW` instead of zero. [15](#0-14) 
6. A recapitalizer later adds at least that floor-denominated amount of cash to restore the market.
7. The attacker calls controller `withdraw` on their surviving supply position with a full-close amount such as `i128::MAX`; `resolve_withdrawal` burns `S` and returns a positive gross amount, while `require_reserves` accepts because the recapitalization cash is present. [8](#0-7) [3](#0-2) 
8. The pool transfers that cash to the attacker, while the recapitalizer receives no corresponding shares or repayment. [16](#0-15)

pub(crate) fn apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(cache: &mut Cache, bad_debt: Ray) {
    let total_supplied_value = cache.supplied().mul(cache.env(), cache.supply_index());

    if total_supplied_value == Ray::ZERO {
        return;
    }

    let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
    let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);

    let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
    let new_supply_index = cache
        .supply_index()
        .mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);

    cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));

/// Admission condition for bad-debt socialization.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
enum BadDebtGate {
    /// Permissionless: insolvent *and* collateral at or below the dust threshold.
    DustCapped,
    /// Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind.
    InsolventOnly,
}

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);

/// Panics with `PoolInsolvent` if the market has a positive backing shortfall.
///
/// Backing = cash + ceiled debt value; claims = floored supply value.
pub(crate) fn require_backed_market(env: &Env, cache: &Cache) {
    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        backing_shortfall(cache) == 0,
        CollateralError::PoolInsolvent
    );
}

/// Asset units by which supplier claims exceed cash + debt (0 if solvent).
pub(crate) fn backing_shortfall(cache: &Cache) -> i128 {
    let supplied_claim = cache.unscale_supply_floor(cache.supplied());
    let outstanding_debt = cache.unscale_borrow_ceil(cache.borrowed());
    let backing = cache.cash().saturating_add(outstanding_debt);
    supplied_claim.saturating_sub(backing).max(0)
}
