### Title
Commission collector can be redirected without delay, letting a vote account's withdrawer misappropriate already-earned commission from the rightful collector - ([File: programs/vote/src/vote_state/mod.rs])

### Summary
The vote program delays the effect of a commission **rate** change by a full epoch via `delay_commission_updates` to prevent last-minute "commission rug pulls." However, the commission **collector** (the account that actually receives the commission lamports, introduced by SIMD-0232/custom_commission_collector) has no equivalent delay. `update_commission_collector` in [1](#0-0)  applies immediately once signed by the authorized withdrawer, while the epoch-reward distribution code always resolves the payee using the *current* (undelayed) vote state's `inflation_rewards_collector`/`block_revenue_collector`, even though it uses the *delayed* commission rate for the same reward. This exactly mirrors the Arrakis bug class: a privileged party (here, the authorized withdrawer, analogous to the vault owner) can redirect compensation that was already economically "earned" during a prior period (the rewarded epoch, analogous to the pool's accrued-but-uncollected fees) away from whoever was actually designated to receive it (analogous to the old manager), to a new destination of their choosing, right before the payout.

### Finding Description
Reward distribution is computed in `redeem_delegation_rewards`, [2](#0-1) :

- The **commission rate** (`commission_bps`) is deliberately fetched from a *delayed* snapshot (`snapshot_epoch_vote_accounts` / `rewarded_epoch_vote_accounts`) when `delay_commission_updates` is active, specifically to stop a validator/withdrawer from bumping the commission rate right before the reward is paid out.
- The **commission payee** (`commission_pubkey`), however, is always resolved from `vote_state.inflation_rewards_collector()` — i.e., the *current*, undelayed `distribution_epoch_vote_accounts` state — with no analogous delay mechanism.

`update_commission_collector`, [1](#0-0) , only requires the authorized withdrawer's signature and takes effect instantly (no epoch-schedule gating), unlike `update_commission`/`update_commission_bps` which explicitly implement the delay rule described in [3](#0-2) .

Because the amount of commission owed for the rewarded epoch is fixed by credits already earned and the (delayed) commission rate, but the *destination* of that commission is only fixed at distribution time, the authorized withdrawer can wait until an epoch's voting/credit activity is complete (i.e., the commission lamports for that epoch are effectively already "earned" under whatever collector was designated during that epoch) and then, just before the epoch-boundary reward distribution runs, call `UpdateCommissionCollector` to point the collector at a different account. The already-accrued commission for the just-completed epoch is then paid entirely to the new collector instead of the account that was the designated collector while the epoch's votes were being cast and rewards accrued.

This is structurally identical to the Arrakis finding: the "owner" (withdrawer) unilaterally reassigns the party entitled to already-accumulated-but-undistributed compensation (the vote account's "manager"/operator equivalent) without any settlement of what was already owed to the prior collector.

### Impact Explanation
An authorized withdrawer (e.g., a custodian in a staking-as-a-service arrangement where the vote account's node operator is compensated via a separate designated `inflation_rewards_collector`/`block_revenue_collector`) can systematically divert commission that was implicitly earned by the operator's validator uptime/voting during a completed epoch, by swapping the collector pubkey immediately before the reward-distribution boundary block. This is a reward-accounting corruption/misdirection bug: funds that should go to the party who was the designated collector during the period the commission was earned are instead unconditionally redirected to a new address chosen after the fact, with no economic settlement of the intervening period. This falls under the "stake and reward accounting" analog class explicitly in scope.

### Likelihood Explanation
The action is reachable by a single, ordinary transaction signed by the vote account's `authorized_withdrawer` — no special leader/validator privilege is required, only an `UpdateCommissionCollector` instruction sent at any time before the epoch-boundary reward distribution runs (as visible from `vote_processor.rs`'s dispatch of `VoteInstruction::UpdateCommissionCollector`). Because `custom_commission_collector` is a feature-gated capability but requires no additional delay/timelock once active, this is trivially and repeatably exploitable every epoch by whoever controls the withdrawer key, exactly as the rate-change rug was before `delay_commission_updates` was introduced for commission rate.

### Recommendation
Apply the same delay semantics used for `delay_commission_updates`/commission rate changes to the commission collector fields: resolve `commission_pubkey` for a rewarded epoch from the same delayed (`snapshot_epoch_vote_accounts`/`rewarded_epoch_vote_accounts`) vote state snapshot used for `commission_bps` in `redeem_delegation_rewards`, rather than from the current/distribution-epoch vote state. This ensures the collector recorded at (or before) the start of the rewarded epoch — not one swapped in immediately before payout — receives the commission earned during that epoch.

### Proof of Concept
1. Vote account `V` has `authorized_withdrawer = W` and `inflation_rewards_collector = C_old` (e.g., the node operator's account) at the start of epoch `N`.
2. During epoch `N`, `V` votes normally, accruing credits/commission that will be paid out based on the (delayed) commission rate observed for epoch `N`.
3. Near the very end of epoch `N` (before the epoch-boundary reward distribution executes at the start of epoch `N+1`), `W` submits `VoteInstruction::UpdateCommissionCollector(CommissionKind::InflationRewards)` (processed via `programs/vote/src/vote_processor.rs` → `vote_state::update_commission_collector`) setting `inflation_rewards_collector = C_new`.
4. At the epoch boundary, `redeem_delegation_rewards` computes the commission for epoch `N` using the delayed commission-rate snapshot, but resolves `commission_pubkey` from the *current* vote state, i.e., `C_new`.
5. `C_new` receives the entire commission payment for epoch `N`, even though `C_old` was the designated collector for the entire duration during which that commission was earned, and `C_old` receives nothing.

/// Update the vote account's commission collector (SIMD-0232).
pub fn update_commission_collector<S: std::hash::BuildHasher>(
    vote_account: &mut BorrowedInstructionAccount,
    target_version: VoteStateTargetVersion,
    new_collector: NewCommissionCollector,
    kind: CommissionKind,
    signers: &HashSet<Pubkey, S>,
    rent: &Rent,
) -> Result<(), InstructionError> {
    let mut vote_state = get_vote_state_handler_checked(vote_account, target_version)?;

    // Require authorized withdrawer to sign.
    verify_authorized_signer(vote_state.authorized_withdrawer(), signers)?;

    let new_collector_key = new_collector.validate_and_resolve_key(vote_account, rent)?;

    match kind {
        CommissionKind::InflationRewards => {
            vote_state.set_inflation_rewards_collector(new_collector_key);
        }
        CommissionKind::BlockRevenue => {
            vote_state.set_block_revenue_collector(new_collector_key);
        }
    }

    vote_state.set_vote_account_state(vote_account)
}
