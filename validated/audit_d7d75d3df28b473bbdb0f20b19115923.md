### Title
Dust Aquarius-LP collateral leg bricks liquidation and bad-debt cleanup when pool value dips below `min_pool_value_wad` - (File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs)

### Summary
The controller prices **every** supply position of an account when computing risk totals for `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt`. The Aquarius LP provider fails closed with `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` whenever the pool's fair value drops below the configured `min_pool_value_wad`. A borrower can supply a dust amount of an Aquarius LP share token as an extra collateral leg; any time that pool's value falls below the threshold — including by the borrower/LP withdrawing their own liquidity — every liquidation and bad-debt cleanup of the account reverts, shielding arbitrarily large debt until it goes bad.

### Finding Description
`build_liquidation_plan` calls `risk::calculate_account_risk_totals` over the account's full `supply_positions` and `borrow_positions` maps [1](#0-0) . Risk totals resolve a strict price for each asset through `Context::cached_price`, which panics with `OracleError::OracleNotConfigured`/propagates the aggregator error when no valid feed is returned [2](#0-1) . The aggregator's Aquarius provider returns `Err(OracleError::InsufficientAquariusLiquidity)` whenever `pool_value_wad < lp.min_pool_value_wad` [3](#0-2) .

A failed price for one collateral leg aborts the entire plan — there is no per-leg fallback or drop path. `clean_bad_debt` has the same dependency: per INV-LIQ-04 it requires "valid required prices" for the account [4](#0-3) . Supply is permissionless and needs no price (DoS.1 confirms supply is not price-gated), so adding the dust leg costs the attacker only the dust itself [5](#0-4) . Because seizure is pro-rata across all supply positions, the dust leg cannot be excluded by the liquidator.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of funds / protocol insolvency. While the LP pool's value is below `min_pool_value_wad`, the shielded account cannot be liquidated (every `liquidate` call reverts inside risk-totals pricing) and cannot be bad-debt-cleaned. Interest keeps accruing on the protected debt; if the collateral's real value decays during the outage, the protocol eats the shortfall through the index write-down at cleanup. The attacker chooses which single feed outage shields the whole account regardless of how large or well-collateralized the other legs are.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. Reachable by a single unprivileged address: `supply` a dust LP leg, then `borrow`. The outage trigger is also unprivileged: an LP in the referenced Aquarius pool can withdraw liquidity below `min_pool_value_wad` (own trades on Aquarius are in scope), and the borrower can be that LP. Even without active manipulation, thin Aquarius pools crossing the threshold during volatility is plausible, making this a recurring rather than one-shot shield. Mitigating factors: requires a listed Aquarius-LP collateral market with a nonzero `min_pool_value_wad`, and the block lifts when pool value recovers.

### Recommendation
- In `build_liquidation_plan` / `calculate_account_risk_totals`, treat a collateral leg whose price fails as zero-value (or skip it from the seizure plan) instead of aborting the whole computation, so one bad feed cannot veto liquidation of unrelated collateral.
- Alternatively, let `clean_bad_debt` value collateral at floor/zero when a required price errors, so cleanup remains reachable during feed outages.
- Consider enforcing a minimum collateral value per supply leg (not just per-account dust) so a shield leg must carry real value.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lists Aquarius LP share token `LPS` in the borrower's spoke with `min_pool_value_wad = X`.
2. Attacker supplies $N$ of a normal collateral (e.g., USDC) plus a dust amount of `LPS` via `supply`, then borrows near max via `borrow`.
3. Price moves push HF < 1. Attacker (an LP in the `LPS` pool) withdraws liquidity so `fair_lp_price_wad * total_shares / 10^decimals < X`.
4. `liquidate(liquidator, victim_id, payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)` reverts: `aquarius::read` returns `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` [6](#0-5) , which propagates through `cached_price` [2](#0-1)  and aborts `calculate_account_risk_totals` [1](#0-0) .
5. `clean_bad_debt` reverts the same way once collateral erodes below debt, so the debt accrues until socialized at a loss when the feed recovers.

### INV-LIQ-04 — Bad-debt socialization is explicit and total

Permissionless cleanup requires ceil risk debt greater than half-up unweighted
collateral and collateral at or below the fixed $5 dust threshold. Owner-only
forced cleanup omits the dust cap. Both require debt, readable account and NFT
state, valid required prices and no active flash guard. Listing flags and
global pause do not block standalone cleanup.

Cleanup reclassifies all remaining collateral shares as revenue and writes off
all remaining debt against each debt's market. It releases spoke usage and
atomically removes account entries and the NFT. It does not net same-market
supply against debt. Standalone cleanup emits `CleanBadDebtEvent` with
pre-cleanup USD totals, without a controller position-update batch.
