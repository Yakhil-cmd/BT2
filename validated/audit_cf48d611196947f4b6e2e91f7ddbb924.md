### Title
Missing minimum-seizure floor lets `liquidate` pull a real debt repayment while delivering zero collateral — (`contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs`)

### Summary
The external report's bug class is "a user-supplied parameter is validated only for positivity, so a transaction succeeds on the paying side while delivering nothing, silently burning the caller's funds." The same shape exists in XOXNO Lending's `liquidate` entrypoint: the liquidator submits `debt_payments` (`Vec<(HubAssetKey, i128)>`), the contract pulls real debt-token repayments from the liquidator, and the seized-collateral plan is computed entirely by rounding logic. When every collateral leg's seizure rounds down to zero tokens (Transfer mode) or zero shares (Credit mode) — which the protocol itself documents can happen for sub-3-decimal collateral legs and USD-proportional rounding — the call still succeeds, the liquidator's repayment is credited, and nothing is seized. The protocol's own operator guidance confirms this is accepted behavior: "The contract accepts a liquidation that repays debt and seizes nothing, so the liquidator would pay and receive no collateral" (`skills/xoxno-lending-liquidations/SKILL.md`).

### Finding Description
`Controller::liquidate` → `process_liquidation` performs these steps in order:

1. `liquidator.require_auth()` and `require_non_empty_payments(env, debt_payments)` — only positivity/non-emptiness of the *offered* repayments is checked.
2. `plan::build_liquidation_plan` produces `result.repaid` (what will actually be pulled) and `result.seized` (per-collateral-leg seizure amounts).
3. `require_non_empty_payments(env, &result.repaid)` — again only the *repayment* side is checked.
4. `apply_liquidation_repayments` pulls `result.repaid` debt tokens from the liquidator into the pool, measuring `received_usd`.
5. `scale_seizures_to_received` scales the seizure legs by the actual received USD.
6. `apply_liquidation_seizures` (Transfer) or `apply_liquidation_share_credit` (Credit) delivers whatever the `seized` vector contains — including an empty vector or all-zero legs.

There is no check anywhere that `seized` is non-empty or that any leg is non-zero. Seizure legs are dropped when they round to zero: "The contract drops a leg whose seizure rounds to zero tokens or zero shares" and whole-unit rounding for sub-3-decimal collateral legs can round down to zero with "a plan left with no seizure reverts" applying only inside the planner's dust-adjustment path, not to the aggregate result. Invariants doc INV-LIQ-02 likewise notes "rounding can leave repayment with no payable seizure." The unit test `liquidation_plan_validate_rejects_zero_seize_amount` rejects a *declared* zero leg inside a constructed plan, but the executor path never re-validates that the scaled result still contains a payable leg before the repayment is pulled.

Entry point and arguments reachable by any unprivileged address:

```
liquidate(liquidator, account_id, debt_payments: [(hub_asset, amount)], seize_mode)
```

with `debt_payments` sized such that the pro-rata seizure of each collateral position rounds to zero units/shares — e.g., a fractional repayment quote against collateral legs whose USD value per whole unit exceeds the proportional seizure.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent loss of user funds: the liquidator's debt tokens are transferred to the pool and the borrower's debt is written down, while the liquidator receives zero collateral in both `SeizeMode::Transfer` (pool cash payout of seized legs) and `SeizeMode::Credit` (share credit of seized legs). This is exactly the "source-side effect succeeds, delivery side produces nothing, no revert" structure of the original `_receiverGas` bug — the transaction completes and the caller's funds are gone with no compensating output. Severity is bounded because the loss is self-inflicted on the caller's own offer, matching the original report's profile; Medium.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any liquidator (permissionless — liquidation requires only `HF < 1` on the target) can submit `debt_payments`. The trigger conditions are realistic and documented: accounts holding collateral legs below 3 decimals seize whole units with down-rounding, and rounding "can leave repayment with no payable seizure." A competing liquidation, price drift, or accrued interest between estimate simulation and execution can also shrink a plan so that what was a payable leg rounds to zero at execution — and `scale_seizures_to_received` can further reduce seizure when a fee-on-transfer or under-delivering token shrinks `received_usd`. The protocol's bot guidance explicitly warns operators to reject estimates with empty `seized_collaterals` precisely because the contract will not.

### Recommendation
In `process_liquidation`, after `scale_seizures_to_received` (or inside `plan::build_liquidation_plan`/`into_result`), require that at least one seizure leg is strictly positive:

```rust
assert_with_error!(
    env,
    seized.iter().any(|leg| leg.amount > 0),
    CollateralError::InvalidPayments // or a dedicated NoSeizure error
);
```

This is the direct analog of "enforce a minimum `_receiverGas`": enforce a minimum *payable seizure* so that a repayment pull without delivery cannot commit. Additionally, the `get_liquidation_estimate` view should keep exposing the same empty-seizure signal so callers can reject such plans before submission.

### Proof of Concept
1. Setup: borrower account supplies a collateral whose price/decimals make whole-unit or proportional seizure rounding dominate (e.g., a high-value low-decimal asset, or a collateral position sized so the pro-rata leg for a small repayment is < 1 unit/share), and borrows enough that `health_factor < 1e18` after a price drop.
2. Liquidator calls `liquidate(liquidator, victim_id, [(debt_hub_asset, small_amount)], SeizeMode::Transfer)` where `small_amount` is the largest repayment whose computed seizure rounds to zero on every collateral leg.
3. Observe: `apply_liquidation_repayments` pulls `small_amount` of debt tokens from the liquidator (transfer succeeds), victim's borrow shares are burned, and `apply_liquidation_seizures` iterates an empty/zero `seized` vector — no token transfer to the liquidator, no revert, `LiquidationEvent` emitted with `repaid_usd_wad > 0`.
4. Assert `token.balanceOf(liquidator)` decreased by `small_amount` and `get_collateral_amount`/`seized_collaterals` delivered 0.
