### Title
Front-runnable bad-debt socialization lets zToken holders evade their share of loss, shifting it onto passive depositors - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar`)

### Summary
`socialize-debt` writes down the vault's `lindex` (the liquidity index that determines the redemption value of every zToken) the moment bad debt is realized during a liquidation. Because the triggering `liquidate` call sits in the public mempool before it lands, and `deposit`/`redeem` charge no fee and have no cooldown, any zToken holder can front-run the incoming loss by redeeming right before it executes and re-depositing right after — exactly the pattern described in the sfrxETH report, but here the "withdrawal fee" is replaced by "bad-debt write-down of the liquidity index."

### Finding Description
`socialize-debt` reduces the vault-wide `lindex` proportionally to the realized bad debt, which instantly devalues every zToken holder's balance because redemption amounts are computed via `convert-to-assets-preview`, itself driven by `lindex`/`index`. [1](#0-0) 

This call is only reachable through the market's liquidation flow (`check-caller-auth` restricts the caller), and is invoked synchronously inside `liquidate` when a position has no remaining collateral, i.e. as part of the same public, mempool-visible transaction: [2](#0-1) 

Because the `liquidate`/`liquidate-multi` transaction that will trigger `socialize-debt` is visible in the mempool before it is mined, any zToken holder observing it can:
1. Call `redeem` to cash out their zTokens at the pre-write-down `lindex` (full value), just before the liquidation lands. [3](#0-2) 
2. Let the liquidation execute, which shrinks `lindex` for everyone who remained in the vault, socializing the bad debt among the shares still outstanding.
3. Call `deposit` right after, receiving more zTokens per unit of underlying at the now-discounted `lindex`. [4](#0-3) 

There is no fee, cooldown, or minimum holding period on `deposit`/`redeem`, and `socialize-debt`'s effect is applied atomically and irreversibly to `lindex` at liquidation time — exactly analogous to SFrxETHAdapter's withdrawal-fee write-down that the report flags as "pro-rated among all holders" but evadable via front-running.

### Impact Explanation
Passive/benign zToken holders who do not (or cannot) watch the mempool and react end up absorbing more than their fair share of the socialized bad debt, because the attacker's principal escapes the write-down window entirely and later re-enters at the discounted rate — a direct value transfer from remaining depositors' principal to the attacker. This is a theft of user funds at rest belonging to other, unrelated vault depositors, caused purely by transaction-ordering manipulation around a shared state variable (`lindex`) that every zToken holder depends on.

### Likelihood Explanation
Liquidations that produce bad debt (no collateral left to cover debt) are visible on-chain before confirmation, and `deposit`/`redeem` are unrestricted, free, and instant. Any sufficiently large zToken holder — or a bot monitoring the mempool for `liquidate`/`liquidate-multi` calls likely to trigger `socialize-debt` — can perform this two-transaction sandwich with ordinary transaction fees as the only cost, making this readily automatable and repeatable across every vault sharing this pattern (`vault-usdc`, `vault-usdh`, `vault-stx`, `vault-sbtc`, `vault-ststx`, `vault-ststxbtc` all implement identical `deposit`/`redeem`/`socialize-debt` logic).

### Recommendation
- Apply the bad-debt write-down to `lindex` before allowing any `deposit`/`redeem` in the same block as a liquidation that produces bad debt, or checkpoint balances so that any zToken minted/burned within the same block as a `socialize-debt` event is settled at the post-write-down index.
- Consider a small deposit/redeem fee or minimum holding period to discourage this style of front-running, consistent with the original report's recommendation for the FRAX case.
- Alternatively, route `liquidate` calls exclusively through private relays/flashbots-style submission to remove the mempool visibility window that enables the front-run, as accepted as a mitigation in the referenced report's discussion.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker holds zUSDC and observes a pending `liquidate` (or `liquidate-multi`) transaction in the mempool that will leave `no-collateral-left` true for a borrower, triggering `vault-socialize-debt` on `vault-usdc`.
2. Attacker submits `redeem` with higher priority fee, burning their zUSDC and receiving underlying USDC at the current (pre-loss) `lindex`. [3](#0-2) 
3. The pending `liquidate` transaction lands, calling `socialize-debt`, which reduces `lindex` for the vault based on `old-total-assets` at that moment (now excluding the attacker's already-withdrawn share, so remaining holders absorb a comparatively larger write-down). [5](#0-4) 
4. Attacker calls `deposit` again with the same USDC, minting zUSDC at the new, lower `lindex`, ending up with more shares than before while having borne none of the loss. [4](#0-3) 
5. Net effect: the remaining zUSDC holders who did not react bear a larger portion of the bad debt than they would have if the attacker's capital had remained in the vault through the write-down.

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))
