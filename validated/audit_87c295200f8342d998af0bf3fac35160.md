### Title
A borrower can plant an unpriceable collateral leg to make their account unliquidatable and un-cleanable, forcing bad debt to accrue to insolvency - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs)

### Summary
`supply` admits a new collateral leg with no price check, while every valuation-dependent exit — `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` — computes risk totals over **all** of the account's supply positions using strict price reads that revert on a stale or invalid feed. An indebted borrower can therefore supply a dust amount of an asset whose oracle is (or can be made) unavailable — notably an Aquarius LP token, whose LPs can drive the LP value below `min_pool_value_wad` by withdrawing liquidity — and permanently block liquidation and bad-debt socialization of their account. This mirrors the CVE-2016-0666 class: an unprivileged user exploiting a reachable, insufficiently-restricted entrypoint to deny a security-critical service. The codebase's own threat model names this exact shield (DoS.1) and harness tests reproduce it.

### Finding Description
- `supply` performs no price read: the audit test `audit_supply_setup_blocks_liquidation_via_stale_dust_leg` confirms `try_supply` of a WBTC dust leg succeeds while its Reflector feed is deliberately stale. [1](#0-0) 
- `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` both fail closed on that leg: `socialize_bad_debt` unconditionally runs `risk::calculate_account_risk_totals` over the account's full `supply_positions` and `borrow_positions` [2](#0-1) , and any leg whose strict price read fails reverts the entire call with `PRICE_FEED_STALE`. [3](#0-2) 
- Even the account's own `withdraw` of the poisoned leg reverts, so the borrower cannot be forced to remove it. [4](#0-3) 
- The borrower controls the trigger: for an Aquarius LP collateral, LP holders can push pool value below `min_pool_value_wad` to induce the outage on demand. [5](#0-4) 
- The shield also blocks the governed `force_socialize_bad_debt` runbook, since it shares the same strict risk-total path. [6](#0-5) 

### Impact Explanation
While the shield holds, the underwater account cannot be liquidated at any size and its bad debt cannot be socialized — even by governance's force path. Borrow interest keeps accruing on uncapped debt, converting a liquidatable position into permanent protocol insolvency borne by suppliers via supply-index write-down when the leg is finally removed or the feed recovers. This is a lasting denial of the protocol's core safety function and a direct path to insolvency, matching the "protocol insolvency" / "temporary freezing of funds" acceptance criteria.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires no privileges, no third-party cooperation beyond ordinary LP activity for Aquarius-priced legs, and costs only a dust deposit. Any listed-but-illiquid or single-source-priced collateral is a candidate shield; the borrower can pick whichever feed is most fragile at the moment their position goes underwater, and can plant the leg in advance since `supply` never checks price freshness. [6](#0-5) 

### Recommendation
Decouple account risk evaluation from per-leg feed availability: value legs whose price is unavailable at a conservative bound (e.g., zero collateral weight for stale collateral feeds) rather than reverting, or let `liquidate`/`clean_bad_debt` skip supply legs whose strict read fails while still seizing priceable collateral. Alternatively, require a fresh strict price at `supply` time for assets that can become collateral, so a leg can never enter the book already unpriceable. Note the symmetric gap: `withdraw` also reverts on the stale leg, so any fix must give the owner a priced-at-zero or skip-leg exit to avoid stranding honest collateral.

### Proof of Concept
Reproduced by `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_supply_stale_shield.rs` and `audit_liquidate_and_clean_bricked_by_unpriceable_dust_leg.rs`:

1. Borrower supplies USDC and borrows ETH at a healthy HF.
2. WBTC's Reflector price is stamped 3,600s stale; borrower calls `supply(account_id, [(WBTC, dust)])` — succeeds (no price check on supply).
3. USDC price is crashed so the account is deeply underwater (HF < 1).
4. `liquidate(liquidator, account_id, [(ETH, 1.0)], Transfer)` → `PRICE_FEED_STALE`.
5. `clean_bad_debt(caller, account_id)` → `PRICE_FEED_STALE`.
6. Borrower's own `withdraw(WBTC, dust)` → `PRICE_FEED_STALE` — the leg cannot be removed by anyone.
7. A control account without the WBTC leg is liquidated normally, isolating the dust leg as the sole blocker. [7](#0-6)
