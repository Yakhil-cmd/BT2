### Title
Flash-loan fee floor-rounds to zero, allowing free flash loans in small chunks - (File: contracts/pool/src/ops/flash.rs)

### Summary
The external report describes fee evasion via floor rounding: `fee = amount * percentFee / denominator` truncates to zero for small amounts, so users split a large action into many small ones and pay nothing. The same class exists in XOXNO Lending's flash-loan path: `FlashTerms::terms` computes the protocol fee as a floor-rounded basis-point product of `amount`, so any `amount` below the rounding threshold produces `fee == 0` while the loan itself succeeds.

### Finding Description
`apply` in `contracts/pool/src/ops/flash.rs:40` executes a flash loan and computes its fee at `contracts/pool/src/ops/flash.rs:107-121`:

```rust
let fee = Bps::from(i128::from(fee_bps)).flash_loan_fee_on(env, amount);
```

`flash_loan_fee_on` (in `common/src/math/fp.rs`, called via `Bps`) is a multiply-then-divide basis-point computation that floors the result. For any `flashloan_fee > 0`, every `amount` such that `amount * fee_bps < 10_000` yields `fee == 0`. The rest of the flow accepts this silently:

- `terms` builds `total_repayment = amount + 0 = amount` (`flash.rs:111-113`) and `balance_after_repayment = pre_balance + 0` (`flash.rs:117-119`).
- `collect_repayment` only requires allowance/balance for `total_repayment`, which equals the principal (`flash.rs:173-179`).
- `book_fee` calls `interest::add_protocol_revenue` with a zero fee, which is an explicit no-op (`contracts/pool/src/interest.rs:60-62`), and `credit_cash(0)` is a no-op.

There is no revert when the computed fee is zero and no minimum-fee floor. `prepare` only requires a positive amount, `is_flashloanable`, and sufficient cash (`flash.rs:76-87`), all satisfiable for arbitrarily small amounts. A single unprivileged address can therefore call `controller::flash_loan` (or the pool's flash entrypoint) repeatedly with sub-threshold amounts and obtain unlimited flash-loan liquidity while the protocol books zero revenue — the exact analog of "fund the pool many times in small chunks to pay no fee."

The same floor-rounding asymmetry appears in interest accrual: `protocol_fee_shares` (`common/src/rates`, invoked from `interest.rs:64`) floor-rounds the revenue share minted per accrual chunk, so the protocol's revenue share is systematically biased downward per `accrue_chunk` call — but the flash-loan path is the directly user-controlled instance.

### Impact Explanation
Protocol revenue from flash loans can be driven to zero for arbitrarily large effective loan volume by chunking. Flash-loan fees are booked as protocol revenue shares (`add_protocol_revenue` mints revenue inside total supply), so evaded fees are lost yield attributable to suppliers/treasury — theft/loss of unclaimed yield rather than principal loss. Additionally, free flash liquidity can be used by the same unprivileged caller inside its own receiver callback (e.g., self-liquidation bonus capture, collateral swaps) at zero protocol cost, amplifying the value extracted per fee-avoided call. Severity is bounded because principal itself is always fully repaid via the balance assertions (`flash.rs:61`, `flash.rs:66`, `flash.rs:179`).

### Likelihood Explanation
Fully reachable by a single unprivileged address: deploy a WASM receiver implementing `execute_flash_loan`, call the flash entrypoint with `amount` chosen so `amount * flashloan_fee_bps < 10_000`, grant allowance for `amount`, and repeat. With e.g. `flashloan_fee = 9 bps`, any `amount <= 111` (in the token's smallest unit) is fee-free; for low-decimals or low-value tokens a meaningful amount can still fit under the threshold, and repeated calls multiply the volume. No timing, oracle, or privileged precondition is required — only `is_flashloanable` on the market.

### Recommendation
Revert the flash loan when the computed fee rounds to zero on a fee-enabled market (i.e., require `fee > 0` whenever `flashloan_fee > 0`), or enforce a minimum fee (e.g., round up via a ceil division, or a `min_fee` parameter). The same ceil/floor asymmetry should be reviewed in `protocol_fee_shares` so per-chunk accrual cannot be biased to zero revenue.

### Proof of Concept
1. Admin configures a market with `is_flashloanable = true` and `flashloan_fee = f > 0` (e.g., 9 bps).
2. Attacker deploys a WASM contract implementing `execute_flash_loan(initiator, asset, amount, fee, pool, data)` that approves the pool to pull `amount` and returns.
3. Attacker calls the controller flash-loan entrypoint with `amount = a` where `a * f < 10_000` (e.g., `a = 111` for `f = 9`).
4. `terms` computes `fee = floor(111 * 9 / 10_000) = 0`; `total_repayment = 111`; `balance_after_repayment = pre_balance`.
5. Loan succeeds; receiver retains full use of `a` during the callback; `book_fee` no-ops on zero fee.
6. Repeat the call `N` times to obtain effective flash volume `N * a` with total fees paid of `0`.
