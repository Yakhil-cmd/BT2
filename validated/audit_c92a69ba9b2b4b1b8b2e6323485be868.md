### Title
An underwater borrower can keep a residual dust collateral leg whose price feed is stale/unpriceable, permanently bricking `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` on their account — (`contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/`)

### Summary
The YoloV2 bug is "a user stays in the selection set after moving funds, and being selected DoSes the round." The XOXNO analog is the inverse direction with the same shape: an account's *entire* supply-position map stays in the liquidation selection set, and liquidation iterates every leg pro-rata with strict pricing. A borrower who retains (or plants) a tiny residual supply leg in a market whose oracle feed has gone stale makes every `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` call against their account revert, so their bad debt can never be unwound — protocol insolvency rather than a single raffle DoS.

### Finding Description
Liquidation builds a multi-leg pro-rata plan over *all* of the victim's `supply_positions` and requires a strict (fresh, in-band) Context price for each leg (`contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/apply.rs`, seizure legs priced via `entry.feed`). There is no mechanism for a liquidator to exclude a leg, and `clean_bad_debt` prices the remaining legs the same way. A borrower can therefore:

1. Supply main collateral (e.g. USDC), plus a dust amount (e.g. 0.001 WBTC) of an asset with a fragile/stale-able feed.
2. Borrow against the collateral.
3. When the account goes underwater, any `liquidate` call iterates the dust leg, hits `PRICE_FEED_STALE`, and reverts. `clean_bad_debt` reverts identically, so the bad debt cannot even be socialized.

The harness test `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs:4-47` demonstrates exactly this: supplying `0.001` WBTC with a stale Reflector price makes `try_liquidate` and `try_clean_bad_debt_by_id` both revert with `errors::PRICE_FEED_STALE`, and they only succeed again once the feed is refreshed. Because removal is only triggered when the position's scaled amount reaches zero (`update_or_remove_supply_position`, `contracts/controller/src/account.rs:190-200`), a sub-dust-threshold residual leg can persist and cannot be forcibly removed by anyone but the borrower — who has no incentive to do so.

This is the analog of "user id not removed from the round": the stale residual leg is never removed from the victim's collateral set, and the victim being "selected" for liquidation DoSes the operation.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of protocol funds / protocol insolvency: an underwater account can never be liquidated nor have its bad debt cleaned while the dust leg's feed is stale. If the feed never resumes (delisted asset, dead source), the protection is permanent; even temporary staleness delays liquidation while debt accrues interest. A motivated attacker can deliberately borrow to the edge and rely on this to extract unrecoverable debt.

### Likelihood Explanation
Fully reachable by a single unprivileged address via `supply` (dust amount), `borrow`, and optionally `withdraw` to leave a residual leg below the dust threshold. The trigger is a stale price feed, which occurs organically (the test simulates it with a one-hour-old timestamp, inside normal Reflector staleness tolerances on other markets). The attacker only needs their chosen market's feed to be stale at liquidation time; no privileged role, leaked key, or bad parameter is required. Residual uncertainty: whether this is considered "fail-closed oracle behavior" by design; however the protocol-specific defect is the absence of any way to skip or strip an unpriceable dust leg in the seizure plan, and the presence of the dedicated regression test indicates this behavior was flagged rather than accepted.

### Recommendation
- In the liquidation plan, skip collateral legs whose strict price is unavailable (or whose seized value floors to zero), instead of reverting the whole call.
- Apply the same skip/tolerance in `clean_bad_debt` so bad debt can be written down even while a dust leg is unpriceable.
- Alternatively, let `clean_bad_debt`/liquidators force-remove below-threshold dust legs (extend the dust-threshold cleanup used for bad debt to seizure planning).

### Proof of Concept
See `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs:4-47`:

```rust
let plant = t.try_supply(borrower, "WBTC", 0.001);      // dust leg, stale feed
assert!(plant.is_ok());
t.set_price("USDC", usd_cents(50));                     // borrower goes underwater
let liq = t.try_liquidate(LIQUIDATOR, borrower, "ETH", 1.0);
test_harness::assert_contract_error(liq, errors::PRICE_FEED_STALE);
let clean = t.try_clean_bad_debt_by_id(borrower_id);
test_harness::assert_contract_error(clean, errors::PRICE_FEED_STALE);
```

A single dust supply leg in a stale-feed market bricks both liquidation and bad-debt cleanup for the entire account.
