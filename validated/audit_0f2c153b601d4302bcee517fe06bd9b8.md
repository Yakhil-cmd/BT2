The reported bug class—vouchers that can be redeemed to mint an NFT at any address chosen by whoever submits the transaction, regardless of the intended recipient—does not have a demonstrable analog in this codebase's FRAME NFT pallet. The polkadot-sdk `pallet-nfts` implements the exact recipient-binding mitigation that the original report recommended as a fix.

In `pallet-nfts`, the pre-signed minting voucher (`PreSignedMint`) explicitly includes an `only_account: Option<AccountId>` field [1](#0-0) . When `do_mint_pre_signed` processes the voucher, it enforces that if `only_account` is set, the account receiving the mint (`mint_to`, derived from the signed origin of the extrinsic) must match it, returning `Error::WrongOrigin` otherwise [2](#0-1) . The `mint_pre_signed` extrinsic also validates the signature against the claimed `signer` and requires that signer to hold the `Issuer` role on the collection before minting proceeds [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) .

The test suite explicitly exercises this front-run protection: a voucher with `only_account: Some(account(2))` is rejected with `Error::WrongOrigin` when submitted by a different account (`user_3`) [5](#0-4) . For `set_attributes_pre_signed`, the namespace/signer binding (`ensure!(account == &signer, ...)`) and item-owner check (`ensure!(item_details.owner == origin, ...)`) similarly bind the redemption to specific accounts [6](#0-5) .

Since the FRAME analog already implements exactly the recipient-binding control the report recommends as a fix (and additionally requires deadline and role checks), and the reported issue class is also explicitly categorized as a pure front-running/economic concern (excluded per the analysis method), there is no reachable, unmitigated analog to report here.
