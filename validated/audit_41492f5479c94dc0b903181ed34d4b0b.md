### Title
Unvalidated `sbtc-token` transfer amounts allow `total-sbtc-staked` to desync from the contract's real sBTC balance, freezing staked/reward sBTC - (File: stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar)

### Summary
`roll-sbtc` in `pox-5.clar` credits `total-sbtc-staked` by the exact `delta` amount requested to be transferred, without ever confirming that the contract's actual sBTC balance increased by that amount. `get-rewards` and every downstream reward/withdrawal computation trust `total-sbtc-staked` as if it always equals the sats actually custodied by the contract, exactly the "amount specified equals amount received" assumption the Morpho report flags as broken for fee-charging ERC20/SIP-010 transfers.

### Finding Description
`register-for-bond`'s L2 sBTC path calls `roll-sbtc` to move a staker's custodied sBTC into a bond [1](#0-0) . `roll-sbtc` does the transfer and then unconditionally bumps the `total-sbtc-staked` variable by the exact `delta` requested, using the *pre-transfer intended amount*, not any post-transfer balance check:

```
(try! (contract-call? ...sbtc-token transfer delta tx-sender current-contract none))
(var-set total-sbtc-staked (+ (var-get total-sbtc-staked) delta))
``` [2](#0-1) 

The refund/decrease branch behaves symmetrically, decrementing `total-sbtc-staked` by the full `delta` while paying `delta` out of the contract's actual (possibly fee-diminished) balance [3](#0-2) .

`total-sbtc-staked` is then treated by `get-rewards` as an exact accounting of sats actually held by the contract, and is subtracted from the live token balance to compute distributable rewards:
```
(- current-balance total-staked-sbtc cur-reserve)
``` [4](#0-3) 

If the `sbtc-token` contract (`SM3VDXK3WZZSA84XXFKAFAF15NNZX32CTSG82JFQ4.sbtc-token`) ever transfers less than the requested amount to the recipient on any call reachable from `roll-sbtc` (a transfer fee, a future upgrade, or any non-1:1 transfer semantics — the exact scenario the Morpho report describes for USDC/USDT), `total-sbtc-staked` becomes permanently inflated relative to the sBTC actually sitting in the pox-5 contract's balance. This breaks the equality `total-sbtc-staked == contract's actual custodied sats` that every downstream sBTC accounting function (`get-rewards`, `get-new-rewards`, and any unstake/rollover payout that relies on `total-sbtc-staked`) depends on.

### Impact Explanation
Once `total-sbtc-staked` is inflated beyond the real balance:
- `get-rewards`'s subtraction `(- current-balance total-staked-sbtc cur-reserve)` can underflow, which traps/reverts in Clarity, breaking `calculate-rewards` for the whole cycle across all bonds/signers/stakers (temporary or permanent freezing of reward distribution).
- Any staker's legitimate decrease/rollover refund in `roll-sbtc`'s second branch withdraws `delta` sats out of the pooled contract balance while it was recorded as staked in `total-sbtc-staked`; since the pool balance is shared across all bond participants, once it is depleted below what `total-sbtc-staked` claims, other stakers' rightful sBTC becomes unwithdrawable — a freezing of legitimately staked sBTC belonging to unrelated stakers, not just the caller's own funds.

This matches the "High/Critical — permanent or temporary freezing of staked sBTC / double-counting a commitment" impact bucket, since the loss is not limited to the acting staker's own funds but corrupts a shared pooled accounting variable used by all bond members.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is low under sBTC's current fee-less transfer semantics — same caveat Morpho itself gave for USDC/USDT ("small likelihood… to turn on fees"). The trigger is entirely dependent on the external, out-of-scope `sbtc-token` contract ever deviating from exact 1:1 transfer amounts (fee, rounding, future upgrade), which pox-5 cannot control but also never defends against by checking actual balance deltas.

### Recommendation
In `roll-sbtc`, measure the pox-5 contract's actual `sbtc-token` balance before and after each `contract-call? ... transfer`, and credit/debit `total-sbtc-staked` with the observed balance delta rather than the requested `delta`, mirroring Compound's `doTransferIn` pattern referenced in the original report.

### Proof of Concept
1. Assume (hypothetically) `sbtc-token`'s `transfer` function begins deducting a fee, so `transfer delta tx-sender current-contract none` moves less than `delta` sats into pox-5's balance.
2. A staker calls `register-for-bond` with `new-sbtc > old-sbtc`, triggering `roll-sbtc`'s increase branch [2](#0-1) . `total-sbtc-staked` is incremented by full `delta`, but the contract's real sBTC balance increases by less.
3. Subsequent calls to `calculate-rewards`/`get-rewards` compute `(- current-balance total-staked-sbtc cur-reserve)` [4](#0-3)  — once the shortfall exceeds the reserve buffer, this reverts, or in the decrease/withdrawal path a later staker's refund attempt fails because the pooled balance can no longer cover the accounted `total-sbtc-staked`, freezing that staker's — and potentially other stakers' — sBTC.
