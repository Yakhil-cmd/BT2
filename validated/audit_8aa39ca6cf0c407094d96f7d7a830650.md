### Title
Underflow in `calc-treasury-lp-preview` DoS's `accrue` and freezes every depositor/borrower in the vault - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar`, also present in `v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`)

### Summary
`accrue` computes a protocol fee share (`reserve-inc`) of newly-accrued interest and mints it to the DAO treasury as LP shares, using `(- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc)` as a divisor/subtrahend. If `reserve-inc` ever exceeds `total-assets-preview`, this subtraction underflows a `uint` and Clarity aborts the entire transaction. Because `accrue` is called at the top of every state-changing entry point (`deposit`, `redeem`, `system-borrow`, `system-repay`), a single condition in shared vault state can permanently revert every user's transaction against that vault, freezing all depositors' and borrowers' funds — exactly the DoS/underflow pattern in the referenced Kerosene report where `numerator = tvl - dyad.totalSupply()` reverted until global state caught up.

### Finding Description
`accrue` recomputes debt accrual and, if a positive fee increment exists, mints treasury LP shares: [1](#0-0) 

The fee amount minted is derived from `calc-treasury-lp-preview` / the inline equivalent in `accrue`: [2](#0-1) 

`ta-preview` (`total-assets-preview`) is `current-assets + interest`, where `current-assets` is the vault's on-hand underlying balance (which shrinks toward the `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` floor as utilization approaches 100%): [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

`reserve-inc` is `debt-delta * fee-reserve / BPS`, where `debt-delta` is the interest that accrued since the last `accrue` call. If the vault is left un-accrued for a long period at high utilization (e.g. via the `accrue` pause flag being toggled off after a long pause, or simply nobody calling any vault function for an extended time while `total-borrowed` is near `cap-debt`), `debt-delta` — and therefore `reserve-inc` — can grow large relative to the small amount of idle `current-assets` sitting in the vault. Once `reserve-inc > ta-preview`, the subtraction `(- ta-preview reserve-inc)` underflows and Clarity aborts the call.

Since `accrue` is unconditionally invoked by `deposit`, `redeem`, `system-borrow` and `system-repay` (see e.g. the call in `system-borrow`), the underflow is not scoped to whichever transaction happened to trigger the large `debt-delta` — it poisons *every subsequent call* to any of these entry points until the underlying condition resolves itself (which, unlike the DYAD case, has no natural "TVL grows past debt" self-healing path here; the vault would need `fee-reserve` lowered by the DAO or assets deposited to dilute the ratio). [5](#0-4) 

### Impact Explanation
Any unprivileged user who is owed a withdrawal/redeem, or any borrower who needs to repay to avoid liquidation, is blocked because their call to `redeem`/`system-repay` routes through the shared `accrue` and reverts. This satisfies the "temporary freezing of funds" High-impact class: it is not the caller's own action alone that is harmed — the shared vault-wide state (`assets`, `total-borrowed`, `principal-scaled`, `fee-reserve`, `index`) primed by the collective borrow/accrual history of *all* users is what causes *every other* user's transaction to fail, including users who did nothing to cause the imbalance (e.g., an LP simply trying to `redeem` their zft shares).

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood depends on utilization staying high for an extended interval without any `accrue`-triggering call and on `fee-reserve` being set to a non-trivial BPS value, both of which are plausible during normal operation (any vault that gets heavily borrowed and has low activity has `current-assets` close to `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY`). No malicious actor is strictly required to trigger it — normal usage/inactivity is sufficient — which raises likelihood relative to the original DYAD finding, which required TVL to be below Dyad's total supply post-migration.

### Recommendation
Clamp `reserve-inc` to at most `ta-preview` (or skip minting the treasury fee entirely) before performing the subtraction, e.g.:
```
(reserve-inc-capped (if (> reserve-inc ta-preview) ta-preview reserve-inc))
...
(if (> reserve-inc-capped u0)
    (mul-div-down reserve-inc-capped (total-supply) (- ta-preview reserve-inc-capped))
    u0)
```
and apply the same guard to the identical logic duplicated in `calc-treasury-lp-preview` and inline in `accrue` across all six vault contracts (`v0-vault-stx`, `v0-vault-sbtc`, `v0-vault-ststx`, `v0-vault-usdc`, `v0-vault-usdh`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc`).

### Proof of Concept
1. Deploy/initialize a vault (e.g. `v0-vault-usdc`) with `fee-reserve` set to a meaningful BPS value (e.g. 2000 = 20%) via DAO config.
2. Borrow near `cap-debt` via `system-borrow`, driving `assets` down to near `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` (u1000).
3. Let a long period elapse without calling `accrue`/`deposit`/`redeem`/`system-borrow`/`system-repay` (interest keeps compounding conceptually via `next-index`, but is not materialized on-chain until the next call).
4. Call `redeem` (or `system-repay`) as any user. Inside, `accrue` executes: `debt-delta` reflects the full elapsed-time interest jump; `reserve-inc = debt-delta * fee-reserve / BPS` can exceed `ta-preview = assets(~1000) + interest`.
5. The expression `(- ta-preview reserve-inc)` underflows and the Clarity runtime aborts the transaction — the caller's `redeem` fails even though they own the shares and simply want to withdraw. Every other party's `deposit`/`redeem`/`system-borrow`/`system-repay` call fails identically until the condition is resolved by an external DAO parameter change, freezing funds for all vault participants.
