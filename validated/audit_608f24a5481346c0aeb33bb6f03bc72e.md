### Title
Suppliers can front-run bad-debt socialization by redeeming before `liquidate` finalizes, forcing later depositors to absorb the write-down - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary
Zest's lending vaults (`v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, etc.) let any depositor `redeem` their `zft` shares for underlying at any time, gated only by `get-available-assets` (idle liquidity), with no cooling-off period. [1](#0-0)  Separately, `market.clar`'s `liquidate` function, when a borrower's collateral is fully exhausted but debt remains, calls `socialize-debt-asset`, which invokes `vault-socialize-debt` on the relevant vault to write down the liquidity index (`lindex`) — i.e., haircut the value of every share outstanding in that vault at that moment. [2](#0-1)  The vault's `socialize-debt` function directly reduces `lindex` proportionally to the loss, which is the state read by every depositor's `convert-to-assets`/`redeem` preview. [3](#0-2) 

### Finding Description
Whether a position is liquidatable is fully deterministic on-chain: `liquidate` computes `current-ltv` from public oracle prices and compares it against the egroup's `LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL`/`LTV-LIQ-FULL` thresholds. [4](#0-3)  Anyone monitoring price feeds/positions can therefore anticipate, exactly as in the Dopex report, when a borrower is about to become undercollateralized enough that `liquidate` will find `no-collateral-left` and trigger `socialize-debt-asset` → `vault-socialize-debt` → write-down of `lindex` in the corresponding vault (`v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, etc.). [5](#0-4) 

Because `redeem` has no lock-up and is permitted as long as `get-available-assets` (idle, unborrowed liquidity) covers the withdrawal, a supplier who anticipates the socialization event — whether by watching oracle price updates, mempool transactions calling `liquidate`, or simply the historical pattern of when liquidations occur — can call `redeem` just before the `liquidate` call lands, withdrawing at the pre-write-down `lindex`/share price, and then `deposit` again immediately after the socialization at the now-discounted price, picking up more shares than before. [6](#0-5)  Depositors who do not react in time remain in the vault while `lindex` is haircut, and their existing shares (via `convert-to-assets-preview`, driven by `lindex`) are worth strictly less afterward. This is not caller-only harm: the attacker (redeeming/re-depositing supplier) improves their position specifically at the expense of the other suppliers who remain in the pool through the socialization event — the shared state being manipulated is the vault's `lindex`, which values every other LP's shares.

This mirrors exactly the two root causes identified in the Dopex report: (1) the price/health threshold that triggers the loss-causing event is fully known/computable in advance, and (2) the pool that will absorb the loss allows unrestricted redemption right up until the loss-crystallizing call.

### Impact Explanation
This is a socialization of a loss (bad debt write-down) that a technically sophisticated LP can avoid while less attentive LPs cannot, i.e. temporary freezing/theft of unclaimed yield for the LPs who remain in the vault at write-down time — their existing principal value (denominated via `lindex`) is reduced by more than their fair share, while the "fast" LP effectively reduces or eliminates their own exposure and gains proportionally more shares upon re-entry. Per the accepted classification of the original report, this lands on the "theft of unclaimed yield" / "temporary freezing of funds" impact category for the disadvantaged depositors, since the loss that should have been spread pro-rata across all suppliers present is instead concentrated onto those who could not react in time.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is comparatively high because:
- Liquidation eligibility and the point of bad-debt exhaustion are computed from public oracle data (`current-ltv` vs `LTV-LIQ-FULL`), not hidden state. [7](#0-6) 
- `liquidate` is permissionlessly callable by anyone, so the moment a position crosses into "no collateral left," any observer (not just the eventual liquidator) can predict a socialization write-down is imminent for that vault.
- `redeem`/`deposit` on the vault have no cooldown or epoch/queue mechanism, and are checked only against idle liquidity (`get-available-assets`), which is exactly the same defect flagged in the referenced report for `PerpetualAtlanticVaultLP`. [1](#0-0) 
- This requires no MEV/front-running sophistication beyond monitoring on-chain state, matching the report's explicit note that the exploit works even without MEV.

### Recommendation
Introduce a mechanism decoupling redeemability from imminent socialization events, e.g.: (a) a short redemption cooldown/queue so shares cannot exit within N blocks of deposit, preventing "flee-then-reenter" cycling around a predictable loss event; or (b) accrue/reserve a portion of protocol fees or a first-loss buffer to absorb bad debt before it is socialized directly via `lindex`, reducing the marginal benefit of exiting early; or (c) snapshot `lindex` write-downs retroactively across an epoch so that LPs who exit shortly before a liquidation that was already "in flight" (unhealthy position publicly visible) still share in the loss proportional to their historical presence.

### Proof of Concept
1. Two LPs, Alice and Bob, each deposit equal amounts of USDC into `v0-vault-usdc.clar` via `deposit`, receiving equal `zft` shares. [8](#0-7) 
2. A borrower's collateral price crashes (via oracle update) such that `current-ltv >= LTV-LIQ-FULL`, making the position fully liquidatable with expected bad debt (collateral will be fully exhausted before debt is repaid). [7](#0-6) 
3. Bob observes the oracle price / position health (all on-chain, public) and calls `redeem` on `v0-vault-usdc.clar` to withdraw his full share value before anyone calls `liquidate`. [6](#0-5) 
4. Any party calls `market.liquidate` on the borrower; since collateral is exhausted, `no-collateral-left` is true and `socialize-debt-asset` calls `vault-socialize-debt`, which reduces `lindex` in `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, cutting the value of all currently-outstanding shares (now only Alice's). [9](#0-8) [3](#0-2) 
5. Bob re-deposits his withdrawn USDC via `deposit`, receiving shares priced at the post-write-down `lindex` (more shares per USDC than before). [8](#0-7) 
6. Result: Alice alone absorbed the full `lindex` write-down while Bob avoided it entirely and re-entered with more favorable share pricing — the same asymmetric-loss outcome demonstrated by the referenced Dopex PoC (Bob vs. Dave).

Note: I was not able to independently verify the exact conditions under which `no-collateral-left` triggers bad-debt socialization for partial (non-full) liquidations, nor whether any additional safeguards (e.g., minimum time-in-pool) exist elsewhere in the market/vault deployment scripts that might mitigate this; a full Devin session with repository access would be needed to build and run an executable PoC against the Clarity contracts to confirm the exact numeric outcome.

(define-private (socialize-debt-asset
                (debt-entry { aid: uint, scaled: uint })
                (acc { borrower: principal, success: bool }))
  ;; Early return if previous socialization failed
  (if (not (get success acc))
      acc
      (let ((borrower (get borrower acc))
            (failed-status { borrower: borrower, success: false })
            (asset-id (get aid debt-entry))
            (scaled-debt (get scaled debt-entry)))

            ;; Socialize in vault - pass scaled directly to avoid rounding
            (unwrap! (vault-socialize-debt asset-id scaled-debt) failed-status)
            ;; Refresh cache with new indexes post-write-down (lindex decreased)
            (map-set index-cache
                     { timestamp: stacks-block-time, aid: asset-id }
                     (unwrap! (vault-accrue asset-id) failed-status))
            ;; Remove from obligation
            (unwrap! (contract-call? .v0-market-vault
                                      debt-remove-scaled
                                      borrower
                                      scaled-debt
                                      asset-id) failed-status)
          acc)
        ))
