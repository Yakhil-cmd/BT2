### Title
Unvalidated `creator` account in `initialize_with_permission` allows arbitrary assignment of the privileged `pool_creator` fee-collection role - (File: `programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/initialize_with_permission.rs`)

### Summary
`initialize_with_permission` lets the transaction `payer` bind the pool's permanent `pool_creator` role to any pubkey of the payer's choosing, without that account signing, consenting, or being tied in any way to the payer or the `permission` PDA. This role is never re-assignable afterward, and it carries exclusive rights over the pool's ongoing creator-fee revenue stream.

### Finding Description
In `InitializeWithPermission`, the `creator` account is declared as a completely unconstrained `UncheckedAccount`: [1](#0-0) 

Unlike the permissionless `Initialize` instruction, where `creator` is required to be the `Signer` who actually pays for and owns the pool (`pub creator: Signer<'info>`), here `creator` has no relationship to `payer`, no signer requirement, and is not cross-checked against the `permission` PDA that is supposed to gate who may create the pool: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

The `payer.key()`-derived `permission` account is loaded but its contents are never actually read/enforced anywhere in the instruction body; only its PDA existence (created earlier via the admin-only `create_permission_pda`) is implicitly required. The `creator` value supplied by the payer is written directly into `pool_state.pool_creator` with no further validation: [4](#0-3) 

`pool_creator` is a privileged, permanent role: only the account matching this field can sign to withdraw accumulated creator fees via `collect_creator_fee`, and even the permissionless variant always routes funds to this same recorded address: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

There is no admin or governance instruction anywhere in `programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/admin/` that can later change `pool_creator`, so whatever value is set at initialization is permanent for the life of the pool. [7](#0-6) 

### Impact Explanation
Because `creator` is fully attacker-controlled data with no signature or ownership check, the entity submitting `initialize_with_permission` (any account holding a valid `permission` PDA) can permanently bind the `pool_creator` role — and therefore all future trading-fee revenue designated as "creator fee" (paid by every subsequent swapper on the pool) — to an address that:
- is not the intended token/project creator (misappropriating future fee revenue away from the legitimate party the payer was supposed to be creating the pool on behalf of), or
- is a dead/unspendable/PDA-owned address with no ability to sign, permanently freezing all accumulated creator fees since `pool_creator` can never be updated post-initialization.

Since swappers' paid fees (a portion of every trade) flow into this creator-fee bucket, this results in permanent freezing or misdirection of real user-generated fee funds with no recovery path.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any account that has been granted a `Permission` PDA (an approved integrator/payer) can trigger this in a single transaction with attacker-chosen `creator` data — no additional privilege beyond holding a valid permission account is required, and the flaw is deterministic (not probabilistic), so it is reachable and reliable to exploit or misuse whenever `initialize_with_permission` is used with a `creator` value the payer does not actually control or intend to authorize.

### Recommendation
Require `creator` to either be the `payer` itself (mirroring `Initialize`), or require `creator` to be a `Signer` co-signing the transaction to explicitly consent to accepting the `pool_creator` role, and/or add an admin/governance-gated instruction to allow correcting/rotating `pool_creator` in case of misconfiguration.

### Proof of Concept
1. An approved integrator `A` calls `create_permission_pda` (admin-gated) is not required by `A`; `A` already has a `Permission` PDA seeded by their own pubkey.
2. `A` calls `initialize_with_permission` as `payer`, supplying `token_0_mint`/`token_1_mint`, and sets the `creator` account field to an arbitrary pubkey `X` that never signs and has no relation to `A` (e.g., a known-unspendable address or a competitor's/attacker's own address).
3. `pool_state.initialize(...)` stores `X` as `pool_creator` permanently.
4. As swaps occur, creator fees accrue in `pool_state.creator_fees_token_0/1`.
5. Only `X` can ever sign `collect_creator_fee`, and `collect_creator_fee_permissionless` will forever route the funds to `X`'s associated token accounts; if `X` is unspendable, the fees are permanently frozen; if `X` is chosen adversarially, the true intended fee recipient never receives their revenue, with no on-chain instruction available to correct `pool_creator`.
