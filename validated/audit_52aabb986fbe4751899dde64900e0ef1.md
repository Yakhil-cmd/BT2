### Title
Insolvent account with a sole sub-3-decimal collateral leg holding less than one whole unit is permanently unliquidatable and uncleanable, stranding bad debt - ([File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs])

### Summary
`normalize_repayment_plan` skips the whole-unit quote promotion for insolvent accounts and trims every repayment leg down to whole debt-token units. When the victim's only collateral is a leg below `MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS` whose seizure rounds to zero units, every `liquidate` call reverts with `InvalidPayments`, while `clean_bad_debt` reverts because the collateral exceeds the fixed $5 `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD`. A single unprivileged borrower can manufacture this state and permanently block both permissionless resolution paths for its bad debt.

### Finding Description
Two unprivileged exit paths exist for an underwater account: `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt`. Both are closed simultaneously in one reachable corner:

1. `normalize_repayment_plan` only calls `whole_unit_repayment` on the solvent partial branch; on an insolvent account (`snap.total_collateral < snap.total_debt`) the ideal stays at the curve quote `floor(C / (1 + base))`. [1](#0-0) 
2. `process_excess_payment` then trims kept repayment legs down to whole token units on insolvent books; a leg whose kept amount rounds to zero is dropped, and when no leg remains `liquidate` reverts with `InvalidPayments`. [2](#0-1) 
3. Even when the call is not trimmed to emptiness on the repayment side, a collateral leg below 3 decimals seizes whole units only: a fractional-unit holding yields a zero-unit seizure, the leg is dropped, and the plan reverts. [3](#0-2) 
4. `clean_bad_debt_standalone` requires `is_socializable_bad_debt`: debt > collateral AND collateral ≤ 5 WAD USD. [4](#0-3) 

For a sub-3-decimal collateral asset whose whole unit is worth more than $5 (e.g. a 0–2 decimal LQV/RWA-style token at $10+ per unit), an account holding less than one whole unit with collateral value between $5 and the unit price satisfies `C > BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` while producing a zero-unit seizure. Every `liquidate` reverts with `InvalidPayments` and every `clean_bad_debt` reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt`. [5](#0-4) 

### Impact Explanation
The bad debt is permanently stuck in the book: debt accrues interest while the residual collateral (worth $5–U) can never be seized or socialized by any unprivileged call. The only recovery is the owner-only `force_socialize_bad_debt`, so the debt share can never be written down permissionlessly and the supply index keeps accounting for unbacked borrows. If the owner never intervenes, suppliers bear a permanent backing shortfall — protocol insolvency, and a permanent freeze on pro-rata recovery of the stranded collateral. [6](#0-5) 

### Likelihood Explanation
An unprivileged borrower can reach this state by supplying a fractional amount (e.g. 0.9 units) of a listed sub-3-decimal asset whose unit value exceeds $5, borrowing within the collateral floor, and waiting for a price decline until `C < D` while `C > $5`. No governance action, oracle manipulation, or cooperation is required; the geometric gap $5 < C < U exists for any listed sub-3-decimal unit worth more than $5. Medium likelihood; impact bounded per account by roughly one unit's value minus $5, plus the accruing unbacked debt.

### Recommendation
In the insolvent branch of `normalize_repayment_plan`, when the collateral-backed quote is positive but every kept leg rounds to zero whole units, promote the seizure of the sole sub-3-decimal leg to the full fractional holding (i.e. let the insolvent full-seizure path take sub-unit amounts), or apply the same `whole_unit_repayment` promotion to insolvent accounts. Alternatively, widen `is_socializable_bad_debt` to admit insolvent accounts whose only collateral leg cannot produce a nonzero seizure, so `clean_bad_debt` can write the debt down.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lists asset `EXP` with 0 decimals and unit price $10 (already exercised in `insolvent_book_with_a_ten_dollar_unit`). [7](#0-6) 
2. Attacker supplies a fractional `EXP` amount valued at $9 and borrows ~$6 of a debt token.
3. `EXP` price moves so that collateral is $9 and debt exceeds it (insolvent), or the debt accrues above $9.
4. `liquidate` reverts with `InvalidPayments` (#16) for every offer: the backed quote `floor(9/1.05)` buys zero whole units, and the seizure leg rounds to zero. [8](#0-7) 
5. `clean_bad_debt` reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` (#114) because $9 > `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD`. [9](#0-8) 
6. The debt is permanently unresolvable by any unprivileged address; the market carries unbacked borrow shares until the owner runs `force_socialize_bad_debt`.

```markdown
A partial seizure leg below 3 decimals takes whole token units only. When the
repayment covers the whole debt, the leg rounds up to the held balance and
the debt closes. Otherwise it rounds down: the dropped fraction's USD value,
divided by `1 + bonus` and floored, is trimmed from the repayment and
refunded, kept amounts rounding up, so the liquidator pays for the units it
receives, and a plan that then seizes nothing reverts with `InvalidPayments`
(16). Neither applies when an insolvent account's repayment reaches the
collateral-backed quote: that call seizes every unit. Such a leg is its
account's only supply position, so it is the whole collateral and the seizure
stays proportional, and an action that leaves debt needs at least 2 whole
units in it.
```

```rust
/// $9 of collateral backs $8.57, less than one $10 EXP unit: the estimate
/// keeps no repayment and refunds the whole offer, and execution reverts.
#[test]
fn test_insolvent_liquidation_reverts_when_no_debt_unit_fits_the_backed_quote() {
    let (mut t, account_id) = insolvent_book_with_a_ten_dollar_unit(usd(9) / 1_000);
    let exp = t.resolve_asset("EXP");
    let payments = vec![&t.env, (hub_asset(exp.clone()), 2)];
    let estimate =
        t.ctrl_client()
            .get_liquidation_estimate(&account_id, &payments, &SeizeMode::Transfer);
    assert_eq!(estimate.max_payment_wad, 0);
    assert!(estimate.seized_collaterals.is_empty());
    let refund = estimate.refunds.get(0).expect("EXP refund");
    assert_eq!((refund.asset, refund.amount), (exp, 2));

    let liquidator = t.get_or_create_user(LIQUIDATOR);
    t.resolve_market("EXP").token_admin.mint(&liquidator, &2);
    let result = map_try_ok_value(t.ctrl_client().try_liquidate(
        &liquidator,
        &account_id,
        &payments,
        &SeizeMode::Transfer,
    ));
    assert_contract_error(result, errors::INVALID_PAYMENTS);
    assert_eq!(t.token_balance_raw(LIQUIDATOR, "EXP"), 2);
    assert_eq!(t.borrow_balance_raw(ALICE, "EXP"), 2);
```
