The strongest policy-enforcement analog is the `no_seize` supply-side bypass. Let me verify the entry gate doesn't check `no_seize`.### Title
Borrower can immunize an underwater account from liquidation and bad-debt cleanup by supplying a `no_seize`-flagged asset - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/mod.rs)

### Summary
`no_seize` is meant to halt seizure of a specific collateral, but seizure in XOXNO Lending is all-or-nothing pro-rata across the account's entire supply set, while the supply-side entry gate (`FreezePolicy::BlockOnEntry`) does not check `no_seize`. A single unprivileged borrower can therefore add a dust amount of a `no_seize` asset to an already-insolvent account, making every `liquidate` call revert, and — by keeping total collateral above the $5 `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` — also make permissionless `clean_bad_debt` revert. The account's bad debt is then frozen in place until governance executes `force_socialize_bad_debt`.

### Finding Description
Three policies combine into a gap:

1. Entry policy ignores `no_seize`. `enforce_spoke_asset_flags` with `FreezePolicy::BlockOnEntry` only asserts `!paused` and `!frozen`; `no_seize` is consulted only under `SeizureLeg`. `require_can_supply`/`validate_position_entry_gates` route every supply through `BlockOnEntry`, so a flagged asset remains supply-able as long as it is listed and `collateralizable`. [1](#0-0) 

2. Seizure is pro-rata and atomic over all collateral. `build_liquidation_plan` runs `enforce_spoke_asset_flags(..., FreezePolicy::SeizureLeg)` over every seized leg and panics with `SpokeAssetSeizureHalted` if any leg is `no_seize` — one flagged position vetoes the whole liquidation. This is even documented: "Nonzero `no_seize` collateral can block the whole proportional liquidation, even if supplied after the flag is set." [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

3. The fallback cleanup is value-gated. Permissionless `clean_bad_debt` (`socialize_bad_debt` with `BadDebtGate::DustCapped`) only admits accounts whose collateral is at or below the fixed $5 threshold; only the owner-gated `force_socialize_bad_debt` omits the cap. Standalone cleanup bypasses the spoke flags, so `no_seize` does not stop it — the dust cap does. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

The enforcement flaw: the `no_seize` policy is enforced at the wrong layer relative to its all-or-nothing composition. Because any single flagged leg aborts the entire seizure, the flag effectively confers a per-account liquidation veto on whatever account holds the asset — yet nothing prevents a borrower from acquiring that veto *after* becoming liquidatable, or even after a third party has already queued the liquidation. `supply` is callable on an account whose HF < 1 (third-party top-ups are restricted to existing assets, but the account owner may add new assets), and `BlockOnEntry` never inspects `no_seize`.

### Impact Explanation
- An underwater borrower permanently blocks every `liquidate` against their account at the cost of one dust supply of a `no_seize` asset, while the position's other collateral stays above $5.
- Their debt keeps accruing interest at the borrow index while liquidation is impossible, deepening protocol insolvency; the collateral the account still holds cannot be distributed to suppliers by any permissionless path.
- Recovery requires the privileged `force_socialize_bad_debt` governance path — i.e., temporary freezing of supplier funds and accruing bad debt bounded only by governance reaction time. Impact class: temporary freezing of funds / protocol insolvency (Medium).

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a spoke listing to be flagged `no_seize` while still collateralizable and listed — a realistic intermediate state during an emergency flag-setting or wind-down (`set_spoke_asset_flags` can only tighten flags, so `no_seize` is exactly the flag an admin would set first on a distressed asset). From that moment until flag relaxation, any liquidatable or soon-to-be-liquidatable borrower in that spoke can self-immunize for the cost of a dust supply. The conditions are permissionless and the attack needs no price manipulation, no flash loan, and no privileged role — a single `supply` call. Note that INV-HALT-02 documents the blocking semantics, so this is partially an acknowledged design consequence; the unmitigated part is that entry-side policy never treats `no_seize` as a supply deterrent, letting the veto be acquired adversarially rather than only inherited.

### Recommendation
Enforce the flag at entry: have `FreezePolicy::BlockOnEntry` (or `require_can_supply` specifically) also reject `no_seize` assets for *new* supply positions, or at minimum for accounts that currently carry debt / are below HF 1 — preserving the ability of existing holders to be seized cleanly while removing the after-the-fact veto. Alternatively, relax the atomicity: skip `no_seize` legs in `calculate_seized_collateral` and seize only the unflagged proportion instead of aborting the whole plan, so the flag constrains seizure of that asset without vetoing the account. Either change removes the borrower's ability to suspend liquidation of unrelated collateral.

### Proof of Concept
1. Admin sets `no_seize` on asset X in spoke S via `set_spoke_asset_flags` (flag-tightening is unrestricted; only relaxation is timelocked).
2. Attacker's account A in spoke S holds collateral worth > $5 and debt; a price move or interest accrual pushes `get_health_factor(A) < 1`.
3. Before any liquidator acts, attacker calls `supply(caller=attacker, account_id=A, spoke_id=S, assets=[(key_X, dust)])`. `require_can_supply` → `BlockOnEntry` passes because `no_seize` is unchecked on entry; `is_collateralizable` is still true.
4. Any `liquidate(liquidator, A, payments, seize_mode)` reverts: `build_liquidation_plan` includes X in `seized_collaterals` (pro-rata, all supply positions) → `SeizureLeg` check → `SpokeAssetSeizureHalted` (#318).
5. `clean_bad_debt(caller, A)` reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` (#114) because `total_collateral > BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD`, even though `total_debt > total_collateral`.
6. Result: the account is unliquidatable and un-cleanable by any unprivileged address; debt accrues until governance runs `force_socialize_bad_debt`.
