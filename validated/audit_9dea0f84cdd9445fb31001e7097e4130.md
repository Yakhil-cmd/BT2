### Title
Rounding of `other-debt-repayable` to Zero Triggers Premature Bad-Debt Socialization onto Suppliers - (File: `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary

### Finding Description
The `liquidate` function in `v0-4-market.clar` decides whether a borrower has "no collateral left" — and therefore whether the borrower's entire remaining debt should be written off and socialized onto the vault's suppliers — using an equality check on a rounded-down USD value, `other-debt-repayable`, rather than checking the actual remaining collateral value directly. [1](#0-0) 

`other-coll-usd` is the notional USD value of collateral assets the borrower holds *other than* the one just liquidated. When `other-coll-usd > u0`, the code converts it to a token amount and then to scaled debt units via `mul-div-down` (round-down) at two separate steps: [2](#0-1) 

Because both conversions round down, a genuinely positive `other-coll-usd` can still yield `other-debt-repayable = u0` whenever the remaining collateral is a small ("dust") amount relative to `debt-price`/`debt-decimals` precision or `+ BPS liq-penalty-max` scaling. The subsequent check treats this rounded-to-zero value as proof that no further debt can be recovered from the borrower's remaining collateral: [3](#0-2) 

When `no-collateral-left` evaluates `true`, the contract strips the just-repaid debt asset, folds in the remaining debt of *all* the borrower's other debt assets, and calls `socialize-debt-asset` for each, which directly write-downs the corresponding vaults' liquidity index (`lindex`) — i.e., a haircut applied to every supplier of those vaults: [4](#0-3) 

The borrower, however, still holds the "other" collateral (its USD value, `other-coll-usd`, was strictly positive — it just rounded to zero tokens of recoverable debt). That dust collateral is never touched, seized, or accounted for. The entire remaining debt across all debt assets is nonetheless socialized onto suppliers as if the position were fully collateral-exhausted.

This is the same bug class as the Meteora `next_bin_array_index_with_liquidity` issue: a boundary/equality condition (`is-eq other-debt-repayable u0`) is used as a proxy for a binary decision (has the position truly run out of recoverable collateral / which direction to take), but the proxy loses information through rounding at the equality boundary, causing the contract to take the wrong branch.

### Impact Explanation
This directly causes "a socialization charged to all suppliers" — one of the explicitly in-scope impact categories. Any liquidator (an unprivileged, ordinary caller of `liquidate`) can trigger full multi-asset bad-debt socialization on a borrower who still retains recoverable collateral value, simply by leaving the borrower with a small enough amount of a secondary collateral asset that `other-debt-repayable` rounds to zero. The victims are the suppliers of every vault corresponding to the borrower's other outstanding debt assets — their `lindex` is written down (their claims devalued) even though the borrower's position had not actually exhausted its collateral. This is a form of premature/unjustified socialization: suppliers absorb debt losses that (at least partially) should have remained the borrower's or should not yet have been recognized as bad debt.

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger condition depends only on asset prices/decimals combinations and the liquidator's choice of which collateral/debt pair and how much debt to liquidate in a given call — all parameters fully controlled by the liquidator (`debt-amount`, `collateral-ft`, `debt-ft`) with no special privilege required. Multi-collateral, multi-debt positions with a very-low-value secondary collateral asset (e.g., a small deposit in a low-price/low-decimal asset) make the rounding-to-zero condition easy to hit deliberately or incidentally during ordinary partial liquidations.

### Recommendation
Do not use the rounded token/scaled-debt approximation of `other-coll-usd` as the sole gate for the "no collateral left" branch. Compare the raw USD notional (`other-coll-usd > u0`) directly, or require socialization only when the borrower's *entire* remaining collateral set (across all assets, not just the derived "other-debt-repayable") is verified to be economically zero using consistent rounding direction (round up when determining "is there still recoverable value" so that dust in the borrower's favor is not silently converted into supplier losses).

### Proof of Concept
1. Borrower opens a position with collateral in Asset A (large value) and a tiny dust amount of collateral in Asset B (e.g., $0.0001 worth after price/decimals conversion), and borrows Debt Asset D against it.
2. Position becomes liquidatable. Liquidator calls `liquidate` targeting Asset A as `collateral-ft` and Debt Asset D as `debt-ft`, sized so that `coll-final-raw` consumes the entirety of the user's Asset A balance (`coll-remaining` for A becomes 0, satisfying the length/asset-count conditions for `no-collateral-left`).
3. `other-coll-usd` (Asset B's dust value) is > 0, but `other-tokens`/`other-scaled` computed via `mul-div-down` round down to 0, so `other-debt-repayable = u0`.
4. `no-collateral-left` evaluates true even though the borrower still holds Asset B collateral.
5. `bad-debt-socialized` executes: the borrower's remaining debt (potentially in multiple debt assets) is fully socialized via `socialize-debt-asset`, writing down `lindex` in the affected vaults — imposing losses on all suppliers of those vaults — while the borrower's dust Asset B collateral is left untouched and unaccounted for. [5](#0-4)
