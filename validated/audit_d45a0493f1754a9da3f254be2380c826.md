### Title
`register-for-bond` rollover to a smaller sBTC amount refunds sats without decrementing the departing bond's per-cycle share totals - ([File: stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar])

### Summary
When a staker rolls from bond `N` into bond `N+6` with a smaller sats amount (`S2 < S1`), `register-for-bond` refunds the difference `S1 - S2` via `roll-sbtc`, but never calls anything analogous to `remove-staker-from-bond-cycles` for bond `N`'s remaining/final cycle. The staker's and bond `N`'s share totals for that cycle stay at `S1` even though only `S2` sats remain custodied, so bond `N`'s last-cycle rewards are computed against sats that were already withdrawn.

### Finding Description
The broken equality: for bond `N`'s final reward cycle, `staker-shares-staked-for-cycle{cycle, some N, staker, signer}` and `total-shares-staked-for-cycle{cycle, some N}` (and `protocol-bonds-total-staked[N]`, populated originally by `add-staker-to-bond-cycles` at bond-`N` registration) should equal the sBTC actually custodied by the contract for bond `N` at that cycle. That equality is violated by a shrinking rollover.

Code path in `register-for-bond` (stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar):
- `old-sbtc` is read via `get-staker-custodied-sbtc tx-sender` (line 682), reflecting bond `N`'s currently custodied sats (`S1`).
- `new-sbtc` is `sats-total` for the new registration (`S2`), when using the sBTC path (line 684-687).
- `verify-bond-rollover-window existing-membership` (line 780) only checks that the call happens inside bond `N`'s last-1/2-cycle unlock window — it does not require `S2 >= S1`.
- `roll-sbtc tx-sender old-sbtc new-sbtc` (line 784) nets the difference and refunds `S1 - S2` sBTC back to the staker immediately.
- The new membership and bond `N+6`'s shares are then added via `add-staker-to-bond-cycles ... sats-total` (lines 799-801) for the *new* bond only.
- Critically, the explicit code comment at lines 796-798 states: *"A roll-over from an ending bond ADDS the new bond's shares but does NOT tear down the old bond's per-cycle shares/delegation (unlike `update-bond-registration`, which removes then re-adds)."* This confirms bond `N`'s `total-shares-staked-for-cycle`, `staker-shares-staked-for-cycle`, and `protocol-bonds-total-staked[N]` for its still-open final cycle are left at `S1`, unchanged.

Because the rollover window is defined as occurring *within* bond `N`'s final cycle (its last-1/2-cycle unlock window), bond `N`'s final-cycle reward accounting is still live when the sBTC is refunded. Reward-per-token settlement (`settle-rewards` / `settle-staker-rewards`, lines 772-776) is invoked for the *new* bond/cycle context, not to true-down bond `N`'s share record. The `<=` allowance and `verify-not-prepare-phase` guards check only the new bond's allowlist and cycle-timing constraints; they do not validate that the departing bond's custody still backs its recorded shares.

### Impact Explanation
The staker retains full reward-share credit for bond `N`'s final cycle on the full original `S1` sats while only `S2 < S1` sats remain under sBTC custody for the remainder of that cycle. Rewards distributed for that cycle (via `rewards-per-token-for-cycle` / `signer-rewards-per-token-for-cycle` proportional splits) will over-credit the staker relative to actual backing sats — an unbacked reward-share credit funded by other stakers' or the reserve's sBTC. This is a theft/unbacked-minting-of-reward-entitlement pattern, matching the Critical category ("theft or unbacked minting of locked STX or sBTC rewards ... double-counting a commitment"). The gain is proportional to `(S1 - S2)` and the remaining fraction of bond `N`'s final cycle, and is repeatable by any allow-listed staker each time they hold a bond nearing rollover.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are attacker-reachable without any privileged role: the attacker must (1) already be an allow-listed staker with an active bond `N` membership (`protocol-bond-memberships`), (2) be allow-listed for bond `N+6` (`protocol-bond-allowances`), and (3) call `register-for-bond` inside bond `N`'s last-1/2-cycle unlock window with `amount-ustx`/`sats-total` computed for a smaller `S2`. All of this is normal, unprivileged usage of the public rollover feature — no admin, signer-impersonation, or miner assistance is required. This is realistically exploitable by any staker who is a member of two allow-lists across a rollover boundary.

### Recommendation
When a staker rolls into a new bond with `new-sbtc < old-sbtc`, `register-for-bond` must proportionally reduce (or fully remove and re-add, as `update-bond-registration` does) the staker's and the departing bond `N`'s remaining per-cycle share records (`staker-shares-staked-for-cycle`, `total-shares-staked-for-cycle`, `protocol-bonds-total-staked[N]`) for bond `N`'s still-open final cycle to reflect only the sats that remain custodied after `roll-sbtc` executes, before crediting the new bond's shares.

### Proof of Concept
Rust/Clarity integration test plan (extending `stacks-node/src/tests/pox_5_integrations.rs`):
1. Set up bond `N` and bond `N+6` with staker `S` allow-listed on both, `S` registers for bond `N` with `sats-total = S1` via sBTC lockup.
2. Advance the chain to bond `N`'s last-1/2-cycle unlock window (satisfying `verify-bond-rollover-window`).
3. Call `register-for-bond` for bond `N+6` with `sats-total = S2 < S1` (`btc-lockup = (err S2)`), asserting the sBTC transfer event sends `S1 - S2` back to `S`.
4. Assert (a) `get-total-sbtc-staked-for-bond(N)` / actual sBTC balance held by the pox-5 contract attributable to bond `N` for its remaining final cycle equals `S2`, but (b) `total-shares-staked-for-cycle{reward-cycle: bond-N-last-cycle, bond-index: (some N)}` and `staker-shares-staked-for-cycle{..., staker: S, ...}` still equal `S1` — demonstrating the two sides of the equality diverge.
5. Assert that a subsequent `settle-rewards`/`settle-staker-rewards` call for bond `N`'s final cycle credits `S` rewards proportional to `S1`, not `S2`, confirming the over-credit.
