### Title
Value-blind `MIN_WHOLE_UNIT_COLLATERAL` floor prices out borrowers on low-decimal collateral and traps accounts reduced below 2 whole units - (File: contracts/controller/src/risk/validation.rs)

### Summary
For every supply position in an asset with fewer than 3 decimals, the post-action risk gate `require_post_pool_risk_gates` requires the account to hold at least `MIN_WHOLE_UNIT_COLLATERAL` (2) *whole token units* whenever any debt remains. Like the `10**(decimals - 1)` formula in M-14, the floor is denominated in raw token units and ignores the token's USD value: for a 0-decimal collateral priced at $1,000 per unit (the documented Liqvid-style listing), the minimum collateral leg just to carry any debt is ~$2,000, and for a 2-decimal asset worth $30,000/unit it is ~$60,000. Accounts that end up below 2 units with debt cannot execute any action that leaves debt outstanding.

### Finding Description
`require_whole_unit_collateral_floor` iterates all supply legs, and for any asset with `asset_decimals < MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS` (3) it unscales the leg to whole units and asserts `units >= MIN_WHOLE_UNIT_COLLATERAL` (2), reverting with `MinBorrowCollateralNotMet`. [1](#0-0)  The constants are value-blind: `MIN_WHOLE_UNIT_COLLATERAL = 2` is a fixed count of whole base units, independent of price. [2](#0-1)  The gate runs after `borrow`, `withdraw`, and every strategy path that leaves debt, so the floor binds not just at borrow time but on every subsequent operation while debt exists. [3](#0-2) 

The runbook's own fixture makes the problem concrete: a 0-decimal collateral market is explicitly supported (`MIN_ASSET_DECIMALS = 0`), with a sanity band up to ~$1,030 per unit, and the documentation confirms the rule is "2 shares" regardless of NAV. [4](#0-3)  At NAV $1,000, an account must retain at least ~$2,000 of that single collateral leg to hold even $1 of debt — the same "minimum denominated in tokens regardless of value" defect as `minDepositAmount` returning $2,000 for WBTC. Separately, liquidation can legitimately leave an account holding 1 whole unit with residual debt (the docs describe a "rest of a 2-share account after a sale"); [5](#0-4)  from that state `withdraw` and `repay_debt_with_collateral` revert under error 126 until the account repays 100% of its debt, so all other collateral legs are frozen behind a full repayment. [6](#0-5) 

### Impact Explanation
Medium. For any listed sub-3-decimal collateral, ordinary users are priced out of borrowing unless they can post at least 2 whole units — arbitrarily expensive for high-value tokens, exactly the mispricing M-14 identifies. Worse, an account that drops to 1 unit while still in debt (a reachable state via liquidation seizure of whole units, per `whole_unit_repayment` in `liquidation/math.rs` and the documented "1-share account" case) is permanently barred from `withdraw`, `repay_debt_with_collateral`, and every debt-leaving path until it fully repays: all its remaining collateral across every market is temporarily frozen behind a complete debt payoff. This is reachable by a single unprivileged user via `borrow`/`withdraw`/`liquidate`/`repay_debt_with_collateral`.

### Likelihood Explanation
The defect activates whenever governance lists a collateral asset with fewer than 3 decimals — a supported configuration (`MIN_ASSET_DECIMALS = 0`, `require_whole_unit_isolation` exists precisely because such legs are listable), and the protocol's own runbook designs such a market with a per-unit value up to $1,000+. The higher the unit price inside the sanity band, the more disproportionate the 2-unit floor; there is no USD-based cap or scaling analogous to `DEFAULT_MIN_BORROW_COLLATERAL_USD_WAD`. The freeze path additionally requires only that liquidation leave the account at exactly 1 unit, which the whole-unit rounding rules make a normal outcome. Likelihood is moderate: it needs a sub-3-decimal collateral listing and either a high unit price or the post-liquidation 1-unit remainder.

### Recommendation
Make the whole-unit floor value-aware instead of a fixed raw count, mirroring M-14's mitigation: compute the minimum leg in USD terms and convert to units using the cached strict price, e.g. require `units * unit_price_wad >= min_sub_decimal_collateral_usd_wad` (a small constant such as a fraction of `DEFAULT_MIN_BORROW_COLLATERAL_USD_WAD`), clamped to at least 1 whole unit so the liquidation whole-unit seizure remains feasible. Alternatively, exempt accounts whose sub-3-decimal leg is at 1 unit *as a result of liquidation* from the floor on `withdraw`/`repay_debt_with_collateral`, letting them deleverage to a debt-free close. Recompute the floor in `require_whole_unit_collateral_floor` (`contracts/controller/src/risk/validation.rs`) and align `docs/reference/errors.md` error 126 and `docs/reference/runbooks/liqvid-listing-params.md` §8.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lists a 0-decimal collateral asset `LIQ` in its own hub/spoke with `can_collateral = true`, LTV 6000/LT 7000, oracle NAV $1,000 (all inside the sanity band), exactly as in `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/zero_decimal_collateral.rs` (`setup`, `LIQ` market, `decimals: 0`, `price_wad` up to band ceiling). [7](#0-6) 
2. Alice calls `supply` with 1 unit of `LIQ` (~$1,000 collateral) plus ample USDC collateral, then calls `borrow` for 1 USDC. `require_post_pool_risk_gates` → `require_whole_unit_collateral_floor` computes `units = 1 < MIN_WHOLE_UNIT_COLLATERAL = 2` and reverts `MinBorrowCollateralNotMet` (#126), even though LTV-collateral and HF are far above the thresholds — a ~$1,000 collateral position cannot support a $1 borrow because it is one token short of a value-blind floor. [1](#0-0) 
3. Freeze path: Bob holds 2 units of `LIQ` and borrows. NAV falls; a liquidator calls `liquidate`, and `whole_unit_repayment` seizes 1 whole unit, leaving Bob with 1 unit and residual debt (the documented 1-share-remainder case). [5](#0-4) 
4. Bob now calls `withdraw` on any other collateral leg or `repay_debt_with_collateral` leaving any debt: every call reverts #126 because his `LIQ` leg is 1 unit < 2. His remaining collateral is frozen until he sources funds to fully repay all debt — a stricter lockup than the $5 `min_borrow_collateral_usd` floor implies, produced entirely by a token-unit threshold that ignores price.

pub(crate) fn require_post_pool_risk_gates(env: &Env, cache: &mut Context, account: &Account) {
    if account.debt_free() {
        return;
    }

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        totals.ltv_collateral >= totals.total_debt,
        CollateralError::InsufficientCollateral
    );

    spec_hooks::solvency_gate_checked(account);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        totals.health_factor >= Wad::ONE,
        CollateralError::InsufficientCollateral
    );

    let floor = storage::get_min_borrow_collateral_usd_wad(env);
    if floor != 0 && totals.ltv_collateral.raw() < floor {
        panic_with_error!(env, CollateralError::MinBorrowCollateralNotMet);
    }

    require_whole_unit_collateral_floor(env, cache, account);
}

fn require_whole_unit_collateral_floor(env: &Env, cache: &mut Context, account: &Account) {
    for (hub_asset, position) in iter_typed_positions(&account.supply_positions) {
        let decimals = cache.cached_price(&hub_asset.asset).asset_decimals;
        if decimals >= MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS {
            continue;
        }
        let supply_index = cache.cached_market_index(&hub_asset).supply_index;
        let units = position
            .scaled_amount
            .mul(env, supply_index)
            .to_asset_floor(env, decimals);
        assert_with_error!(
            env,
            units >= MIN_WHOLE_UNIT_COLLATERAL,
            CollateralError::MinBorrowCollateralNotMet
        );
    }
