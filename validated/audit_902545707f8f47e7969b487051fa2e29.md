### Title
`repay` performs no post-transaction egroup/health check, letting anyone force a victim's remaining debt into a stricter egroup by clearing a dust debt on their behalf - (File: `local-testing/contracts/market/market.clar`, `local-testing/contracts/market/market-vault.clar`, `local-testing/contracts/registry/egroup.clar`)

### Summary
Attacker A calls `repay` with `on-behalf-of: (some B)` to fully clear B's dust-sized Y-debt (scaled-debt = 1). This flips B's `mask` bit for Y-debt off, forcing `market-vault`'s registry to re-resolve B into a different, less favourable egroup for B's large, healthy X-debt position, without any health check being run inside `repay` itself. A then calls `liquidate` on B's now-unhealthy X position in the same block.

### Finding Description
`repay` in `market.clar` reads B's `mask`/`account-scaled-debt`, computes `repaid-scaled-debt`, and unconditionally calls `debt-remove-scaled`: [1](#0-0) 

Notice the precondition list: `ERR-AUTHORIZATION` only checks `contract-caller == tx-sender`, never that the caller is B - any principal can repay any account's debt via `on-behalf-of`. Crucially, unlike `borrow`, which explicitly recomputes the `future-mask`/`future-group` and asserts `is-healthy-with-mask` on the resulting position before allowing the state change: [2](#0-1) 

`repay` has **no** analogous check at all - it never resolves the post-repay egroup nor validates the health/LTV of the remaining position.

The shared state A writes and B's later liquidation reads is the `mask` field in `market-vault`'s obligation registry. `debt-remove-scaled` clears the debt bit from `mask` as soon as the scaled debt for that asset reaches zero: [3](#0-2) 

Egroup resolution (`egroup.clar`) picks the smallest-population egroup whose mask is a superset of the user's current mask (most specific match first), per the documented algorithm: [4](#0-3) 

The only cross-egroup ordering guarantee enforced by the DAO at registration time is the *superset invariant*: for any two **registered egroups** where one mask is a strict superset of the other, the superset's LTV values must be lower-or-equal: [5](#0-4) 

This invariant guarantees that `DEFAULT` (mask = `MAX-U128`, superset of every mask) always has LTV values ≤ every specific egroup, including the favourable `egroup-XY` that covered B's combined X+Y position. It says nothing about ties among "sibling" egroups that are not in a subset relationship with each other, and it does nothing to stop the *fallback* itself: once B's mask shrinks from `{coll, debt-X, debt-Y}` to `{coll, debt-X}` because A cleared the 1-unit Y debt, `resolve` may no longer find any registered egroup that is an exact/tight match for the reduced mask, and falls back to a strictly more conservative egroup (potentially all the way to `DEFAULT`) with materially lower `LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL`/`LTV-LIQ-FULL` thresholds than `egroup-XY` provided.

Because `repay` never re-checks health or egroup-implied capacity after this mask mutation (the check that exists in `collateral-add` for exactly this class of problem, see `docs/High-Level-Overview.md` §"Collateral-Add Egroup Transition Protection", is absent from `repay`), B's large X position - healthy one block earlier under `egroup-XY`'s generous thresholds - becomes immediately liquidatable under the stricter fallback egroup, purely as a side effect of A's dust repayment on Y. A repaying B's *other* dust debt (X's own trivial companion, if any) would not clear the bit that matters for X's egroup membership, so the choice of "which of B's two dust debts to clear" is asymmetric and directly attacker-controlled.

### Impact Explanation
B loses the collateral seized (plus liquidation penalty) in the `liquidate` call that follows in the same block, i.e. direct theft of funds at rest belonging to B. This is Critical: direct theft of user funds via an attacker-triggered, attacker-timed liquidation that would not have occurred but for A's unsolicited `repay` on B's behalf.

### Likelihood Explanation
A's capital cost is trivial - only the dust amount needed to fully clear B's smallest scaled-debt entry (as small as 1 unit, chosen by design of the attack). A does not need any privileged role: `repay`'s `on-behalf-of` parameter is open to any principal, and A needs no special timing beyond calling `repay` then `liquidate` in the same block. The attack is repeatable against any account holding two egroup-relevant debts where the combined egroup is materially favourable versus the fallback that results from dropping one debt asset. It requires a specific real-world configuration (an `egroup-XY` with much better LTV parameters than what the mask maps to once Y is dropped) - this is realistic given the protocol's stated design of highly favourable custom pairwise egroups (e.g. the sBTC/USDC 70% LTV vs a 60%/default fallback documented in `local-testing/tests/security/egroup.test.ts:59-104` and `local-testing/contracts/proposals/proposal-create-egroup-sbtc-usdc.clar`).

### Recommendation
Add the same egroup/health re-validation to `repay` that already exists in `borrow` and `collateral-add`: after computing the post-repay `mask`, resolve the new egroup and assert that the resulting position (using current oracle prices) remains healthy (`LTV <= LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL` or better) before allowing the debt-removal to finalize, or explicitly assert that egroup capacity/LTV-liq thresholds do not deteriorate when the caller is not the account owner. Alternatively, restrict third-party dust repayments below a materiality threshold, or require `min-collateral-expected`-style post-checks analogous to those used in liquidation-adjacent flows.

### Proof of Concept
Simnet plan (Clarinet/vitest, two accounts A and B, plus egroups XY and DEFAULT/fallback pre-configured with the properties above):
1. Configure `egroup-XY`: mask = collateral-X + debt-X + debt-Y, LTV-BORROW/LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL/LTV-LIQ-FULL generous (e.g. 70/85/95%).
2. Configure `DEFAULT` (or a second, stricter egroup matching `collateral-X + debt-X` alone at a different popcount): LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL/FULL noticeably lower (e.g. 55/60%).
3. B deposits collateral-X, borrows a large healthy amount of debt-X (sized to be healthy under `egroup-XY` but unhealthy under the stricter fallback), and borrows 1 unit (dust) of debt-Y.
4. Branch 1 (control): B alone continues to hold the position - assert B's mask still resolves to `egroup-XY` and X position remains healthy.
5. Branch 2 (attack): A calls `market.repay(Y-ft, u1, (some B))`. Assert B's `mask` no longer contains the Y-debt bit, `resolve-safe(B)` now returns the stricter fallback egroup, and B's X position is now unhealthy (`LTV > LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL` of fallback) though it was healthy before the call and no price move occurred.
6. In the same block, A calls `market.liquidate(B, ...)` targeting debt-X, and assert it succeeds (would have failed with `ERR-UNHEALTHY` pre-repay) and A receives collateral-X plus liquidation penalty.
7. Contrast with a third branch where A instead repays the dust portion of debt-X (not Y): show B's mask/egroup and health are unaffected, confirming the asymmetric, attacker-chosen impact depending on which bit is cleared.

### The Resolution Algorithm

```
(define-read-only (resolve (mask uint))
```

#### Steps:

1. Calculate minimum population (# of bits set in user's mask)
2. Get active buckets ≥ minimum
3. Search buckets from smallest to largest
4. Return first egroup where `user_mask ⊆ egroup_mask`
5. If no match, return DEFAULT (mask = MAX-U128, covers everything)

#### Example walkthrough

```
User has: Assets 0, 2 → mask = 0b0101 → popcount = 2

Available egroups:
- ID 1: mask 0b0101, popcount 2 (exact match)
- ID 2: mask 0b0111, popcount 3 (superset)
- ID 3: mask 0b1111, popcount 4 (superset)

Resolution:
1. min_pop = 2
2. Check bucket 2 first (popcount 2)
3. Find ID 1 (0b0101) - user mask is subset ✓
4. Return ID 1 parameters
```

;; -- Superset invariant validation ------------------------------------------

;; Check invariant between new egroup and one existing egroup
;; Ensures that if egroup B's mask is a superset of egroup A's mask,
;; then B's LTV values are LOWER OR EQUAL to A's values
(define-private (check-egroup-invariant
  (id uint)
  (acc { new-mask: uint,
         new-ltv-borrow: uint,
         new-ltv-liq-partial: uint,
         new-ltv-liq-full: uint,
         exclude-id: (optional uint),
         max-id: uint,
         valid: bool }))

  ;; sanity check
  (if (or (not (get valid acc))
          (>= id (get max-id acc))
          (is-eq (some id) (get exclude-id acc)))
      acc
      ;; check invariant
      (let ((existing (lookup id))
            (existing-mask (get MASK existing))
            (new-mask (get new-mask acc)))

        ;; skip if equal
        (if (is-eq existing-mask new-mask)
            acc
            (let ((existing-ltv-borrow (buff-to-uint-be (get LTV-BORROW existing)))
                  (existing-ltv-liq-partial (buff-to-uint-be (get LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL existing)))
                  (existing-ltv-liq-full (buff-to-uint-be (get LTV-LIQ-FULL existing)))
                  (new-ltv-borrow (get new-ltv-borrow acc))
                  (new-ltv-liq-partial (get new-ltv-liq-partial acc))
                  (new-ltv-liq-full (get new-ltv-liq-full acc))
                  ;; determine relationship
                  (holds
                    (if (subset existing-mask new-mask)
                        ;; new is proper superset | LTVn <= LTVe
                        (and (<= new-ltv-borrow existing-ltv-borrow)
                             (<= new-ltv-liq-partial existing-ltv-liq-partial)
                             (<= new-ltv-liq-full existing-ltv-liq-full))
                        (if (subset new-mask existing-mask)
                            ;; existing is proper superset | LTVn >= LTVe
                            (and (>= new-ltv-borrow existing-ltv-borrow)
                                 (>= new-ltv-liq-partial existing-ltv-liq-partial)
                                 (>= new-ltv-liq-full existing-ltv-liq-full))
                            ;; no subset relationship
                            true))))

              (merge acc { valid: holds }))))))
