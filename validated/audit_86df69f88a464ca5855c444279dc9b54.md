### Title
Reward-claim withdrawals decrement contract sBTC balance without decrementing `last-accounted-rewards-only`, causing `calculate-rewards` under-flow / freezing of future reward distribution - (File: `stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar`)

### Summary
`pox-5.clar`'s reward machinery derives "new rewards received" purely from a balance snapshot (`get-rewards`) minus a running accounting counter (`last-accounted-rewards-only`), the same balance-snapshot pattern that caused the Alchemix `RevenueHandler.checkPoint()` bug for non-alchemic tokens. Unlike `RevenueHandler`, `calculate-rewards` does subtract a running "already accounted" baseline instead of blindly re-adding the whole balance — but that baseline is only ever *increased* (on `calculate-rewards`) and is never decreased when previously-accounted rewards are physically paid out and leave the contract's sBTC balance (via staker/signer claims). This breaks the equality `get-rewards() ≥ last-accounted-rewards-only` that `get-new-rewards` implicitly relies on.

### Finding Description
`get-rewards` computes the reward pool as a live balance delta: [1](#0-0) 

`get-new-rewards` then subtracts the last recorded baseline from that live balance: [2](#0-1) 

`calculate-rewards` uses `get-new-rewards` as `gross-accrued-rewards`, distributes it to bonds/reserve/stakers, and then **increases** `last-accounted-rewards-only` by `(gross-accrued-rewards - reserve-deposit)`: [3](#0-2) 

`last-accounted-rewards-only` is intended to represent "the sBTC that has already been recognized as reward" so that it is excluded from `get-rewards()`'s next computation (mirroring the comment "Used to keep track of which sBTC is just for rewards, vs from staking"): [4](#0-3) 

However, once a staker/signer actually **claims** their earned rewards, sBTC physically leaves the contract's balance (the reward liability is settled and paid out), but nothing in the codebase decrements `last-accounted-rewards-only` to reflect that the "already-accounted" bucket has shrunk. I was not able to locate the `claim-staker-rewards`/`claim-signer-rewards` transfer logic in the indexed portion of `pox-5.clar` to confirm this with an exact line citation (grep found matches only in `signer-manager.clar` test helpers, and the relevant contract lines were not returned within the tool budget), so this final step is asserted based on the documented reward-distribution/claim architecture and could not be pinned to an exact line in this pass.

Given the design as shown, the invariant `current-sbtc-balance - total-sbtc-staked - reserve-balance ≥ last-accounted-rewards-only` holds only as long as no previously-accounted reward has been withdrawn. As soon as any staker claims earned rewards (which is the entire purpose of the accounting), `current-sbtc-balance` drops by the claimed amount while `last-accounted-rewards-only` stays the same. The next call to `calculate-rewards`/`get-new-rewards` then computes `(- rewards-balance last-accounted-rewards)` in Clarity's checked-uint arithmetic, which **aborts the transaction on underflow** whenever `rewards-balance < last-accounted-rewards-only` — i.e. whenever claimed-but-still-"accounted" sBTC exceeds any newly-deposited sBTC since the last distribution. This freezes `calculate-rewards` (and therefore reward accrual for every bond and every STX staker for the affected cycle/half-cycle) until enough fresh sBTC revenue arrives to push the balance back above the stale baseline — an attacker-uncontrollable, but reachable, denial of the reward pipeline that the description explicitly calls out as in-scope ("temporary freezing of staked funds ... signing weight or reward slots exceeding locked value").

### Impact Explanation
This breaks the equality between "sBTC recognized as reward" and "sBTC still held by the contract for reward purposes." Any legitimate claim of previously-computed rewards permanently reduces the contract's ability to correctly recompute `get-new-rewards` on the next cycle, causing `calculate-rewards` to abort (temporary freezing of reward computation/distribution for all bonds and stakers) until new revenue backfills the gap. This is a High-impact "temporary freezing of staked/reward funds" per the rules, since legitimate reward claims by unprivileged stakers are enough to trigger it — no privileged action required.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate-to-high: it requires no attacker privilege, just the normal, expected sequence of `calculate-rewards` → staker/signer `claim-*-rewards` → `calculate-rewards` again before enough new sBTC revenue arrives to offset the claimed amount. Given claim timing is fully staker-controlled and unclaimed rewards not fully "backed" by future revenue is common, this can occur under ordinary usage rather than a contrived attack path.

### Recommendation
Decrement `last-accounted-rewards-only` by the amount of rewards actually paid out whenever a claim function transfers previously-accounted sBTC out of the contract, so that `last-accounted-rewards-only` always tracks "still-held, already-recognized" sBTC rather than a monotonically increasing counter. Alternatively, replace the balance-snapshot-and-baseline pattern with an explicit ledger of (a) total rewards ever recognized and (b) total rewards ever paid out, and derive `get-new-rewards` purely from tracked deposit events rather than from `current-balance - total-staked - reserve - baseline`, eliminating the underflow-prone dependency on live token balance.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual sequence (exact claim-function line references could not be confirmed within the tool budget for this pass; a Devin session with full repo access should verify the precise `claim-staker-rewards`/`claim-signer-rewards` implementation before filing):
1. Deposit sBTC revenue, call `calculate-rewards` — `last-accounted-rewards-only` increases by `(gross-accrued-rewards - reserve-deposit)`.
2. A staker/signer claims their full earned reward for that cycle — sBTC leaves the contract, but `last-accounted-rewards-only` is untouched.
3. Call `calculate-rewards` again with no (or insufficient) new sBTC deposited — `get-rewards()` (now lower due to the payout) minus the still-high `last-accounted-rewards-only` underflows in `get-new-rewards`, aborting the transaction and freezing further reward distribution for all remaining stakers/bonds until new revenue arrives.
