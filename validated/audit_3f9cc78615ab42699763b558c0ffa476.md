### Title
Unvalidated `creator` account in `initialize_with_permission` decouples pool-creator privileges from the permission check, enabling creator-fee misdirection/freezing - (File: `programs/cp-swap/src/instructions/initialize_with_permission.rs`)

### Summary
`initialize_with_permission` gates pool creation on a `Permission` PDA derived from the **payer's** key, but the `pool_creator` identity actually recorded in `PoolState` (and later entitled to collect all creator trading fees) is taken from a completely unconstrained `creator` account that is neither a `Signer` nor tied by any `constraint`/`address` check to `payer` or to the permission grant.

### Finding Description
In `InitializeWithPermission`, the `creator` field is declared as: [1](#0-0) 
an `UncheckedAccount` with no signer requirement and no `constraint`/`address` binding it to `payer` or to `permission.authority`. The permission gate itself only verifies that a `Permission` account exists at a PDA seeded by `payer.key()`: [2](#0-1) 
Nothing in the account validation or in the handler checks that `creator` equals `payer`, equals `permission.authority`, or is a signer. Yet the value is used directly to set the pool's permanent, fee-earning identity: [3](#0-2) 

By contrast, the sibling permissionless instruction `Initialize` requires `creator` to actually sign the transaction: [4](#0-3) 
so the security guarantee that exists in the "normal" path (the recorded creator consciously authorized the pool) is silently dropped in the permission-gated path, even though the permission check is meant to establish *who* is allowed to become a privileged pool creator.

`pool_creator` recorded here is later relied upon as the sole beneficiary of accumulated creator fees, both in the signer-gated path: [5](#0-4) 
and in the permissionless path, where the fee destination ATA is simply derived from whatever `creator` pubkey is stored in `pool_state`: [6](#0-5) 

Because `creator_token_0`/`creator_token_1` are created via `init_if_needed` for any `associated_token::authority = creator` without requiring that authority to ever sign, a permission-holding `payer` can bind `pool_creator` to an address for which no private key is ever presented (e.g. a PDA of another program, a burn address, or any arbitrary pubkey they do not control). This is exactly the CVE-2017-15200 bug class: an authorization check performed against one identity (`payer` holding a `Permission` PDA) is used to grant a persistent privileged benefit (creator-fee ownership) to a different, unverified identity supplied as ordinary instruction data (`creator`), with no check that the two are related.

### Impact Explanation
All future creator fees accrued by the pool (`pool_state.creator_fees_token_0/1`, funded out of ongoing swap fees paid by every swapper) can be permanently and irrecoverably locked: once `pool_creator` is set to an address nobody controls, `collect_creator_fee` can never succeed (it requires that exact pubkey to sign) and `collect_creator_fee_permissionless` will transfer the fees into an ATA whose owner has no private key, effectively burning them. This is a permanent freezing of accumulated fee funds contributed by pool users, achievable purely by the account list supplied in a single `initialize_with_permission` transaction.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any account that already holds a `Permission` PDA (an entity previously approved to launch permissioned pools) can trigger this with a single, otherwise-normal pool creation call by simply supplying an unrelated pubkey as `creator`; no additional signatures, timing, or race conditions are required.

### Recommendation
Add an explicit constraint tying `creator` to a verified identity, e.g. require `creator` to be a `Signer`, or add `constraint = creator.key() == permission.authority` (and/or `payer.key()`), so the permission check and the recorded `pool_creator` refer to the same, verified party — matching the guarantee already enforced in `Initialize`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Party `P` obtains a `Permission` PDA via `create_permission_pda` (seeded by `P`'s pubkey), granting `P` the right to call `initialize_with_permission`.
2. `P` calls `initialize_with_permission`, signing as `payer`, but passes an unrelated pubkey `X` (e.g. a freshly generated keypair whose private key is discarded, or a PDA with no signer authority) as the `creator` account. No signature from `X` is required because `creator` is an `UncheckedAccount`.
3. `pool_state.initialize(...)` stores `pool_creator = X`.
4. As swaps occur, `creator_fees_token_0/1` accrue in `pool_state`.
5. `collect_creator_fee` can never be called (no one can sign as `X`); `collect_creator_fee_permissionless` sends the accrued fees into an ATA owned by `X`, an address with no controlling keypair — the funds are permanently frozen/lost.
