### Title
Suppliers can front-run bad-debt socialization by withdrawing before `clean_bad_debt` executes, shifting the entire loss onto remaining suppliers - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs)

### Summary
Staking-style shares in XOXNO Lending are RAY-scaled supply positions valued at `scaled_amount × supply_index`. Bad debt is socialized by writing `supply_index` down (`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`), which is exactly analogous to a staker absorbing a player's loss. The permissionless `clean_bad_debt` path (and any liquidation that leaves bad debt) is publicly triggerable only *after* a price move makes an account insolvent — creating a deterministic race window in which a supplier can call `withdraw` at the pre-write-down index and escape its pro-rata share of the loss. Conversely, there is no delay between supplying and earning index-denominated value, so no symmetric "stake before the win" leg is needed: the profitable direction is exit-before-socialization, repeated across every bad-debt event.

### Finding Description
- `clean_bad_debt(account_id)` is permissionless under the `DustCapped` gate: any caller whose auth is provided can socialize an insolvent account whose collateral is at/below the dust threshold [1](#0-0) .
- The borrow-side seize leg writes the unpaid debt off against `supply_index` and burns the debt shares, so every outstanding supply share loses value pro-rata [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) .
- `withdraw` burns shares and pays out at the current `supply_index` with only reserve/utilization/solvency guards — there is no cooldown, epoch, or delay between a supplier's last index state and exit [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) .
- The triggering condition (collateral price drop pushing `total_debt > total_collateral`, or dust-capped insolvency) is observable on-chain from the price aggregator plus `get_bulk_indexes`/`get_account_positions` views before anyone submits `clean_bad_debt` or a loss-making `liquidate`.

So the sequence open to any unprivileged supplier is:

1. Supply token T to a spoke via controller `supply`.
2. Observe an oracle update/price crash that makes some borrower's T-debt socializable (dust-capped insolvency) — this is visible before the `clean_bad_debt` transaction exists, purely from public state.
3. Submit `withdraw` (amount `0` = full close) in the same ledger or an earlier one; the pool pays `floor(shares × supply_index)` at the **pre-write-down** index and debits cash.
4. Any caller (including the attacker or a keeper) later executes `clean_bad_debt` / `liquidate` with bad debt; `seize_positions` writes the loss into the index, borne only by suppliers who did not exit.
5. Re-supply after the write-down at the depressed index if desired.

The reverse direction also holds: a supplier who stayed in can monitor for governance-queued or keeper-initiated bad-debt events and exit during the same window, so only passive/uninformed suppliers absorb losses.

### Impact Explanation
Each execution transfers the exiting supplier's pro-rata share of the bad debt onto the remaining suppliers and the protocol revenue shares (which are themselves supply shares via `accrue_revenue`/`absorb_supply_as_revenue`). This is a direct, repeatable loss of user funds: remaining suppliers' claims are written down by more than their fair share, and in the extreme a large supplier exiting first leaves small suppliers holding the entire write-down — the same "staker avoids the losing game" harm as the reference report. Over many events the strategy is statistically profitable at the expense of passive suppliers.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires: (a) a market that experiences socializable bad debt (an insolvent account with dust-capped collateral for the permissionless path, or a liquidation that leaves bad debt), and (b) enough unutilized cash in the market for the attacker to withdraw (guarded by `require_reserves` and `require_utilization_below_max`). Both are realistic: bad-debt events are inherent to lending, and front-running/ordering on Stellar is achievable via fee bidding since transaction order within a ledger is not content-protected. No privileged role, leaked key, or oracle manipulation is needed — only the same public price/account state anyone can read.

### Recommendation
Introduce a withdrawal delay or epoch-based redemption for supply shares (e.g., `withdraw` becomes a two-step request/settle with shares valued at the index at settle time), or accrue/share losses at a granularity that makes intra-event exit unprofitable — e.g., snapshot the supply index used for withdrawal at the earlier of request-time and the last index update before the insolvency became knowable. Short of that, a timelock on `supply` shares (minimum holding period before withdrawal) raises the attacker's exposure window so they bear their pro-rata share of any bad debt arising while locked. Any delay must be bounded and non-blocking to avoid a DoS on honest exits.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bob and Carol each supply 50 units of ETH; Alice supplies USDC collateral and borrows ETH (dust-scale position, e.g. 0.003 ETH).
2. Oracle reports a USDC price crash so `total_debt > total_collateral` and collateral ≤ dust threshold — the account now satisfies `BadDebtGate::DustCapped` (`is_socializable_bad_debt`) [6](#0-5) .
3. Before anyone calls `clean_bad_debt`, Bob submits controller `withdraw` with amount `0` (full close). `ops::withdraw::accounting` resolves his whole scaled position at the still-high `supply_index` and pays out, passing `require_reserves` since cash is available [7](#0-6) .
4. Any caller then calls `clean_bad_debt(account_id)`; `seize_positions` burns Alice's debt shares and calls `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`, lowering `supply_index` [8](#0-7) .
5. Carol's 50 ETH claim is now written down by the *full* bad debt (not half), while Bob exits whole. Net effect: Bob's avoided loss is transferred to Carol — matching the harness behavior in `force_socialize_does_not_net_collateral_against_debt`, where the surviving supplier absorbs the socialized debt pro-rata [9](#0-8) .

/// Admission condition for bad-debt socialization.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
enum BadDebtGate {
    /// Permissionless: insolvent *and* collateral at or below the dust threshold.
    DustCapped,
    /// Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind.
    InsolventOnly,
}

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);
}

pub(crate) fn apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(cache: &mut Cache, bad_debt: Ray) {
    let total_supplied_value = cache.supplied().mul(cache.env(), cache.supply_index());

    if total_supplied_value == Ray::ZERO {
        return;
    }

    let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
    let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);

    let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
    let new_supply_index = cache
        .supply_index()
        .mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);

    cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));

pub(crate) fn accounting(
    env: &Env,
    is_liquidation: bool,
    entry: &PoolWithdrawEntry,
) -> WithdrawOutcome {
    require_nonneg_amount(env, entry.protocol_fee);
    let (mut cache, position) = ops::load_leg(env, &entry.action);

    let (burned, gross_amount) = resolve_close_or_partial(&cache, entry.action.amount, position);
    // Burn first: `protocol_fee_shares` caps the fee mint at `i128::MAX - supplied`.
    let remaining = burn_position(env, &mut cache, position, burned);
    let net_transfer = withhold_liquidation_fee(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        gross_amount,
        is_liquidation,
        entry.protocol_fee,
    );

    // A footprint-only close must not add a utilization gate to same-market
    // net settlement: it burns no shares and moves no cash.
    let empty_close = position.raw() == 0 && entry.action.amount == i128::MAX;
    gate_and_debit(env, &mut cache, net_transfer, is_liquidation || empty_close);

    let snapshot = cache.commit();
    let mutation = cache.position_mutation(remaining, gross_amount);
    WithdrawOutcome {
        cache,
        mutation,
        snapshot,
        net_transfer,
    }
}

fn resolve_close_or_partial(cache: &Cache, amount: i128, position: Ray) -> (Ray, i128) {
    let (burned, gross_amount) = cache.resolve_withdrawal(amount, position);
    assert_with_error!(
        cache.env(),
        gross_amount == 0 || burned.raw() > 0,
        GenericError::WithdrawRoundsToZeroShares
    );
    (burned, gross_amount)
}

/// Burns `burned` from market supply and returns the user's remaining scaled position.
fn burn_position(env: &Env, cache: &mut Cache, position: Ray, burned: Ray) -> Ray {
    cache.burn_supply(burned);
    position.checked_sub(env, burned)
}

/// Enforces reserve, utilization, and solvency guards, then debits cash for
/// the net transfer. Liquidations and footprint-only closes skip utilization.
fn gate_and_debit(env: &Env, cache: &mut Cache, net_transfer: i128, skip_utilization_check: bool) {
    cache.require_reserves(net_transfer);

    if !skip_utilization_check {
        guards::require_utilization_below_max(env, cache);
    }
    guards::require_supply_for_debt(env, cache);
    cache.debit_cash(net_transfer);
}

#[test]
fn force_socialize_does_not_net_collateral_against_debt() {
    let mut t = setup();

    t.supply(BOB, "ETH", 100.0);
    t.supply(ALICE, "USDC", 10.0);
    t.borrow(ALICE, "ETH", 0.003);

    let alice_id = t.resolve_account_id(ALICE);
    let bob_before = t.supply_balance(BOB, "ETH");
    let alice_collateral_before = t.supply_balance(ALICE, "USDC");
    let usdc_rev_before = t.snapshot_revenue("USDC");

    // Crash the collateral so debt > collateral, satisfying the `InsolventOnly` gate.
    // Alice keeps real collateral: no liquidator has taken it.
    t.set_price("USDC", usd_cents(10));
    t.assert_liquidatable(ALICE);

    t.force_socialize_bad_debt_by_id(alice_id);

    let bob_loss = bob_before - t.supply_balance(BOB, "ETH");
    let usdc_rev_gain = t.snapshot_revenue("USDC") - usdc_rev_before;

    // Alice's untouched collateral becomes USDC protocol revenue...
    assert!(
        usdc_rev_gain > 0,
        "collateral should become USDC protocol revenue, gain={}",
        usdc_rev_gain
    );
    // ...while ETH suppliers absorb the debt with no credit for that recovery.
    assert!(
        bob_loss > 0.0,
        "ETH supplier must absorb the socialised debt, loss={:.9}",
        bob_loss
    );
