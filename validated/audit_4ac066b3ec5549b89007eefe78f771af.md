Permissionless third-party `supply` restamps a foreign account's cached risk parameters, bypassing the health-factor floor and forcing accounts into liquidation - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs)

### Summary
`supply` permits any unprivileged caller to top up an existing supply position on an account it does not own. Every merged supply leg calls `refresh_supply_risk_params` with `RiskRefreshScope::FullTuple`, which restamps the position's cached BPS risk tuple (LTV / liquidation threshold) to the currently listed values — with no health-factor floor. The dedicated permissionless restamp entrypoint `update_account_threshold` enforces a post-update HF ≥ 1.05 guard precisely so it "cannot be used to push an account into liquidation"; the `supply` path performs the same restamp without that guard. An attacker can therefore donate a dust amount of a collateral asset the victim already holds, restamp the victim's cached (higher) liquidation threshold down to tightened listing values, push the account below HF 1, and liquidate it — mirroring the confused-deputy class where a helper path silently re-binds a victim's stale authority/parameters to attacker-timed current state.

### Finding Description
Account supply positions carry cached BPS risk parameters stamped at deposit time (positions.md: supply entries "carry their BPS risk parameters"; docs/reference/endpoints.md:39 `update_account_threshold` "Refresh LTV; optional risk refresh requires final HF >= 1.05"). The health factor is computed from those cached per-position parameters, not the live listing.

Two relevant code paths:

1. `require_third_party_existing_supply` (contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs:78-97) admits a non-owner/non-delegate `caller` provided every supplied `HubAssetKey` is already in `account.supply_positions`. No HF or solvency check applies to `supply` (docs/reference/endpoints.md:55: "Ordinary `supply` and `repay` skip these checks").
2. `merge_supply_leg` (contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs:275-324) unconditionally calls `refresh_supply_risk_params(..., RiskRefreshScope::FullTuple)` on the merged position before updating its scaled amount — for owner and third-party suppliers alike.

When governance tightens a listing's liquidation threshold (a keep-or-tighten flag/risk edit reachable through delayed ops), accounts that supplied earlier retain the old, more permissive cached tuple. The protocol's own invariant commentary acknowledges the danger of restamping: `update_account_threshold` with `has_risks` reverts unless the account clears a 1.05 HF floor afterward (scripts/permissionless_entrypoints.txt:76, INV-RISK-01). The third-party `supply` path performs the identical `FullTuple` restamp with no such floor and no solvency gate, reachable via a permissionless entrypoint with a dust-cost donation.

This is the on-chain shape of the reported bug class: a seemingly benign "helpful" action on a victim's account that silently re-binds the victim's cached state (their stale authorization-equivalent — the old risk tuple) to fresh attacker-chosen inputs, converting a stored consent artifact into a live loss.

### Impact Explanation
- Attacker picks any account holding an existing supply position in a market whose listed risk parameters were tightened below the account's cached tuple.
- Attacker calls `supply(caller=attacker, account_id=victim, spoke_id=victim.spoke, assets=[(hub_asset, dust)])`. `require_third_party_existing_supply` passes because the victim already holds that market; `merge_supply_leg` restamps the position to the tightened tuple.
- The account's cached liquidation threshold drops; its health factor — recomputed from cached params on the next `liquidate` call — falls below 1 even though the account was healthy under its stamped parameters and would still pass the 1.05 floor applied by `update_account_threshold`.
- Attacker then calls `liquidate` in the same or a follow-up transaction, seizing collateral at the HF-scaled bonus — theft of user funds via forced liquidation. Severity: High (unprivileged, single address, direct collateral extraction), bounded by requiring a governance tightening of risk parameters to have occurred since the victim's last stamp.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a precondition: listed risk parameters stricter than the victim's cached tuple. This occurs naturally whenever governance tightens LTV/threshold on an existing listing; positions are explicitly designed to keep stale stamps until `update_account_threshold` or a position mutation refreshes them. The attack costs only a dust top-up of an asset the victim already holds, needs no privileges, and both legs (`supply`, `liquidate`) are permissionless. The one element I could not fully verify in the available context is the exact contents of `RiskRefreshScope::FullTuple` in `contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs` — specifically whether it restamps the liquidation threshold component the HF reads — but the invariant documentation (`update_account_threshold` "restamps LTV only" vs `has_risks` HF-floor path, and the supply entries carrying "their BPS risk parameters") plus the `FullTuple` name strongly indicate the full risk tuple is restamped on every supply merge.

### Recommendation
Restrict the `FullTuple` risk refresh in `merge_supply_leg` / `process_deposit` to owner/delegate callers, or apply the same post-operation health-factor floor used by `update_account_threshold` (revert if the restamp would drop the account below the 1.05 threshold) whenever the caller is a third party. Alternatively, gate the restamp on `RiskRefreshScope` such that third-party top-ups only update usage/scaled amounts without overwriting the position's cached risk tuple.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lowers `liquidation_threshold` on listed asset `X` from 0.85 to 0.70 (timelocked listing edit).
2. Victim's account holds a supply position in `X` stamped at 0.85 and debt sized so HF = 1.10 under 0.85 but < 1.00 under 0.70.
3. Attacker calls `controller.supply(attacker, victim_id, victim_spoke, [(X_key, 1)])` — allowed by `require_third_party_existing_supply` since `X` is already in `supply_positions`; `merge_supply_leg` executes `refresh_supply_risk_params(..., FullTuple)`, restamping the position to 0.70.
4. Attacker calls `controller.liquidate(attacker, victim_id, debt_payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)`; the risk totals now read the restamped 0.70 threshold, HF < 1 admits the liquidation, and the attacker seizes bonus-scaled collateral.
5. Compare: the same restamp via `update_account_threshold(attacker, has_risks=true, [victim_id])` reverts under the 1.05 HF floor — the guard the `supply` path omits.
