### Title
Liquidator can repay debt and receive zero collateral — `liquidate` has no minimum-seizure bound (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs)

### Summary
`liquidate` lets any caller submit per-leg debt payments and computes the seized collateral entirely from oracle-priced plan math. There is no `min_seized`/`min_out`-style parameter: the planner prorates `repay_usd * (1 + bonus)` across supply positions and drops every leg whose seizure rounds to zero tokens or zero shares, yet still accepts and pulls the repayment. The liquidator can therefore pay debt tokens and receive nothing — the exact analog of the splits-oracle "trader pays more quoteToken than expected" class, mapped onto XOXNO's measured-plan settlement.

### Finding Description
In `normalize_repayment_plan` (`contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs:173-224`), offered payments are converted to USD, trimmed to the curve quote, and excess is scheduled for refund — but a payment *below* the ideal is always accepted as offered. `calculate_seized_collateral` (`math.rs:363-504`) then distributes `total_seizure_usd = repay_usd * (1 + bonus)` pro-rata across supply legs, flooring each leg's seizure to token units and skipping legs where `capped_ray <= 0` (`math.rs:419-430`). Planning drops zero-rounding legs and, per the liquidation skill doc, "the contract accepts a liquidation that repays debt and seizes nothing, so the liquidator would pay and receive no collateral" (`skills/xoxno-lending-liquidations/SKILL.md:178-180`). Only the narrow sub-3-decimal "raised quote" path reverts with `InvalidPayments` when nothing is seized (`SKILL.md:128-130`); the general case has no non-empty-seizure guard and no caller-supplied floor.

Because seizure is priced off `feed.price` and pro-rated by each leg's share of total collateral (`math.rs:384-398`), a stale-but-in-bands price move, a competing liquidation that reshapes the position list between estimate and execution, or rounding on small legs can shrink the realized seizure to zero while the debt transfer still executes. The liquidator authorizes the exact pull (`composing.md:147-150`), so the loss is real and unbounded up to the offered amounts — there is no way for the liquidator to express "revert unless I receive at least X collateral."

### Impact Explanation
Theft/loss of user funds: an unprivileged liquidator transfers debt tokens to the pool, the borrower's debt is reduced, and the liquidator receives zero collateral in return. This satisfies "theft of user funds" — the liquidator's payment is consumed with no corresponding asset receipt, and no `min_seized` escape exists in the `liquidate` arguments.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by any address calling `liquidate` with positive `debt_payments`. The triggering condition is a plan whose seizure legs all round to zero — achievable via small payments against multi-leg collateral, collateral decimals/price configurations that floor seizures, or a state change (competing liquidation, price update within tolerance) between `get_liquidation_estimate` and execution. The docs explicitly document this accepted state rather than treating it as unreachable. Severity is Medium: loss is bounded by what the liquidator offers, requires an adverse price/state shift or careless offer, and the victim is the liquidator itself rather than a passive depositor.

### Recommendation
Add a `min_seized` (or per-leg `min_out`) parameter to `liquidate`, checked after `build_liquidation_plan` against the planned seizures, reverting if the aggregate or any leg falls below it. Alternatively, revert with `InvalidPayments` whenever `debt_payments` is non-empty but all seizure legs are dropped, making "pay and seize nothing" impossible by default.

### Proof of Concept
1. Account holds debt plus multiple small collateral legs priced such that a modest repayment pro-rates to sub-unit seizures on every leg.
2. Liquidator calls `liquidate` with a positive `debt_payments` vec covering part of the debt.
3. `normalize_repayment_plan` accepts the payment (below ideal → kept as offered, `math.rs:184-205`); `calculate_seized_collateral` drops every leg (`capped_ray <= 0`, `math.rs:419-430`) and returns empty seizures.
4. Execution pulls the offered debt tokens to the pool, credits the repayment to the borrower's debt positions, and transfers nothing to the liquidator — confirmed as intended behavior by `SKILL.md:178-180` ("The contract accepts a liquidation that repays debt and seizes nothing").
