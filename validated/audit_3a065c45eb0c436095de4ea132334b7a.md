### Title
Vault depositors can front-run bad-debt socialization to exit at the pre-loss share price, shifting the loss onto remaining depositors - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary
Zest v2 vaults price zTokens (shares) using a global `assets` variable and liquidity index (`lindex`) that is written down atomically inside `liquidate` whenever a borrower's collateral is fully seized and residual debt must be socialized across all suppliers of that debt asset's vault. Because `redeem`/withdrawal is permissionless, has no cooldown, and is priced off the *current* (pre-socialization) `assets`/share ratio, any depositor who observes an impending `liquidate` call in the mempool that will trigger bad-debt socialization can front-run it with a `redeem`, locking in the old exchange rate and escaping their pro-rata share of the loss. That loss is instead absorbed entirely by the depositors who remain in the vault — an unprivileged-vs-unprivileged harm mediated by shared pool state, structurally identical to the reported staking/unstaking front-run pattern.

### Finding Description
`socialize-debt` (called from `market.clar`'s `liquidate` when a liquidated position has no collateral left) directly reduces the vault's `assets` variable and writes down `lindex` proportionally to the loss: [1](#0-0) 

This is invoked from the `liquidate` flow in the market hub as soon as a borrower's collateral is exhausted: [2](#0-1) 

Withdrawals from the vault (`redeem`) are permissionless, have no delay/cooldown, and convert shares to assets using the vault's *current* `assets` balance at the time of the call: [3](#0-2) 

Because `assets` (and thus the zToken exchange rate) only drops *after* `socialize-debt` executes, a depositor who sees the pending `liquidate` transaction in the mempool (which the current code cannot execute atomically same-block as the underlying borrow, per the `ERR-LIQUIDATION-BORROW-SAME-BLOCK` check, but is otherwise unrestricted with respect to depositor activity) can submit `redeem` with a higher fee to land first, extracting their assets at the pre-loss ratio. Depositors who do not react in time absorb a larger-than-fair share of the socialized bad debt because the numerator (total assets) shrinks while their share of the loss is spread over a smaller remaining pool of shares.

The `liquidate-multi` comment explicitly acknowledges front-running concerns around bad-debt socialization ("Prevents front-running attacks that prevent bad debt socialization") but that protection is scoped to borrower-side manipulation across a batch of positions, not to depositor-side withdrawal races against a pending socialization event: [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation
This directly parallels the referenced report's core mechanic: an unprivileged actor with mempool visibility exits a shared pool immediately before a loss materializes, converting what should be a socialized loss for all pool participants into a loss concentrated on the remaining, non-front-running depositors. This is a temporary freezing/redistribution of unclaimed yield/principal for the victim depositors — value that would otherwise have been fairly distributed is instead retained disproportionately by the front-runner at the expense of the remaining pool participants.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is **Low-to-Medium**: it requires (a) a borrower position deep enough underwater to trigger full collateral seizure and bad-debt socialization, (b) the liquidator's transaction being visible in the mempool before confirmation, and (c) the depositor being able to get a `redeem` transaction ordered ahead of the `liquidate` transaction. Bad-debt events are relatively rare, but when they occur, sophisticated MEV searchers/vault whales are strongly incentivized to race for the exit, and the underlying mechanism (no exit delay, exchange rate lags the loss event) is entirely deterministic and repeatable.

### Recommendation
Consider one or more of: (1) applying interest/loss accrual and any pending socialization checks atomically before allowing redemption in the same block as a liquidation affecting that vault; (2) introducing a short withdrawal delay/queue so that pending bad-debt events cannot be dodged via same-block or same-mempool-window front-running; (3) socializing bad debt proportionally to a snapshot taken before the liquidation transaction is broadcast (e.g., via a two-phase commit or checkpointed index) so exchange rate degradation cannot be avoided by last-moment withdrawal.

### Proof of Concept
1. Vault V (e.g., `vault-usdc`) has depositors Alice and Bob, each holding zUSDC shares worth 500k USDC (`assets` = 1,000,000 USDC total).
2. A borrower's collateral price crashes such that upon liquidation, their sBTC collateral is fully seized but debt remains unpaid — this is destined to hit the `no-collateral-left` branch in `liquidate`, which calls `socialize-debt` on vault V.
3. Bob observes the pending `liquidate` transaction for this borrower in the mempool and immediately submits `redeem` for all his zUSDC shares with a higher fee, landing first in the block.
4. Bob's `redeem` executes against the pre-socialization `assets` value (1,000,000 USDC pool, no loss yet reflected), so he receives his full 500k USDC.
5. `liquidate` then executes, calling `socialize-debt`, which reduces `assets` and writes down `lindex` — since Bob has already exited, the entire loss is now borne solely by Alice's remaining shares, which are devalued disproportionately relative to what pro-rata socialization across both depositors would have produced.

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))
