### Title
Stale Aquarius LP collateral valuation in `flash_position` enables undercollateralized debt minting - (File: contracts/controller/src/strategies/flash_position.rs)

### Summary
`flash_position` caches all relevant oracle prices before minting debt and invoking the attacker-controlled receiver. A receiver can manipulate a listed Aquarius stable-LP collateral’s fair value during the callback, deposit the devalued LP tokens, and still pass final solvency using the stale pre-callback price.

### Finding Description
`process_flash_position` calls `prefetch_strategy_prices` with the debt asset and every declared collateral before the flash-position callback. [1](#0-0) 

`Context::fetch_prices` permanently stores each fetched price, and `cached_price` later returns that stored value instead of querying the aggregator again. [2](#0-1) 

The function then mints the debt and forwards the measured receipt to the receiver before invoking `execute_flash_position`. [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

For an Aquarius stable LP collateral, the aggregator derives its price from the pool’s current reserves, total shares, amplification coefficient, and the two underlying prices. [5](#0-4) 

The stable-LP valuation uses the current invariant `D`, the lower underlying price, and current share supply, so a sufficiently imbalanced pool produces a lower LP price than the balanced state observed before the callback. [6](#0-5) 

After the callback, the controller measures only the token-balance delta and deposits that amount through the normal supply path. [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7) 

`strategy_finalize` then performs the final LTV and health-factor checks with the same `Context`, which still contains the collateral’s pre-manipulation price. [9](#0-8) [10](#0-9) 

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged borrower can open a leveraged account whose real collateral value has fallen below the minted debt while the transaction is still judged solvent under the stale cached price. [11](#0-10) 

The attacker retains the forwarded borrowed assets, while the protocol holds LP collateral whose current fair value is lower than the value used by the final risk check. [12](#0-11) 

This can create bad debt in the borrowed-asset market and ultimately drain suppliers when the insolvent account is liquidated or cleaned up.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attacker only needs a deployed Wasm receiver, a listed Aquarius stable-LP collateral market, enough LP shares or capital to execute the skewing trade, and a flashloanable debt market accepted by `flash_position`. [13](#0-12) 

The callback is intentionally allowed to call external contracts, and the controller reentrancy guard does not prevent the receiver from trading directly on Aquarius. [14](#0-13) 

The required state transition is under the attacker’s control because the receiver can execute the pool trade and then transfer the collateral during the same callback.

### Recommendation
Do not use prices fetched before the receiver callback for final collateral valuation.

After `invoke_receiver` returns, create a fresh `Context` or explicitly invalidate the cached `token_prices` and `market_indexes`, then remeasure collateral, deposit it, and run the final risk checks against post-callback prices and indexes. [15](#0-14) 

For Aquarius LP collateral specifically, the post-callback valuation must re-read reserves, total shares, amplification, and both underlying prices rather than retaining the pre-callback aggregate result. [5](#0-4) 

### Proof of Concept
1. Deploy an attacker-controlled Wasm receiver holding a large balance of a listed Aquarius stable-LP token.
2. Call `Controller::flash_position` with `account_id = 0`, a valid `spoke_id`, `mode = Multiply`, a flashloanable `debt` market, the attacker receiver, `collaterals = [(HubAssetKey { hub_id, asset: lp_token }, minimum)]`, and no refund assets. [16](#0-15) 
3. Before the callback, the controller caches the LP token price while the Aquarius pool is near balance and therefore at a higher stable-LP fair value. [1](#0-0) 
4. The controller mints the requested debt and transfers the measured proceeds to the receiver. [4](#0-3) 
5. Inside `execute_flash_position`, the receiver executes a large trade on the Aquarius stable pool to imbalance its reserves, reducing the invariant-derived fair value of the LP token, then transfers the LP collateral to the controller. [6](#0-5) 
6. The controller measures the controller balance increase and deposits the collateral amount actually received. [7](#0-6) 
7. `strategy_finalize` still evaluates the deposited LP collateral at the cached pre-manipulation price, allowing the new account to pass LTV and health-factor checks despite being undercollateralized at the current pool state. [2](#0-1) [11](#0-10)
