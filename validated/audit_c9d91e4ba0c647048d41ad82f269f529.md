Anchor's `init` account constraint on `amm_config` in `CreateAmmConfig` and on `lp_mint`/`observation_state` in `Initialize`/`InitializeWithPermission`, combined with the explicit ownership check in `create_pool` (`pool_account_info.owner != &system_program::ID` → error), already prevents any pool or config PDA from being initialized more than once — there is no "if state == address(0)" style check to bypass here.
