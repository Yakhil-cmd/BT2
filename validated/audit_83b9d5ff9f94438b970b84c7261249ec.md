### Title
Unprivileged direct sBTC transfers to pox-5 are misclassified as protocol rewards, allowing double-counting/theft of reserve and staker payouts - (File: `stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar`)

### Summary
`pox-5.clar`'s reward accounting mirrors the reported bug class exactly: it derives "new rewards" from the delta between the contract's raw sBTC token balance and its internally tracked liabilities (staked + reserve), rather than from a value that can only change via legitimate protocol yield. Any unprivileged account can call the standard SIP-010 `transfer` on the sBTC token contract to send sBTC directly to the pox-5 contract address, inflating `get-rewards`/`get-new-rewards`, which are then distributed as if they were earned bond/staker yield.

### Finding Description
`get-rewards` computes the distributable pool as the contract's current sBTC balance minus what accounting believes is staked and reserved: [1](#0-0) 

`get-new-rewards` then takes the delta against the last-accounted snapshot: [2](#0-1) 

This gross figure feeds directly into `calculate-rewards`, which uses it to pay out bond yield, credit the reserve, and credit STX-staker rewards-per-token accumulators: [3](#0-2) 

Because `current-balance` in `get-rewards` is read via a plain `get-balance` call on the sBTC token contract, it captures the *entire* SIP-010 balance held by the pox-5 principal, not just balance changes attributable to legitimate bond yield. Since sBTC transfers are open to any caller (any account holding sBTC can call `transfer` to the pox-5 contract address), an unprivileged account can inflate `current-balance` without any corresponding increase in `total-staked-sbtc` tracking. The contract has no mechanism to distinguish "yield that was earned by staked bonds" from "sBTC that just showed up in the balance," exactly the flaw described in the external CollateralManager report, where `totalAssets()` deltas were assumed to be yield when they could also be third-party deposits.

### Impact Explanation
Once such a foreign deposit is absorbed into `get-new-rewards`, `calculate-rewards` distributes it as `gross-accrued-rewards`: a portion is paid to bond holders via `rewards-per-token-for-cycle`, a `RESERVE_RATIO` cut is credited to `reserve-balance`, and the remainder is credited to STX stakers' `cumulative-rewards-per-ustx`. All three of these effects grant *claimable* value (via `claim-rewards`, which performs an actual `as-contract? ... transfer` of sBTC out of the contract) that was never earned by locked stake or signer bond performance. This directly breaks the equality "sBTC rewards paid == sBTC actually earned by locked capital," letting bond/stake holders siphon out sBTC that came from an unrelated deposit rather than yield, i.e., "sBTC rewards paid that were not earned." This matches the specified High severity impact class (theft of reserve/fees, temporary freezing of staked-fund value proportionality, reward slots exceeding locked/earned value).

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is high: sending SIP-010 tokens to an arbitrary principal via `transfer` is a normal, permissionless, single-transaction action requiring no special privilege — the caller just needs to hold sBTC and know the pox-5 contract's principal (public information). No admin, signer, or victim key is required; the attacker/griever does not even need to be a stacker or signer in the protocol. The only cost is the sBTC being "donated" (which is recoverable in most such attacks because the amount deposited would itself be redistributed back partially to bonds the attacker may also hold, or simply griefed against protocol solvency by any third party).

### Recommendation
Track protocol-earned sBTC explicitly rather than deriving it from the raw token balance of the contract:
- Maintain an internal ledger (e.g., a `sbtc-received-as-yield` var or per-bond credited-yield map) that is only incremented through a controlled entry point where legitimate yield sources deposit funds (e.g., a signer/oracle-attested `deposit-yield` call, analogous to tracking vault shares instead of `totalAssets()` in the referenced report).
- Alternatively, if arbitrary transfers-in must be supported, require depositors to call an explicit `fund-rewards` function that increments an internal counter atomically with the SIP-010 transfer, and have `get-rewards`/`get-new-rewards` read from that counter instead of `get-balance`, so that a bare `transfer` call from an unrelated principal cannot be picked up as distributable revenue.

### Proof of Concept
1. Any account `X` (not required to be a signer, staker, or bond holder) holds sBTC and calls `('SM3VDXK3WZZSA84XXFKAFAF15NNZX32CTSG82JFQ4.sbtc-token transfer AMOUNT X pox-5-contract-principal none)`.
2. This raises the raw sBTC balance of pox-5 by `AMOUNT`, with no change to `total-staked-sbtc` or `reserve-balance` (per `get-rewards`, `stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar:2134-2145`).
3. Anyone then calls `calculate-rewards` (`stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar:2158`); `gross-accrued-rewards` includes `AMOUNT` because `get-new-rewards` (line 2147-2156) computed it as part of the delta.
4. `AMOUNT` (minus the reserve cut) is distributed to active bonds' `rewards-per-token-for-cycle` and to `cumulative-rewards-per-ustx` for STX stakers; a `RESERVE_RATIO` slice is credited to `reserve-balance` (lines 2190-2221).
5. Existing bond/stake holders subsequently call `claim-rewards` (`stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar:2387-2420`) and receive an actual sBTC `transfer` out of the contract corresponding to yield they never earned from locked capital — funded by `X`'s unrelated deposit rather than genuine bond performance. (define-public (calculate-rewards (bond-periods (list 6 uint)))
    (let (
            (last-calc (var-get last-reward-compute-height))
            (calculation-height (- (distribution-cycle-to-burn-height (current-distribution-cycle))
                u1
            ))
            (cur-reserve (var-get reserve-balance))
            (gross-accrued-rewards (get-new-rewards))
            (stx-cycle (burn-height-to-reward-cycle calculation-height))
        )
        ;; ensure no reentrancy through signer-manager trait calls
        (try! (validate-no-reentrancy))

        ;; verify that we are able to compute here
        (asserts! (> calculation-height last-calc)
            ERR_DISTRIBUTION_ALREADY_COMPUTED
        )

        ;; Verify that all active bonds are included
        (try! (assert-all-active-bonds-included bond-periods calculation-height))

        (let (
                (bond-distributions (try! (fold calculate-bond-rewards bond-periods
                    (ok {
                        last-bond-stx-value-ratio: none,
                        available-rewards: gross-accrued-rewards,
                        last-bond-index: none,
                        calculation-height: calculation-height,
                        reward-cycle: stx-cycle,
                    })
                )))
                (remaining-rewards (get available-rewards bond-distributions))
                (reserve-cut (/ (* remaining-rewards RESERVE_RATIO) u10000))
                (stx-staker-rewards (- remaining-rewards reserve-cut))
                (cycle-staked-ustx (get-total-shares-staked-for-cycle stx-cycle none))
                (current-rewards-per-ustx (get-rewards-per-token-for-cycle stx-cycle none))
                (prev-accounted-rewards (var-get last-accounted-rewards-only))
                ;; If no STX is staked this cycle, the staker cut will be applied to the reserve.
                (no-stx-stakers (is-eq cycle-staked-ustx u0))
                (accrued-rewards-per-ustx (if no-stx-stakers
                    u0
                    (/ (* stx-staker-rewards PRECISION) cycle-staked-ustx)
                ))
                (cumulative-rewards-per-ustx (+ current-rewards-per-ustx accrued-rewards-per-ustx))
                ;; When no STX is staked, fold the staker cut into the reserve, otherwise zero.
                (unallocated-staker-cut (if no-stx-stakers
                    stx-staker-rewards
                    u0
                ))
                (reserve-deposit (+ reserve-cut unallocated-staker-cut))
                (new-reserve-balance (+ cur-reserve reserve-deposit))
            )
            (var-set reserve-balance new-reserve-balance)
            (var-set last-reward-compute-height calculation-height)
            (var-set last-accounted-rewards-only
                (+ prev-accounted-rewards
                    (- gross-accrued-rewards reserve-deposit)
                ))
            (map-set rewards-per-token-for-cycle {
                reward-cycle: stx-cycle,
                bond-index: none,
            }
                cumulative-rewards-per-ustx
            )
