### Title
AA balance/reserve state can be silently inflated by sub-bounce-fee "dust" transfers, corrupting AMM-style pricing without invoking any case logic - (File: aa_composer.js)

### Summary
Any AA that implements an AMM/market-maker pattern (e.g. the reference `uniswap_like_market_maker.oscript`) derives its exchange rate from `balance[asset]` read live from `aa_balances`. `ocore` credits this balance unconditionally for every payment received by the AA address before any of the AA's `if` cases are evaluated, and when the credited amount is insufficient to cover `bounce_fees`, the engine silently keeps the coins with no case matched and no state/response message emitted, exactly mirroring the reported "asset balance changes without invoking any of the price-relevant hooks" bug class.

### Finding Description
`updateInitialAABalances()` adds `trigger.outputs` to `aa_balances`/`objValidationState.assocBalances` before any `if` condition of the AA is evaluated [3](#0-2) . Immediately after, `handleTrigger` checks whether the received amounts cover `bounce_fees`; if any asset amount is below its configured `bounce_fees[asset]` it calls `bounce()` "silently" per the inline comment `// if not enough asset received to pay for bounce fees, ignore silently` [4](#0-3) . Inside `bounce()`, when the fee exceeds the received amount for a given asset, the loop returns `finish(null)` without ever emitting a bounce-refund message for that asset [5](#0-4) . `finish(null)` then commits the state via `saveStateVars()`/`addResponse()` [6](#0-5)  — there is no compensating `revert()`/`ROLLBACK TO SAVEPOINT initial_balances` call in this branch (that rollback only happens in `revert()`, used for state-update/secondary-trigger errors, at [7](#0-6) ). As a result, the coins already credited by `updateInitialAABalances` remain permanently in the AA's `aa_balances`, without any of the AA's `if` cases (which contain all the AMM's reserve-ratio/price logic, e.g. `$asset_balance = balance[$asset] - trigger.output[[asset=$asset]]` in `uniswap_like_market_maker.oscript`) ever being evaluated for that trigger. Analogous to HydraDX's Omnipool/StableSwap, where a raw asset transfer changes pool reserves without going through the exchange/deposit/withdraw hooks that the EMA oracle listens to, here a raw sub-fee "dust" transfer changes the AA's on-chain balance (the state an AMM-style AA uses as its own price oracle) without going through any of its exchange/deposit/withdraw `if` cases.

### Impact Explanation
Any unprivileged unit poster can post a payment to a live AMM-style AA (constant-product market maker, order book, bonding curve, etc.) with an amount below the AA's configured `bounce_fees` for that asset. This "donation" permanently and silently increases the AA's counted reserve of that asset without matching any pricing case, skewing the constant-product invariant (`$p = $asset_balance * $bytes_balance`) used by subsequent legitimate trades. Because every future trade computes price directly from `balance[asset]`, an attacker can incrementally bias reserves in one direction (repeated dust transfers of the scarcer asset) to manipulate the exchange rate perceived by other users/integrators reading `balance[]` or later trades, enabling profitable arbitrage extraction from the pool or corrupting price data consumed by other AAs (e.g. via `var[other_aa][...]` cross-AA reads) — a direct analog of "changing the AMM price without updating the oracle/hooks" and a path to fund loss for AA users trading against the skewed pool.

### Likelihood Explanation
Exploitation only requires a single ordinary unit payment of an amount smaller than `bounce_fees[asset]` (frequently the protocol default, e.g. `constants.MIN_BYTES_BOUNCE_FEE` for base, or a small custom fee for a token) directed at any deployed AMM-style AA — no special privileges, hub cooperation, or timing dependency are needed, and it can be repeated cheaply to accumulate skew over time.

### Recommendation
When a trigger's outputs are insufficient to cover `bounce_fees` for one or more assets, do not simply keep the credited balance silently; either (a) always route to the AA's normal case-matching/response logic before falling back to fee-insufficiency handling so an author-defined "if" case can decide how to treat sub-threshold amounts, or (b) reject/refund the received asset in full (bounce with underlying reversal via `ROLLBACK TO SAVEPOINT initial_balances`) rather than allowing it to remain as an uncounted-for reserve increase. At minimum, document this behavior loudly for AA authors and provide a standard guard pattern, and consider making the "silent absorption" path explicit in `trigger`/`response` so downstream AAs relying on `balance[asset]` can detect and account for uninitiated deposits.

2. After the pool has liquidity (`var['mm_asset_outstanding']` set), send a plain payment unit with `outputs: [{address: AA, amount: 50}]` in `$asset` (amount `< bounce_fees[$asset]`, and with `base` output also below `bounce_fees.base`, e.g. omit/zero it) — see `handleTrigger`'s check at [8](#0-7)  and the silent-drop branch in `bounce()` at [5](#0-4) .
