### Title
Permissionless `update_account_threshold` lets any address downgrade a foreign account's stored LTV and freeze its withdrawals — ([File: contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs])

### Summary
`update_account_threshold` is callable by any authenticated address for any `account_id` and unconditionally restamps every listed supply position's `loan_to_value` to the currently listed value, with no owner/delegate check and no health-factor gate in `LtvOnly` mode. Because post-action solvency for `borrow` and `withdraw` is proven against LTV-weighted collateral, an attacker who front-runs a governance LTV reduction can force the downgrade onto a victim's account immediately, reverting the victim's withdrawals and borrows.

### Finding Description
The Azuriom report is a missing-authorization bug: a low-privilege authenticated caller reaches a state-changing route on another user's account. The analog is `controller::update_account_threshold`:

- `sync_account_thresholds` loads any `account_id` and calls `refresh_supply_risk_params`, which sets `position.loan_to_value = effective_config.loan_to_value` unconditionally in `LtvOnly` scope (params.rs:34-35). The `favors_liquidator` downgrade guard and the HF >= 1.05 gate only protect the liquidation tuple in `FullTuple` mode (params.rs:76-93, 221-233).
- The only auth is `validation::require_authorized_caller(env, &caller)` on the caller argument (params.rs:130) — no `require_owner_or_delegate` on the target account, unlike `borrow`, `withdraw`, and every strategy entrypoint.
- Accounts keep cached per-position LTV snapshots; governance LTV reductions do not propagate until a refresh. The victim cannot avoid the forced refresh — anyone can submit it, and `restamp_listed_supply_ltv`/the withdraw path do not restore the old value.
- After restamping, the post-pool solvency check in `withdraw`/`borrow` (LTV-weighted collateral must cover debt and meet `min_borrow_collateral_usd`) evaluates against the reduced LTV, so a victim carrying debt against that collateral can have withdrawals and new borrows revert.

The permissionless designation was presumably meant as benign keeper maintenance (the invariants note only that HF does not read LTV), but LTV is read by the solvency gate, so the missing owner authorization is exploitable.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of funds: an attacker can lock a leveraged victim's collateral withdrawals and borrow capacity by forcing an LTV downgrade onto the account the moment a listing's LTV is reduced, before the victim can deleverage or exit under the old snapshot. The freeze persists until the victim repays enough debt to satisfy the lower LTV-weighted coverage — which may itself require selling collateral they can no longer withdraw.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a governance change that lowers a listed asset's LTV (e.g., risk tightening or delisting preparation) and a victim account holding that asset as supply with debt near its coverage limit. The attack transaction is a single permissionless call, costs nothing, and can be front-run against the victim's own refresh/exit. Severity is bounded to Medium: it needs a config change as a precondition and causes temporary, not permanent, freezing.

### Recommendation
Restrict `update_account_threshold` so that `LtvOnly` refreshes that would *decrease* a position's stored `loan_to_value` require the account owner or an active delegate (`require_owner_or_delegate`), or apply a `favors_liquidator`-style direction check for LTV as well: permit permissionless LTV increases, but gate decreases behind the same solvency/authorization treatment used for the liquidation tuple in `apply_gated_liquidation_params`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lowers USDC `loan_to_value` in the victim's spoke from e.g. 80% to 50%.
2. Victim holds USDC supply with debt such that LTV-weighted coverage is adequate at 80% but fails at 50%.
3. Attacker calls `update_account_threshold(caller=attacker, has_risks=false, account_ids=[victim])`. The call succeeds: `caller.require_auth` passes for the attacker's own address, and `refresh_supply_risk_params` writes `loan_to_value = 50%` into the victim's stored position (params.rs:35, 205, 218).
4. Victim's subsequent `withdraw` (and any `borrow`) reverts on the post-pool LTV solvency check, freezing collateral until the victim repays debt.
