### Title
A single dust supply leg with a stale price feed bricks liquidation and bad-debt cleanup for the whole account - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs)

### Summary
The analog of "deleverage reverts for markets in closed hours" is XOXNO Lending's account-wide price requirement: both `liquidate` and the permissionless `clean_bad_debt` compute account risk totals over **every** supply and borrow position via `risk::calculate_account_risk_totals`, which needs a fresh, in-band oracle price for each leg. A borrower can plant a negligible dust supply position in an asset whose feed is liable to go stale; while that feed is stale, the entire safety mechanism reverts — the position cannot be liquidated and bad debt cannot be socialized, even though the toxic leg is economically irrelevant.

### Finding Description
`process_clean_bad_debt` → `clean_bad_debt_standalone` → `socialize_bad_debt` loads the account and calls `risk::calculate_account_risk_totals` over all positions before admitting cleanup [1](#0-0) . The documented invariant confirms the gate: cleanup requires "readable account and NFT state, valid required prices and no active flash guard" — flags and pause do not block it, but a missing/stale price does [2](#0-1) .

The same risk-totals path is used by `liquidate`. The repository's own harness test demonstrates the exploit end-to-end: an underwater borrower supplies 0.001 WBTC (a dust leg) while WBTC's Reflector feed is stale; afterward `try_liquidate` reverts `PRICE_FEED_STALE`, `try_clean_bad_debt_by_id` reverts `PRICE_FEED_STALE`, and even `withdraw` reverts [3](#0-2) . A second test shows the leg is accepted by `supply` despite the stale feed, so planting it is cheap and permissionless [4](#0-3) .

Unlike the HMX fix (allow deleveraging in closed markets), here there is no fallback path that skips the unpriceable leg: liquidation planning needs prices for pro-rata seizure, and socialization needs `total_debt`/`total_collateral` for the dust gate, so the whole flow aborts rather than degrading.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of funds / delayed liquidation leading to protocol insolvency. For the cost of a dust deposit, a borrower gains an option: whenever the planted asset's feed goes stale (a routine oracle event), their account becomes un-liquidatable and un-cleanable. During the blackout window the main collateral can crash further, converting a liquidatable account into deep bad debt that lenders must absorb via supply-index write-down. All lenders in the debt market bear the socialized loss.

### Likelihood Explanation
Low-cost to set up (one dust supply), but requires the chosen feed to actually go stale during the vulnerable window, which is an exogenous event. The attacker can pre-plant legs in the least reliable listed feed and wait. Impact is bounded by how far the account deteriorates during the stale window, so Medium severity is appropriate.

### Recommendation
Decouple the cleanup path from per-leg pricing: let `clean_bad_debt` compute the insolvency gate using the last cached/stored per-position valuations, or treat legs with invalid prices at a conservative floor (e.g., zero collateral value) rather than reverting. For `liquidate`, allow the plan to drop collateral legs whose price is unavailable (they simply cannot be seized) instead of aborting the whole pro-rata liquidation. At minimum, reject new supply into an asset whose feed is currently stale so the poison leg cannot be planted during an outage.

### Proof of Concept
See the existing harness tests, which already encode the attack:

1. `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_supply_stale_shield.rs` — Alice supplies USDC, borrows ETH; after WBTC's feed timestamp is set an hour stale, she supplies 0.001 WBTC (accepted). Crashing USDC makes her liquidatable, but `liquidate`, `clean_bad_debt`, and `withdraw` all revert with `PRICE_FEED_STALE` until the feed recovers.
2. `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs` — same pattern; confirms both safety entrypoints are bricked simultaneously and recover only after `set_price` refreshes the feed.

fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);

### INV-LIQ-04 — Bad-debt socialization is explicit and total

Permissionless cleanup requires ceil risk debt greater than half-up unweighted
collateral and collateral at or below the fixed $5 dust threshold. Owner-only
forced cleanup omits the dust cap. Both require debt, readable account and NFT
state, valid required prices and no active flash guard. Listing flags and
global pause do not block standalone cleanup.

Cleanup reclassifies all remaining collateral shares as revenue and writes off
all remaining debt against each debt's market. It releases spoke usage and
atomically removes account entries and the NFT. It does not net same-market
supply against debt. Standalone cleanup emits `CleanBadDebtEvent` with
pre-cleanup USD totals, without a controller position-update batch.

Ordinary liquidation and cleanup apply no final account-health or full-backing
assertion. The index floor can leave a shortfall. Recapitalization fills that
shortfall without restoring the lost index or deleted account.
