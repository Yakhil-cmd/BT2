### Title
Direct token donations to an Aquarius pool inflate the LP fair-value price, enabling over-borrowing against LP collateral - (File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs)

### Summary
The Aquarius LP price source reads `get_reserves`/`get_total_shares` spot state on every `read` and computes fair value as `2 * sqrt(value_a * value_b) / supply` (constant product) or `D * min(price_a, price_b) / supply` (stable). A permissionless direct `token::transfer` into the Aquarius pool contract raises the observed reserves without minting shares, so the computed LP price rises. Because the controller uses this price in the LTV/health-factor gates for `borrow`/`multiply`/`flash_position`, an attacker holding a large share of the LP token can inflate the collateral valuation, borrow beyond true LTV, then redeem their LP shares to recover the donation in the same transaction.

### Finding Description
`providers::aquarius::read` calls `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` and `aquarius_total_shares_call` at price-read time and feeds raw reserves into `fair_lp_price_wad` / `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` [1](#0-0) . The fair-price formulas are swap-resistant — a swap preserves `k`/`D` — and the test `swap_cannot_move_the_price` proves that [2](#0-1) . But they are not donation-resistant: `fair_lp_price_wad` computes `2 * sqrt(value_a * value_b) / share_supply_wad` [3](#0-2) , so pushing extra `token_a`/`token_b` balances into the pool raises `value_a`/`value_b` while `total_shares` is unchanged — inflating the LP price by `sqrt((r_a+d_a)/r_a)` (or `(1 + d/r)` when both legs are topped up symmetrically), and for stable pools `D` grows roughly linearly with a balanced donation. There is no cap, no TWAP, and no comparison against the pool's own tracked/virtual reserves; the only floor is `min_pool_value_wad`, which a donation satisfies more easily, not less [4](#0-3) .

The controller fetches this price via `Context::fetch_prices` → `cached_price` and feeds it into `calculate_account_risk_totals`, where `ltv_collateral += effective_ltv * value_floor(scaled_amount, supply_index, price)` gates borrowing [5](#0-4) . Since the valuation happens inside the `borrow` invocation while the donated balances are still sitting in the Aquarius pool, the inflated price is what the solvency gate sees.

### Impact Explanation
Theft of user funds / protocol insolvency: the attacker borrows more than their LP collateral is truly worth, withdraws the borrowed assets, then redeems LP shares to recover the donation. The resulting position is undercollateralized at true prices; liquidation cannot recover the excess, so the loss becomes bad debt socialized via `clean_bad_debt` supply-index write-down.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by any unprivileged address with: (1) an Aquarius LP token listed as collateral (`AquariusLp`/`AquariusStableLp` source), (2) enough free LP shares (or shares plus a flash-funded mint) to redeem the donation afterwards, and (3) temporary capital for the donation — obtainable via the protocol's own `flash_loan`/`flash_position`, since the donation only needs to exist during the `borrow`'s price read. Cost scales quadratically for constant-product pools (a `k`-fold price increase needs ~`k²` reserve growth on one leg, or `k` symmetric), so thinner pools with high `loan_to_value` are the realistic targets; required capital is bounded by the pool's TVL, not by the protocol's. This is the same spot-reserve manipulation class as the reference report — the on-chain reserve read is the manipulable trigger — with the twist that fair-value math only removes *swap* manipulation, not balance inflation.

### Recommendation
Sanity-bound the implied reserve value: cross-check `get_reserves` against an expected balanced split derived from the leg prices (reject/clip when `value_a`/`value_b` deviates beyond tolerance, or price the LP as `2 * min(value_a, value_b) / supply` for constant product — matching the stable pool's `min_price` treatment). Alternatively compare the computed LP price against a TWAP/virtual-price reading from the pool, and bound per-block movement of LP-derived prices.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker holds a large fraction `f` of Aquarius pool P's LP shares; P's share token is collateral on spoke S with `loan_to_value` L.
2. In one transaction: `flash_loan` (or own funds) token A; `token::transfer(A → P_pool)` to raise `reserve_a` to `r_a * k²` (or donate both legs to raise each reserve `k`-fold).
3. `supply` a modest amount of LP collateral (or reuse existing) and call `borrow` for asset B: `fetch_prices` reads the inflated reserves, `fair_lp_price_wad` returns ~`k×` the true price, `ltv_collateral` passes a debt of `k·V·L > V`.
4. `withdraw` LP shares (the free ones) from pool P, recovering the donated tokens plus proportional reserves; repay the flash loan.
5. Position now has true collateral `V` and debt `k·V·L` > `V`; attacker keeps the borrowed B. Liquidation seizes only the LP collateral; the deficit is written down via `clean_bad_debt`.
