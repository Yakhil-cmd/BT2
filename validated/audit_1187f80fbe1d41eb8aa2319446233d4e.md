Based on my analysis, this reported bypass does not hold for this codebase.

**Key finding:** `AllExceptReapStash` is used as **both** the XCM `SafeCallFilter` **and** the `frame_system::Config::BaseCallFilter` in the Asset Hub Polkadot runtime: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

`AllExceptReapStash::contains` bars `Staking::reap_stash` regardless of wrapping context, and this is verified by an existing unit test: [3](#0-2) 

**Why the proposed bypass fails:** In the Substrate/polkadot-sdk dispatch machinery, `frame_system::Config::BaseCallFilter::contains(&call)` is checked on *every* invocation of `Dispatchable::dispatch()`, not merely on the top-level extrinsic. `pallet_utility::batch`/`batch_all`/`force_batch`, `Utility::as_derivative`, and `pallet_proxy::proxy` all dispatch their inner calls via `call.dispatch(origin)` (not `dispatch_bypass_filter`), which re-triggers the `BaseCallFilter` check for each nested call independently of the outer call's shape. Because `BaseCallFilter` here is the *same* `AllExceptReapStash` filter, a `Staking::reap_stash` call nested inside `Utility::batch` (or `Proxy::proxy`) will be rejected with `frame_system::Error::CallFiltered` the moment `pallet_utility`/`pallet_proxy` attempts to dispatch that inner call — irrespective of whether the outer `Transact` call passed the `SafeCallFilter` check, and irrespective of the origin (`SovereignAccount`, `Xcm`-derived Signed origin, etc.) produced by `OriginConverter`.

So while `SafeCallFilter` itself is evaluated non-recursively on the outer `Transact` call only, the *second*, independent, and inherently recursive `BaseCallFilter` layer (which happens to enforce the identical `AllExceptReapStash` policy in this runtime) closes the gap. The XCM `Transact` origin conversion does not grant any special bypass of `BaseCallFilter` — only pallets that explicitly call `dispatch_bypass_filter` (e.g., `pallet_sudo`) can skip it, and neither `pallet_utility` nor `pallet_proxy` do so for inner calls.

This is also consistent with the proxy-filter unit tests in this repo, which specifically assert that nested calls via `Utility::as_derivative`/`dispatch_as`/`with_weight` are treated distinctly and validated, showing the runtime authors are aware of nested-call filtering semantics: [4](#0-3)
