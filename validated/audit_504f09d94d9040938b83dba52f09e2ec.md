### Title
Third-party dust supply force-restamps a victim's cached LTV downward, silently cutting borrowing power and freezing withdrawals — ([File: contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs](https://github.com/Ellentat/rs-lending-xlm--012/blob/main/contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs))

### Summary
The "incorrect security UI" class maps onto XOXNO Lending's cached per-position risk stamps: the account presents its stored LTV/LT tuple as its effective security state, but any unprivileged address can overwrite that presentation. `supply` lets a stranger top up an existing supply leg of a foreign account (`INV-AUTH-03`), and `merge_supply_leg` unconditionally restamps the stored `loan_to_value` from the spoke listing — LTV "rides outside the gate" that protects the liquidation tuple. A one-stroop top-up after a governance LTV cut silently applies the new lower LTV to the victim without their auth.

### Finding Description
- `process_supply` only requires the supplied asset to already exist in the victim's supply map (`require_third_party_existing_supply`, supply.rs:78-97); the caller's dust is credited to the victim.
- Every supply write calls `refresh_supply_risk_params`, which restamps `position.loan_to_value` unconditionally — only the LT/bonus/fee tuple is gated by `apply_gated_liquidation_params` and the 1.05 HF floor (risk/params.rs:68-93, and test `regression_supply_skips_bonus_only_raise_below_min_hf` asserting "LTV rides outside the gate").
- The harness PoC `poc_third_party_top_up_force_restamps_ltv` (tests/test-harness/tests/controller/security_audit.rs:507-532) demonstrates it: after governance lowers USDC LTV 8000→5000, `BOB` supplies 1 unit to `ALICE`'s account, her stored LTV drops to 5000, and her subsequent `borrow` reverts with `INSUFFICIENT_COLLATERAL` even though she never interacted with the protocol.
- Because the borrow limit and `min_borrow_collateral_usd` floor are computed from the stored LTV (`get_ltv_collateral_usd`, per `docs/reference/runbooks/liqvid-listing-params.md` lines 367-368), the forced downgrade also blocks `withdraw` for any indebted account whose LTV-weighted collateral now falls below the floor — collateral is frozen until the victim repays debt or governance restores the listing.

### Impact Explanation
The victim's displayed/effective collateral parameters are falsified by a third party: an account that appeared able to borrow or withdraw under its stamped LTV is actually bound by a stranger-installed lower LTV. For accounts with debt near the `min_borrow_collateral_usd` floor this is temporary freezing of funds (withdrawals revert until debt is repaid or params change); otherwise it is a forced, unsolicited reduction of borrowing capacity. Cost to the attacker is one minimum-unit token transfer.

### Likelihood Explanation
Fully permissionless: requires only `supply(caller, victim_account_id, spoke_id, [HubPayment{hub_asset, amount:1}])` after any governance `edit_asset_in_spoke` that lowers LTV — a routine operation (the runbook documents such edits). No timing, price, or health-factor precondition applies to the LTV leg. The attacker loses only dust, which is even credited to the victim's collateral (self-neutralizing cost). Severity is bounded to Medium: tightening of LT/bonus/fee is HF-gated and cannot be forced, and the freeze resolves when debt is repaid or governance re-raises LTV.

### Recommendation
Apply the same `favors_liquidator`-style direction gate to LTV restamps triggered by third-party supplies: skip the LTV restamp in `refresh_supply_risk_params` when `caller` is neither owner nor delegate and the change tightens, or defer LTV restamping to owner-initiated writes and `update_account_threshold`. Alternatively, skip `refresh_supply_risk_params` entirely on the third-party top-up path (the supply adds collateral anyway, so a stale LTV cannot harm the victim).

### Proof of Concept
Existing harness test, already in-repo: `poc_third_party_top_up_force_restamps_ltv` at `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/security_audit.rs:507`.

```rust
t.supply(ALICE, "USDC", 10_000.0);
// governance lowers LTV 8000 -> 5000 (and LT -> 5500)
t.edit_asset_config("USDC", |c| {
    c.loan_to_value = 5_000;
    c.liquidation_threshold = 5_500;
});
// stranger's dust top-up silently restamps the victim's LTV
t.try_supply_to_account(BOB, ALICE, "USDC", 1.0).expect("allowed");
assert_eq!(supply_ltv_and_lt(&t, id, "USDC").0, 5_000);
// victim's own borrow now reverts
assert_contract_error(t.try_borrow(ALICE, "ETH", 3.5), errors::INSUFFICIENT_COLLATERAL);
```

For the freeze variant, set `min_borrow_collateral_usd` above the victim's post-restamp LTV-weighted collateral; the victim's `withdraw` then reverts with `CollateralError::HealthFactorTooLow`/`InsufficientCollateral` until the position is deleveraged.
