## Title
Largest-remainder (Hare-quota) weight apportionment in `pox_5_make_signer_set` lets a staker gain extra reward-slot weight by splitting stake across multiple signer-manager identities, exceeding the weight their aggregate locked STX would receive as a single stake - (File: `stackslib/src/chainstate/nakamoto/signer_set.rs`)

### Summary
`NakamotoSigners::pox_5_make_signer_set` allocates the fixed pool of `reward_slots` via `floor(stacked_amt / threshold)` plus a "largest fractional remainder" leftover round, applied independently per `signer_key` bucket rather than per staker's total locked value. Because floor-division is sub-additive across a split (`floor(a/t)+floor(b/t) <= floor((a+b)/t)`) while each half retains a large *independent* remainder, a staker who spreads the same total STX across two signer-manager identities can rank higher in the leftover round than a single consolidated stake would, capturing a reward slot that would otherwise go to another honest staker.

### Finding Description
The broken equality is:

`total_weight(staker, split across N signer_keys) == floor(total_ustx_locked_by_staker / threshold)` (mod the same tie-break rule that would apply to one consolidated entry)

`pox_5_make_signer_set` aggregates locked STX per `signer_key` (not per staker) at [1](#0-0) , then computes, per bucket independently:

```
weight = stacked_amt / threshold
remainder = stacked_amt % threshold
``` [2](#0-1) 

and hands out the `leftover = reward_slots - assigned` slots to the buckets with the largest remainders, sorted descending, ties broken by key [3](#0-2) .

An attacker deploys two distinct signer-manager contracts (each independently `register-signer`'d, giving two distinct `signer_key`s), then calls `stake` twice from accounts they control - once per signer-manager - splitting a given total into two halves. Each `stake` call is a normal, unprivileged public entry point of `pox-5.clar` [4](#0-3) ; the resulting per-signer totals are read back by `pox_5_stake_entries` via `get-amount-delegated-for-signer` and fed as two separate `RawPox5Entry` buckets into `pox_5_make_signer_set` [5](#0-4) .

**Worked example** (threshold=100, reward_slots=4, total system-locked=400 fixed by three other participants + attacker):
- Unsplit: Attacker A=150 (base=1, remainder=50), Competitor C=151 (base=1, remainder=51), D=99 (base=0, remainder=99). base sum=2, leftover=2. Ranking by remainder: D(99) > C(51) > A(50) - the top 2 (D, C) win the leftover slots. **Attacker's final weight = 1** (loses the leftover round outright, no tie-break needed).
- Split: Attacker splits into A1=75, A2=75 (each base=0, remainder=75). base sum=1 (C only), leftover=3. Ranking: D(99) > A1(75)=A2(75) > C(51) - top 3 (D, A1, A2) win. **Attacker's final weight = A1+A2 = 2**, while C's weight drops from 2 to 1.

The same 150 uSTX locked by the attacker yields weight 1 unsplit and weight 2 split - a full extra reward slot manufactured purely by bucket fragmentation, at the direct expense of honest staker C's rightful slot. Total assigned weight is still conserved at `reward_slots` system-wide, so this is not an inflation of the total pool, but a redistribution that lets the attacker's per-locked-STX signing weight exceed the value locked while another honest staker's weight is diluted below its fair share.

None of `verify-not-prepare-phase`, `signer-manager-call-active`/reentrancy guards, `check-pox-lock-period`, or `verify-signer-key-grant` touch this: those guard staking mechanics and reentrancy, not the cross-bucket rounding fairness of `pox_5_make_signer_set`. The existing property tests in `stackslib/src/chainstate/nakamoto/tests/signer_set.rs` (`check_make_signer_set`) only assert global conservation (`total_weight == base + min(leftover, N)`) and per-entry weight bounds; they never compare a staker's split-bucket total against the equivalent single-entry allocation, so this split-to-gain-a-slot behavior is unguarded and untested. [6](#0-5) 

### Impact Explanation
The attacker gains an extra reward-slot's worth of signing weight (and therefore a proportional share of sBTC rewards paid per reward slot) without locking any additional STX, at the expense of another honest staker whose bucket loses the leftover-round competition it would otherwise have won. This matches the in-scope High-impact category "signing weight or reward slots exceeding locked value." It is fully repeatable every reward cycle for any staker willing to operate two (or more) signer-manager identities, and scales: with `k` signer identities an attacker can craft `k` moderate remainders to outcompete fewer, larger-remainder honest buckets for multiple leftover slots simultaneously.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are trivial and fully within an unprivileged attacker's control: register two signer-manager contracts (any account can do this), fund two accounts (self + a second address they control), and call `stake` outside the prepare phase for each. No coordination with the bond admin, pause admin, or victim is required. The attack's success depends on the remainder landscape of the other participants in a given cycle (as shown, only specific remainder relationships trigger a net gain), but an attacker choosing their own split amounts can often engineer favorable remainders adaptively across cycles, and even when it fails to gain a slot it never costs the attacker anything beyond normal staking of their own funds.

### Recommendation
Aggregate `total_ustx_locked` per staker (not per `signer_key` bucket) before computing `weight`/`remainder`, or equivalently compute the largest-remainder apportionment at the staker level and then sum staker weights that share a `signer_key`. Alternatively, replace the Hare/largest-remainder method with a split-proof monotonic apportionment method (e.g., a highest-averages/divisor method such as Jefferson/D'Hondt) which is provably immune to this "list-splitting" manipulation.

### Proof of Concept
Add a deterministic unit test to `stackslib/src/chainstate/nakamoto/tests/signer_set.rs` alongside the existing tests:

```rust
#[test]
fn splitting_stake_across_signer_keys_can_gain_a_slot() {
    let pox_constants = test_pox_constants(1); // reward_slots() == 4
    assert_eq!(pox_constants.reward_slots(), 4);

    // Fixed background so total_ustx_locked == 400 and threshold == 100 in both cases.
    let c = signer_key(0xC0);
    let d = signer_key(0xD0);

    // Case A: attacker stakes as ONE signer_key.
    let attacker_single = signer_key(0xA0);
    let entries_unsplit = vec![
        RawPox5Entry { signer_key: attacker_single, amount_ustx: 150 },
        RawPox5Entry { signer_key: c, amount_ustx: 151 },
        RawPox5Entry { signer_key: d, amount_ustx: 99 },
    ];
    let mut iter = entries_unsplit.into_iter().map(Ok);
    let unsplit = NakamotoSigners::pox_5_make_signer_set(&mut iter, &pox_constants).unwrap();
    let attacker_weight_unsplit: u128 = unsplit.signer_set.iter()
        .filter(|e| e.signing_key == attacker_single)
        .map(|e| u128::from(e.weight)).sum();
    assert_eq!(attacker_weight_unsplit, 1);

    // Case B: SAME attacker, SAME 150 uSTX, split across two signer-manager identities.
    let a1 = signer_key(0xA1);
    let a2 = signer_key(0xA2);
    let entries_split = vec![
        RawPox5Entry { signer_key: a1, amount_ustx: 75 },
        RawPox5Entry { signer_key: a2, amount_ustx: 75 },
        RawPox5Entry { signer_key: c, amount_ustx: 151 },
        RawPox5Entry { signer_key: d, amount_ustx: 99 },
    ];
    let mut iter2 = entries_split.into_iter().map(Ok);
    let split = NakamotoSigners::pox_5_make_signer_set(&mut iter2, &pox_constants).unwrap();
    let attacker_weight_split: u128 = split.signer_set.iter()
        .filter(|e| e.signing_key == a1 || e.signing_key == a2)
        .map(|e| u128::from(e.weight)).sum();

    // BROKEN EQUALITY: same 150 uSTX locked, but split yields strictly MORE weight.
    assert_eq!(attacker_weight_split, 2, "splitting doubled the attacker's weight for the same locked STX");
    assert!(attacker_weight_split > attacker_weight_unsplit,
        "attacker gained a reward slot purely by splitting stake across signer identities, \
         violating weight-conservation relative to locked value");

    // Corroborate the loser: honest staker `c` loses a slot it would have won unsplit.
    let c_weight_unsplit = unsplit.signer_set.iter().find(|e| e.signing_key == c).unwrap().weight;
    let c_weight_split = split.signer_set.iter().find(|e| e.signing_key == c).unwrap().weight;
    assert_eq!(c_weight_unsplit, 2);
    assert_eq!(c_weight_split, 1);
}
```

Both assertions (`attacker_weight_split == 2 > attacker_weight_unsplit == 1`, and `c`'s weight dropping from 2 to 1) demonstrate the reward-slot weight/locked-value equality is broken by bucket splitting, confirming the vulnerability against the existing `pox_5_make_signer_set` implementation.
