### Title
Front-running `calculate-rewards` lets a staker capture accrued sBTC/STX rewards for a period they were not staked - (File: stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar)

### Summary
`calculate-rewards` distributes rewards accrued since the last computation (`gross-accrued-rewards`) across stakers using the *current* total staked amount read at call time, rather than a value snapshotted at the start of the accrual window. A staker can front-run a `calculate-rewards` transaction (or the sBTC transfer that funds it) by staking immediately beforehand, diluting the pool's total shares and capturing a share of rewards that accrued before they ever staked, at the expense of stakers who were locked in for the whole period.

### Finding Description
`calculate-rewards` computes rewards for the STX-staker pool as: [1](#0-0) 

`cycle-staked-ustx` is fetched via `get-total-shares-staked-for-cycle stx-cycle none`, which reflects the total staked **at the moment `calculate-rewards` executes**, not the amount staked during the accrual window (since the last `last-reward-compute-height`). The accrued-rewards-per-token increment `accrued-rewards-per-ustx` is then computed by dividing `stx-staker-rewards` (rewards accrued over the whole elapsed period) by this current total, and added into the cumulative `rewards-per-token-for-cycle` accumulator: [2](#0-1) .

Because staking settles the staker's `rewards-per-token-paid` at the *current* cumulative rpt value before adding their new shares to the pool (as shown in `add-staker-to-signer-for-cycle`, which calls `settle-rewards` and `settle-staker-rewards` prior to updating `signer-shares-staked-for-cycle`), [3](#0-2) , a newly-joined staker's baseline is set just before the rpt jump that `calculate-rewards` is about to apply. When `calculate-rewards` runs immediately after, the new staker's shares are already counted in `cycle-staked-ustx`, so they receive a full pro-rata cut of `accrued-rewards-per-ustx` on the *entire* period's accrued rewards — even though they only staked for one block of that period. This dilutes the earned rewards of stakers who held their position for the whole window.

This is the direct analog of the External Report's staking-contract front-run: a party can observe an imminent, profitable state transition (here, an sBTC transfer into the contract followed by `calculate-rewards`) in the mempool and insert a stake transaction ahead of it to harvest rewards proportional to funds it never had locked during the accrual period.

### Impact Explanation
Every reward realization event (a `calculate-rewards` call following an sBTC transfer into the contract) is subject to this dilution attack. Honest long-term stakers receive fewer sBTC/STX rewards than they actually earned, while the front-runner captures value without having borne risk or lockup for the accrual period. This is a temporary/permanent misallocation of already-accrued staking rewards among participants — a fairness and reward-integrity break, though it does not create new tokens or permanently freeze funds; it redistributes already-realized rewards unfairly.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is Low-Medium: it requires monitoring the mempool for sBTC reward transfers/`calculate-rewards` calls, which are visible transactions on Stacks. Because Stacks lacks a private mempool/MEV auction comparable to Ethereum, an attacker capable of quickly submitting a `stake` transaction ahead of a known `calculate-rewards` call (e.g., anticipating routine reward funding) could repeat this every reward cycle for consistent, low-risk skimming.

### Recommendation
Compute `cycle-staked-ustx` from a snapshot taken at the start of the accrual window (i.e., total shares as of `last-reward-compute-height`) instead of the live total at `calculate-rewards` call time, or accrue rewards using a time-weighted average of staked shares over the period, so that stakers only earn rewards proportional to the time and amount they actually had staked.

### Proof of Concept
1. `Staking` cycle has an existing pool of stakers with total `cycle-staked-ustx = X` uSTX staked since the start of the accrual window.
2. sBTC rewards accumulate in the pox-5 contract balance (`get-new-rewards` growing) — visible to anyone watching contract balance / pending transfer transactions.
3. An observer sees an imminent `calculate-rewards` call (or the sBTC funding transfer preceding it) in the mempool.
4. The observer front-runs with a `stake` call for a large amount `Y`, which calls `add-staker-to-signer-for-cycle`, settling their rpt baseline at the pre-distribution rpt value [3](#0-2) .
5. `calculate-rewards` executes, computing `accrued-rewards-per-ustx = stx-staker-rewards * PRECISION / (X + Y)` [4](#0-3) , diluting the per-token reward that the original `X` stakers would have received, while the front-runner immediately becomes eligible for `Y`'s pro-rata share of the whole period's `stx-staker-rewards`.
6. The observer can then unstake or repeat this each reward-calculation cycle.
