### Title
Reward rounding dust from `calculate-rewards` is permanently locked in the pox-5 contract - (File: stackslib/src/chainstate/stacks/boot/pox-5.clar)

### Summary
`calculate-rewards` computes `accrued-rewards-per-ustx` (and, in `calculate-bond-rewards`, `accrued-rewards-per-sat`) via integer division against `cycle-staked-ustx` / `total-sats`. Whenever `stx-staker-rewards * PRECISION` (or `earned * PRECISION`) does not divide evenly, the remainder is silently dropped from the per-token accumulator. However, the *full* `stx-staker-rewards` amount (not just the rounded-down portion actually reflected in the accumulator) is folded into `last-accounted-rewards-only`, which is the value subtracted from the live sBTC balance to compute future `get-new-rewards`. This is the same class of bug as Ethos Finance H-03 (LQTYStaking `F_Collateral` truncation), just moved from a `.mul(DECIMAL_PRECISION).div(totalStaked)` collateral-fee accumulator to pox-5's sBTC-per-uSTX/per-sat reward accumulator.

### Finding Description
In `calculate-rewards`: [1](#0-0) 

`accrued-rewards-per-ustx` is `(/ (* stx-staker-rewards PRECISION) cycle-staked-ustx)`, truncated by integer division. The leftover remainder `stx-staker-rewards mod (cycle-staked-ustx/PRECISION-adjusted)` is never captured anywhere — there is no error-carry variable analogous to Liquity's `lastLQTYError`. Yet `last-accounted-rewards-only` is advanced by `(gross-accrued-rewards - reserve-deposit)`, i.e., the entire `stx-staker-rewards` figure is marked "already accounted for" regardless of how much of it actually made it into `cumulative-rewards-per-ustx`.

The same pattern repeats in `calculate-bond-rewards` for `accrued-rewards-per-sat`: [2](#0-1) 

Here `earned` (the bond's target yield, capped by `available-rewards`) is fully subtracted from `available-rewards` in the fold regardless of the truncation in `accrued-rewards-per-sat = (/ (* earned PRECISION) total-sats)`.

Downstream, `get-rewards` / `get-new-rewards` compute future distributable rewards strictly as `current-balance - total-staked-sbtc - reserve-balance - last-accounted-rewards-only`: [3](#0-2) 

Once `last-accounted-rewards-only` has absorbed the untruncated `stx-staker-rewards`/`earned` figures, the truncation remainder becomes indistinguishable from sBTC that was legitimately already paid out or reserved — it can never again surface as "new rewards" in a future `calculate-rewards` call, and no staker's `rewards-per-token-for-cycle` accumulator reflects it. It sits in the contract's raw sBTC balance forever, uncredited and unclaimable, because `get-rewards`/`get-new-rewards` treat it as spoken-for.

### Impact Explanation
This breaks the equality "sBTC actually held for future distribution == sBTC that `last-accounted-rewards-only` says is disbursed/reserved." Any residual dust from the division is a case of sBTC reward funds being permanently frozen in the pox-5 contract: it is neither returned to the reserve, nor credited to any staker/signer's accumulator, nor recoverable by any subsequent `calculate-rewards` call (since the contract's bookkeeping already marked it as accounted for). This matches the report's "Critical — permanent freezing of ... sBTC rewards" impact category, though the magnitude per call is bounded by `PRECISION / max(cycle-staked-ustx, total-sats)` sats rather than large absolute amounts (uSTX/sats denominators are integers, not high-decimal WBTC-style tokens), so in practice the per-call loss is at most a few sats/µSTX-worth of sBTC, but it compounds over every `calculate-rewards` invocation (potentially every half-cycle, indefinitely) and across every active bond.

### Likelihood Explanation
This triggers automatically on essentially every `calculate-rewards` call whenever the division isn't exact — no attacker action or privileged access is required, and it doesn't depend on any adversarial input; it's a deterministic accounting/rounding defect inherent to the formulas at [4](#0-3)  and [5](#0-4) . Because `calculate-rewards` is callable by anyone (per its docstring, "Callable once per half-cycle"), a caller need only invoke it repeatedly across cycles for the dust to accumulate; no special condition beyond a non-exact division (the common case) is needed.

### Recommendation
Introduce an error-carry state variable per accumulator (analogous to Liquity's `lastLQTYError`) that stores the truncated remainder of `stx-staker-rewards * PRECISION` (and similarly for bond `earned * PRECISION`) and adds it back into the numerator on the next `calculate-rewards` call before dividing, so the remainder is retried rather than lost. Alternatively, only advance `last-accounted-rewards-only` by the amount that was actually reflected in the per-token accumulator (i.e., `(accrued-rewards-per-ustx * cycle-staked-ustx) / PRECISION`, plus the equivalent bond-side reconciliation), leaving the true remainder available to be picked up as "new rewards" in the following call.

### Proof of Concept
Given `PRECISION` as pox-5's fixed-point scale and a cycle where:
1. `cycle-staked-ustx` = some value `N` such that `stx-staker-rewards * PRECISION` is not an exact multiple of `N` (trivially true for almost any nonzero `stx-staker-rewards`),
2. `accrued-rewards-per-ustx = (stx-staker-rewards * PRECISION) / N` truncates, dropping remainder `r = (stx-staker-rewards * PRECISION) mod N`,
3. `last-accounted-rewards-only` is nonetheless incremented by the *full* `stx-staker-rewards` (via `gross-accrued-rewards - reserve-deposit` in [6](#0-5) ), so `r/PRECISION` sats worth of sBTC become permanently untracked by any staker accumulator or reserve topping-up path, while remaining in the contract's live sBTC balance forever.

Repeating `calculate-rewards` across many half-cycles accumulates this dust without bound, and it can never be reclaimed since `get-new-rewards` only reports balance beyond `total-staked-sbtc + reserve-balance + last-accounted-rewards-only`, and the dust is already counted inside `last-accounted-rewards-only`.
