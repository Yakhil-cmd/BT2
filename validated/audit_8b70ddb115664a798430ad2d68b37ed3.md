Searching the codebase for signature-replay patterns analogous to the `ChainlinkAtlasWrapper.transmit()` issue shows that every comparable "signed report" surface in `polkadot-sdk` already enforces single-use / staleness checks:

- BABE and GRANDPA equivocation reports explicitly reject re-submission via `InvalidTransaction::Stale` and `Error::<T>::DuplicateOffenceReport` [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) .
- BEEFY double-voting reports are similarly rejected as stale on replay, verified by dedicated tests [3](#0-2) .
- Disputes/slashing reports reject duplicate reports with `Error::<T>::DuplicateSlashingReport` [4](#0-3) .
- Permit-style signature verification in `pallet-permit`/precompiles increments a nonce on use and rejects any replay of the same `(v, r, s)` because the digest recomputed with the new nonce no longer matches [5](#0-4) .
- Bitfield/statement/approval distribution subsystems also track per-validator/per-fingerprint "already seen" state to reject duplicate signed messages [6](#0-5) .

No file in the repository implements a `ChainlinkAtlasWrapper`-like contract or an unsigned/permissionless "trusted transmitter" report-submission entry point lacking such single-use enforcement, so there is no demonstrable analog of the reported EVM issue in this FRAME/Substrate codebase.
