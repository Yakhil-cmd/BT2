### Title
Divide-by-Zero Panic in `consolidatedNode.Split` Crashes Proposer Node When Both CN and CL Staking Amounts Are Zero — (`kaiax/staking/staking_info.go`)

---

### Summary

`consolidatedNode.Split` divides by `totalAmount = c.StakingAmount + c.CLStakingInfo.CLStakingAmount` with no zero guard. When a block proposer has both staking amounts rounded down to 0 KAIA (each < 1 KAIA in wei), `totalAmount == 0` and Go's `big.Int.Div` panics. The panic propagates unrecovered through `FinalizeState`, crashing the proposer's node and aborting block production for that round.
