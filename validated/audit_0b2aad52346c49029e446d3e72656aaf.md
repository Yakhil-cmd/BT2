### Title
Permissionless `repay` can push an underwater account into the whole-unit "margin band" where every liquidation offer reverts - ([File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs](contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs))

### Summary
The GitLab report is a denial-of-service class bug: a publicly reachable action makes a resource unusable. The analog in XOXNO Lending is the permissionless `repay` entrypoint combined with the whole-unit collateral leg logic in `whole_unit_repayment`. Any address may repay part of any account's debt. For an account whose only supply position is a sub-3-decimal asset holding at least one whole unit, there is a narrow debt band, `floor(U / (1 + b)) - R < D <= ceil((U + m) / (1 + b))`, in which no liquidation quote applies: rule 1 (full close) and rule 2 (raise to one unit) both miss, so `whole_unit_repayment` returns the unraised curve quote, which backs less than one whole unit. The plan then seizes nothing and `liquidate` reverts with `InvalidPayments`. A griefer can park a victim's debt inside this band with a single `repay` call, after which every liquidation attempt on the account reverts until interest accrual or a price move pushes `D` out of the band.

### Finding Description
- `controller::repay` is permissionless: "Anyone may repay any account's debt... the target's liabilities can only fall" (`scripts/permissionless_entrypoints.txt`, INV-AUTH-03). The payer funds the repayment from their own balance.
- `whole_unit_repayment` in `contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs:235-290` applies only when the account has exactly one supply position in an asset with `asset_decimals < MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS` holding `held_units >= 1`. It returns `snap.total_debt` only when `unit_at_bonus >= D + R`, returns the raised `unit_repayment` only when `unit_repayment < D`, and otherwise leaves the curve quote unchanged (lines 275-289).
- When `D` sits in the gap `unit_at_bonus - R < D <= unit_repayment` and the curve quote backs less than one whole unit, the seizure rounds to zero whole units, the plan has no seizure leg, and `liquidate` reverts with `InvalidPayments` (docs/reference/formulas.md:294-308; INV-LIQ-02). The band is confirmed executable by `tests/test-harness/tests/fuzz/whole_unit_liquidation.rs:1289-1311`, where offers of 1%, 100% and 300% of the debt all revert until a day of accrual moves `D` out of the band.
- Because `repay` accepts arbitrary partial amounts against any account, an attacker can compute the band bounds off-chain (`U` from the oracle price, `b` from the bonus curve, `R` from debt-leg decimals) and repay exactly `D_orig - D_band` to land the victim's debt inside it in one call.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of funds / impaired liquidation. While `D` is in the band, every `liquidate` call on the account reverts regardless of offer size, SeizeMode, or liquidator. The account is unhealthy (HF < 1 is compatible with the band, e.g. one unit with LT ~50%), so the protocol cannot de-risk it and accruing debt drifts toward insolvency. `clean_bad_debt` does not help: it requires `total_collateral <= $5`, which fails while the account still holds a whole unit. Recovery is not permissionless on demand — the band ends only when accrual or a price move pushes `D` above `raised`, and a fresh price can also re-enter the band. The attack is griefing-priced (the attacker donates a partial repayment to the victim) but cheap relative to the protocol damage on a thin-collateral account.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a listed collateral asset below 3 decimals (the codebase contemplates these: `MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS` gating, whole-unit leg tests, zero-decimal collateral suite) and an underwater account whose sole supply leg is that asset — forced by the design, since such a leg must be the account's only supply position. Preconditions (liquidatable account on a low-decimal listing) are externally visible; execution is one `repay` call by any address. Cost is the partial repayment amount, so this is a griefing vector, not profit-extraction — consistent with Medium.

### Recommendation
When the curve quote backs less than one whole unit and rules 1-2 do not apply, promote the quote to `snap.total_debt` (full close) rather than leaving it unchanged: the whole-unit rounding on full repayment already takes the held units, so a debt-sized offer would settle instead of reverting. Alternatively, treat `unit_repayment >= snap.total_debt` as the full-close case when `held_units >= 1`, since the seized unit covers `D` at the cap bonus.

### Proof of Concept
1. Victim account: supplies `k` whole units of a listed sub-3-decimal asset `L` (only supply position, as required), borrows debt asset `X` so that `D` is just above `U/(1+b)` — account liquidatable, curve quote backs `< 1` unit.
2. Attacker (any address) computes band bounds and calls `controller::repay(attacker, victim_id, [(hub_asset_X, D_orig - D_target)])` so the residual `D_target` satisfies `floor(U/(1+b)) - R < D_target <= ceil((U+m)/(1+b))` while `HF` remains `< 1`.
3. Any liquidator calls `controller::liquidate(liq, victim_id, payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)` with any offer — `whole_unit_repayment` leaves the quote (math.rs:287-289), seizure rounds to zero units, and the call reverts `InvalidPayments`.
4. `controller::clean_bad_debt` reverts `CannotCleanBadDebt` — collateral `> $5`.
5. The account stays frozen until accrual raises `D` past the band (mirroring `wul_two_units_inside_the_margin_band_refuse_every_offer_until_accrual`, `tests/test-harness/tests/fuzz/whole_unit_liquidation.rs:1289`).
