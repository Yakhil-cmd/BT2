### Title
A flash-loan initiator can force an approved receiver contract to pay the fee - (File: contracts/pool/src/ops/flash.rs)

### Summary
`Controller::flash_loan` authenticates only the initiator while accepting an arbitrary WASM `receiver`; the pool then pays that receiver, invokes its callback, and pulls `amount + fee` from the receiver through token allowance. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

### Finding Description
`controller::flash_loan(caller, asset, amount, receiver, data)` requires `caller.require_auth()` and checks that `receiver` is a WASM contract, but it never requires `receiver == caller`, `receiver.require_auth()`, or receiver consent to the selected loan. [3](#0-2) 

The pool transfers `amount` to `receiver`, invokes `execute_flash_loan`, verifies that its allowance covers `amount + fee`, and executes `asset.transfer_from(pool, receiver, pool, total_repayment)`. [2](#0-1) [4](#0-3) 

Although the principal is first delivered to the receiver, the final pull is `principal + fee`; a successful no-op callback therefore leaves the receiver exactly `fee` poorer when it already has sufficient allowance for the pool. [5](#0-4) [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged initiator can force a compatible receiver contract to spend an existing token allowance on an unsolicited flash-loan fee, causing direct loss of user or integration-contract funds. [4](#0-3) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack is permissionless because `flash_loan` only authenticates the caller and does not authenticate the receiver. [6](#0-5) 

It requires a flash-loanable market with reserves, a WASM receiver whose callback completes, and receiver allowance at least equal to `amount + fee`; the allowance may be pre-existing or created by the receiver's own callback. [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7) [4](#0-3) 

### Recommendation
Bind the loan to the paying receiver by requiring `receiver == caller` or by requiring explicit receiver authorization for the flash-loan initiation, rather than relying on a callback that may not validate `initiator`. [3](#0-2) [9](#0-8) 

### Proof of Concept
1. Victim contract `V` implements a benign `execute_flash_loan` callback and has approved the pool for at least `amount + fee` of a flash-loanable asset. [10](#0-9) [4](#0-3) 
2. Attacker calls `controller::flash_loan(attacker, hub_asset, amount, V, data)` and signs only as `attacker`. [11](#0-10) [6](#0-5) 
3. The pool transfers `amount` to `V`, invokes `V.execute_flash_loan(attacker, asset, amount, fee, pool, data)`, and then pulls `amount + fee` from `V` using the allowance. [2](#0-1) [4](#0-3) 
4. After the transaction, `V` retains no principal and has paid the flash-loan fee despite never authorizing initiation of that loan. [5](#0-4) [4](#0-3)

            pool.clone(),

    );
