Based on tracing the exact code path, the premise of the question is factually incorrect.

**The equality claimed to break:** `total-sbtc-staked == sum of physically custodied sBTC across all live memberships`.

**Why it does not break:**

`unstake-sbtc` in [1](#0-0)  computes `num-cycles = bond-end-cycle - first-changed-reward-cycle`, i.e. it rebases the drained bond's per-cycle shares from the *current* cycle all the way through to the bond's *end*, not just the current cycle. The subsequent writes at [2](#0-1)  zero out `protocol-bonds-total-staked` for that bond-index and decrement `total-sbtc-staked` by the full withdrawn amount for a full unstake, immediately and for the entire remaining bond term — not just the present cycle. This is confirmed by the test `'sbtc bond participant can fully unstake and stops earning bond rewards'`, which asserts `getTotalSbtcStaked()` is `0n` and `getEarned(signer, 1n, 0n)` is `0n` after a full unstake [3](#0-2) .

When the attacker then calls `register-for-bond` for a later non-overlapping bond, `old-sbtc` is read via `get-staker-custodied-sbtc`, which reads `amount-sats` straight from the (already-zeroed) old membership [4](#0-3) . Since `old-sbtc` is `0`, `roll-sbtc` requires the attacker to deposit the *entire* new amount as fresh sBTC (no netting), and the new bond's `protocol-bonds-total-staked` / `total-sbtc-staked` are incremented by exactly that fresh deposit [5](#0-4) . The `bond-overlaps-new-position?` gate that permits this second registration only checks reward-cycle ranges, not `amount-sats`, so it correctly allows a non-overlapping rollover regardless of whether the old bond's sats were already withdrawn [6](#0-5) .

The cited test `'register-for-bond after old bond expires nets sBTC forward'` demonstrates exactly this: `getTotalSbtcStaked()` stays consistent at every step (`sbtcAmount` → `sbtcAmount` after rollover with equal amounts and no transfer → `0` after the final `unstake-sbtc`) [7](#0-6) . The only residual state is that the staker's STX remains locked through the new bond's unlock height with `0` sBTC backing that particular bond membership [8](#0-7)  — but reward/share weight in this system is driven by `amount-sats` (bond shares), not by the STX lock amount, so a locked-STX/zero-sats state does not inflate any reward-share weight or double-count a commitment; it is simply an STX-lock-duration side effect, out of scope for this question's claimed equality.

No path was found where the old bond's `protocol-bonds-total-staked` or per-cycle shares remain nonzero after a full `unstake-sbtc` while `total-sbtc-staked` also credits a new bond for the same custodied sBTC.

### No Vulnerability found for this question.
