### Title
Accrual `scaled_to_original` RAY-value overflow permanently freezes a whale market - (contracts/pool/src/cache/scale.rs)

### Summary
The `binutils` heap over-read in `d_expression_1` is a crafted-input crash: an input shape the parser never expected drives it into an unrecoverable fault. The lending analog is the market-accrual path in `contracts/pool/src/cache/scale.rs`: `calculate_utilization` (and every unscale helper) calls `scaled_to_original(scaled, index)`, which multiplies RAY-scaled shares by a RAY index and panics with `MathOverflow` when the product exceeds `i128::MAX`. Because every controller verb accrues the market first, once `scaled * borrow_index` crosses the `i128` ceiling the market is permanently frozen — no supply, borrow, repay, withdraw, or liquidation can execute — locking all supplier funds. The overflow happens before the `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` cap can engage, so the cap does not protect it.

### Finding Description
`Cache::calculate_utilization` unscales `self.borrowed` and `self.supplied` via `scaled_to_original` (`contracts/pool/src/cache/scale.rs:23-24`), and `unscale_supply`/`unscale_borrow_ceil` do the same for positions (`scale.rs:50-87`). `scaled_to_original` (in `common::rates`) computes `scaled_shares * index / RAY` and panics through `mul_div_*` → `GenericError::MathOverflow` when `scaled * index` overflows `i128` (`common/src/math/fp_core.rs:108-159`). Scaled share counts grow with `amount * RAY / index`, so a market holding ~`i128::MAX / index` asset units of scaled debt — reachable at ~1e27 raw units on an 18-decimal market after ~170x index growth — drives the product past `i128::MAX`. The integration test `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` in `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:316-361` confirms: `update_indexes` fails with `MATH_OVERFLOW` while `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`, and subsequent `withdraw` and `repay` hit the same panic because accrual runs before every op.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of funds: once the RAY-value ceiling is crossed, every verb that touches the market reverts inside accrual, so suppliers cannot withdraw and borrowers cannot repay — all supplied tokens in that (hub, token) book are stuck forever. Unlike a fail-closed revert on a bad single input, here no valid input avoids the panic, so this is a protocol-level freeze, not user error.

### Likelihood Explanation
Fully unprivileged: the trigger is `supply`/`borrow` with very large amounts plus time at high utilization. It requires whale-scale capital (test uses ~1e27 raw units of an 18-decimal asset and ~98% utilization for multiple years), so it is economically heavy but requires no privilege, no leaked key, and no oracle manipulation. Medium likelihood at best; severity tracks Medium like the source advisory.

### Recommendation
Bound accrual so `scaled * index` cannot overflow `i128`: e.g. engage `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`/`MAX_SUPPLY_INDEX_RAY` before the value ceiling (clamp the index rather than panic), or route `scaled_to_original`/`unscale_*` through the `I256` widening path and saturate the index, so the market freezes the rate instead of freezing the funds.

### Proof of Concept
Already encoded as `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:321-356`): a whale supplies `1e9 * 10^18` units, a borrower takes 98%, time is advanced yearly, and within 40 years `update_indexes` returns `MATH_OVERFLOW` with `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`; `try_withdraw_raw` and `try_repay` then also fail with `MATH_OVERFLOW`, demonstrating the market — and all funds in it — is frozen.
