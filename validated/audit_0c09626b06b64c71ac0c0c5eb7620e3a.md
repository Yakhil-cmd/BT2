### Title

Deposits accepted while forced bad-debt socialization is pending are diluted by the queued write-down - ([File: contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs])

### Summary

`ForceSocializeBadDebt` operations are timelocked at the Sensitive delay tier, but the controller has no pending-operation check or deposit warning. While the operation is waiting, `supply` still accepts deposits into the affected debt market because the pool counts outstanding debt as backing. Anyone can execute the ready governance operation with `executor = None`, causing the queued cleanup to burn the debt and write down the market’s supply index. Suppliers who deposited during the delay lose part of their claim even though that debt was already insolvent when they entered.

### Finding Description

Governance resolves `AdminOperation::ForceSocializeBadDebt(account_id)` to the controller’s `force_socialize_bad_debt` function under `DelayTier::Sensitive`. [1](#0-0)  The operation waits for the configured sensitive delay, and `execute` permits `executor = None`, making execution permissionless once ready. [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

A forced cleanup only requires `total_debt > total_collateral`; unlike permissionless cleanup, it has no collateral dust cap. [4](#0-3)  Cleanup sends all borrow positions to `pool_seize_positions_call`. [5](#0-4)  For each borrow position, the pool converts the debt to an asset-denominated loss, calls `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`, and burns the debt shares. [6](#0-5)  That function proportionally reduces `supply_index`, imposing the loss on every current holder of supply shares. [7](#0-6) 

Before execution, however, `supply` admits a new deposit whenever `backing_shortfall == 0`. [8](#0-7)  The shortfall calculation counts the full ceil-valued outstanding debt as backing alongside cash, so a market containing the insolvent debt remains acceptable to depositors until the queued operation actually executes. [9](#0-8)  Controller `supply` performs no governance-operation check or other queued-socialization warning before crediting the deposit. [10](#0-9) 

The governance interface exposes `get_operation_state` and `get_operation_ledger`, but only for a caller that already knows the operation id; it exposes no reverse lookup from controller/account/market to the pending `ForceSocializeBadDebt` operation’s target and arguments. [11](#0-10)  Therefore, the controller entrypoint and pool market views do not give a depositor a direct way to discover that a market is queued for an index write-down.

### Impact Explanation

A supplier who deposits during the Sensitive delay is included in the supply-share denominator when the bad debt is later socialized. Their newly minted shares are written down by the same `supply_index` reduction as pre-existing shares, producing an immediate loss despite the debt having been insolvent before their deposit. [12](#0-11) 

This is a loss of user funds and can also transfer value to earlier suppliers or protocol revenue: the fresh deposit increases market cash before the common supplier claim is reduced. The condition is analogous to depositing after a slash request but before finalization.

### Likelihood Explanation

Likelihood is moderate because the prerequisite is a governance-proposed `ForceSocializeBadDebt` operation for an insolvent account whose collateral exceeds the permissionless dust threshold. Once that operation is queued, no privileged attacker action is needed: a depositor can enter normally, and any unprivileged address can execute the operation after it becomes ready. The absence of a controller-level pending-cleanup guard makes the affected deposit path reachable whenever the delayed operation is pending.

### Recommendation

- Store a market/account-level pending-bad-debt marker when `ForceSocializeBadDebt` is proposed, or expose a canonical governance lookup that maps a queued operation to its decoded controller call and `account_id`.
- Reject or explicitly override-gate `supply` and other share-minting flows for debt markets with a pending socialization that would write down their `supply_index`.
- At minimum, add a controller/pool view returning pending forced socializations affecting a `HubAssetKey`, and ensure frontends block or prominently warn before deposits.
- If deposits are intentionally left enabled, provide an override entrypoint with an explicit acknowledgement and emit an event containing the affected markets and expected write-down.

### Proof of Concept

1. Governance proposes `AdminOperation::ForceSocializeBadDebt(A)`, where account `A` has `total_debt > total_collateral` but collateral above the dust threshold. The resolved controller call uses the Sensitive delay. [1](#0-0) 
2. Before the delay expires, suppose the debt market has supplier claims of 100 tokens, cash of 50, and outstanding debt of 50. `backing_shortfall` is zero because backing is `50 cash + 50 debt = 100 claims`, so a new supplier deposits 10 more tokens and receives 10 shares at index `1.0`. [13](#0-12) 
3. After the sensitive delay, an unprivileged caller invokes `execute(None, controller, "force_socialize_bad_debt", [A], predecessor, salt)`. The ready operation invokes controller cleanup. [14](#0-13) 
4. Cleanup seizes the 50-token borrow position, computes `bad_debt = 50`, and reduces the debt market’s `supply_index` from `1.0` to approximately `60 / 110 = 0.545454`. [6](#0-5) [12](#0-11) 
5. The new supplier’s 10 shares are now worth approximately 5.45 tokens despite having deposited 10 tokens, while the market still has the supplier’s cash. The supplier had no controller call or market query that directly revealed the pending write-down before entering.

```rust
pub(crate) fn apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(cache: &mut Cache, bad_debt: Ray) {
    let total_supplied_value = cache.supplied().mul(cache.env(), cache.supply_index());

    if total_supplied_value == Ray::ZERO {
        return;
    }

    let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
    let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);

    let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
    let new_supply_index = cache
        .supply_index()
        .mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);

    cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));
```

```rust
/// Panics with `PoolInsolvent` if the market has a positive backing shortfall.

/// Backing = cash + ceiled debt value; claims = floored supply value.

/// Asset units by which supplier claims exceed cash + debt (0 if solvent).
pub(crate) fn backing_shortfall(cache: &Cache) -> i128 {
    let supplied_claim = cache.unscale_supply_floor(cache.supplied());
    let outstanding_debt = cache.unscale_borrow_ceil(cache.borrowed());
    let backing = cache.cash().saturating_add(outstanding_debt);
    supplied_claim.saturating_sub(backing).max(0)
```

```rust
    account_id: u64,

        account::AccountGuard::Supply,
        &mut cache,

    require_third_party_existing_supply(env, account_id, acct_id, caller, &account, &aggregated);

    process_deposit(env, caller, &mut account, &aggregated, &mut cache);

    finalize_position_flow(
        env,
        acct_id,
        &account,
        &mut cache,
        PositionSides::Supply,
        false,
    );
    acct_id
}
```
